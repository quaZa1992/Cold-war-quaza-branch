## Readable requirements

Internal signed, pending, cooldown, receipt, scenario and routing predicates remain executable inside native `hidden_trigger` blocks. Complete NOT/OR predicates are hidden together so the tooltip does not show an empty negation around an internal flag. Actual country existence, membership, party requirements and named construction sites remain inspectable.

Each agreement has a matching inverse tooltip. Prospective rewards for both countries remain unconditional display-only previews. Recipient approval and one-time transaction guards are preserved.

Resubmission entries disappear after ratification and while a reply is pending. A rejected or expired offer has a localized 180-day waiting-period requirement. No new offer source or automatic foreign acceptance is introduced.
