# IMPERIA COLD WAR: Cuba foreign policy approval design

This is the requested replacement design for Cuba's foreign-policy section, not an installation of new runtime code. Cuban national focuses select each specific proposal. The recipient RP country's acceptance or refusal decision controls signature and both countries' rewards. There is no Cuban decision menu for selecting those proposed packages.

The concept has 25 foreign focuses and 120 overall. Eleven consolidation or relocation measures free twelve old focus slots. The complete 13-focus Fidel programme remains. The exact proposed merges are in the model and the preview's budget panel. Full costs, focus duration, buildings with matching slots, and one-time research uses remain with their retained route. Soviet air defence becomes a shared military focus after FAR reorganisation, and limited advisory service remains compatible with territorial defence.

The new focus families cover Soviet commodity or industrial credits, separate COMECON admission and a membership-gated specialisation agreement, Mexican fuel or agricultural projects, Spanish machinery or training, Canadian rail or fisheries, East German training and the symbolic versus territorial island alternatives, staged American negotiations, Jamaican and Algerian health cooperation, and overseas advisory versus expeditionary policy with separate Angolan and Ethiopian offers.

Signed bilateral spirits have no calendar expiry. Bilateral war, a native embargo, or any diplomatic, financial, economic or military sanction between the signatories suspends both. Reconciliation restores the spirits once the restrictions end. A refused offer costs nothing and can be reopened only by the recipient through that recipient's diplomacy. A completed Cuban focus is not repeated. Contract effects describe policy and service arrangements; they do not create literal oil or fish production, trains, or deployed volunteers unless an explicit asset effect is listed.

Volunteer readiness proposes a 25% lower division requirement and a five-point lower tension gate, followed by either one or two extra volunteer divisions. Those two threshold modifiers need Cuban provider wiring at implementation. The native MD minimum is seven divisions, and the scenario starts without fielded Cuban units. Actual units, the usual volunteer diplomacy and recipient approval remain necessary. No available-workforce penalty is proposed for overseas service or medical cooperation.

`FOREIGN_POLICY_CONCEPT.json` is the reviewable source model. `tools/cuba_1970/foreign_concept/build.py` generates it and the preview without touching loaded game providers. `BROWSER_VALIDATION.json` records browser checks at 320, 736 and 1024 pixels, all 120 inspectors, real click after pan, zoom, and native art. `FINAL_CONCEPT_REVIEW.md` independently reviews coordinates, graph, counts and connectors; `MECHANICS_REVIEW.md` reviews APIs and whole-route stacking. These are source and browser checks, not an engine or multiplayer execution test of the redesigned tree.

Historical precedents and citations are in each new focus inspector and `tools/cuba_1970/FOREIGN_POLICY_RESEARCH.md`. Facts, historical institutions, alternate-history offers and proposed balance numbers are distinguished. Additional primary sources include the Canadian 1977 fisheries treaty and Jamaica's Ministry of Foreign Affairs confirmation that bilateral medical cooperation began in 1976. The island's 1972 dedication remains a historical symbolic act; a genuine transfer is explicitly alternate history.
