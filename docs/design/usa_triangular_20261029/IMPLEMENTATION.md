IMPERIA COLD WAR: American Triangular Diplomacy
=============================================

Implemented on 29 October 2026. The approved extension adds twelve focuses to the original forty-one-focus Kissinger chapter. The USA tree now registers 354 focuses, with 353 visible cards. Existing country politics, Kurdish campaigns and Gulf war entry remain intact.

## Strategy and consent

Trade and Exchanges offers a four-year programme from Chinese acceptance: China receives Productivity Growth +10%, the USA Consumer Goods Factor -3%, with Bureaucracy Cost +1% for both. Production growth uses the native multiplicative `country_productivity_growth_modifier`, not flat productivity growth. The stored 1460-day deadline continues during suspension. An expiry event and bounded refresh handle cleanup; restoring cooperation cannot restart the term or repeat the setup charge.

Play the China Card and Consolidate Détente are mutually exclusive preferential programmes. Both ordinary diplomatic channels and their basic cooperation remain available. Their additional American spirits and weekly funding require the corresponding accepted cooperation to remain active. The American Balance provides treaty review and further initiatives; it does not reset the exclusive focus choice.

The Chinese industrial mission, strategic consultations and regional package each require real Chinese consent. The regional package is concretely a recall of China's own military missions from a recipient selected by Beijing and approved by Washington. Final Chinese acceptance checks the current missions, recall permissions, Political Power, Command Power and American funding. It does not impose a fictional ceiling on lend-lease or end another government's war.

The Soviet research, supply, strategic-consultation and regional programmes are explicit clauses in the existing USA-SOV treaty paper. The counterpart reviews and signs the actual revision. Existing treaties can receive separately signed programme addenda without repeating old equipment reductions, recalls or sanctions fees. An old consultation receipt does not authorise new obligations. Migration removes the obsolete standalone Moscow spirits and charges while preserving actual treaty membership and assets.

Programme benefits and weekly payments follow the same conditions. Suspension and restoration preserve completed setup transactions. Shared regional influence is one American spirit even if independently signed Chinese and Soviet packages are both active; the USA still funds each recipient's actual separate programme. Public statements require their partner's separate approval before the temporary American spirit and international announcement.

Linkage requires an actually approved public statement, not only its completed focus badge. The funded Soviet regional programme also checks continuing absence of the selected military missions in each named recipient. Covered redeployment or a missing recipient suspends benefits and funding. Removing those missions restores the programme without repeating setup fees. The eight new treaty programme spirits and Chinese programme/statement spirits explicitly prevent civil-war inheritance.

## Costs and native presentation

All new setup charges and recurring payments are shares of current annual GDP. Weekly rates are actual weekly MD billions, not annual rates divided by 52. Quotes are recalculated at the final transaction; affordability failure leaves the proposed transaction unsigned and its assets intact. Signed weekly obligations may contribute to an ordinary budget deficit.

Focuses and recipient decisions show complete prospective bilateral spirits, signed modifiers, setup charges and weekly amounts. The Chinese exchange preview includes native 1460-day timed-idea grants for both countries. Treaty review shows actual selected protocols, quantities and programme clauses. Existing Cold War tension relief, treaty membership, news, expiry and withdrawal remain authoritative.

The compact layout retains the three triangle entries and moves the eight Iraqi cards and one Angola card to make room. IDs, prerequisite logic and old rewards are preserved. Three display captions are shortened to fit the current MD title field: A Wider Settlement, Linkage and Restraint, and The American Balance. All new focus and idea art references existing native assets. No image generation was used for this expansion.

## Historical precedents and balance choices

The Office of the Historian records [the Chinese opening and the Sino-Soviet strategic context](https://history.state.gov/milestones/1969-1976/rapprochement-china). This supports parallel American channels and strategic alternatives. The four-year productivity and consumer-goods figures are approved gameplay abstractions, not claims about measured historical economic effects.

Kissinger's [15 May 1972 memorandum on bilateral agreements](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76v14/d227) describes scientific, health, environmental, space and commercial cooperation. It supports reciprocal civilian programmes with administrative funding. The model's exact bonuses and GDP shares are balance choices.

The [16 May 1972 Kissinger-Huang Hua conversation](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76ve13/d129) records American consultation with Beijing about forthcoming Moscow agreements. It supports diplomatic signalling while retaining both channels. In gameplay, a joint public statement still requires the relevant human government's approval.

The State Department's [record of the 1972 grain agreement](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76v15/d7) supports the commercial programme's subject. It does not justify automatic virtual grain production or guaranteed treasury income. Those are therefore represented by the real commercial protocol, a separately funded supply programme and ordinary loan negotiations.
