IMPERIA COLD WAR
# Argentina 1970-1990 concept mechanics review

Audit of the approval-stage concept and its source builder, not runtime implementation. I reviewed `COLDWAR_ARG_design.json`, `COLDWAR_ARG_CONCEPT.md`, and `build_concept.py` as read-only source data, then checked the actual project target 2.0.3. No builder execution, game changes, or tests were performed. Findings distinguish concept conflicts from work intentionally deferred to integration.

## Initial defects and refreshed-model disposition

The initial draft had the three conflicts recorded below. The refreshed 299-focus model resolves them; they are retained as audit history, not outstanding blockers. The current source still has the approval/integration gaps listed in the final recheck.

### Uranium survey gate conflicts with target startup deposits

The target's `on_startup` handler calls `COLDWAR_uranium_setup_1970_deposits` in a 1970 game (`common/on_actions/COLDWAR_quaza_zzzzzz_imp_uranium_on_actions.txt:12`). That setup already calls `imp_uranium_add_deposit` for Cordoba state 450 with one mine slot and three output per level (`common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_quaza_imp_uranium_effects.txt:472`); the table also seeds known deposits in Cuyo state 443, Noroeste state 446 and Patagonia state 454. The refreshed `COLDWAR_ARG_uranium_survey` now treats Los Gigantes as an already surveyed reserve and grants feasibility research instead of registering it again.

More importantly, the live target trigger for paid mine investment checks the deposit modifier, mine-per-level data, mine-slot count and free state capacity (`common/scripted_triggers/00_investment_scripted_triggers.txt:665-697`); it does not check a concept survey receipt. The refreshed model explicitly leaves that route available independently, and its sponsored mine checks no existing or under-construction mine plus current ownership, control and native capacity before charging. The model states that the $4 billion focus funds only one sponsored mine, and that an earlier native investment bypasses the grant. This resolves the duplicated deposit and hidden native-investment gate at design level. No raw state-history uranium or automatic mine output is proposed.

### Sale proceeds can be collected twice

In the initial draft, `COLDWAR_ARG_menem_reform` and `COLDWAR_ARG_rapid_sales` could both grant sale proceeds. The refreshed privatization event now authorizes policy only and charges its selected option; its text names `asset_sales` as the sole receipt-bearing transaction. The decision grants the $3 billion once under an incumbent and replay guard. This removes the duplicate sale receipt.

### Timed Barracks Resistance durations disagree

In the initial model, the `accountability` option attached 365-day Barracks Resistance while its option effects lasted 730 days, but the model did not encode those durations separately. The refreshed event now carries `duration = 730` and `add_spirit_duration = 365` for accountability, while campaign inquiry uses 365 for both. The description states which duration applies to each effect. This resolves the serialization ambiguity.

The prior apparent equipment-cost concern is resolved in current data: `milcost` maps to target-native `equipment_cost_multiplier_modifier`, labelled "Equipment Operating Expenditure". It is registered at target `common/modifier_definitions/money_modifier_definitions.txt:44`, consumed in `common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt:3281,3291` and included in defence cost breakdowns at :3328 and :3382. Positive values increase and negative values reduce this equipment operating-cost component. The newer installed donor's different `military_spending_cost_multiplier_modifier` math is not target evidence.

## Source-supported values and reachability

- The builder's `STATS` map uses supported native or project-defined modifier keys. In particular, `agricolture_productivity_modifier` is the official MD spelling; `resource_export_multiplier_modifier` changes resource-export cash, not production; `civilian_factories_productivity` is read by MD tax/GDP calculations, not HOI factory throughput. `industrial_capacity_factory`, `industrial_capacity_dockyard`, native `production_speed_*_factor`, military performance, fiscal cost and foreign-influence modifiers have separate real consumers. Line evidence and key semantics are recorded in `mechanics_reference.md` under "Verified effect readers and material limits". Avoid silently presenting MD GDP/productivity as physical throughput.
- Direct military experience rewards, if later added, must use `army_experience`, `navy_experience` and `air_experience`; the current concept has no XP reward. Doctrine reduction is supported for `land_doctrine` and `naval_doctrine`; the concept has no doctrine reward and should not infer an air-doctrine category.
- Target categories are real and have Cold War rows: `CAT_industry` occurs on 1936-era technologies and is used by the 1970 ISR tree; `CAT_computing` includes a 1945 technology; `CAT_aircraft` appears in target non-BBA rows including `mdcw_jet_generations.txt` (1963, 1972, 1975 and 1976 rows) and `mdcw_air_reconnaissance_rpvs.txt` (1964 and 1978); `CAT_tanks` includes `MBT_1` (1965) and `MBT_2` (1975); `CAT_surface_ships` is used by the target naval tech tree; `CAT_nuclear_reactors` begins in target reactor techs in 1985. These research bonuses can be used during the horizon and do not grant technology by themselves.
- The target's dynamic doctrine reward examples support land and naval doctrine cost reductions. Native XP effects are direct `army_experience`, `navy_experience` and `air_experience`; the concept currently has no XP or doctrine rewards.
- The concept has no en dash or em dash in player-facing draft text. No workforce-size penalty is present.

## Agreement arithmetic and mechanisms

- Bolivian gas is arithmetically consistent with the stated design: ARG pays $0.003 billion weekly, split as $0.002 billion transferred to BOL and $0.001 billion operating expense; BOL receives $0.002 billion and pays a separate $0.0005 billion weekly operating expense. The builder contains a correction comment documenting this split. Do not model gas flow with `resource_export_multiplier_modifier` or automatic resource production.
- Soviet grain is arithmetically consistent: SOV pays $0.007 billion weekly, split as $0.006 billion transferred to ARG and $0.001 billion operating expense; ARG receives $0.006 billion and pays its own $0.001 billion operating expense. This is a monetary transfer and the text correctly avoids the mineral-export modifier.
- Other agreement setup costs and fixed weekly expenses are displayed separately in billion-dollar MD units. Agreement focus cost is zero by design because the one-time setup debit occurs on recipient acceptance. The contract spirits and one-time research/building assets are described as non-repeating.
- The contract policy states both-country consent, no expiry, reciprocal suspension/restoration during war, native embargo or any of four mirrored Imperia sanction classes, and active parent requirements. These are sound design requirements. Argentina-specific provider and receiver decisions, dual-spirit ownership, mirrored guards, replay receipts and consumers in the weekly income/expense aggregation are not in runtime yet. Treat that absence as an integration requirement, not an existing-game defect. Weekly transfer readers and consumers are in `mechanics_reference.md`.

## Building state ownership and transaction requirements

Concept/build-builder building targets are consistent with 1970 state ownership for ARG states 448, 449, 450, 453, 454 and 457; URG state 893; and CHL state 468. For the cross-border offers, state 893 is correct for Uruguay and state 468 for Central Chile. The relevant history files set the corresponding owners and, where specified, 1970 controllers to those countries. Argentina state 450 (Cordoba) contains a pre-existing nuclear reactor level in 1970; no proposed free Atucha reactor should be added. State 457 is owned and controlled by ARG and includes port province 11009, Río Grande. State 26 (Falklands) is owned and controlled by ENG in 1970.

Every listed building reward carries an equal shared-slot addition in builder data. The concept states the required same-transaction ordering. Implementation must still gate on the actual country owning and controlling the target state, actual free/native capacity and building level limit. Province-level construction also requires a valid target province. Dynamic agreement construction must not activate on proposal, preview, or a rejected decision. These are integration checks rather than design defects.

## Party and leader reachability

- The January 1970 ARG history loads ruling_party 22 / Nationalist Military Junta at 60%, with slot 13 / Justicialist Party at 17%, Onganía incumbent and an expiry of 1970-06-18 (`history/countries/ARG - Argentina.txt:435-468`). Exact native ideology triggers exist, including `nationalist_military_junta_are_in_power`, `neutrality_neutral_autocracy_are_in_power`, `western_social_democrats_are_in_power`, `western_liberals_are_in_power`, `emerging_communist_state_are_in_power` and the exact trigger for every other MD ideology slot. Party registry IDs and MD ideology IDs must remain distinct.
- The concept correctly requires branches to stay visible, uses actual party requirements for execution, preserves completed national gains, and plans only the incumbent party's spirit/weekly cost to apply. CGTA and proposed PSD are mutually exclusive. The PSD identity and slot-18 mapping, UCeDe succession from the PCI tradition, and reconciliation between Argentina's local registry and MD native ideology/leader pools remain explicit approval/integration gaps, not complete native support.
- Existing native Argentina leader pool references cover only selected shared ideology slots (2, 20 and 22) and include Raúl Alfonsín, Juan Perón, Carlos Menem and Roberto Marcelo Levingston. Those identities do not naturally correspond to Argentina local registry IDs UCRP slot 3, PJ slot 13 and the intended Levingston route. `ARG_political_leaders.txt` defines `set_leader_ARG`, but a repository-wide search found no caller. The concept's "actual leader" requirements are not implementable until those identities and call sites are reconciled.
- In the initial draft, exact leader guards used spellings that differed from target pool names. The refreshed model now stores `Juan Perón`, `Raúl Alfonsín`, and both `Juan Carlos Ongania` and `Juan Carlos Ongánía`, matching target pool/history forms (`mdcw_historical_native_pools.txt:378,395,415,450,13771`; `ARG_political_leaders.txt:488`; `history/countries/ARG - Argentina.txt:456`). This resolves the exact-name mismatch. Actual government and leader installation remains an integration requirement. The concept's June transition still names Lanusse/Levingston as actual incoming leaders; no existing Argentina leader installation caller was found.
- `equipment_cost_multiplier_modifier` scales only the equipment-operating-cost portion of defence expenditure, not the defence-industry spending slider or personnel costs. The concept's label "Equipment Operating Expenditure" matches the target consumer more precisely than the generic "Military Expenditure" label used by the newer donor.
- The event concept uses the historically plausible 9 June 1970 command crisis, but start history expires Onganía 18 June 1970. The incumbent check and event scheduling must resolve that nine-day discrepancy and avoid relying on a leader who native on-action code has already retired. This is an integration gap identified by the concept itself.
- The Menem asset-sale decision requires Menem actually incumbent and a permanent receipt, which is the right anti-replay guard. The native Menem pool entry is attached to a shared ruling-party ideology that does not match the local PJ registry slot, so do not infer that the current election/leader system can make the decision reachable without reconciliation.

## Overall disposition

The concept's structural decisions on visible party branches, conditional permanent agreements, setup fees, weekly transfer arithmetic and separate one-time receipts are supported at design level. The initial uranium, exact leader-name, privatization replay and event-duration conflicts have been corrected in the refreshed model. The weekly payment providers, consent events, conditional spirit synchronization and local political transitions remain intentionally unimplemented and are not classified as runtime failures. The current concept correctly names URG's construction state Rio de la Plata, which is state 893 and is owned and controlled by URG in 1970; state 300 is Huila, not Uruguay.

### Remaining integration dependencies, not known model defects

- Argentina-specific provider/consumer code for government/leader programme activation, suspension and reinstatement, as well as weekly upkeep and agreement transfers, is not implemented. Permanent focus gains and one-time transactions must not replay when the current programme changes.
- Local registry identities and native ruling-party ideology checks are not interchangeable. Fifteen programmes can use existing slot triggers as preliminary branch guards, but those triggers alone do not identify all local parties or leaders. PSD remains a proposed exclusive incumbent identity under shared slot 18 and conflicts with CGTA unless an explicit identity discriminator is implemented and approved. UCeDe remains a post-1982 identity under PCI tradition, not a starting party.
- The target native historical leader pool enables Argentina for shared slots 2, 20 and 22. It does not by itself provide normal PJ slot-13 or UCR slot-3 transitions. Perón, Alfonsín and Menem availability and installation therefore still need real election/succession integration. The model's June 9 command crisis still needs scheduling against Onganía's June 18 history expiry and actual incumbent state. This is an integration/date-consistency requirement, not a false claim that a focus can install a leader.
- For the final UK treaty, all transfer, payment and spirit transaction effects must share the same accepted decision and one-time guard. Suspension of the standing agreement must not reverse the already accepted territory transfer or repeat compensation.
- Effect serialization is only proposed code. Runtime implementation still needs to prove tooltip display, decision/event scopes, loaded-state capacity, multiplayer recipient consent, and that proposal/preview never charges or grants. These are integration dependencies, not known defects in the reviewed model. No runtime files or tests were part of this review.
