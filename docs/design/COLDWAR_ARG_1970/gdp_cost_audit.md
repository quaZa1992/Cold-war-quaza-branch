# Argentina 1970 GDP cost audit

Read-only economic baseline and cost recommendations for the Argentina national programme. Runtime code and generators were not changed.

## January 1970 baseline

The native ARG startup fixture log records the completed calibration on 1 January 1970: `gdp_total = 107.38543`, displayed GDP `31.58395`, GDP/c `1.32207`, and population `23.88955` million. The source target is `31.584` billion historical 1970 dollars, multiplied by `3.4` to keep Millennium Dawn's internal 2000-price money scale. The value used for costs in runtime should therefore be ARG's live `gdp_total`, about 107.385 billion internal dollars, not the display variable or the 31.584 historical-dollar target.

The same native log reports weekly income 2.553, weekly expenses 2.13938, balance 0.41362 and treasury 0. The focus fees currently generated by `core.py` are one-time treasury changes. They must not be divided by 52. Divide by 52 only when converting an annual GDP share into a recurring weekly treasury flow.

## Installed GDP cost schedule

`tools/argentina_1970/budget.py` maps the approved legacy cost weights to one-time shares of the paying country's live `gdp_total`: weight 1 = 0.2%, 2 = 0.4%, 3 = 0.6%, 4 = 1.0%, 5 = 1.5%, and 6 = 2.0%. At the observed ARG baseline (107.38543 bn internal GDP), these equal 0.21477, 0.42954, 0.64431, 1.07385, 1.61078, and 2.14771 bn respectively. Zero-weight actions remain free. The same `money(weight)` generator is used for focus/event/decision preview and guarded payment; positive-cost choices also require `gdp_total > 0` in their hidden execution/availability guards. No treasury balance guard was added.

The generated focus effects use `gdp_total × -share` for both `preview_focus_*` and the guarded `complete_*` payment. Preview rewards are display-only through the existing effect-tooltip path. The 299-focus and 7-decision set has a share-weight total of 1.13 GDP across its mutually exclusive focus branches, before decisions; this is not a single route burden. Route totals still need to be computed from whichever path is being balanced.

The asset-sale decision deliberately retains its previously approved +3 bn flat treasury proceeds (`money = -3`), so that proceeds are not changed by this cost-only repricing. This is an explicit exception, not an accidental GDP-scaled cost.

## Recurring programme and agreement flows

Legacy recurring weights are converted to annual GDP shares by multiplying the source weight by 0.5, then to weekly MD units by multiplying live GDP and dividing by 52. Thus a legacy 0.001 weight is 0.05% of annual GDP, paid weekly as `gdp_total × 0.0005 ÷ 52`. Focus previews use the same converter. Programme refresh resets the aggregate weekly share, rebuilds it from the currently eligible applied programme flags, then scales once by `gdp_total / 52`. Event refresh follows the same reset, rebuild and scale pattern. Daily refresh does not make another setup payment or accumulate duplicate recurring charges; treaty refresh reconciles ideas and does not charge setup fees.

Treaty preview and execution share the same setup and recurring calculation helpers. Each signatory's own recurring operating burden uses its own GDP. The explicitly modeled trade transfers instead use ARG GDP in both the payer and receiver calculation so the amount debited matches the amount credited:

- Bolivia gas: Argentina's operating charge is 0.05% of ARG GDP per year plus a 0.10% ARG-GDP transfer to Bolivia; Bolivia also pays 0.025% of BOL GDP per year. The transfer is added to BOL income at the same weekly amount charged to Argentina.
- Soviet grain: the Soviet Union pays 0.05% of SOV GDP per year plus a 0.30% ARG-GDP transfer to Argentina; Argentina also pays 0.05% of ARG GDP per year. The transfer is credited to Argentina at the same weekly amount charged to the Soviet Union.
- UK sovereignty settlement: Argentina pays 2.0% of ARG GDP at signing, including a 1.5% ARG-GDP compensation transfer to Britain. Britain pays 0.2% of its own GDP setup cost. Preview and accepted effects both include these transfers and the state transfer.

Treaty offer and acceptance guards require positive GDP for both signatories. Acceptance rechecks consent, both pending flags, pair conditions, parent treaty, territory/ownership and positive GDP in the execution effect. Recurring flows are added only while the signed agreement is active; refresh does not replay setup, buildings, research or territorial transfer. Static inspection found no treasury-balance gates and no scripted monetary decimal with more than five fractional digits in the emitted Argentina core/treaty effects or decisions.

Static audit boundary: these findings establish generator/source parity and emitted formulas. They do not prove exact game-rendered amount formatting or behavior under live GDP changes; the native preview inspector and runtime fixture remain the evidence for those behaviors.
