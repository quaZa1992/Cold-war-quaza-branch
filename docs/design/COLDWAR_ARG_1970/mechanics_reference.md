## Shared Imperia systems relevant to Argentina

- **Diplomatic relationships and sanctions:** Existing bilateral eligibility patterns check existence, peace, native embargo and all mirrored classes: diplomatic, financial, economic and military. Each class has both `_on_us` and `_by_us` arrays. The closest patterns are `common/scripted_triggers/COLDWAR_CHL_1970_diplomacy.txt:1-33` and `common/scripted_triggers/COLDWAR_CUB_1970_bilateral.txt`. A permanent signed agreement should be held by both countries' spirits, deactivate on war or a mutual sanction/embargo, and restore without setup fees or one-time reward replay. The recipient country must accept or refuse. No Argentina-specific bilateral offer/contract package was found; shared diplomacy is a reusable API pattern, not evidence an Argentina agreement already exists.
- **Agreement display and state:** Use the established recipient-event and spirit ownership patterns in the reviewed Cold War country modules. The dynamic idea in both countries must have a current active-parent and conditions check. Acceptance-preview effects should remain display-only; execution guards must do consent and one-time payment/assets separately. Don't retain a no-expiry agreement after peace or sanction eligibility fails.
- **Uranium:** The live economic resource, trade and mine system exists. Investment availability requires a surveyed deposit modifier and `imp_uranium_per_level`, enough mine slots, and free building capacity (`common/scripted_triggers/00_investment_scripted_triggers.txt:664-697`). MD money code reads actual built uranium mine levels and produced uranium, and the uranium resource is connected to storage/exports (`common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt` around lines 3388, 3704-3729, 4728-4766, 5092-5098, 5302-5303). No Argentine uranium deposit or built mine was found in the loaded state-history search. Any Argentine uranium branch should start with zero production, require a survey then paid mine construction, and must not write automatic resource output into state history.
- **Nuclear energy and weapons:** MD has nuclear-reactor investment and technology/economy systems, and Argentina has historically credible civilian nuclear institutions; reactor development is supportable if the approved design is about CNEA, research, power and fuel infrastructure. It must be distinct from a nuclear-weapons route; do not grant a warhead or weapons research by implication.
- **Space:** The `space_race_1970` and `COLDWAR_space_race_cooperation` modules are present with their own project-specific capabilities, partner operations, acceptance and crew/infrastructure rules. A small state should join a real partner programme or provide a historically plausible ground station/research role rather than receive superpower launch capacity. Confirm Argentina eligibility in the actual space programme triggers before promising an effect.
- **Organisations:** Current native Argentina military industrial organisations exist under `common/military_industrial_organization/organizations/MD_ARG_organizations.txt`, including Fabricaciones Militares, Bersa, CITEFA, TAMSE and Cicaré. These are eligible MIOs; a focus may support them only through valid MIO APIs and actual history/availability. Regional organisations such as MERCOSUR have real dynamic modifiers, but MERCOSUR's historical 1991 foundation is outside a 1970-1990 horizon. Do not use an early membership spirit unless the concept explicitly models an alternate history and its admission/consent mechanics.


## Verified effect readers and material limits

- Standing bilateral treasury flows require central weekly calculation hooks; a focus-time treasury adjustment is a one-time stock change and must not be repeated each week. The weekly update calls `calculate_additional_income_rate` and `calculate_additional_expense_rate` at installed `common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt:258-270`. The former aggregates into `additional_income_rate` (starts at :801), the latter into `additional_expenses_rate` (starts at :3357). `update_display` includes those values in recurring income and expense, then sets `treasury_rate = display_income - display_expense` at :4254-4321. Existing bilateral-ish/project patterns are implemented as guarded variable contributions within these central readers; merely placing a weekly-looking value in a country idea does not make the economy consume it. A standing foreign payment needs payer and recipient contributions in the relevant readers, keyed to the accepted active agreement and the same peace, country-existence and mutual-sanction/embargo conditions. Model grain/resource delivery separately through real production or transfer mechanics; `resource_export_multiplier_modifier` changes export cash revenue and does not transfer grain.
- Project-target categories include `CAT_industry` (`common/technologies/industry.txt:68`, used by `common/national_focus/ISR70_focus.txt:1172-1173`), `CAT_computing` (`common/technologies/engineering.txt:796+`), `CAT_nuclear_reactors` (`common/technologies/industry.txt:4985+`), `CAT_surface_ships` (`common/technologies/naval.txt:68+`), `CAT_tanks` (`common/technologies/armor.txt:85+`) and `CAT_aircraft` (`common/technologies/mdcw_jet_generations.txt:37+`, `common/technologies/mdcw_air_reconnaissance_rpvs.txt:25+`, as well as `bombers.txt` and BBA aircraft techs). The target has non-BBA Cold War aircraft rows, so this reward is not limited to BBA. Verify exact date and prerequisite of the intended technology; category bonuses do not grant future technologies.
- Native focus rewards add experience with direct keys `army_experience`, `navy_experience` and `air_experience`, as shown in installed `common/national_focus/00_generic.txt:4315, 5201, 5915`. Do not generate an `add_army_experience`-style effect name. Land and naval doctrine reductions are used by MD focus rewards; no supported general `air_doctrine` reward should be inferred from the other two.

- `civilian_factories_productivity`, `military_factories_productivity`, singular `dockyard_productivity`, and the misspelled native key `agricolture_productivity_modifier` are registered country modifiers in installed MD `common/modifier_definitions/money_modifier_definitions.txt:360-407`. Their actual readers in `common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt` are finance calculations: `calculate_tax_gain` multiplies factory counts by the modifier at lines 461, 482 and 503; agriculture tax reads it at 545. `calculate_gdp` reads these modifiers at lines 5635, 5642, 5649 and 5663. They alter MD modelled output, tax and GDP, not a directly proven HOI factory throughput multiplier. The dockyard key is `dockyard_productivity`, not `dockyards_productivity`. Use native `industrial_capacity_factory` or `industrial_capacity_dockyard` if the intended promise is actual factory production; MD maps these in dynamic modifiers but the engine-side operation is not the same as the MD GDP formula.
- The agriculture productivity key is `agricolture_productivity_modifier` (official spelling). It enters the MD agri tax multiplier at `00_money_system.txt:404-409,545`, and agricultural district GDP at 5663. The distinct `production_speed_agriculture_district_factor` drives building output through installed `common/dynamic_modifiers/00_money_dynamic_modifiers.txt:41-58`.
- `resource_export_multiplier_modifier` is registered at `money_modifier_definitions.txt:121-125` and read for oil, steel, aluminium, tungsten, chromium, rubber, microchips and composites export revenue at `00_money_system.txt:4138-4229`; these values are added to `resource_sale_rate` at 4238-4268 and the helper is called by the economy update at 255. The computed `*_exports` feed the recurring sale rate; any player-facing reward should say resource export revenue, not resource production.
- `bureaucracy_cost_multiplier_modifier`, `police_cost_multiplier_modifier`, `education_cost_multiplier_modifier`, `health_cost_multiplier_modifier`, and `social_cost_multiplier_modifier` are percentage-valued country modifiers, with definitions in `money_modifier_definitions.txt:58-114`. Their live consumer is `update_population_spending_rates` at `00_money_system.txt:3087-3118` and `update_education_rate` at 3171. They scale base expense formulas; positive values raise spending and negative values lower it, subject to the shared `common_money_modifier`.
- `CAT_industry` is present on many active technologies, for example the production-line technology at `common/technologies/industry.txt:68`; it is also used by the existing 1970 tree at `common/national_focus/ISR70_focus.txt:1172-1173`. `CAT_agriculture` is real and appears on the agriculture technology series around `industry.txt:7862`, but those technologies start in 1990, so it is only useful at the end of this design horizon. The project's non-BBA `mdcw_jet_generations.txt`, `mdcw_air_reconnaissance_rpvs.txt` and other Cold War aircraft tech rows use `CAT_aircraft` within the concept period.
- `imp_sanction_resistance` is registered as a country-scope percentage in `common/modifier_definitions/mdcw_imperia_globalism_modifiers.txt:22-27`, exposed through dynamic modifier mappings such as `common/dynamic_modifiers/mdcw_imperia_globalism_dynamic_modifiers.txt:6`. The consumer is `common/scripted_effects/imp_sanctions_effects.txt:135-152,196,248,322`: it computes `1 - resistance`, multiplies diplomatic, financial, economic and military sanction burden, then clamps the outcome to 0..1. Resistance +0.10 reduces those calculated sanction burdens by 10% before other limits; it is not resistance to a specific country or native embargo.
- The start scenario has a concrete south Atlantic port: `history/states/457-Tierra del Fuego.txt:12-24,41-43` assigns state 457 to ARG and places naval base level 4 at province 11009, localized as Río Grande (`localisation/english/victory_points_l_english.yml:4342`). Other Argentina ports in 1970 state files include Bahía Blanca province 1639 at level 4 and Buenos Aires province 12364 at level 6 in state 453; Comodoro Rivadavia province 10953 and Río Gallegos province 5173 have level 2 each in state 454. For construction, check current ownership, control and slot capacity.
- The current project target does not register or consume `military_spending_cost_multiplier_modifier`; that key appears only in the newer installed donor. Use target-native `equipment_cost_multiplier_modifier` when promising Equipment Operating Expenditure. It affects equipment maintenance within MD defence expenditure, not the whole military-spending slider or personnel costs.
- Interest, inflation and debt are real stateful systems, but their update pathways are not interchangeable. `interest_rate_multiplier_modifier` contributes to an annual percentage interest-rate figure and the formula clamps to 0.8-50 at `00_money_system.txt:2600-2645`. Inflation is an economy variable used to scale costs and spending; do not promise a direct inflation change from a cost multiplier. Prefer the established policy/event effects that change the live monetary variables or use explicit cost modifiers with the correct preview.

## Building and state safeguards

Any focus, decision or event adding a building level must first grant one shared building slot per level in that same state, including infrastructure and forts. Put the slot grant and construction inside the same one-time transaction guard. Separately verify that Argentina owns and controls the state and that the state can accept the intended building. This rule applies even where a building does not consume shared slots. For recipient-approved construction, show the exact state, level, slots, setup costs, recurring costs and other material effects in the locked focus's preview.

## Confidence boundary

The evidence establishes source-level support and current MD argument forms. It does not prove rendered focus tooltip, trigger scope at runtime, game-load acceptance, balance, or multiplayer consent flow. The eventual concept must be validated against history and current design approval; implementation, if later approved, needs static checks and a live 1970 start/tooltip/party-transition test.
