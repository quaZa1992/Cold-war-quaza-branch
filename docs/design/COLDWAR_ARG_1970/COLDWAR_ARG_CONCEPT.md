IMPERIA COLD WAR
# Argentina: January 1970 to 1990

**Approval-stage concept. 299 focuses, 67 mutual-exclusion pairs, 20 bilateral offers and 16 events. No loaded game files have been changed.**

The preview, catalogue and spatial fixture share one coordinate model. Default focus cost 5 means 35 days; selected reviews cost 3 means 21 days. Values are proposed multiplayer balance, not measurements of historical causal effects.

Native MD comparison: installed workshop version 2.0.4, local Cold War base metadata 2.0.3, HOI4 1.19. France and Russia are reference trees; Argentina uses its own institutions, parties and budget. All player-facing language is English.

## Reading and political ownership

Proposed: preserve earned values per exact party and leader. Refresh on government/leader changes, weekly tick and load. Only the current owner contributes its programme spirit and weekly costs; earned buildings, cash, XP, research and ratified contracts never replay.

National economic/institutional choices remain earned across governments. A government change is not a free policy reset. Programme-level exclusions remain saved and visible.

All party branches remain visible. Exact native current-party triggers gate execution, and actual leader identity additionally gates named leader programmes. CGTA and the provisional PSD alias cannot be incumbents simultaneously. The native Yes/No party requirement resolves the actual Argentine party name, without inventing a governing-current label.

The corporate-charter modifiers operate only while that constitutional settlement remains in force. Actual constitutional restoration removes A Revolution without Consent and deactivates the corporate institutional programme, even if a different party conducts the transition. Earned technical skills and one-time assets retain their separate ownership.

Party-sourced event programmes require their exact incumbent party and, for the Menem mandate, exact leader. They deactivate when the owner leaves; a return before fixed expiry restores only the remaining term. National crises and already incurred liabilities follow their separately stated lifetimes. Event option modifiers and an additional attached spirit have separate explicit durations.

Precedent dates are research anchors, not automatic unlock dates for every negotiated policy. Earlier bilateral agreements are explicit alternate-history accelerations requiring both players. Named formations and leaders still require real organizational/leadership transitions. Native party trigger ideology names identify engine array slots, while visible Argentine names come from the actual local registry.

The 1970 startup already registers Los Gigantes in state450: one mine slot, three uranium per level. Raw production and built mines start at zero. Feasibility adds no deposit and never gates native investment; the sponsored mine checks existing construction and capacity before its payment. No automatic raw state-history production.

## Starting spirits

### A Revolution without Consent

The presidency claims a mandate to reorganize society while parties remain dissolved and the largest popular movement is excluded.

- Political Power Gain +8%; Stability -10%
- Remedy: Constitutional restoration removes this spirit. A corporatist settlement replaces it with its own benefits and liabilities.

### The Cordobazo Aftermath

Worker and student mobilization has broken the promise of social peace. Disputes at the industrial centers continue to disrupt production.

- Stability -7%; Factory Output -6%; Police Expenditure +4%
- Remedy: National labor settlement or a completed party labor compact removes it. Repression replaces it with a different burden.

### The Stop-Go Economy

Imported capital goods, contested incomes policy and uneven foreign exchange earnings make sustained industrial expansion difficult.

- Construction Speed -7%; Interest Rate +0.75 pp
- Remedy: A funded stabilization programme replaces this problem. This does not reset native inflation or public debt.

### Universities after Intervention

Intervention and the departure of researchers have damaged an established scientific community. Argentina retains considerable technical capacity.

- Research Speed -7%
- Remedy: University autonomy, technical restoration or a party science programme removes it. No workforce-size penalty.

### Competing Service Commands

The services remain political arbiters as well as military institutions. Rival chains of influence complicate common doctrine and budgets.

- Division Organization -5%; Naval Organization -5%; Equipment Operating Expenditure +6%
- Remedy: Joint staff reform removes the operational penalty; civilian control separately settles political authority.

### A Republic of Uneven Corridors

Industrial concentration in the littoral contrasts with distant northern and southern communities and expensive transport connections.

- Infrastructure Construction Speed -8%; Supply Consumption +6%
- Remedy: Funded north-south transport and provincial services remove this burden.
