# Argentina 1970 political leader routing

## Purpose

The runtime leader router creates a historical officeholder only after a real change in the governing party. This note records the death-date boundaries and identifies the explicitly counterfactual officeholders used to keep approved party routes playable from 1970 through 1990. An incumbent who remains in office in an alternate history is not removed merely because the historical date passes. On a later party transition, the router selects the officeholder eligible for that date.

## Historical boundaries and counterfactual continuity

| Party route | Historical record | Runtime routing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UDELPA, party 0 | The Casa Rosada biography records that former president Pedro Eugenio Aramburu was killed on 1 June 1970. | Aramburu may be newly selected before 1 June 1970. After that date, a later UDELPA transition selects the counterfactual UDELPA National Executive. |
| UCR, party 3 | The Argentine Chamber of Deputies records Ricardo Balbín's death on 9 September 1981. Raúl Alfonsín was elected president in 1983. | Balbín may be newly selected before the approved 10 September 1981 boundary. Alfonsín is available from 10 September 1981 as an explicitly counterfactual early electoral alternative. |
| Communist Party, party 4 | CEDEINCI's biographical dictionary records Gerónimo Arnedo Álvarez's death on 12 June 1980. Sources consulted differ on the later date at which Patricio Echegaray became secretary general, so this router does not assign an unverified individual successor. | Arnedo may be newly selected before 12 June 1980. A later transition selects the counterfactual Communist Party Central Committee. |
| Ateneo, party 6 | Biographical references identify Mario Amadeo as living from 1911 to 1983; the date used by the runtime roster is 19 March 1983. | Amadeo may be newly selected before 19 March 1983. A later transition selects the counterfactual Ateneo Republican Council. |

The collective councils are invented officeholder identities for alternate-history continuity, not claims that those bodies historically held the Argentine presidency. They use the registered ideology type of their existing party route and do not create a new party or ideology.

Where no verified portrait is available, the router uses the existing native-sized `gfx/interface/coldwar_politburo/portrait_unknown.dds` silhouette (156 x 210). This is an explicit missing-photograph placeholder, not an invented historical likeness. The existing asset's original author and separate redistribution permission are unknown; see the project's main notice. No portrait binary was modified or newly distributed by this integration.
