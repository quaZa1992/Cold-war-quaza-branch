# Argentina 1970-1990 concept: independent history and design coherence review

## Scope and overall assessment

The first-pass review below assessed the earlier 296-focus catalogue. The final disposition at the end audits the generated 299-focus JSON, 20 agreements and 16 events and supersedes any earlier finding that changed. No builder or game files were changed.

The final concept retains a strong historical spine: a difficult but not yet 1980s-debt-crisis January 1970, real 1970 and later succession points, and a 1982 war whose outcome depends on the actual campaign. Menem, Alfonsín, Brazil rapprochement, nuclear safeguards and postwar UK restoration are contingent routes. No starting workforce penalty is present. The final disposition below records which first-pass concerns were resolved and which remain.

The effects, weekly costs and balance values are design proposals. The cited historical records support the existence and direction of a policy problem or relationship; they do not validate any particular percentage, $billion payment or recurring weekly transfer.

## Political routes and chronology

- **FFAA / Onganía / Levingston / Lanusse:** the core sequence is sound. Onganía remained in office until the commanders forced his resignation on 8-9 June 1970; Levingston followed in June 1970 and Lanusse in March 1971. `The Commanders and the President` offers the real Levingston succession and clearly marks an early Lanusse appointment as alternate history. `COLDWAR_ARG_ongania_corporate` carries an Onganía-incumbent guard. Preserve these exact leader checks. `COLDWAR_ARG_ferrer_industry` and Levingston-associated policies can be alternate earlier policy choices, but should not imply Aldo Ferrer already held the economy ministry in January; he took office in October 1970. [FRUS, 10 June 1970 on Onganía's fall](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76ve10/d63); [World Bank 1971 paper on the Ferrer shift](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/088121924460069302-0560011971/original/WorldBankGroupArchivesfolder30275575.pdf).
- **PJ and Menem:** the social-pact, labor, provincial and leadership-conflict choices fit Peronism's breadth. Perón arbitration, Menem's actual-incumbent guard and later market path are separated well. Keep Menem's reforms in 1989. Law 23,696 was sanctioned 17 August 1989 and directly concerned state reform, privatizations and private-capital participation. `COLDWAR_ARG_terms_restructuring` is a misleading event title for asset sale/service obligations; rename it to an asset-transfer or privatization terms title so it does not confuse enterprise sales with sovereign-debt restructuring. The second event option's treasury gain must remain a one-time proposed sale receipt, never an ordinary fiscal effect. [Argentine Law 23,696, 17 August 1989](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/---98/actualizacion).
- **UCR:** Balbín as the early opposition leader and Alfonsín as the later elected president are correctly distinguished. Alfonsín alone has the actual-incumbent gate in the presented catalogue. `COLDWAR_ARG_balbin_compromise` is named as Balbín's but appears gated only by party, not leader. Either add a real Balbín leader condition if that named programme is meant personally, or retitle it as a party programme. The 1970 cross-party `La Hora del Pueblo` precedent is well used. The Menem, Alfonsín and Onganía event leaders should continue to use the existing real leader registry, as the concept says.
- **MID and UCeDe:** Frondizi/Frigerio developmentalism is a historically grounded political tradition, and the concept correctly marks UCeDe's actual formation in 1982 and Menem's 1989 alliance as later. UCeDe's 1982 organization should not be presented as the 1970 PCI's already-existing identity. The current provisional-successor note is the right safeguard. `COLDWAR_ARG_ucede_formation` must continue to require the real post-1982 party reorganization, unless an explicitly approved early divergence is chosen.
- **PSD / CGTA / 62 Organizations:** the new exclusive provisional PSD identity is a reasonable small constitutional alternative and does not conflate it with CGTA. The PSD received 109,068 presidential votes (0.91%) in March 1973, supporting minor coalition-dependent weight rather than a dominant national base. PSP formed from PSA-derived and other currents in April 1972; PST formed separately from PRT-La Verdad and the Coral PSA faction in 1972. CGTA is a union confederation split from the CGT in 1968, not a Socialist party; its archive run ended in February 1970, so a long 1970-90 route represents an alternate successor labor bloc, not uninterrupted existence of the historical body. 62 Organizations is also a Peronist labor group, not an ordinary party. Preserve each as an explicit alternate incumbent only if the approved faction/political slot identifies it accurately. [IPU, 1973 election figures](https://data.ipu.org/election-summary/PDF/ARGENTINA_1973_E.PDF); [UNLP/CLACSO on PSP formation](https://biblioteca-repositorio.clacso.edu.ar/bitstream/CLACSO/4871/1/Un-nuevo-partido.pdf); [CEHTI on CGTA's split and publications](https://cehti.org/cgt-de-los-argentinos/).
- **Montoneros / PRT-ERP:** the text warning that armed movements are not normal parties is correct; their full government programmes are large counterfactual outcomes and should remain reachable only through real events, elections or multiplayer war/transition. Montoneros' first public action on 29 May 1970 followed the January start. PRT was already established, but its ERP armed wing came from the July 1970 fifth congress. Date or formation-state guards are appropriate and may be hidden from normal tooltips. Do not route either group through a generic legal left government as though electoral support and armed capacity were the same thing. [CONICET on Montoneros](https://bicyt.conicet.gov.ar/fichas/produccion/en/703264); [UNLP on PRT/ERP](https://www.sociohistorica.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/article/download/SHe049/9425).
- **PDC, Ateneo, UDELPA, PD Mendoza and SRA:** these have credible institutional traditions, but PDC is a small party, Ateneo a Catholic intellectual network, UDELPA an Aramburu-linked political organization, PD Mendoza a provincial party, and SRA an agricultural interest association. The concept usually explains these limits and presents their national governments as alternate. Keep that explicit. UDELPA's continuity note is apt because Aramburu was kidnapped on 29 May 1970 and cannot serve as an immortal leader requirement. The eight-focus SRA cabinet is a lobbying-group capture route, not historical mass party rule. The SRA agrarian incentives and export logic are credible proposals, with all numeric consequences balance estimates.

## Starting spirits and macroeconomic period

The six spirits fit January 1970 as harsh but recoverable. Cordobazo discontent is current; university intervention follows the 1966 Night of the Long Batons; political exclusion, uneven industrial capacity and service intervention are grounded. No starting spirit imports the 1975 hyperinflation, 1976-83 state terror as a completed historical fact, 1980s stagnation, or 1989 hyperinflation. `COLDWAR_ARG_debt_crisis` requires an external-finance shock and debt above 75 percent and is explicitly not automatic on 1 January 1970. Keep this boundary. World Bank histories describe 1968-70 inflation as below 20 percent and treat the 1975 inflation surge and the 1980s debt/stagnation as later breaks. [World Bank inflation review](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/756861468739164141/pdf/multi0page.pdf); [World Bank financial instability overview](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/644441468002112850/pdf/multi-page.pdf).

The spirit modifiers themselves are authored challenge values rather than measured history. The broad "+6% supply consumption / -8% infrastructure construction speed" in `COLDWAR_ARG_start_federal_corridors` should be presented as national connectivity and administrative-cost gameplay, not a claim that the entire Argentine national economy lacked usable infrastructure. Service command organization and military expenditure modifiers likewise express multiplayer balance. The other spirits are coherent provided their remedies replace the original spirit rather than stacking contradictory historical claims.

### Agreement reward and counterplay coherence

The agreements largely satisfy the roleplay requirement: one Argentine focus originates each offer, the partner responds, both parties' complete prospective effects are shown, and current war/embargo/sanction conditions suspend execution. The largest mismatches are the unbounded recurring Soviet grain transfer, military cooperation that survives supplier restrictions, and repeated industrial/infrastructure bonuses presented next to historic treaties without consistently stating that those numbers are game estimates. Weekly amounts are within the project's five-fractional-digit rule. No effect grants workforce size.

The Falklands route has genuine counterplay because `Authorize Operation Rosario` only starts the real war and Britain remains a separate human or AI opponent; the assessment does not hand Argentina victory. This is preferable to a forced surrender event. The optional addition is an explicit British diplomatic/war-cabinet response event after the invasion, particularly to expose the real 1982 US support for UK communications and France's arms embargo. It should not replace native combat or pre-decide its result. At minimum, the country-event response to naval demonstrations should give Britain a real decision where the offer is meant to require British consent.

## FINAL DISPOSITION: final 299-focus, 20-agreement, 16-event catalogue

This disposition supersedes the provisional findings above where the generated design changed. The final JSON now exists, reports 299 focuses in 22 groups, 16 party programmes, 20 agreements and 16 events, and reports no model/source errors. All 20 agreement records carry an explicit `historical_mode`; an early offer is intentionally a negotiated alternate-history acceleration with recipient consent, not a claim that a later historical treaty existed in 1970.
