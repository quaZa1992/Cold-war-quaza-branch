## Historical distinction

The original 48 is a recognizable basic statutory profits-tax rate. The [IRS historical rate table](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-soi/09sprbul.pdf), Table 24, printed page 245, actually gives 49.2% for tax year 1970 including its 2.5% surcharge, versus the basic 48% for 1971-74. Neither number establishes that a similar share of national output was taken as corporate tax or that national construction stopped. IRS reports distinguish business receipts, deductions, taxable profits, credits and tax actually collected. The [IRS 1970 corporation return report](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-soi/70cocrar.pdf) is the primary statistical record. The contemporaneous [1971 Economic Report of the President](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/title/economic-report-president-45/1971-8142/fulltext) describes high interest rates, lower profits, repeal of the investment credit and weak real investment, while investment continued. [BEA nonresidential fixed investment data](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/PNFI) record $123.796 billion at the 1970 first-quarter annual rate. [BEA federal corporate tax receipts](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/FCTAX) record $27.122 billion for calendar 1970; a different fiscal cash-receipts measure is not interchangeable with that figure.

These precedents justify retaining recession, investment and Vietnam pressure while correcting the MD input. They do not establish that 20 was the historical effective tax rate. Twenty is explicitly a calibrated MD setting, selected against the installed construction and fiscal readers.

## Save behavior and implementation

`tools/usa_1970_expansion/economy.py` owns `common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_USA_1970_economy.txt` and `economy_hooks.json`. The parent coordinator owns the call sites. `COLDWAR_USA_1970_economy_initialize` belongs inside the old once-only systems initializer, at its original corporate-tax assignment. `COLDWAR_USA_1970_economy_migrate` belongs only in startup and USA daily hooks after the old initializer has returned. It must not be called from generic reconciliation or a leader callback: the original systems receipt is set before its fiscal assignments, so such a call can reenter initialization too early.

Migration requires actual tag USA, the 1970 scenario, and the original USA systems receipt. It runs once. The recognized legacy default 48 becomes 20. The explicitly supplied save value 42 becomes 17.5, retaining its relative 42/48 reduction from the old default. Other rates, including 20 and 25, remain unchanged and receive the migration receipt. Later tax changes are never forced back. Recalculation calls native `calculate_tax_rate`, `force_update_dynamic_modifier` and `ingame_update_setup`; it does not apply an extra weekly treasury payment. Existing saved games need to load the changed mod for this code to run.
