IMPERIA COLD WAR: Independent USA expansion concept review

Reviewed `build_proposal.py` and emitted `proposal.json` on 24 October 2026. This review concerns the concept state during preparation. The main author received the findings before finalization and may have corrected them afterward. No runtime scripts were edited or tested by this review.

The direction is sound: substantive sector choices, finite research and doctrine rewards, a small number of relevant exclusions, shared ordinary development, separately accepted partner follow-ups and explicit temporary versus standing programmes. Keeping local institutions, native army laws and existing Vietnam/Gulf content is essential.

## Findings sent to the main author

1. **Preserve native military-law consequences.** D1/D2 cannot be presented as just a new small organisation or recruitment bonus. Volunteer army already grants major quality and XP benefits in exchange for substantially higher payroll and lower raw conscription. The original draft law has its own non-core combat penalties and core offsets. Show the complete law transition and reconcile any extra programme bonuses and payroll charges. The main author was already correcting this.

2. **Correct the advisory recipient to SVN.** The existing USA code consistently uses SVN for South Vietnam and VIE for North Vietnam. A provisional VIE recipient would direct aid to the wrong player. The main author was already correcting this.

3. **Use an unambiguous native trade label.** If F1 means `trade_opinion_factor = 0.10`, show a +10% trade-deal opinion factor, not a flat "trade agreement opinion +10". Native examples include `common/dynamic_modifiers/05_commonwealth_dynamic_modifiers.txt:5` and `common/modifiers/00_static_modifiers.txt:909`. Diplomatic opinion points and the trade factor are distinct effects.

4. **Give interventionist doctrine a stronger distinct outcome.** Every doctrine may pursue the same geography, which is appropriate. However, -10% programme expense makes commercial diplomacy useful across virtually every new contract. The interventionist alternative's +5% army XP and +3% war support at +10% programme expense is comparatively thin. A one-time land/air doctrine reduction or a concrete funded military training programme would make the security choice more attractive without a general permanent attack buff. F2's PP/stability package is a third useful dimension; future programme cost multipliers still need reconciliation with the real ledger and visible frozen prices.

5. **Do not strand long-term diplomacy behind missed Nixon focuses.** Japan, China, Moscow and South Vietnam originally pointed only to early Nixon offer/summit focuses. Those have presidency/date windows. Missing that administration should not permanently delete an entire Pacific or rival-diplomacy branch. Provide a later national diplomatic entry or an OR route from the general foreign-policy branch. Where a programme really depends on a previous signed parent, use the actual accepted receipt and active paired spirits as meaningful requirements. Completing a sending focus alone does not prove agreement. Relevant existing receipts are `USA_1970_nixon_okinawa_reversion_accepted`, `USA_1970_nixon_arvn_assistance_accepted`, `USA_1970_nixon_beijing_summit_accepted` and `USA_1970_nixon_moscow_summit_accepted` in `events/USA_1970_nixon_diplomacy_events.txt:341`, `:416`, `:188` and `:263`.

6. **Use verified money consumers for naval costs.** Current MD defines global equipment cost and separate naval personnel cost, but no reviewed naval-equipment upkeep modifier. `money_modifier_definitions.txt:30` defines `navy_personnel_cost_multiplier_modifier`; `00_money_system.txt:3209` reads it and `:3219` applies it. "Naval personnel cost +X%" is therefore a concrete supported alternative for exercise/carrier maintenance burdens. A ship-construction cost or equipment expense would need its own exact provider and consumer. Do not silently map a naval-only label to the global equipment-cost modifier.

7. **Resolve counterintelligence to an exact effect.** A generic "Counterintelligence ±X%" did not correspond to a verified native country modifier in the reviewed provider set. Clear existing alternatives include encryption or foreign influence defence, which the USA already uses at `99_USA_dynamic_modifiers.txt:49` and `:55`. These are different mechanics, so the visible label must change with the mapping. Other proposed modifiers do have native examples: agency upgrade time, naval organisation, submarine detection, convoy escort efficiency, army/navy XP factors, army fuel consumption and production efficiency retention.

8. **Make nuclear demonstration useful alongside efficiency.** The initial nuclear proposal costs $30 plus $0.03 weekly for faster reactor construction, generic research speed and finite research discounts. Efficiency costs $18 and gives immediate energy savings, factory output and later retention, with only a temporary construction malus. A signature nuclear generation bonus on the existing N3 reactor assets, or a separately funded reactor with a matching slot and capacity checks, would better justify the first package. The native nuclear generation consumer is already present in `USA_dynamic_modifier_department_of_energy`. Reconcile the prerequisite with N3 so the investment has useful assets to affect. This is a balance recommendation, not proof one route is strictly dominated.

9. **Define the mediation peace rule precisely.** A Rogers negotiating mandate should require the USA to be at peace with both recipients. Requiring Egypt and Israel already to be at peace makes an ongoing-war mediation channel unusable. Their conflict may continue while they both authorize talks, provided this is only a negotiating channel and does not impose a ceasefire. Any later ceasefire/peace transaction needs separate real consent. The proposed recipient package of temporary +2% stability for $1 and -3% army XP is thin; a modest one-time PP grant or another small civilian benefit would give both recipients a practical reason to enter talks.

## Compatibility and stacking observations

- Public industrial renewal versus private investment is a suitable funding-model choice. Opening E3/E4 after either route removes an arbitrary agriculture/office lock while preserving different specialist follow-ups.
- Intelligence oversight should not determine whether a country can develop AirLand Battle or precision technology. The proposed independent D5/D7 access resolves that conceptual mismatch.
- Carrier readiness versus escort/undersea priority is an allocation of the next major appropriation, not a claim that the US ceases operating the unselected fleet type. This scope should remain clear in names and descriptions.
- Civil-rights administration alternatives should both require enforceable civil-rights implementation. State negotiation must not reward continued segregation. Both federal and local paths can then provide credible benefits with different time, political and fiscal costs.
- Germany's forward-defence and local-sustainment offers are a useful bilateral fork. One-time factory/research grants must not replay on suspension or restoration, and the matched offset payment must appear once on each side of the same weekly transaction.
- Independent China and Soviet channels are suitable for triangular diplomacy. Neither should automatically change recognition, Taiwan, faction alignment, borders, nuclear restrictions or war participation.
- Programme spirit values must replace explicitly mapped overlapping old rewards. General language about replacing "overlapping helpers" is not sufficient for implementation: retain essential unlock/transaction flags while removing the old numerical grants being superseded.
- Compatible domestic, bilateral, native-law, internal-faction and later election bonuses remain cumulative. The proposal appropriately avoids a new research slot or universal combat uplift; finite sector rewards should continue to replace repeated global research-speed layers when integration occurs.

## Evidence boundary

The recommendations establish design and provider consistency where the cited source supports it. They do not prove HOI4 modifier behavior, category eligibility, exact native tooltip rendering, bilateral replay safety or multiplayer synchronization. The finished concept should identify unverified provider/category mappings as implementation work and keep them out of claims that the feature already works.
