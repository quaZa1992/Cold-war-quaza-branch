## Executed mechanics

- The Desert Storm goal requires the actual Iraqi-attacker record from the Gulf module. Restoration of Kuwaiti ownership/control of states172 and488 and peace with Iraq closes that opportunity. A war goal never declares an American war.
- Hosts accept actual military and air-base access. Staging and northern agreements have separate once-only invoices. Repeat consent does not replay either invoice. Coalition invitations allow a voluntary real treasury contribution without automatic war or faction membership.
- The common initial Kurdish package removes $0.25bn and1,000 actual US `infantry_weapons_1` items. Existing-country acceptance transfers the cash/equipment; the absent-country route commits equipment to one ledger and spends the operational money. Both share a permanent consumed marker. Cache delivery credits only its recorded remaining balance and never debits or generates a second package.
- The common support contract costs $0.05bn/week, prorated daily. One accepted recipient receives that same amount and the native targeted `defense_bonus_against = 0.08` against IRQ. Underground support spends it on operations instead. Cash failure or route loss ends ongoing support. Previously committed local weapons survive a funding withdrawal.
- No-country resistance retains Iraqi cores. States164/633/630 bind to KUR;1078 requires actual PUK formation and binds to PUK. Initial resistance reaches a60-point minimum once per state, in at most two states. A removable own target contribution adds25 points. Funded30-day reinforcement pulses add at most10 current points without pushing that pulse above80;80 is not an engine cap.
- Iraq's counters exist independently of American focus completion. A2% or4% GDP preparation invoice buys a90-day national military-overhead programme: army personnel/equipment upkeep modifiers of35%/20% or60%/35%. At least two actual divisions must remain in the north for thirty consecutive days before the one-time resistance reduction. Withdrawal keeps the sunk invoice and ninety-day cooldown.
- Two supplied, organized states at80 resistance for thirty consecutive qualifying days, plus an actual Iraqi military opening, start the public45-day warning. It pauses below its condition and resets after fifteen such days. A completed respected settlement ends it. A supplied movement can continue after American funding withdrawal.
- A genuine rising transfers only the qualifying tracked northern strongholds, preserves Iraqi cores, and creates at most one300-man two-battalion local column per actual actor with zero initial equipment. No general release, Kirkuk grant, foreign-core transfer or free equipped army is used. The actual country/war then replaces the own underground resistance phase: own forced overrides are cleared, own remaining IRQ-core resistance is cancelled, existing MD protests are preserved, and the native90 guard ends. Later reconquest cannot revive the once-only campaign or permanently suppress unrelated native revolt handling.
- Northern Air Shield uses the validated native90-day country ideas. Renewal explicitly removes/replaces them. Fresh host consent, live access, non-hostility and a controlled airbase must come from the same host; recipients accept separately. Readiness/liaison personnel costs persist to expiry after basing loss unless USA explicitly withdraws. Actual aircraft, capacity, range and war rules remain necessary. There is no geographic flight ban.
- Relief costs $0.50bn once plus$0.025bn/week and grants accessible authorized northern controllers25% infrastructure repair speed. Protection logistics costs$0.05bn/week and supplies actual USA/KUR/PUK-controlled northern states with0.10 local controller supply. Both use real state dynamic modifiers with live access/funding/control gates. They do not transfer territory or make forces immune.
- Existing local administrations must all accept an autonomy proposal before the global funded settlement is fulfilled. Iraq becomes overlord under a new fixed regional status with zero civilian/military factory or manpower tribute. An unconsenting PUK at war cannot be pacified by KUR's answer. De facto local control remains distinct from a recognized settlement. No actor becomes a USA subject.
- Assembly/recovery grants require actual recipient acceptance and real US money. Local infrastructure projects spend$0.20bn of actual local treasury and check current ownership/control/capacity, once per state.

This is one bounded northern intervention programme per1970 campaign. Initial-package, seed and rising ledgers intentionally survive governments, withdrawal and save/reload. They are not an unlimited repeatable arms or revolt generator.

## Integration and verification

The two external executable APIs are the IRQ-scope `COLDWAR_IRQ_campaign_active` trigger and `COLDWAR_IRQ_begin_kurdish_negotiations` effect. The latter owns domestic local responses when no separate Kurdish country exists. Political formation uses `COLDWAR_IRQ_puk_formed`. The Gulf module's permanent `COLDWAR_IRQ_gulf_iraq_attacked_kuwait` global flag records the actual attacker.

`test_usa_module.py` parses and executes the emitted AST in seventeen focused scenarios: affordability boundaries, actual stock/cash movement, duplicate rejection, exact daily upkeep, expiry, pulse bounds, real force presence, warning pauses, bounded rising, cache conversion, two-party consent, fresh-host consent, explicit air withdrawal and treasury-skip protection. Unsupported interpreted statements fail the test. Repeated generation is byte-identical, and art/localisation closure passes.

`render_installed.py` renders actual installed426-focus Iraq and229-focus USA sources, including unchanged USA neighbours. All426 Iraqi coordinates and the translated fifteen USA coordinates/prerequisites/exclusions match the approved input. The new Iraqi and USA labels fit the native147-pixel title field. The unchanged USA host has140 pre-existing overlong labels. Static renders use native textures and bitmap font; connector routing and locked-state appearance remain approximations.

HOI4 loading, new-country initialization, native resistance identity/garrison UI, threshold callback ordering, cancellation timing, native diplomacy, save migration, battles and multiplayer synchronization have not been tested in the engine. The AST tests and source render do not establish those engine behaviors.
