# IMPERIA CW — Cold War: Saddam domestic-policy proposal

This is an approval-stage concept. No focus, idea, event, decision, history or localisation file loaded by HOI4 was changed.

The interactive view replaces the current18-focus Saddam footprint with25 proposed focuses and retains392 currently installed neighbours as context. Its417 cards use the current resolved coordinates, native MD artwork/titlebars and the game's bitmap font. Engine connector routing and live effect tooltips remain untested.

## Policy priorities

- Presidential Rule / Security Directorates versus Ministerial Authority / Retain Specialists.
- Public Corporations / Integrated Works versus Private Contractors / Merchant Credit.
- A Literate Generation / Provincial Schools versus Republic's Engineers / Technical Institutes.
- Strategic Guard / Guard Supply versus The Regular Army / Staff Colleges.
- The Social Wage / Family Services versus Production Incentives / Performance Contracts.

Each priority has one reciprocal exclusion at its entry, with a distinct follow-up on either side. It does not exclude a whole economic sector or delete the unpreferred type of military formation. Public/private industry and broad/technical education historically coexisted. These choices allocate the next finite programme budget. Stronger ministerial autonomy is an alternative emphasis inside the dictatorship.

The five shared focuses have concrete outcomes: the presidency's authority, coordinated construction/research, Salahuddin infrastructure, a Basrah military factory and a Basrah office. No proposed focus relies on a decision unlock as its principal reward.

There are32 valid combinations, each with15 completable focuses and679 focus-days. The sum of stated one-time appropriations is34.5% of annual GDP. This sum combines percentages quoted at different completion dates and is not a fixed treasury invoice or a cashflow forecast. Recurring expense modifiers and native building operating costs apply separately.

## History and timing

January1970 retains Ahmed Hassan al-Bakr as president and Saddam as deputy. This branch requires Saddam actually leading a Baath government. It does not appoint him or replace the succession system. His formal historical accession was16July1979; earlier accession remains alternate history.

Free education/compulsory primary schooling began before his presidency. This branch provides additional literacy or technical funding. Private-enterprise reform and performance-based employment have a particular1987 precedent. A Guard strategic-reserve emphasis reflects its expanded combat role after1982, not a large Guard field army in1970. All exact numerical rewards and specific new provincial building allocations are design proposals.

Sources and per-focus distinctions are in CONCEPT.json and the interactive inspector. Reference reward shapes were compared with the current original MD Iraq, Russia and Iran files. Independent layout review also sampled current Generic/Russia spacing.

## Integration boundary for later implementation

Existing IDs are retained where a focus keeps its role. Current Saddam-specific foreign-policy and finance nodes are outside this domestic redesign's central theme. Before any implementation, explicitly migrate their one-time receipts and preserve Iran-dossier, Gulf-credit and debt-service access through the existing shared crisis/finance systems. The viewer is not proof that such migration has been implemented. No new diplomatic acceptance, debt cancellation, war goal or foreign transfer is granted here.

Replace the existing Saddam programme contributions with the approved replacement; never add the new package on top of the previous package. Active policy modifiers follow Saddam/Baath office holding; durable buildings, literacy, XP and limited research grants are awarded once. Restore earned programme modifiers on return to government without replaying those durable rewards. Keep real leader/government gates visible and internal routing/duplicate predecessor receipts hidden.
