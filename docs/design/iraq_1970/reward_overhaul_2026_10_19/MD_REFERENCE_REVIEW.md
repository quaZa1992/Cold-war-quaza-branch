## What the original trees actually reward

These trees regularly put substantial outcomes directly in focus completion: several physical projects, sizeable experience, limited-use research/doctrine, actual equipment output, a meaningful national-spirit upgrade and permanent specialised capabilities. A paid project is often accompanied by useful technical knowledge. Russia's national institutions can change several related capability variables together. Iran gives industrial/energy/service assets that have different real consumers. The native Iraqi army and air-force milestones are much larger than a repetitive two-point political/stability filler reward.

The benchmark is **what a player receives**, not a quota to copy every 2000 quantity into 1970. The original trees also contain expensive helpers, modern equipment, research targets unavailable in 1970 and some extremely generous population/nuclear rewards. Those must be traced and translated, not copied blind. Original `cost` is focus cost; the dollar amounts below are separate treasury effects.

## Review contract for the two new authored reward files

The independent data/code review must check:

1. A focus's main reward executes directly. A removed accounting/unlock step is not replaced by a new menu performing the same basic development. Recipient-controlled international or constitutional agreements remain genuine agreements; removing an unlock must never fabricate another player's acceptance.
2. Every new building has the current actual type, correct Iraqi state/province, count, ownership/control and matching non-shared/shared capacity form. A building already delivered before this overhaul is credited once; new technical rewards do not replay that building or its invoice.
3. Every modifier is registered/native and has an actual reader or native engine application. Category names need real early consumers; equipment needs a registered period model and correct producer. A country's original variable names must not be copied into Iraqi rewards.
4. Timed ideas/bonuses keep truthful duration, scope and cost, do not stack repeatedly from retries, and clean up according to their actual owner. Permanently constructed domestic capacity remains after a party change; active political programmes still use exact party/leader/contract gates.
5. Compatible whole programmes remain attractive and meaningfully different. More base stability that is discarded at the existing cap, duplicate army output across parties or a renamed bureaucracy toll do not answer the request. Strong direct benefits do not require an arbitrary negative on every focus; actual funding, limited uses, commitment time and mutually exclusive capabilities can supply the opportunity cost.
6. Report source and emitted results separately. A valid authored JSON is not proof that the compiler emits it, previews it truthfully or preserves save invoices. Installation/engine/MP claims require their respective checks.

### Root integration/source review

The inspected integration uses the old `load_design(False)` rewards to emit the v2 migration on purpose. The new v3 path separates old successful transactions, actual one-time receipts and new current-programme markers. Source inspection confirms these intended boundaries:

- A mere completed focus/policy marker is not historical asset financing.
- Ordinary pending invoices are rescaled from their existing positive frozen basis; a recovered retired-child historical GDP basis is reused for the additional v3 scope.
- Existing v2 pending supplements must finish before their v3 expansion. The old standing programme remains while its genuine outstanding expansion is not financed.
- Only a positive increase in the focus's GDP share creates a v3 supplementary bill; it is quoted once. Completed assets and existing experience/literacy/research uses are credited rather than replayed.
- A zero-price expansion whose entire new one-time payload is already credited can adopt its new standing programme without remaining stuck on a redundant pending card.
- Shared/non-shared capacity guards compute the **remaining** actual building count after credit, check owned/controlled states before payment, and allow a fully credited old physical asset without rebuilding it.
- Actual payment/delivery stays in guarded helpers; effect-tooltip previews do not execute it. Initialisation runs the v2 migration before the v3 migration/quote refresh, followed by situation/programme reconciliation.

One deliberate conservative migration boundary is worth preserving in any user explanation: historical limited research uses are credited semantically by category/use count, not recreated merely because a new course name or bonus strength differs. This prevents receiving a second complete course solely through renaming. Native pending research/UI behavior is still an engine boundary; this source review is not a certificate of every old save's tooltip.
