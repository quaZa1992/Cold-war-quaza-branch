# COLDWAR_IRQ — Iraq, January1970–1990

**Approval concept. No loaded game files have been changed.**

The final concept contains426 focuses in23 programme areas,80 decision designs and seven starting-situation cards. All proposed game text is English. The current user correction is authoritative: give the significant historical parties and leaders substantial paths, rather than invent a separate government tree for every communist official. Consequently, the separate hypothetical Khairi, Amir Abdullah, Baha al-Din Nuri and Central Command government trees were removed before this version.

The focus catalogue is [COLDWAR_IRQ_1970_CONCEPT.json](COLDWAR_IRQ_1970_CONCEPT.json). [PREVIEW.html](PREVIEW.html) presents that catalogue with native artwork, titlebars, focus spacing and inherited bitmap typography. It changes the camera rather than rearranging selected branches. `Entire tree` shows all23 areas; select a programme, drag from background or any focus, scroll with the wheel (Shift for sideways, Ctrl for zoom), or choose `Fit branch` to inspect its whole shape. Select any focus to see the exact proposed modifiers, invoice, ownership, conditions, exclusions and native effect body. `Starting Spirits` and `Branch Decisions` expose their designs.

Complete branches at native scale are also supplied as `FULL_BRANCH_<group>.png`; these show complete branches, including lower joins (18focuses in the original programmes;30in Iran: Road to War). The ordinary browser screenshots show only the camera's visible portion. Browser connector routing, glows, political availability and engine evaluation remain approximations. Native title strings are measured against the inherited UbuntuBold16 glyph advances, not made to fit by shrinking global fonts.

## January1970 and the historical alternatives

The current scenario starts Ahmed Hassan al-Bakr under Baath/Nationalist Fascist slot21, with Saddam an important deputy rather than already president. Existing party21, ICP4 and KDP6 mappings are preserved. The current script's exact KDP predicate is `emerging_reactionaries_are_in_power`; its mechanical name is not a claim about the KDP's historical ideology. Native2000 Iraq places communist content in a different slot and is not copied over the1970 registry.

They continue under different governments. A historical party's national accession remains an alternate outcome where it did not historically govern Iraq.

## Structure and native presentation

Two horizontal tiers reserve complete branch footprints: eleven areas above and twelve below after adding Iran: Road to War at(94,15). Standard leaves use two X units and ordinary rows one Y unit, matching the measured MD grid:96×130px. There are compact, broad, left/right-offset and conditional-delivery variants. Industry/factory delivery, oil contracts, autonomous budgets and similar conditional gains use real side spurs so a refused agreement or unfunded asset does not veto all domestic institutional work.

Every focus has a stable `COLDWAR_IRQ_` ID, absolute position, emitted relative offset/root anchor, grouped prerequisites, exclusions, exact governing/office conditions, days, modifiers, costs, decisions, ownership and source artwork. The JSON and outside-runtime script specimen use those same coordinates. Multiple prerequisite blocks are AND; focus entries within one block are OR. Late industry/agriculture/social capstones require delivered domestic programme components. Defence uses a mixed `(land OR coastal) AND fuel-discipline` completion. Political/diplomatic and peaceful-war capstones use appropriate alternatives so another player cannot veto every domestic continuation.

The camera starts at al-Bakr's presidency; nine native-style shortcuts point to real programme roots. The continuous-focus panel is reserved below the entire tree at pixel position(0,4300). Initial selection uses the native focus-ID form, not an invented GUI unit conversion.

Actual MD icons were inspected. General Iraqi artwork with the post1991 flag was rejected. Baath/ICP emblems anchor their own branches; Saddam's portrait belongs only to his path; educational, judicial, diplomatic, logistical, water, industrial and welfare art follows each focus's subject. Focus labels fit the native147px text region using maximum143px advance width. Global fonts, UI sizes and other trees are untouched.

Artwork selection, 18 October 2026: Enes supplied the young Saddam portrait in a gold wreath for his leadership programme. `COLDWAR_IRQ_saddam_leadership` replaces the earlier MD portrait reference in Saddam's entry and five portrait-fallback focuses. The original transparent 1316×1195 PNG is preserved unchanged under `tools/iraq_1970_concept/assets/`; the preview fits its entire frame into 100×91 pixels. The artwork manifest records its source hash and the future DDS path. This is a concept-local asset awaiting sprite/texture registration during approved implementation. The browser retains a Saddam government inspection view and now opens Iran: Road to War; the actual January1970 start and its al-Bakr camera target are unchanged.

## Effects, costs and programme lifecycle

All used modifier keys and underlying treasury, construction, literacy, resource and political helper effects were traced to current source providers/consumers. For example, `agricolture_productivity_modifier` retains MD's exact spelling; `mdcw_cw_support_effectiveness` improves foreign-party-support campaigns, not allied combat power. Organization bonuses are labelled country-wide. No fictional Kurdish loyalty, oil-dependence, free manpower or empty custom variable is offered as an executable reward.

The catalogue contains207 explicitly paid focus packages and22 guarded one-time building grants. Money uses a saved quote from `gdp_total`; every debit goes through the existing `modify_treasury_effect`. No instant benefit executes if the current party/office, contract, treasury, ownership/control or building-specific capacity condition fails. A completed but undelivered programme gets a pending flag and an always-accessible retry. Its dependent focuses require **implemented** prerequisites. A retry reuses the original quote and once-only delivery guard; it does not issue a second award or reset the price. There is no deposit, so cancellation creates no artificial refund. Bilateral credit changes a real lender balance and Iraqi treasury/debt only after actual accepted terms.

Project GDPshares are explicit **new Iraqi1970 balance proposals**. The native2000 helper prices are retained beside them for review, not copied as unscaled charges: a7.5-billion factory would equal roughly229% of the current1970 IraqiGDP target3.281. No automatic native historical discount or universal Imperia construction factor is claimed. Scoped `add_building_construction` is used with one explicit project invoice; a native helper that already charges is not additionally called. The office-only project does not secretly grant the power plant bundled in one native office helper. Affordability and cumulative administrative/welfare expenditure still need a live1970 budget test.

National infrastructure, resources and literacy persist after government changes. Party and leader packages become inactive when their exact current authority ends, but retain earned progress for a legitimate return. Contract packages also require their current contract status; historical signature alone is insufficient. Emergency-war packages require actual war and stop at peace. Temporary readiness cycles expire without recreation during resynchronization.

Reconciliation derives caches only from implemented, selected, currently eligible packages. It must never replay money, construction, resources, XP or literacy. Changes inside one outlook, actual office swaps, initialization, save/load and a defensive weekly resync are separate hook requirements. The native Russia `SOV_update_political_modifiers` and `change_ruling_party_effect` pipeline are source-backed patterns for detaching/restoring programmes.

Positive policy stability uses one transparent aggregate: `min(sum of positive active contributions, +0.15) + sum of negative active contributions`. Original negative national-situation modifiers remain separate. A negative policy cost therefore remains real even after the positive cap binds; a focus's+3% contribution is not falsely promised as a guaranteed+3 realized delta above the cap.

Selected policy revisions replace active packages rather than reactivate every historical choice. Irreversible focus exclusions remain genuine completed choices. A later paid policy revision must define its selected replacement package and conditions; it does not silently complete an excluded focus, reset the tree, or replay old rewards. Exact decision/event providers and per-policy revision transitions remain implementation specifications.
