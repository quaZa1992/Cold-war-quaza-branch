# Native MD reward palette and budget-unit verification

19 October 2026. Read-only review of the installed main Millennium Dawn 2000 country trees and the current Cold War consumers. This report changes no game file or generator. The Iraqi applications below are audit candidates, not an implemented redesign or newly approved values.

## Result

Main MD supplies much more varied consequences than repeated bureaucracy charges: a better-trained army can mobilize more slowly; faster formations consume more supplies; specialised infantry needs longer training; an education programme raises its own education expense; industrial development spends treasury or scarce resources; foreign investment can surrender domestic influence; an offensive commitment can reduce stability. Limited research, genuine capacity and temporary targeted advantages give substantial rewards without accumulating permanent generic national bonuses.

The Iraqi bureaucracy repetition is financially real. Its modifier is **summed and then applied to the bureaucracy spending calculation**. It is not a percentage-point share of the whole state budget. There is no Iraqi bureaucracy cap analogous to the positive-stability cap. A concrete compatible four-chapter development route already contributes `+0.52` before the state, party, leader, foreign, defence, war or Iran programmes. That is a serious recurring administrative burden, not a harmless display label.

## Budget units and maximum-stack significance

### Bureaucracy

The registered modifier is country-scoped, percentage-displayed: [money_modifier_definitions.txt:51](<C:/Users/Enes/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/mdcoldwar/common/modifier_definitions/money_modifier_definitions.txt:51>). Its real reader [update_bureaucracy_rate:3299–3322](<C:/Users/Enes/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/mdcoldwar/common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt:3299>) computes:

```text
B = population_total × common_money_modifier × 0.0007 × selected_spending_ladder
bureaucracy_gain = max(0, B × (1 + summed_bureaucracy_modifier))
common_money_modifier = inflation_multiplier × (1 + GDP_per_capita × native_scaling)
```

The expense streams are then added to `display_expense` at [4571](<C:/Users/Enes/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/mdcoldwar/common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt:4571>). Thus ten compatible `+.02` contributions supply **+.20 to the one multiplier**, not `1.02^10`, not twenty percentage points of GDP, and not a twenty-point increase in the bureaucracy law.

There is a civil-servant salary addition inside `calculate_gdp` at 5844. The ordinary weekly aggregator calls `update_state_variables`/`calculate_gdp` **before** `update_bureaucracy_rate`, which resets the line, then updates the display. It must not be silently added as a second late multiplier denominator in this normal weekly calculation. This report does not broaden into a redesign of that separate salary/call-order behavior.

The Iraqi programme cache [COLDWAR_IRQ_1970_programmes.txt](<C:/Users/Enes/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/mdcoldwar/common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_IRQ_1970_programmes.txt:3>) sums eligible contributions. Its source [compile.py:179](<C:/Users/Enes/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/mdcoldwar/tools/iraq_1970/compile.py:179>) only caps **positive stability**, at `.15`; it does not cap bureaucracy. Different parties/office holders do not all operate at once. A selected policy's side and its unselected rival cannot both be summed.

These envelopes sum to **+1.40**, but that is a conditional chapterwise bound, **not a proven single live campaign total**. It deliberately excludes party/leader families, starting spirits, temporary contracts and foreign ideas. Do not advertise it as an observed +140% in-game total or add all political chapters to it. For example, Baath +.11 can coexist with Bakr +.11 **or** Saddam +.09; Bakr and Saddam cannot simultaneously be the office holder. KDP +.18 cannot simply be added to the Baath/leader total.

The reward reviewer separately identified a concrete compatible peacetime **47-focus** set: nationalised oil/revenue +.14, light industry/public finance +.10, cooperatives/local irrigation +.18 and teacher-first/targeted services +.10. Independent chapter enumeration agrees with its **+.52** bureaucracy sum. The set assumes the actual frozen invoices are paid and home-state ownership/control/capacity exists; it requires no foreign acceptance and includes no political/war programme. Its 1,729 focus-days are sequential focus time, not a campaign completion guarantee. Its 87.5% sum of GDP-priced fees is a same-GDP illustration, not actual cumulative cash spending or a growth forecast.

For a frozen economic state with pre-existing other bureaucracy modifiers `M0`, that set changes the line by:

```text
absolute extra bureaucracy expense = .52 × B
relative increase over the old bureaucracy line = .52 / (1 + M0)
```

The ordinary starting July State gives `+.08` and Patronage gives `+.10` ([starting ideas:13](<C:/Users/Enes/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/mdcoldwar/common/ideas/COLDWAR_IRQ_1970_starting_ideas.txt:13>) and [45](<C:/Users/Enes/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/mdcoldwar/common/ideas/COLDWAR_IRQ_1970_starting_ideas.txt:45>)). When those two remain, the deliberately economic-only route reaches **+.70 before laws/other ideas**. Holding the economic base fixed, its comparison is `1.18B` → `1.70B`: approximately **44.1% more than the starting bureaucracy expense**, not +70 points of the whole budget. Actual campaign growth, inflation, a later patronage audit or a later political settlement can change that comparison. Its exact share of the deficit depends on population, law, GDP per capita, taxes, exports and all other expenses. Replacing every bureaucracy charge with another budget multiplier would preserve the same repetitive problem.

### Consumer goods, tax and exports

- [Consumer goods reader:4670–4678](<C:/Users/Enes/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/mdcoldwar/common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt:4670>): `actual_consumer_goods_percent_display = consumer_goods_expected_value × (1 + consumer_goods_factor)`. Thus `+.02` is **+2% relative to the expected consumer-goods share**, not +2 percentage points. With an otherwise unmodified expected share of 20%, it changes 20% to 20.4%. The modifier does not itself change the nominal corporate/population tax-rate variables. Corporate taxation separately supplies its tax-cost/consumer-goods input at 316–320.
- [Tax reader:424–430](<C:/Users/Enes/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/mdcoldwar/common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt:424>) and [population reader:770–790](<C:/Users/Enes/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/mdcoldwar/common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt:770>): `tax_gain_multiplier_modifier` enters the covered channels as `(1 + modifier)`, not an extra nominal tax-rate percentage point. Subsistence agriculture has its own earlier expression and should not be blindly described as identical coverage. Sector modifiers such as `military_industry_tax_modifier` (481) and `office_park_income_tax_modifier` (523) apply only to their actual building channel. A country without that sector receives little or no benefit.
- [Oil export reader:4508–4542](<C:/Users/Enes/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/mdcoldwar/common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt:4508>): oil export revenue uses actual exported oil, actual world oil price and `1 + resource_export_multiplier_modifier + oil_export_multiplier_modifier`, with existing Cold War embargo/OPEC deductions. `+.18` changes this coefficient additively; it is +18% of the unmodified export-revenue base, not necessarily +18% of an already modified observed income. It grants neither oil resources nor world-price control and is ineffective without actual exports.
- [Personnel reader:3183–3198](<C:/Users/Enes/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/mdcoldwar/common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt:3183>): generic personnel and army-only personnel multipliers are applied separately to the actual force cost. An army-specific maintenance drawback is a genuinely military burden, but adding generic +10% personnel and army +10% is approximately `1.1×1.1`, not one additive +20% at this reader. Avoid using it indiscriminately for schools, farms or commercial accounting.

## What to carry into the proposed Iraqi revision

1. Strengthen the existing mutual choices with **different capabilities**: specialists versus mobilisation, speed versus supply use, education catch-up versus lasting teaching capacity, industrial output versus consumer availability, immediate oil receipts versus a real diversified sector.
2. Use a clear price or historically relevant opportunity cost. A properly funded physical project already costs money and focus time and uses workforce/capacity; every companion focus does not need another national administrative surcharge.
3. Reserve bureaucracy for genuine proliferation/duplication of ministries, parallel chains of authority or regional administration. Replace weak accounting filler with its institution or delivery rather than painting the same cost a different colour.
4. Keep difficult starting spirits and war/debt conditions. Reward variety is not a reason to erase historical problems or grant every party the same powerful generic buffs.
5. Before implementation, check whole compatible programmes, caps, current research targets, recipients and repeat/cleanup behavior. Main-MD examples establish supported patterns; Iraqi ownership, party cleanup, prices and multiplayer consent still need their own actual code.

One deliberately rejected shortcut: Russia visibly uses `corruption_cost_factor` in an oligarch focus/dynamic modifier, but this audit did not establish a current local custom registration or an actual MD financial reader for that exact key. It is **not** recommended here as a verified substitute cost merely because its name looks historically appropriate. Likewise there is no proposed generic switch from bureaucracy to social expense.
