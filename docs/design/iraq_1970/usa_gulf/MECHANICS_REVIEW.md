## Current resistance extension

The no-country route uses genuine native core resistance rather than presenting a new organization variable as the game's resistance meter. Primary installed-game `documentation/effects_documentation.html:3826–3836` explicitly supports force-enable on cores, paired with `start_resistance` at6967–6975. Main MD `events/protests.txt:30–43` demonstrates this on owned, controlled cores. The concept correctly uses IRQ as occupier even when initiated from USA scope; it preserves IRQ cores. Vanilla `common/operations/LaR_FRA_operations.txt:46–69` and `common/scripted_effects/POL_scripted_effects.txt:53–62` support the chosen tag-binding primitive. Formation of a PUK movement is now an explicit condition for the1078/PUK binding; no premature movement or silent KUR core grant is assumed.

The initial resistance minimum60 is once per state per crisis, never a downward replacement of a higher value. The additional target contribution is25points, not an absolute target80. Reinforcement is limited to one+10-point pulse per30days and cannot push the value above80 through that pulse; normal engine growth may exceed80. Existing Iraqi countermeasures are not cancelled by a daily floor reset. Expansion requires continued funding, and touched states cannot be reseeded. Seed markers and pulse/operation cooldowns persist across renewal, withdrawal and government change.

The source-supported target effect is `add_resistance_target` with a removable ID, duration and occupier filter (`effects_documentation.html:1846–1858`); ID removal is at5390–5396. Renewal replaces or extends one contribution instead of stacking copies. Iraqi reductions are separate contributions, removed with the Iraqi operation rather than deleting the USA contribution. Implementation must preserve this separation and existing native resistance providers.

Four Iraqi responses have crisis-based availability and no USA-focus unlock requirement: security appropriation, major operations, negotiations and withdrawal. The two military packages share one90-day cycle:2%GDP preparation with+35%army-personnel/+20%equipment upkeep, or4%GDP with+60%/+35%. The larger replaces the smaller; mid-cycle switching cannot replay the30-day reduction. Both require a viable actual force/garrison commitment. Paid security blocks the scripted USA pulse, but does not supply free garrison strength or guarantee settlement.

Financial keys are registered in workspace `common/modifier_definitions/money_modifier_definitions.txt:23–49`; consumers in `common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt:3182–3197` scale the native army-personnel and equipment-upkeep components. They are country-wide, additive within their respective multipliers, and distinct from procurement prices or a geographically exact northern bill. Ordinary war scaling and native garrison losses remain. The stated preparation invoice is a separate sunk operation expenditure. Balance and actual deficit/debt consequences remain untested.

No money or equipment country effect targets absent KUR/PUK. The underground route spends USA operational money and debits actual donor stock into one proposed cache. Negative stockpile additions are supported by primary `effects_documentation.html:1171–1181` and MD `05_GRE_decisions.txt:578–580,600–604`. Direct-country and clandestine aid share the initial-package marker and remaining support term. A rising consumes the remaining cache once; peaceful emergence requires one accepted handover. A resolved movement without valid handover writes the cache off without refund or replay. Withdrawal does not manufacture a new stockpile. This ledger and transition logic are explicit new integration, not existing helpers.

The uprising warning requires two eligible states at80+ for30consecutive days, organization/supply, unresolved grievances and an actual military opening. Its45-day warning pauses below qualification and resets after15consecutive days below it; an implemented respected settlement cancels it. The proposed outcome is northern and bounded, excludes automatic Kirkuk/transnational release, and supports an existing actor instead of creating a duplicate.

**Required implementation boundary:** native MD `resistance_modifiers.txt:89–146,255–299,332–335` has a90%core-group handler which can create a country, transfer all controlled states sharing an occupied tag, spawn equipped units and declare war. The current catalogue expressly requires coordination/isolation against this handler; it does not claim an engine cap or a safe ready-made Kurdish uprising. This coordination, exact tag binding, garrison UI, callback ordering, observer cleanup and save/load cases must be implemented and tested before the design is installed. A daily clamp is not proof of safe ordering. Detailed evidence is retained in [RESISTANCE_MECHANICS_RESEARCH.md](RESISTANCE_MECHANICS_RESEARCH.md).
