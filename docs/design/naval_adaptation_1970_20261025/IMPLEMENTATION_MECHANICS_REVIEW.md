## Final behavior and stacking

The existing UAE compiler removes national-focus treasury vetoes, and its revision test checks that policy transitively. The new naval profile now explicitly opts into the same policy with `allow_deficit=true`. Only that country with the literal boolean flag omits the treasury-affordability comparison. Its full invoice is still charged, including from a negative balance. Positive GDP and quote, MD_skip, ownership, control, port-province and native capacity checks remain. All other countries retain funded delivery. The three actual UAE transactions and the twelve other funded transactions pass their separate regressions.

Twenty nodes each are installed for USA and SOV; DDR, Iraq and UAE receive 15 coastal nodes each. Every new parent ID resolves in its actual country tree. Carrier and helicopter-operator alternatives are still exclusive. Required parent groups are rechecked at execution, and funded USA/SOV/DDR/IRQ rewards wait when cash or complete construction capacity is missing. UAE retains its country-specific deficit-finance rule and complete capacity checks. Returning eligibility delivers the complete bundle once.

Prices refresh before execution: blue-water ports cost 0.5 billion, one yard 7.5 and two yards 15; coastal ports cost 0.002 GDP and yards 0.008 GDP per level. The industrial arms-factory grant retains the actual donor waiver. Every asset transaction includes its slots before construction. Quotes, pending retries and previews do not charge twice.

The 30 new idea definitions match all native donor fields except their political names. They are additional programmes alongside existing national dynamic bonuses. Their permanent/365 day/730 day distinctions remain; expiry does not renew a completed focus. The combined-industry economic modifiers feed their native GDP/tax components and must not be presented as +40% GDP.

Late special-project progress has been replaced with registered research categories. The entry gives one 50% naval-modules coupon; materials give one 50% industry and one 50% naval-engine coupon; former stealth gives two 50% naval-electronics uses. No free hull, completed modern technology or foundation-project completion is granted. Actual hull technology project gates remain unchanged and can delay use of research coupons.

UAE profile focus_cost 10 is an explicit timing exception: all 15 nodes take 70 nominal days, including the donor follow-ups that otherwise cost 5. This is recorded rather than represented as unchanged donor timing.

A second reviewer independently passed the final UAE finance delta against emitted source. With writes blocked, the reviewer reran all 16 asset and 14 lifecycle tests, recursively checked all 15 UAE availability/ready predicates, and exercised three additional loss-of-control cases. After GDP changed from 100 to 200 and control returned, Treasury=-3 paid the exact 0.400 port invoice or 1.600 yard/drydock invoice once. Other countries retained their treasury guards. See [independent delta and hook review](DATA_PROSE_AND_HOOK_REVIEW.md). Native debt ticks, engine UI and multiplayer were not tested.

## Canonical retention and limits

The USA, SOV, DDR, Iraq and UAE owner emitters all contain the integration hook and shared naval runtime generation. The review read those hooks without running a broad owner build.

The model evaluates receipts, amounts, branch guards and emitted source transactions. It records native faction/research/mastery calls and does not emulate their full engine behavior. Synthetic state fixtures do not prove real port geography or starting OOB/project state. Native visual/tooltips, engine transaction behavior, save/rehost and network tests remain separate.
