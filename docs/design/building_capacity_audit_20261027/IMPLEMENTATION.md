## Native evidence

The private native tests confirmed actual state-scoped renewable construction
from level 18 to 19, the country's skip-payment behaviour, and no assets or
charges when all eligible states are full at level 20. Plaintext save evidence
confirms the additional shared building slot. Fifteen exact-count and strict
comparison observations establish the native collection behaviour at zero,
one and two; the implementation follows these observations rather than the
installed documentation's inaccurate inclusive-comparison note.

The controlled queue baseline independently proved that an ordinary
`instant_build = no` request cannot bypass a missing manual construction
permission. Available civilian factories and moderate construction boosts were
verified. Civilian-industry and researched radar controls completed, while
infrastructure and air-defence requests without their construction permission
did not enter the native construction statistics. That negative baseline is
retained as evidence, not presented as a successful queued-project test.

The follow-up comparison also established that `free_building_slots` ignores
waiting building levels, including a queued final level at the physical limit.
A second paid order can therefore be discarded despite a positive slot query.
`include_locked` does not bypass independent-state technology limits, and
`set_building_level` builds immediately even with `instant_build = no`.

The chosen correction converts the five reviewed Greek and Vietnamese asset
grants to completion at the end of their focus. Original focus duration, GDP
price, state and building quantity are retained. Additional civilian-factory
construction time is removed. Physical capacity and actual control are checked
before the complete original payment and reward transaction. This explicitly
changes their former queue timing rather than leaving the native parallel-order
failure hidden. No construction-permission or future MD technology is installed.
