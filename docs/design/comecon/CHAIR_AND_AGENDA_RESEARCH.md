IMPERIA COLD WAR: COMECON chairmanship and member proposals
=========================================================

Research requested by the user on 25 October 2026. No new chair-selection policy is implemented by this note. The installed Soviet-first human fallback is the existing game rule, not a historical election procedure.

Historical basis
----------------

The 1959 Charter separates the rotating host delegation's session chair (VI.3), proposals from members and Council bodies (VI.5), and the Moscow Secretariat with its Council-appointed Secretary (IX). Article IV requires consent of interested members and allows non-participation. The original text predates the 1962 Executive Committee reform.

- Primary source: [UN Treaty Series 368, No. 5245, pp. 268-278](https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/UNTS/Volume%20368/volume-368-I-5245-English.pdf).
- Period legal analysis: [Alexander Uschakow, APuZ 2/1966](https://www.bpb.de/shop/zeitschriften/apuz/archiv/527378/der-rat-fuer-gegenseitige-wirtschaftshilfe-comecon/). Its amended-structure account describes Executive Committee chair rotation by Russian alphabetical country order and distinguishes the permanent Secretariat.
- Contemporary UN legal survey: [UNCITRAL Yearbook 1968-1970, p. 32, para. 112](https://uncitral.un.org/sites/uncitral.un.org/files/media-documents/uncitral/en/yb_1968_1970_e.pdf) confirms the 1962 Executive Committee addition. A later 1974 charter excerpt should not silently substitute for the 1970 institutional state.

Recommended game adaptation
---------------------------

Use a human presiding delegation distinct from the permanent Moscow Secretariat. Rotate the presiding role by Council session, skipping AI countries and Soviet republics under the user's participation policy. No evidence found here supports a competitive majority election or a freely chosen permanent presidency. The human-only restriction is an explicit game adaptation.

All human players should browse the full programme catalogue independently of the active shared contract. Human Council members may request an agenda item without committing money or changing another government's draft. The presiding delegation prepares the shared agreement. Actual recipients and financiers retain the existing sealed review, consent and invoice guards. Capital contributions must not become extra votes.

Show the actual current chair's country name and native flag, the permanent Secretariat location, the chair-selection rule, and the steps for catalogue browsing, agenda requests, draft preparation and signature. Catalogue selections must remain separate from shared financial terms and gameplay receipts. A new browsing interface and the rotation mechanism still require implementation and validation.
