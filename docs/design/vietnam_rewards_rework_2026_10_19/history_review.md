# Vietnam 1970–1990: historical review of all focus rewards

Date: 19 October 2026. Research and design guidance only. No game files changed.

## Scope and conclusion

I read the titles and typed rewards of all **307 focuses in all 18 programmes** in `docs/design/vietnam_impact_audit_2026_10_19/inventory.json`, including the 68 campaign additions. I examined descriptions and exclusions where the title/reward relationship needed clarification. This is a historical and thematic review; it does not certify engine support, calculate balance, or claim that each effect has been observed in a running game.

The largest problem is the repeated use of political power, general research and generic productivity as substitutes for very different institutions. Numerous concepts are historically useful but receive a benefit in the wrong field. Other focuses initiate expensive work without giving the player a substantive result from that work. An attractive reward should express the focus's practical purpose: trained administrators, reliable deliveries, functioning workshops, trained aircrew, recovered land, enforceable contracts, a safer frontier or an affordable deployment.

**Party Schools and Hear the Economists should both be useful. Neither represents the absence of the other's competence.** Their current descriptions already permit this reading, and the two focuses are not mutually exclusive in this inventory. A future fork between them should represent the allocation of a particular training/advisory budget, not a permanent historical split between Party members and people who understand economics.

All benefit/cost suggestions below are **design inferences**, not claims of measured historical percentage effects. English names are suitable candidate labels. A benefit must use a supported MD mechanic selected by the implementation audit; this memo does not authorize invented statistics.

## Historical anchors

These short findings establish the historical limits of the recommendations. Institutional commemorations are useful for offices, curricula and stated policies, but their positive assessments are not independent evaluations of policy success.

1. **Cadre education was practical as well as ideological.** Tô Hiệu Political School's own institutional history describes late-1960s/early-1970s courses covering agriculture, industry, trade, finance, Party organization and local field study. Its 1969–1971 activity included substantial agricultural cadre training. A school therefore could improve the administration of a district or enterprise; it was not simply a propaganda multiplier. [School institutional history](https://truongchinhtritohieu.haiphong.gov.vn/Bai-viet-chuyen-de/Truong-Chinh-tri-To-Hieu---Chang-duong-lich-su-72-nam-xay-dung-va-truong-thanh-90728.html)

2. **Economic expertise existed inside the socialist apparatus.** In a retrospective first-person memoir, Võ Văn Kiệt's former assistant Vũ Quốc Tuấn describes advice from socialist-trained specialists, enterprise officials and people with experience of the former southern administration. He also identifies advisory work supporting Trường Chinh's preparation for renovation in 1983–1986. His account distinguishes these precedents from Kiệt's later, formal 1993 advisory organization. Do not import that 1993 institution into the 1970–1990 period. [Vũ Quốc Tuấn's participant account](https://nhandan.vn/vo-van-kiet-mot-bo-oc-chien-luoc-post726089.html)

3. **Food, consumer goods and exports were complementary renovation priorities.** The Sixth Congress's 1986–1990 programme explicitly linked them and redirected investment toward them, with heavy industry supporting those priorities. A finite investment allocation is a credible choice; a permanent ban on producing food while pursuing exports is not. [Contemporary Congress economic programme](https://tulieuvankien.dangcongsan.vn/ban-chap-hanh-trung-uong-dang/dai-hoi-dang/lan-thu-vi/phuong-huong-muc-tieu-chu-yeu-phat-trien-kt-xh-trong-5-nam-1986-1990-1492)

4. **Cooperative and household activity could coexist.** Resolution 30-NQ/TW of 17 December 1985 called for developing both the collective and household economies. “Collective production” cannot be used as shorthand for forbidding every household contract or plot. The allocation of labor, specific land-use responsibilities and the rights to retain above-contract output can create narrower conflicts. [Contemporary Resolution 30](https://tulieuvankien.dangcongsan.vn/van-kien-tu-lieu-ve-dang/hoi-nghi-bch-trung-uong/khoa-v/nghi-quyet-so-30-nqtw-ngay-17121985-hoi-nghi-lan-thu-chin-ban-chap-hanh-trung-uong-dang-khoa-v-ve-phuong-huong-nhiem-1098)

5. **Foreign investment did not require political pluralism.** The 29 December 1987 investment law provided for foreign capital and technology, including joint ventures and wholly foreign-funded enterprises, while retaining state approval. Access, legal safeguards and screening belong to an investment-policy axis separate from whether the Party permits independent political competition. [Original investment law in the Ministry of Justice database](https://vbpl.moj.gov.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=2565&Keyword=)

6. **Postwar economic management faced concrete input constraints.** The Ministry of Industry and Trade's institutional account of 1975–1985 discusses transformation of southern enterprises, mixed forms, shortages of materials and spare parts, and subsequent criticism of oversimplified transformation. These are better foundations for rewards than an assumption that a new organizational slogan immediately increases every kind of production. [Ministry account](https://moit.gov.vn/gioi-thieu/cac-thoi-ky-phat-trien/giai-doan-1975-1985.html)

7. **The historical Front allies were not already competing governments.** The Democratic and Socialist parties operated as allied organizations in the Front. They ended their activity in 1988. Their independent liberal or social democratic governing programmes in the concept are alternate-history mandates, not descriptions of historical competitive elections. [Democratic Party institutional history](https://dcs.nhandan.vn/tu-lieu-tham-khao-cuoc-thi-trac-nghiem-tim-hieu-90-nam-lich-su-ve-vang-cua-dang-cong-san-viet-nam/tu-lieu-90-nam-lich-su-dang/thanh-lap-dang-dan-chu-viet-nam-536979.html), [Fatherland Front reference, Socialist Party entry](https://static.mattran.org.vn/Uploaded/buidoanhung/2020_09_23/thuat_ngu_mttqvn_11_8_2020_MWVJ.pdf)

8. **Kiệt's programme is an alternative national leadership route.** He held important economic and government responsibilities and was acting chairman of the Council of Ministers for part of 1988. He was not the historical General Secretary succeeding Nguyễn Văn Linh. Keep the programme available through the approved alternate leadership arrangement without rewriting the real succession. [Government cabinet record](https://chinhphu.vn/thanh-vien-chinh-phu-qua-cac-thoi-ky-68352?governId=618)

9. **Military lessons should reflect actual operations.** The 1972 offensive involved large conventional formations and modern weapons; local revolutionary organization and conventional operations were not mutually exclusive national identities. Southern revolutionary warfare must also remain distinct from the later FULRO resistance. A UN report still records the surrender of FULRO remnants in 1992, beyond this concept's endpoint. [US Army campaign summaries](https://history.army.mil/Research/Reference-Topics/Army-Campaigns/Brief-Summaries/Vietnam/), [UN report S/25124, paragraph 84](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/158376/files/S_25124-EN.pdf)

10. **Campaign victory, withdrawal and diplomatic settlement were different achievements.** The ECCC judgment dates the large Cambodian offensive to late December 1978 and the capture of Phnom Penh to 7 January 1979. The UN's Cambodia background describes the subsequent conflict and opposing coalition. Neither capital capture nor a friendly government automatically ended resistance or occupation costs. [ECCC judgment, paragraphs 79–80](https://www.eccc.gov.kh/sites/default/files/documents/courtdoc/20100726_Judgement_Case_001_ENG_PUBLIC.pdf), [UN Cambodia background](https://peacekeeping.un.org/sites/default/files/past/unamicbackgr.html)

11. **China's short 1979 invasion and prolonged frontier confrontation require different rewards.** Contemporary US records distinguish the withdrawal completed by 16 March from continuing mobilization and tensions; Vietnamese military logistics history dates the invasion's start to 17 February. Japan's 1991 diplomatic review records the September 1990 Chengdu meeting and renewed civilian border trade. The tree may prepare before a crisis and reward verified de-escalation, without claiming formal normalization happened by 1990. [Contemporary FRUS document and editorial footnote 3](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1977-80v22/d48), [Vietnamese military logistics history](https://hc.qdnd.vn/lich-su-hau-can/nhung-thanh-cong-ve-cong-tac-hau-can-trong-cuoc-chien-dau-bao-ve-bien-gioi-phia-bac-481665), [Japanese diplomatic review](https://www.mofa.go.jp/policy/other/bluebook/1991/1991-4-1.htm)

## Party schools, administrators and economic expertise

| Current focus | Semantic assessment | Meaningful positive result | Plausible cost or limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `COLDWAR_VIE_chinh_schools` — Party Schools | +3% political-power gain alone does not express its description's practical training purpose. | **Train District Administrators:** better implementation of procurement, enterprise accounts and local public services; a more competent, coordinated cadre service. | Teachers, accommodation, education funding and officials temporarily away from their posts. Training is not instant mastery of advanced technology. |
| `COLDWAR_VIE_chinh_experts` — Hear the Economists | Generic productivity can be an eventual proxy, but merely listening does not create output. | **Commission a Planning Review:** identify input bottlenecks, correct unrealistic targets, improve project selection and trial accounting or price changes. | Specialist secondment, data collection and review delay; the benefit depends on implementing recommendations. No automatic transfer of political authority to an opposition party. |
| `COLDWAR_VIE_state_cadres` — Professional Cadres | General research speed gives the wrong dominant outcome. | Professional records, consistent instructions and less waste in administration or project delivery. | Recruitment/training spending; a larger paid professional service can cost more while performing better. |
| `COLDWAR_VIE_kiet_skills` — Train the Managers | The direct outcome is enterprise management, not all national science. | Cost accounting, purchasing, inventory control and responsible enterprise management. | Training time and retaining scarce skilled personnel; managers initially removed from production. |
| `COLDWAR_VIE_front_experts` — A Technical Cabinet | A cabinet can improve decisions without discovering military technologies. | Better coordination of economic ministries and execution of funded programmes. | Salaries, consultation and a narrower supply of experts for enterprises and provinces. |
| `COLDWAR_VIE_soc_planning` — Planning Boards | Small stability plus bureaucracy expense gives little reason to create the institution. | Coordinate investment, procurement and employment commitments; reduce contradictory orders. | Reporting and staff costs; risks of slow approval or poor information. |

**Exclusivity test:** Party schools and expert review can coexist. If a fork is wanted, name a specific allocation: **Expand District Training** versus **Second Specialists to the Planning Review**. Both consume the next tranche of instructors, trained personnel and funding. Completing one should not assert that the other institution no longer exists. Their strongest outcomes should be distinguishable: reliable local execution versus improved diagnosis and project selection.

Likewise, competent planning and evidence are not opposites. `chinh_orthodox` / `chinh_evidence` currently frame authority against growth through “Planning Discipline” / “Evidence before Dogma.” A defensible fork could prioritize **Enforce the Existing Delivery Plan** versus **Revise Targets through Provincial Trials**. The first stabilizes immediate deliveries but preserves rigidity; the second corrects poor allocations but temporarily complicates instructions and reporting. Both require economic knowledge.

## Specific reward corrections

### State institutions and political programmes

- **`state_western`, `state_criticism`, `dem_root`, `dem_rights`, `front_reconcile`, `linh_pluralism`:** constitutional debate, criticism, reconciliation and association do not inherently increase every research field. Their direct benefits are legitimacy, corrected abuses, confidence in procedures, retention of qualified citizens and better public information. Costs can be the staff needed to hear complaints, negotiate mandates and administer new protections. A narrow scientific benefit requires an actual education/research component.
- **`state_discipline`, `chinh_guardians`, `front_vanguard`, `linh_monopoly`, `dem_executive`, `nat_president`, `junta_security`, `junta_permanent`:** repeated authority gains purchased with general research penalties construct a false universal rule. A concentrated command can execute a priority faster or maintain a chain of responsibility. Its plausible price is fewer independent checks, slower correction of bad information, additional security administration or opposition to the chosen mandate. Do not claim that all technical learning slows simply because a government is authoritarian.
- **`state_assembly`, `state_charter`, `chinh_documents`, `chinh_handover`, `junta_assembly`:** give constitutional and succession work a defined outcome: accepted procedures, a broader institutional bargain, reduced succession uncertainty or a completed governing mandate. Writing Congress documents is not itself technological research. An orderly handover should express continuity, rather than being another nearly interchangeable small stability purchase.
- **`state_school`, `soc_rural`, `dem_skills`, `industry_skills`, `soc_intellect`:** distinguish basic literacy, vocational education, technical college capacity and research institutions. The first three principally create capable workers/administrators and improve practical adoption. `soc_intellect` is the most natural place for a general scientific capacity benefit when the description actually funds institutions and researchers. All can use education costs; they should not provide identical research rewards for different historical activities.
- **`state_women`:** productive participation is credible when the programme provides training, childcare, health support and access to administrative work. These commitments explain a social/education cost. Merely mobilizing already overburdened women should not be described as a costless reserve of extra labor.
- **`state_national`:** “National Sovereignty” is broader than support for the military-council slot. If the gameplay purpose is to grow that outlook, describe the actual military-nationalist organizing campaign, while allowing other parties to defend sovereignty through their own programmes.

### Lê Duẩn and postwar priorities

- **`duan_shock` — The Main Force:** arms-factory output is an indirect response to a focus about military force structure. The main benefit should prepare concentrated formations: trained staffs, combined-arms readiness or sustainable operational supply. A credible cost is equipment/fuel demand, training time or labor diverted to military needs.
- **`duan_heavy`, `duan_balanced`, `duan_goods`, `duan_combine`:** decide which investment projects receive scarce machinery, technicians and imported inputs first. Heavy industry should develop machinery, repair or productive capacity as appropriate; it should not be reduced to ammunition output. Food and consumer supply can strengthen workers' ability to remain productive. A heavy-first path can be desirable through long-term capacity while demanding more present consumption restraint.
- **`duan_collective`, `duan_household`:** a national slogan about collective mastery does not directly manufacture arms. If the choice concerns agriculture, specify who assigns labor and retains marginal output on the same participating land. If it concerns enterprise governance, explain that institutional domain instead. Household activity was not an ideological switch that had to wait until Lê Duẩn's death.
- **`duan_transform`, `duan_gradual`, `duan_integration`:** rapid transformation can consolidate command over assets and tax/delivery channels; gradual integration can preserve experienced staff and functioning supplier networks. Those are concrete, different benefits. Rapid transformation also risks disruption and the loss of expertise. Do not make “more political power” the entire practical reward for restructuring a whole economy.

### Trường Chinh, Linh and Kiệt

- **`chinh_procurement` — Procurement Targets:** its description promises predictable state deliveries, while the reward is +4% arms-factory output and −5% agricultural productivity. Unless the focus explicitly reallocates inputs to weapons manufacture, the positive result belongs to food procurement, urban/military provisioning or a maintained reserve. The realistic cost is poor marginal incentives under onerous quotas or low administered prices; procurement itself is not the same activity as growing rice.
- **`chinh_ration`:** a ration guarantee distributes goods. It need not directly lower agricultural yields. Budget, storage, transport and administration are direct costs. If an agricultural penalty is intended, identify the procurement-price or labor mechanism responsible.
- **`chinh_field`, `chinh_evidence`, `chinh_prices`, `chinh_autonomy`:** inquiries and pilot reforms can reveal mistakes, improve allocations and enable enterprise decisions. Reward implementation of the findings, not an instant harvest merely because a delegation visited a province. Autonomy is about operational powers and retained returns; household protection can remain under either allocation system.
- **`linh_inflation` — Fiscal Stabilisation:** reducing the bureaucracy-cost multiplier is not by itself an anti-inflation programme. Its historical function needs an explicit constraint on monetary/fiscal accommodation, credit or enterprise loss coverage, with a resulting stabilization benefit. Short-run demand, employment or enterprise funding pressure can be the price. A general productivity loss plus cheaper administration does not clearly achieve the stated aim. The government's account treats enterprise autonomy, supply-subsidy reform and financial measures as separate components. [Government account of the 1987–1992 term; apply only measures within 1990](https://chinhphu.vn/giai-doan-1986-2003-muoi-tam-nam-su-nghiep-doi-moi/i-chinh-phu-nhiem-ky-quoc-hoi-khoa-viii-1987-1992-2916)
- **`linh_limited`, `linh_guarantee`:** exposure and globalism mostly describe vulnerability or orientation. Add a practical investment outcome from a legal framework, funded industrial facility, credible contracting capacity or an accepted investment project. Screening and access should both be possible under Party-led renovation. A wider application pipeline can cost supervisory capacity; a strategic-project priority can concentrate scarce power, land and trained staff.
- **`linh_survival`, `diplomacy_transition`:** a bloc crisis should not independently create growth. A positive programme can replace lost supplies, diversify export buyers, retain useful technicians or reorganize commitments. The gain comes from those measures, with real transition costs.
- **`kiet_trade` — Goods for the Cities:** better urban distribution is not automatically higher crop yields. The direct reward is more reliable supply, market connection or fewer losses between producers and consumers. Higher production needs an additional incentive/input mechanism.
- **`kiet_enterprise` — Retained Earnings:** retaining profit can finance maintenance, stocks and enterprise investment. It does not inherently reduce weapons output. A realistic price is revenue foregone by the central treasury or reduced ministerial discretion over those funds.
- **`kiet_exports`:** a programme needs an exportable product, production/logistics capacity and an actual buyer or access arrangement. Globalism and market exposure alone leave the promised experiment without a useful commercial result. An initial export budget can be a credible choice against a domestic procurement project using the same goods and working capital.
- **`kiet_local`, `kiet_national`, `kiet_results`, `kiet_responsibility`:** pilot design, expansion after evaluation and named managerial responsibility should be a coherent delivery chain. National replication and local experimentation are not lifetime enemies. Accountability can improve completion/maintenance, with audit and staffing costs rather than a perpetual arbitrary loss of political power.

### Front, Democratic, Socialist and nationalist alternatives

- **`front_councils`, `front_experts`, `front_cadres`:** councils can supply local information and consent; technical ministers and a common civil service can execute decisions. The real authority question is who has the final right to approve a specific plan, not whether consultation permits expertise. If these are an exclusive pair, specify one binding decision procedure.
- **`front_cooperative`, `front_households`, `soc_socialise`, `soc_mixed`, `soc_competition`:** voluntary cooperative inputs, marketing and credit can serve household producers, while public key industries coexist with private/cooperative activity. Use a finite state-investment priority or competing rights over the same enterprise/land. Avoid economy-wide bans unsupported by the title.
- **`dem_tender`, `dem_champions`:** competitive tenders and state enterprises are compatible. A genuine conflict is whether the same procurement contract is competitively awarded or directly reserved for a designated domestic supplier. Benefits: lower-cost/verified purchasing versus predictable domestic orders and retained capacity. Costs: tender administration/import dependence versus a less competitive price or subsidy requirement.
- **`dem_courts`, `dem_executive`, `dem_order`:** an emergency mandate and courts can coexist. For an authority fork, use a defined final-review rule or scope/duration of emergency powers. Both paths should provide an operational state, with different oversight and speed costs, rather than courts providing society's only competence.
- **`soc_labour`, `soc_bargain`, `soc_tripartite`, `soc_arbitrate`:** labor organization can improve representation, retention and the peaceful resolution of disputes. Independent unions are not naturally a weapons-output debuff, nor does bargaining necessarily reduce aggregate productivity. Specify the costs of wage commitments, bargaining time, training or dispute administration. Arbitration may enforce a bargaining settlement rather than exclude it.
- **`soc_universal`, `soc_target`:** allocate the next social budget between broad baseline coverage and intensive provision in the poorest rural districts. Both need meaningful service results. Targeting requires administration and risks missed households; universal provision needs a larger budget. Do not imply that one path permanently forbids care for the other population.
- **`front_welfare`, `soc_housing`, `soc_rural`, `soc_planning`:** clinics, housing, health/literacy and planning institutions should do their named jobs. Confidence/stability can be part of the reward, but a social programme should not become an expensive political ceremony without healthier, housed or better-trained participants.
- **`dem_west`, `nat_guarantee`:** external guarantees are bilateral diplomatic achievements, not a unilateral reward for an ideological speech. Before acceptance, reward negotiating capacity or a credible proposal; after acceptance, use the actual agreement's tangible benefit and obligations.
- **`front_nonaligned`, `dem_neutral`, `nat_autonomy`, `junta_sovereignty`:** sovereignty or neutrality alone cannot manufacture import substitutes or defeat a sanctions regime. A resilience benefit needs stocks, domestic maintenance, alternate suppliers or a confirmed trade arrangement. The cost can be expensive local production or less favorable commercial terms, without implying that autonomy requires total isolation.
- **`nat_security`, `junta_service`, `junta_officers`, `junta_screen`:** integrating factions and setting officer standards can genuinely improve command. Vetting can establish accountable service or prevent infiltration, but removing experienced personnel has a practical cost. Widespread coercive exclusion cannot be rewritten as harmless administrative tidying simply to keep every negative tiny.

### Productive economy and agriculture

- **`industry_root`, `farm_root`, `army_root`:** surveys and reviews deserve a completed planning/intelligence/training result or an immediate useful first intervention. A cost and an unlock are thin when the focus itself takes 70 days. The result must follow from the review's actual subject; the review does not create a free factory.
- **`industry_disperse`:** dispersed workshops principally preserve production and repair capacity under attack, at the price of transport, duplication and lower economies of scale. A national infantry-defense bonus misses the main industrial function unless the description specifically includes civil-defense workshop support.
- **`industry_repair`:** mobile repair crews should restore damaged roads, utilities or machinery. Faster new infrastructure construction can be a secondary proxy, but the main title is repair. An agricultural cost is defensible only when rural workers are explicitly taken from seasonal farm work.
- **`industry_capital`:** capital-goods production supplies machines and industrial equipment. Military factory output alone makes the economic choice read as “weapons versus civilians.” Give a heavy-investment branch a productive machinery/repair/construction outcome if the native mechanics allow it; reserve a primarily arms-output reward for an explicit armaments programme.
- **`industry_depot`, `kiet_spares`, `army_repair`:** maintenance, spare parts and equipment repair should recover usable capacity or improve the efficiency of the relevant production system. Repairing old equipment does not intrinsically slow all scientific research. If it diverts development funds, describe the specific modernization budget being deferred.
- **`industry_engineers`, `diplomacy_specialists`:** contracts and specialists can transfer practical engineering, improve construction/maintenance and train operators. Their strongest gain should depend on actual work or assistance; contact alone is not an all-technology discovery. Housing, translation, supervision and imported spares explain costs.
- **`industry_machine`:** “Northeastern Industry” currently builds in state 524. Verify the state against the actual map: the installed English mapping identifies 524 as Northwest Vietnam and 523 as North Vietnam. Use the geographic title that matches the chosen site. This is a placement/narrative mismatch, not a recommendation to move a building without checking ownership and slots.
- **`farm_procure`, `farm_plots`:** the current first fork is **Raise Procurement** versus **Expand Plot Incentives**, not “cooperatives versus private plots.” This can represent a credible near-term priority: secure a predictable state delivery commitment versus let households retain more marginal production. Both should feed the country through a distinct mechanism, with the opportunity cost made explicit.
- **`farm_reserves`:** the beneficial result is an available emergency buffer and more reliable distribution, paid for by buying/storage/transport and spoilage management. A reserve is not a new source of annual grain production. If the existing spirit-stage relief is substantial and actually active, credit it as part of that reward rather than looking only at its operating-cost line.
- **`farm_market`, `farm_distribution`, `farm_surplus`, `linh_markets`:** local trade and distribution connect buyers and producers, reduce losses and make incentives effective. They do not mechanically improve biological yields without input or incentive changes. If agricultural productivity is the only supported useful proxy, the description should clearly include those production responses.
- **`farm_inputs`:** fertilizer, tools and irrigation can directly support production. Foreign exposure is a reasonable cost only when inputs depend on imported machinery, chemicals or credit; it is not unavoidable for every domestic tool programme.
- **`farm_ration`, `farm_contract`:** universal rations govern entitlements/distribution; farm contracts govern production obligations and marginal returns. They are not historical opposites. Replace permanent mutual exclusion with a finite administrative/budget allocation, or choose two competing rules for the same procurement obligation.
- **`farm_district`:** state 523 can contain agricultural activity, but a title emphasizing the main northern rice-producing districts should be checked against state 522/Red River Delta before settling the project site. Do not imply that all northern rice belongs to an upland border region.

### Army, integration and foreign policy

- **`army_aircrew`:** reward actual aircrew competence, instructor capacity, technical maintenance or training progress. A general research bonus does not deliver the trained crews promised by the focus. The cost is flight hours, fuel, maintenance, instructors and education funding.
- **`army_civil`:** evacuation, medical support, shelter, emergency repair and continuity of civilian supply are the primary civil-defense functions. A land-army organization increase is indirect. Existing mobilization-spirit relief may be useful if it reflects reduced disruption rather than pretending the war is over.
- **`army_coastal`:** coastal guns, local naval maintenance, observation and protected approaches have a defensive function. A globalism reduction is not a necessary result of defending a coast. Use upkeep, manpower, equipment or a competing naval investment as its cost. Coastal defense and fleet repair can coexist; a fork should allocate the same limited coastal budget.
- **`army_local`, `army_reserve`, `army_drill`, `army_staff`:** local defense, seasonal reserves and staff professionalism can be desirable through different military functions. Lost farm/construction labor is a credible cost when training or mobilization actually absorbs those workers; longer courses may produce better officers. Do not apply an unrelated national production penalty solely because every focus needs a red number.
- **`army_postwar`, `unity_peace`, `guerrilla_civilian`, `guerrilla_veterans`:** releasing soldiers and reintegrating veterans can return labor and reduce emergency burdens, with pensions, training, healthcare and temporarily lower available readiness. The dividend should require the relevant demobilization or cessation of fighting, rather than trigger while the same commitment remains fully mobilized.
- **`unity_aftermath`, `unity_united`, `unity_registry`:** recovery administration can reconnect records, welfare entitlements, family tracing, civil documentation and supply networks. The reward should help accomplish those tasks. Registration is not inherently a permanent stability penalty; a short transition cost must identify the dispute, capacity pressure or vetting policy responsible.
- **`unity_strict`, `unity_amnesty`, `unity_citizens`, `unity_resettle`:** distinguish security vetting, restoration of civil rights, return of skilled people and funded housing/land/medical support. Amnesty does not automatically create productivity; safe return to work can. Coercive screening can cause lost skills and fear. Resettlement needs consent, viable land, water, health and transport, not merely a relocated population count.
- **`unity_paris`, `duan_talk`, `diplomacy_confidence`, `diplomacy_normalise`:** negotiations can establish communication, confidence measures or accepted arrangements. They do not automatically remove enemies, create a treaty or unlock all research. Give diplomatic labor a useful preparation result and reserve the peace/trade dividend for acceptance and compliance.
- **`diplomacy_moscow`, `diplomacy_balance`, `diplomacy_specialists`, `diplomacy_alternatives`:** donor relationships and supplier diversification should improve access to actual needed inputs, maintenance or training. Differing equipment standards can initially complicate logistics. “Balance” should not imply that deteriorating Chinese relations can always be canceled out by a focus's name.
- **`diplomacy_opening`, `diplomacy_isolation`, `diplomacy_autarky`:** offer an attractive, concrete commercial or resilience result. Controlled openness can develop approved trade/industrial connections; self-reliance can fund domestic repair and substitutes. Both should carry a small relevant operating cost rather than one being only exposure and the other only expensive isolation.

### Revolutionary warfare, Cambodia and China

- **`guerrilla_training`, `guerrilla_staff`, `guerrilla_review`, `guerrilla_record`:** training and an actual campaign review are credible sources of military learning. Doctrine or training progress fits the theme better than generic research. Explain whether a timed modifier represents an active funded school/operational programme; acquired experience should not be narrated as forgotten after 180 days.
- **`guerrilla_networks`, `guerrilla_guarantees`, `guerrilla_services`, `guerrilla_security`, `guerrilla_screening`:** liaison and locally credible guarantees can improve information, recruitment/consent and access to services; security deployments can protect routes and officials. Do not make ethnic identity itself a penalty or claim every Highlander belonged to FULRO. A political settlement requires actual local arrangements and credible implementation, not only a national speech.
- **`guerrilla_settlement`, `guerrilla_aftermath`:** political settlement and an end to the long war deserve the practical dividend of lower deployment/administrative burdens and civilian reintegration. Army experience alone does not capture their principal achievement. Retained reserves and civilian return should compete over a specified manpower commitment, not represent eternal national identities.
- **`cambodia_question`, `cambodia_mandate`, `china_frontier`:** political questions, channels and statements of war aims do not automatically teach troops. Military experience is justified when the description adds real staff planning, reconnaissance, exercises or an evidenced campaign review. A diplomatic preparation focus can instead improve the ability to reach an actual agreement.
- **`cambodia_partner`:** a secure southwest can permit farmers to return and release Vietnamese forces from some duties. A friendly Cambodian regime alone cannot guarantee a permanent +6% national agricultural-productivity increase. Tie the dividend to actual border security, recovered activity or lower commitment, and avoid implying all Vietnam's agriculture is improved by regime alignment.
- **`cambodia_direct`, `cambodia_partner`, `cambodia_train`:** a Cambodian partner and Vietnamese forces historically coexisted. If the fork remains, make it the **primary responsibility and next security budget**: expand the partner's capacity versus maintain a larger direct Vietnamese security apparatus. Both can have an attractive security benefit; one pays training/coordination costs and the other a greater deployment/administration burden. Do not ban all partner training while Vietnamese troops remain.
- **`cambodia_restore`:** a Cambodian reconstruction programme must benefit the land or recipient being reconstructed. A Vietnam-wide repair modifier is thematically insufficient if no damaged Cambodian infrastructure receives work. Funding, workers and supplies should produce a real civilian recovery result.
- **`cambodia_dry`, `cambodia_stay`:** sustained deployments should improve the particular security task: protected routes, trained local units or reduced losses in a real relevant operation. Their cost is fuel, maintenance, personnel rotation and workers unavailable at home. Anti-occupation guerrillas are not automatically the same thing as the engine's generic occupied-state resistance.
- **`cambodia_leave`, `cambodia_verify`, `cambodia_talks`, `cambodia_border_settlement`:** preparations cost staff and transport, but a verified drawdown or accepted border settlement should deliver a real reduction in the burden it resolves. The current verified-drawdown idea adds bureaucracy cost and reduces organization; it needs the positive consequence of the verification somewhere in the completed chain. Withdrawal is not a claim that the Cambodian civil war has ended. [Contemporary Japanese review of the 1989 process](https://www.mofa.go.jp/policy/other/bluebook/1989/1989-3-2.htm)
- **`china_survey`, `china_liaison`, `china_hold`, `china_depth`:** field works, command, supply and preparedness fit the northern threat. Prepare before war when the historical short-war window makes a late focus useless. Defense in depth and a bounded counterattack can be different emphases for the same committed force, while neither should forbid tactical defense or subsequent negotiation.
- **`china_soviet`, `china_assistance`, `china_self`:** a Soviet commitment requires an actual accepted assistance relationship. The benefit comes from delivered training/equipment/coordination; autonomy requires a practical domestic preparation programme. Neither the Soviet relationship nor self-reliance should be a magical source of all military knowledge.
- **`china_opening`, `china_standdown`:** both currently award temporary bureaucracy expense and reduced army organization, alongside diplomatic unlocks. Negotiating can cost staff time, but a **verified** reduction in exceptional confrontation should ease deployment, release some labor or enable agreed border activity. Preserve readiness through a small relevant cost; do not make successfully verified diplomacy an intrinsically bad result. A verified agreement must not automatically relocate Chinese forces.
- **`china_citizens`, `china_rebuild`, `china_security`:** civil-rights guarantees, repaired northern communities and a sustainable frontier have distinct civilian and military functions. Link reconstruction to damaged northern capacity and reduced disruption; do not turn every final peaceful settlement into another doctrine discount with no civilian dividend.

## Which choices genuinely compete?

| Policy distinction | Appropriate exclusivity | Both sides' useful results | Small cost grounded in the choice |
|---|---|---|---|
| District cadre courses / specialist planning secondment | Next training/advisory allocation, not permanent institutions. | Better execution / better diagnosis and project selection. | Training funding and officials absent / expert staff and review time. |
| Enforce a current delivery plan / revise it through trials | Authority over the same current targets and rollout. | Predictable deliveries / correction of unrealistic allocations. | Rigidity / transition and reporting complexity. |
| Direct collective labor assignment / household responsibility for above-contract output | Rights over the same participating work and fields. | Coordinated labor/input tasks / stronger marginal incentives. | Supervision / revised accounting and coordination. |
| Central investment pool / enterprise retained earnings | Control of the same retained funds. | Strategic coordinated projects / maintenance and local reinvestment. | Slower local decisions / less discretionary central revenue. |
| Strategic state project / broader licensed investment pipeline | The next scarce power, transport, land and supervisory budget. | Concentrated national capacity / more diversified productive projects. | Concentration risk / oversight and external exposure. |
| Competitive procurement / reserved domestic order | Award method for the same contract. | Price/quality verification / predictable local capacity. | Tender administration / protected price and subsidy burden. |
| Broad basic services / intensive poorest-district provision | The next social-spending tranche. | Wider baseline coverage / deeper improvement where deprivation is worst. | Larger recurring bill / targeting and coverage gaps. |
| Larger direct Cambodian deployment / partner capacity first | Allocation of the same next security resources and responsibilities. | Immediate direct capacity / lower eventual Vietnamese burden. | Deployment upkeep / training and partner coordination. |
| More frontier formations / verified de-escalation and lighter posture | Current exceptional deployment after real reciprocal measures. | Greater immediate readiness / lower sustained burden and civilian recovery. | Workers and supplies committed / monitoring and reduced surge capacity. |

**Do not create permanent exclusions for:** ration entitlements versus farm production contracts; cooperative services versus every household holding; political pluralism versus investment access; foreign-investment screening versus legal guarantees; economic advice versus Party education; food versus consumer goods versus exports; unions versus all arbitration; state enterprises versus all tenders; tactical defense versus any counterstroke; negotiation versus any continuing defense; a Cambodian partner versus every Vietnamese troop.

For a 70-day focus, the small cost can be a purchase, recurring staffing commitment, training time, a specified foregone allocation or a temporary transition burden. It does not have to be a permanent nationwide productivity/research penalty. Conversely, policies known to involve severe disruption should not be made harmless merely by using a smaller number; use a narrower and more defensible policy concept if a mild tradeoff is wanted.

## Recommended thematic priorities for the rework

1. Give schools, managers, experts, institutions and project administration a practical function before increasing their numbers.
2. Separate crop production, procurement, reserves and distribution; let each solve a different part of food security.
3. Separate industrial machinery from armaments, civil defense from field-army organization, and aircrew competence from general research.
4. Make preparation useful on completion, agreements useful on acceptance, and verified demobilization/settlement useful when the burden actually falls.
5. Make both sides of each fork effective at their chosen task. Use competing budgets, manpower or decision rights where the historical programmes were otherwise compatible.
6. Keep later developments conditional, alternative party mandates explicit and national leadership offices historically distinct.
