# Vietnam campaign reward rework — all68 focuses

Proposed data only; no game or generator files were edited. The JSON supplies complete absolute idea values and finite typed effects for every existing guerrilla, Cambodian and Chinese focus. It preserves existing cash costs, focus duration, real outcomes and consent unless a separately explicit preparation/activation contract moves the wartime check. Current source is the immutable approval plus its installed defence/timing corrections, read without running the compiler.

## Design outcome

Training schools become enduring institutions with genuine finite Army XP, land doctrine support and two research bonuses in a category containing1965/1975/1985MDtechnologies. Operational plans are earned before a crisis; an actual war activates a prepaid term immediately. There are no daily gifts, fake victories, free divisions, fictional food/supply resources or automatic wars. Signed productivity-growth modifiers are removed from this campaign proposal, rather than advertised as assured growth.

The exact homeland defence requests remain untouched: Hold3entrenchment/20%digging/5%core/5%forest-jungle and planning−3%; Depth5/25%/8%/10%and planning−5%. Home defence has no supply or military factory malus. Counterstroke instead offers8%CHIattack/3%defence/5%planning/3%supply; bounded objectives10%/3%/5%/4%, so the offensive choice is actually desirable.

## Why the exclusive choices are real

- **guerrilla_concentrate / guerrilla_disperse:** Same scarce command training is committed to planned concentration rather than dispersed recovery. The choices are mutually exclusive allocations, not mutually exclusive existence of regular and local forces.
- **guerrilla_guarantees / guerrilla_security:** General safeguards favor a civilian economic and stability settlement. Accountable security favors army cohesion and readiness during actual unrest. General safeguards do not need an invented crisis.
- **guerrilla_civilian / guerrilla_reserve:** An actual postwar allocation of personnel: civilian work offers economic recovery, while reserve service improves reinforcement and training readiness.
- **cambodia_intervene / cambodia_limited:** Exclusive strategic objective against the limited route. This plan can change a hostile regime only through actual war and settlement; it never installs a puppet or annexes Cambodia automatically.
- **cambodia_direct / cambodia_partner:** The alternative to a consenting partner is a real occupied-territory administration, not free annexation. Benefits have no occupation effect on Vietnamese cores.
- **cambodia_leave / cambodia_stay:** A financially attractive genuine drawdown rather than a policy card containing only costs. It cannot retain the occupation benefits after occupation ends.
- **china_counter / china_hold:** Genuine offensive utility versus Hold, without being a weaker number weighed down by several major national penalties.
- **china_self / china_soviet:** Useful assured domestic doctrine/production versus organization/research liaison and possible external assistance.
- **china_mobilise / china_opening:** Forward military readiness versus domestic recovery and a real negotiated reduction of frontier burdens.

## Lifecycle and scope requirements

- **no scenario changes:** No1970starting factories/GDP/army/OOB/leader/party edits. This document changes rewards of the existing68focuses only.
- **permanent vs timed:** duration_days=null means earned national institutional base, not a forever refreshed paid operation. Preserve its selection across party/leader changes; attach only while explicit real conditions hold. Timed operating cards retain a true expiry and never get a new full duration from daily update.
- **fallback:** When a genuine timed Soviet mission tier expires, only the already earned permanent liaison foundation may resume. Do not restore the expired mission idea. Similar family fallbacks must restore only durable earned institutions, never an unused opposing route.
- **foreign offers:** Recipient owned event; recheck exact actor, current pending/inbox, countries, real conditions, sender cash and expiry on acceptance. Free donor request needs no VIE treasury>=0 gate. Contributions charged only on a valid current acceptance; no double acceptance. Native donor pays and recipient accepts the real military programme separately.
- **limited outcome:** No-war plus no Vietnamese divisions in ALL Cambodian states511/512/513/514 plus no continuing non core occupation and current accepted terms. Removing divisions while retaining occupied states is not a non occupation settlement. Never award the25XP+two20%actual-victory package for merely signing limited terms.
- **true victory:** Keep actual observed war/capital objective and a normal postwar outcome plus the appropriate lawful Cambodian government checks. Do not classify victories through a questionable on_peace FROM contract. Use actual capitulation/observed outcomes as already verified.
- **actual withdrawal:** No scripted peace, army movement, annexation, instant puppet, automatic end of paid native aid or refund. Observe actual full drawdown/no occupation; actual continuing native aid can retain a light burden. Renewed troops/occupation invalidate drawdown benefits.
- **civil rights:** No automatic ethnic rebellion, mass disloyalty or painless coercion. Generic safeguards need no invented grievance. Security funding alone must NOT set domestic_settlement and erase the real internal-pressure burden. Actual grievance resolution or accepted local terms must be verified separately.
- **only native resources:** Native treasury, existing army XP, finite doctrine/research bonuses, national modifiers and actual diplomacy/state/war conditions. No guerrilla meter, fictional supplies, free daily PP/XP/equipment or fake food resource.
- **national scope:** All proposed idea modifiers are countrywide. Targeted bonuses apply against the named opponent on every front. Occupation keys apply to every qualifying VIE non core occupation. These are never described as state-local bonuses.

The preparation split is an implementation recommendation, not a claim that the current generator already supports it. Northern Emergency Planning replaces the historical-crisis predecessor trap while the observer still records actual Chinese wars independently. Every stored initial activation is nontransferable and consumed once. A stronger stage retires an unused weaker entitlement. An expired operating idea never returns simply because a government, treaty or war context reappears.
