IMPERIA COLD WAR

Artwork method: built-in OpenAI image generation, followed by built-in image
editing. The final atlas has transparent gutters and twelve 4-by-3 cells. It is
concept art, not an installed sprite sheet. PNG originals and a compressed WebP
copy are retained in the thread-scoped visualization directory. No direct
third-party raster donor was used. Separate distribution licence is unknown.

Initial prompt:

> Create ONE polished game-art concept sheet containing exactly TWELVE separate Hearts of Iron IV / Millennium Dawn Cold War national-focus medallion icons, in a perfectly regular 4 columns by 3 rows grid. This is one atlas artwork for a design preview, not a full interface. Equal square cells, generous transparent gutters, each icon fully contained, perfectly aligned centers. Genuinely transparent background outside the medallions; NO text, NO letters, NO labels, NO numbers, NO borders around the sheet. All twelve share a native historical-strategy-game style: intricate but readable cast bronze and aged gold laurel wreaths, layered brushed-steel heraldic objects, muted navy and olive enamel, strong silhouette readable as an 80px focus icon, painterly realistic objects, subtle directional highlights. No glossy app icon squares, no neon, no modern flat vector art. Each wreath is a complete standalone cutout. Row 1 left to right: (1) recognizable Henry Kissinger circa 1972, thick dark glasses, dark suit and tie, head-and-shoulders painted portrait, restrained US eagle behind him inside the wreath; (2) National Security Council cabinet table and closed confidential papers, small US eagle seal; (3) Cold War balance of power, opposing chess pieces in front of a steel globe; (4) Kurdish mountain landscape and a gold sun with radiating rays, dignified mountain-resistance symbolism. Row 2: (5) Iraq, bronze Iraq-shaped relief emblem over northern mountains and Tigris river symbolism; (6) Iran, Iranian-shaped bronze relief with Zagros mountains and a small antique Persian lion motif; (7) Turkey, Turkish-shaped bronze relief with crescent and star, northern mountain background; (8) Syria, Syrian-shaped bronze relief with northern hills and a small Damascus architectural silhouette. These are symbolic relief icons, do not add contemporary flags or political party insignia. Row 3: (9) discreet shortwave radio and headphones, folded dispatch; (10) opened supply crate containing period-correct 1960s infantry rifles and ammunition, no people; (11) government countermeasures, a mountain watchtower, an army truck and treasury ledger; (12) Desert Storm, period 1991 tank in dunes beneath a restrained lightning motif. Keep all details and wreaths well separated. The subject must sit inside each wreath, with no card background. No extra icons, no watermarks, no extraneous decoration.
