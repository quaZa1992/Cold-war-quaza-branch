## Delivered regional system

One unrest bar, a short status and one warning expose the regional situation.
The host can fund security with at least two actual divisions in the region,
invest in local institutions or offer a political settlement. Sustained operations
build lasting stabilisation; direct troop effects stop on withdrawal, while earned
calm fades gradually. There is no automatic deployment or campaign expiry.
Renewed pressure can revive unrest later, including after containment or reconquest.

Mobilisation requires actual funding, access, material, sufficient unrest and a
completed warning. The USA must then select a mobilisation decision. Runtime
guards recheck the current conditions before transferring fully controlled eligible
territory or opening a war. A newly created regional actor receives its own bounded
startup, rather than copying the host's treasury, debt, army or equipment.
Its owned technical minimum is undated and also covers the new country tags.
Imported rifles explicitly use the American producer, allowing actual delivery
without giving the recipient 1965 manufacturing research.
The native column constructor's generated equipment is cleared only within the
new-actor branch. Existing regional RP armies never enter that cleanup. The native
test observes zero initial stored/deployed weapons and exactly 2,000 real weapons
after accepting the two committed packages, with no duplicate on a second attempt.

Iraqi KUR and PUK countries are preserved. The other theatres use separate KPI,
KPT and KPS identities in bounded regions. The PUK's actual formation condition
remains required. Recognition requires actual land and the local authority's
request. The Kuwait liberation war-goal path remains independent of the Kurdish
chapter, including an unresolved invasion after Kuwaiti capitulation.

All recurring costs use weekly MD units. The preserved Gulf wartime contract
retains 1,000 actual weapons and its distinct wartime programme. Its money now
costs 0.025% of US GDP at setup and 0.005% of US GDP/week. New lower-cost political programmes have their own explicit terms. Already
paid legacy assistance and security receipts migrate without another setup fee.
New regional commitments, including optional Gulf arms support, respect the same
two/four campaign limit. Nominal weekly obligations are added after the native
expense inflation calculation so the displayed contract remains the charged amount.

The new focus art consists of 12 registered native-size medallions with matching
native shine variants. English localisation, event routing, spirits, weekly budget
consumers, native decision panels, country definitions, flags and generator ownership
are integrated. Script files use UTF-8 without BOM; English localisation uses BOM.
The same malformed naval shortcut number found by the engine was also corrected
from `.8` to `0.8` in Iraq, the Soviet Union and UAE. Each is a single numeric
spelling repair matching the existing naval generator, without changing programmes
or geometry.

## Scope and historical boundaries

The historical core is American assistance to Iraqi Kurds through Iran from 1972.
A hostile programme against the Shah or NATO Turkey is an explicit alternative
policy with diplomatic costs. The Syrian programme is also an alternative, not a
claimed historical Kissinger operation. No later PKK, PYD, YPG or SDF organisation
is imported into the 1970 start. The 1991 rising is not represented as an automatic
US creation of a client state before Desert Storm.

The installed simplified system aggregates unrest by host. Two eligible northern
Iraqi states are still required before the Iraqi birth effect. Iranian, Turkish
and Syrian release geography is limited to states 408, 162 and 193 respectively.
These mixed game regions are not an ethnic map. Actual division count is used;
division strength, supply and equipment quality do not weight the regional input.
No additional automatic battlefield-defeat unrest modifier is claimed.
