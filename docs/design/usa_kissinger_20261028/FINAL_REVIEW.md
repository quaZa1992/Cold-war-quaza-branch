local canvas is explicitly schematic; its display pixels are separate from the

corrected by the parent and rechecked in the emitted fragment:

1. Angola and the Iranian dossier initially shared world position51,5; Chile
   and the Turkish dossier shared59,5. The other-theatre continuation now uses

   Shuttle Diplomacy card. Its outside rail now runs through x700, clear of

   Their inspector now explicitly labels them reference-only and discloses that

refresh confirms the following in the final emitted fragment:

- Country-navigation cards now use the same `dragging.moved` guard as focus
  cards, preventing a completed pan from triggering country navigation.
- The Iraqi campaign now requires an active accepted delivery route, rather
  than merely a historical acceptance receipt.

  setup money, political power, equipment and weekly funding. The former generic

  forty-five warning days and fifteen-day reset. This avoids the earlier
  seven-day rounding of the displayed timers.
