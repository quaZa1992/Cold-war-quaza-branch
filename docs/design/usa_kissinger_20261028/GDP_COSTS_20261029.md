# GDP-indexed programme financing

Player-requested revision, 29 October 2026. This supersedes the nominal monetary
amounts in the earlier implementation and regional-control records. It applies
to the Kissinger chapter, four regional campaigns, its diplomatic agreements,
Chile/Angola programmes and the integrated new Gulf armed-support transaction.
It does not convert unrelated countries or other USA programmes.

All new monetary charges are percentages of the payer's current annual
`gdp_total`, which uses MD billions. The native amount is calculated as GDP
multiplied by the published percentage, divided by 100. Literal percentages
have at most five fractional digits. The setup payments and weekly flows use
the real treasury and additional-income/expense providers, including the
existing after-inflation insertion for nominal programme expenses.

in `tools/usa_kissinger_implementation/fiscal.py`. They range from 0.0002% to

these live rates are the later player-authorised financing revision.

Positive transit and material-assistance transfers use US GDP on both sides.
The recipient does not recalculate an American grant against its own GDP.
Moscow's consultation expense is its own 0.00002% of Soviet GDP, separate from
the American consultation expense. Unsupported or suspended cooperation still
removes both spirits and flows according to the existing consent/relations
guards. Recalculation resets the contributions before accumulation.

## Quotes, payments and presentation

Native effect fields show the signed GDP percentage and its currently calculated
monetary equivalent, even in prospective assistance previews. Standing budget
spirits display both their GDP percentage and the calculated weekly amount.
Percentage formatting uses only the decimal places needed by the rate: 4%,
0.004% and 0.00005%. The 4% repression charge and all other prices are unchanged.
Routine quotes refresh from GDP; actual purchases recheck a newly calculated
invoice before granting their assets. Focus setup payments retain their existing
deficit behaviour. This conversion introduces no general prohibition on budget
deficits.

An already funded one-time cash grant retains the amount committed when its
GDP-based invoice was paid. Later GDP growth changes continuing costs and new
purchases; it does not duplicate or revalue money already placed in that grant.
Existing legacy receipts migrate without another setup payment. The positive
legacy-rate variable is a preserved programme selector, not a fixed currency
amount in the new calculation.

The historical programme precedents remain those documented in the earlier
FRUS-backed review. These GDP percentages are game-balance choices requested
by the player, not historical claims about actual covert-action budgets.

## Verification boundary

The current suite contains 55 emitted-AST model tests, including GDP growth,
different payer/recipient GDPs, exact matching transfers, repeated recalculation,
live-price revalidation, escrow, locked preview inertness and a check forbidding
fixed nonzero treasury/weekly programme literals in the installed provider.
The targeted source, complete USA integration and English localisation checks
are recorded separately. The native fixture has been updated for the new rates
but remains unlaunched, following the user's earlier source/model-only choice.
No new engine, native rendering, MP or save/reload result is implied.
