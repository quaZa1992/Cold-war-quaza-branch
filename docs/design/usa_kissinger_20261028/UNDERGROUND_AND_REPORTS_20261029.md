## Historical precedent and alternate paths

The US government approved cash and arms assistance to Barzani's Kurdish
movement in 1972, before an independent Kurdish state existed. Its documented
purpose included keeping the Iraqi government off balance. The source separates
financial support from munitions and describes transit through Iran:
[FRUS, document 321](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76ve04/d321).
The continuing 1973 programme funded and equipped an existing guerrilla force:
[FRUS, document 207](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76v27/d207).
These are precedents for movement support, not evidence for the exact game
percentages, organisation points or a historical US campaign against all four
governments. The Iranian, Turkish and Syrian extensions remain alternate-history
RP choices with existing access and political constraints.

## Specific reports and international delivery

Every successful coercive action records the exact state selected for its real
population deduction. Country-specific reports identify government troops and
security forces as the perpetrators, Kurdish civilians as the victims, the
recorded state by its native name and the exact simulated casualty count.
The selected state is recorded only inside the paid transaction. Hovering a
preview cannot change it. Four country-specific mobilisation warnings also
name the Kurdish regions and the government facing the prospective rising.

The host, USA and existing regional authorities receive their distinct reports
and options. One action-time `every_country` dispatch informs every other
existing country, including all other MP players. Named recipients are excluded
from that dispatch to avoid duplicates. International receipt itself imposes
no war, sanctions or diplomatic response. Failed payments and repeated blocked
actions send no additional reports. There is no new recurring world scan.
