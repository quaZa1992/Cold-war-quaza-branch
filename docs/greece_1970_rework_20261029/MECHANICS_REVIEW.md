## Findings requiring concept updates or explicit implementation contracts

### P1. Give each party programme an active owner

The focus records carry exact party slots and triggers, but the programme spirits are granted as ordinary modifiers and have no party-dependent activation or reconciliation field (`tools/greece_1970_rework_concept/design.py:23,80-129`; generated spirit rewards begin at `concept-draft.json:1799`). A later change of governing party therefore leaves prior `Military Government Programme`, `Conservative Reconstruction`, `Liberal Reconstruction`, `National and Social Programme`, `Left Government Programme`, or `Royal Constitutional Programme` modifiers active. Since each focus remains completed, a player who changes governments can accumulate the alternatives as permanent national bonuses. This can combine substantial PP, stability, research, industry, office income, army and navy modifiers from mutually different governments.

Keep each completed programme inspectable, but make its modifier spirit active only while its exact native party slot governs, then restore it when that party returns. Preserve completed one-time research and construction. The parent can implement this through party-owned modifier enable conditions and existing party-change refresh hooks. A party gate on focus completion alone does not own the resulting spirit.

### P1. Make the Greek staff preparation reversible with the existing withdrawal choice

`staff_network` raises Cyprus officer penetration by 10 and gives a 180-day planning spirit (`design.py:153-154`). The existing shared `gre_withdraw` effect subtracts 20 penetration, removes the Greek network spirit, and marks the network withdrawn (`common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_CYP_R2_crisis.txt:144-163`). If Greece completes both network focuses and then withdraws before launching the coup, the added 10 remains in Cyprus after the existing 20-point reduction. Add a one-time preparation receipt and account for it in withdrawal or crisis cancellation, or make that 10 explicitly temporary with matching expiry cleanup. The current description says withdrawal ends support, so retaining the extra 10 needs a deliberate explanation and effect preview.

The coup math itself is coherent: baseline 45 + the shared Greek network's 20 = 65, which ties the starting defence 40 + preparedness 25 and therefore fails. The new preparation adds 10, making 75 against 65 if Cyprus takes no additional safeguard. A further 25 preparedness raises defence to 90, so preparation does not guarantee success. Sources: `common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_CYP_R2_crisis.txt:98-123,144-163`; `events/COLDWAR_CYP_R2_crisis.txt:32-48`.

### P1. Make EEC recipient count, payments, and prospective membership terms dynamic together

The association and accession records say recipients are a one-time snapshot of `global.EU_member`, but their data still carries the six static tags and a fixed Greek weekly expense of 0.006% GDP (`contracts.py:47-52`). Each recipient's income is 0.001% of Greek GDP. That balances only for six recipients. If the live member set has five members Greece pays 0.006% while partners receive 0.005%; with seven members the transfer promises 0.007% while Greece pays 0.006%. Compute the expense from the exact recipient snapshot, and display that same total in the Greek focus and each recipient event preview.

There is no generic native `EU_join` effect. Native membership is the `EU_member` idea; its `on_add` maintains `global.EU_member`, initializes europeanism and calls `EU_adopt_passed_laws` (`common/ideas/Organizations.txt:4-27`). Use the existing current-member array for one-off recipient fanout and add `EU_member` only after the required approvals. Do not create another membership list. The Six are the 1970 founding members in `COLDWAR_EC_1970_initialize` (`common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_EC_1970_effects.txt:26-50`).

The draft lists the seven base `EU_member` spirit modifiers, but `EU_adopt_passed_laws` also checks `global.EU_passed_votes` and may add ideas, tech sharing, stability, and party-popularity changes (`common/scripted_effects/99_eu_scripted_effects.txt:2076-2220+`). Those live law effects are not represented by `native_membership_values`, and the phrase “Current native Community laws apply” is not a numeric preview. The accession proposer focus and every recipient event need a planned native preview helper that reports the exact laws currently present in `EU_passed_votes`, their effects on the proposed member, the base `EU_member` modifiers, setup charge, recurring charge, and research reward. Static six-recipient prose will not cover future membership changes.

### P1. Clear the false former-member state after the scenario correction

The history source adds `EU_member` to Greece inside its 1970.1.1 block (`history/countries/GRE - Greece.txt:1-2,315-350`). The concept's historical correction should remove that erroneous membership so Greece begins with restricted association. Native removal is appropriate for clearing the idea and its member-array entry, but `EU_member.on_remove` calls `leaving_EU`; that helper marks a country `EU_former_member` and applies `EU_rejoin_cooldown` for 1,095 days (`common/ideas/Organizations.txt:17-22`; `common/scripted_effects/99_eu_scripted_effects.txt:342-353`). Greece was not a former EEC/EU member in January 1970. After native cleanup, clear these artificial former-member/cooldown flags, or explicitly make a three-year cooldown part of the proposal. Also exclude GRE itself from every accession or association recipient snapshot.

### P2. Make conditions transactional, not only visible prose

The generated focus data carries meaningful human requirements, but the condition text is not itself an execution trigger. `staff_network` must still verify Cyprus exists, Greece's network/footprint is active, the coup has not launched, and the penetration receipt has not already been granted when applying the +10. `operational_initiative` must verify actual coup success, the coup government still controls Cyprus, Greece and Cyprus remain at peace, and that the reward has not already been issued when it applies the spirit, doctrine reduction and expense stream (`design.py:153-155`). Recheck those conditions in the apply helper at focus completion, since a war or cancelled crisis can begin after focus selection.

The leader choices require new recipient/country events `COLDWAR_GRE_R2_politics.10`, `.20`, and `.30` (`design.py:82,93,129`). They are not defined in the current runtime. Implement and validate the actual leader snapshots, ruling-party changes, and party-programme refresh before treating those choices as executable. The 12 contracts similarly require actual accept/refuse country events and pending, retry, and lifecycle cleanup; `contracts.py` is only design data.

### P2. Define relationship eligibility across the whole four-party settlement

The four-party Cyprus settlement names CYP, TUR and ENG as recipients and requires all four governments to agree (`contracts.py:71-75`), but the common validity string is bilateral: both sides must be at peace and free of embargoes and mirrored sanctions (`contracts.py:77-80`). Define the relationship matrix explicitly for the four parties. If the settlement is intended to remain a four-power compact, reconcile all six unordered pairs, and suspend/reinstate each country's paired spirit and associated payment consistently. The existing generic two-country rule alone does not specify what happens when, for example, TUR and ENG impose sanctions while each remains clear toward GRE.

## Quantitative checks for the coup opening and ongoing costs

- `staff_network` costs 0.75% of Greek GDP once, adds 10 Cyprus penetration, and grants +20% planning speed with -5% office income tax for 180 days.
- `operational_initiative` grants +25% planning speed, +10% maximum planning, +15% naval invasion preparation, -10% office income tax, -10% foreign-influence defence, and one 50% land-doctrine cost reduction. Its 0.003% Greek GDP weekly payment for 180 days totals about 0.078% GDP at roughly 26 weekly charges, subject to the actual week-tick boundary. The data also says it ends while the coup government no longer controls Cyprus.
- Those two timed spirits overlap for about 117 days in the uninterrupted shortest sequence: 14 days for the Greek coup focus, 14 days for the coup warning, then 35 days for the post-coup focus. Together they provide +45% planning speed and -15% office income tax during that overlap. The shared Joint Staff focus (+15% planning speed) and the separate Aegean deterrence focus (+20%) can also coexist, bringing this designed peak to +80% planning speed before any other modifiers. This is a large but short payoff, paid for by the 0.75% setup charge, the original 100 PP coup action and 0.5% Cyprus-GDP coup expense, followed by the 180-day expense. Retain the explicit exposure and cancellation guards.
- The reusable defence/foreign permanent-programme streams can reach about 0.021% of Greek GDP per week, or 1.09% per year, in a plausible Ioannidis and professional-cadres route: Army School 0.002, Professional Cadres 0.003, Aegean Command 0.002, Sea Control 0.004, Close Support 0.003, External Room 0.001, Aegean Deterrence 0.003, and Security Budget 0.003. This is before standing agreements; the association/accession is 0.006% weekly at six members, France 0.002%, Germany 0.002%, Britain 0.001%, Cyprus trade 0.001%, and the active volunteer mission 0.002% while its 180-day idea is active. These charges are not automatically excessive, but the programme ownership and actual suspension conditions must be explicit in the runtime stream receipts and player previews.
