Focus quality: implementation

20 October 2026 · implemented; source checks passed

The 16 approved findings from the focus-quality audit, not certification of every focus or modifier in the mod.

SDE-01Soviet financial units
Interest rewards now use percentage points: banks -0.25 pp; Open Gates -0.10 pp. Runtime, tooltip and design preview agree.
SDE-02DDR internal factions
Military-industry effects target the initialized industrial_conglomerates faction. Procurement faction changes occur only after actual settlement.
SDE-03DDR debt repayment
Pay and retire the smaller of 3% GDP or remaining debt. No new borrowing or unfunded reward. A funded, once-only settlement grants -0.25 pp interest for 730 days, +8% sanction resistance and the stated political effects.
SDE-04Egyptian programme gates
46 repeated debate/accounting connectors merged; six distinct shared historical or constitutional entries retained. Original reward guards and prerequisite alternatives preserved. Party roots remain at their original row.
SDE-05DDR political narratives
Seven priority political, financial and procurement descriptions now explain their programmes in English; canonical design, page and live text match.
SDE-06DDR Soviet procurement
The import route requests 90 real Soviet stock tanks for 1% frozen DDR GDP. Assembly requires an actual Soviet licence, costs 1.6% GDP, and grants two military factories plus 3% military factory productivity only on settlement. Soviet consent, stock, designs, cash and capacity are rechecked. No future tank technology is unlocked.
GIIL-01Libyan capstone
The 70-day capstone always calls native healthcare advancement, including its 150 PP maximum-tier fallback. It also grants +3% stability and -5% healthcare costs for 730 days.
GIIL-02Iraqi prisoner review
Only living unresolved cases inside their rescue windows remain actionable. One 30 PP session and a frozen 0.5% GDP invoice; invalidated sessions refund PP once without charging cash or applying the negotiation penalty.
GIIL-03Libyan descriptions
67 descriptions rewritten as historical programmes instead of raw effect catalogues or implementation metadata.
GIIL-04Israeli descriptions
20 party finales and 80 follow-through entries receive individual English narratives. Existing rewards and disabled-path safeguards retained.
GCVU-01Greek, Cypriot and US descriptions
821 curated English descriptions: 313 Greece, 295 Cyprus and 213 USA. The 16 existing US Gulf additions are preserved.
GCVU-02Greek military connectors
The air opening grants 25 Air XP instead of repeated PP. Its radar follow-up costs 0.25% GDP and builds only with funding and capacity. The empty navy connector is bypassed and its successor requirements rewired.
GCVU-03Cypriot incumbent entry
Makarios administrative programme is directly reachable: 35 days, +3% stability factor and -5% bureaucracy costs for 0.25% GDP, once and only for the correct governing party.
GCVU-04Focus requirements UI
Internal routing, scenario and redundant receipt checks hidden while real guards remain. Meaningful party and membership requirements stay visible; Nixon presidency and the 20 January 1973 deadline are named.
GEN-01Generic two-office projects
Eleven projects deliver two office/power-plant phases, including in a single-state country. Each completed phase is charged once; unavailable phases stay pending. Existing net prices are preserved and the separate infrastructure invoice remains separate. No periodic global callback added.
GEN-02Generic military narratives
64 individual descriptions: 58 army, navy and air entries plus six R&D precursors. Existing military rewards and the 70-day Air Cavalry research programme retained.

Practical limits

  • No HOI4 engine, live tooltip, save/reload or multiplayer execution performed. Native stock transfer, production licence and construction calls have explicit modeled boundaries in AST tests.
  • Full changes should be tested on a new 1970 start. Previously completed focus rewards and removed Egyptian connectors are not retroactively migrated or regranted in old saves.
  • Retired Greek and Cypriot connector IDs remain hidden bypassed compatibility markers, not a guarantee of general old-save compatibility.
  • Original USA generator named in its historical header is absent; the maintained overlay is documented in the live header.
  • Deferred general event-art work and unrelated simultaneous changes were left outside this pass.
