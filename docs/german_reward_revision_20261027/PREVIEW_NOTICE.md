The six PNG diagrams in this directory are generated review documents. They reuse the existing registered focus sprites from this checkout and the installed Millennium Dawn mod, plus the installed Hearts of Iron IV focus title bar and `hoi_16mbs` bitmap font. They are not game screenshots or new runtime artwork.

Source locations: this checkout's `interface/*.gfx` and referenced textures; `E:/SteamLibrary/steamapps/workshop/content/394360/2777392649`; `E:/SteamLibrary/steamapps/common/Hearts of Iron IV/gfx`.

Modifications: existing textures are decoded, resized and composited into diagrams using the current emitted focus coordinates, English titles and approximate connectors. Original binary textures are unchanged. `PREVIEWS.json` records source hashes, focus IDs, bounds, scale and rendering limitations. `tools/german_reward_revision_20261027/render_preview.py` reproduces the diagrams.
