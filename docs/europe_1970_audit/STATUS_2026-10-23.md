# IMPERIA COLD WAR: European Communities interface and active-code audit

Reviewed on 23 October 2026. This is a static inspection of the current local workspace, not a live-game or multiplayer test. The restoration reports dated 18 October describe an earlier rollback. They do not describe the current files: the `COLDWAR_EC_1970` integration is present and called by the native startup effect again. The review does not establish when or by whom it was restored.

## Interface changes in this request

- The topbar title and description use the existing `COLDWAR_EC_GetOrgName` resolver instead of `EUU.GetName`. The existing Community-mode flag selects **European Communities**; the existing fallback is **European Union**. This request does not add a treaty transition or migrate saves missing that flag.
- The fiscal tab is **Budget**, the information tab is **Members**, and the withdrawal button is **Withdraw**. The common budget is not presented as an already existing central bank. The withdrawal tooltip no longer attributes the 1970 procedure to the later Article 50.
- Membership, office descriptions, Council headings, integration campaigns and numeric budget wording use the same organisation name or neutral institutional labels. The Commission President is no longer incorrectly called President of the European Union. The parliamentary description distinguishes national parliamentary delegations from direct elections through the existing flags.
- The native title uses `x = 150` and `maxWidth = 360`. Its centre remains at 330 within the 659-pixel window. The native English `hoi_36header` glyph advances measure **European Communities** at 320 pixels, exceeding the previous 295-pixel field. Adjacent help and close buttons and the five navigation controls were inspected. Navigation labels remain shorter than the original controls; the two office labels fit their 270-pixel fields.
- English-only replacements are isolated in `localisation/english/replace/COLDWAR_EC_UI_l_english.yml`. No reward, cost, membership, voting, office or budget execution code is changed. Native numeric budget previews and click bindings are retained.

## Remaining gaps found during inspection

1. **The historical agenda package is missing.** The initializer comments promise a later package but add no entries to `global.EU_potential_votes`. Setting `EU_initialized` skips the modern agenda setup. A clean 1970 start therefore does not receive the ordinary initial reform catalogue through this path. Existing saves can have different arrays.
2. **The historical transition flags have readers but no writers in active scripts.** No active setter was found for `COLDWAR_EC_direct_elections`, `COLDWAR_EC_monetary_system` or `COLDWAR_EC_economic_and_monetary_union`, and no clearer was found for `COLDWAR_EC_1970_mode`. The promised direct-election, monetary and organisation-name transitions are not a complete reachable progression.
3. **Council rules remain modern MD rules.** `EU_check_majority` requires more than 65% of population and more than 55% of members. The historical 17 weighted Council votes have not been implemented. The UI continues to state the actual runtime rule.
4. **Office and withdrawal procedures largely remain native.** The permanent Council presidency still requires Lisbon; there is no new six-month rotating 1970 presidency. The withdrawal action still runs the native exit mission, office cleanup and news event. A text rename does not create a different treaty procedure.
5. **Group abbreviations and later institutions remain partly modern.** The five historical long names do not replace every short party label. Later central-bank, foreign-policy, Frontex and defence offices retain their native names and availability requirements. They were not relabelled as institutions already operating in 1970.
6. **Older documentation is stale.** `native_restore.md`, `docs/main_md_eu_restore/README.md` and `tools/europe_1970/README.md` remain historical rollback records. Their claims that all adaptations are absent are contradicted by the currently active integration. They were preserved as historical records.

These findings are recorded for follow-up. This request changes presentation only and does not restore the retired CWEC system or introduce new national policy content.

## Historical naming references

- [European Parliament: Merger Treaty](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/about-parliament/en/in-the-past/the-parliament-and-the-treaties/merger-treaty): the three Communities share a Commission and Council from 1967 and are collectively known as the European Communities. This supports the umbrella name rather than narrowing the interface to the EEC alone.
- [European Union: Founding agreements](https://european-union.europa.eu/principles-countries-history/principles-and-values/founding-agreements_en): the Maastricht Treaty established the European Union and entered into force on 1 November 1993.
- [European Parliament: Historical background](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/factsheets/en/sheet/11/parlamentul-european-istoric): the name European Parliament dates to 1962; members were appointed from national parliaments before the first direct elections in June 1979. Keeping **Parliament** is appropriate for 1970.

## Provenance and verification boundary

The underlying interface, localisation, office descriptions, numeric previews and native fonts originate from Millennium Dawn and Hearts of Iron IV. The native GUI credits AngirestBird and Doolittle; those credits are retained. Individual authors of the inherited localisation and fonts, their applicable standalone licences and separate redistribution permissions are unknown in this record. New wording and bounded layout changes were prepared for this user request. No images, flags or font assets were modified or copied into the mod.

The original Workshop reference was accessed read-only. All 21 files checked by `python tools/europe_1970/check_original_md_reference.py` match the previously reviewed original-source baseline. This verifies the reference installation, not equality of the adapted mod with that installation.

Validation covers localisation syntax and unique replacement keys, dynamic-localisation references, retained native click bindings and numeric budget-preview tokens, UTF-8 BOM rules, structural brace balance, exact preservation of execution files and native-font label widths. Rendering and behaviour in a running game remain unverified.
