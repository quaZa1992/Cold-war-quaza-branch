# Air Command: numerical concept catalogue

Design only, 29 October 2026. All proposed numbers are balance abstractions, not measured historical effects.
Read [the main brief](air_doctrine_concepts_20261029.md) for scope, historical rationale, native limits and required corrections.

Grand doctrines cost 100 Air XP; subdoctrines cost 75 Air XP. Each new sub has five 100-mastery rewards. Grand milestones follow the six listed track slots.
Activation values below remain active during mastery. Final values are cumulative contributions from that choice alone. Country, equipment and unit layers remain separate.
All displayed numbers are percentage modifiers. Percentage changes to accident or XP-loss modifiers are not percentage-point changes in actual losses.
Air force personnel cost uses the current MD weekly budget channel. Production IC cost changes aircraft manufacturing cost, not the treasury.

# Source register

- **SWE**: [Swedish Armed Forces, wartime air-base history](https://www.forsvarsmakten.se/siteassets/6-information-och-fakta/historia/vagbaserna/fortv-2007-1.pdf). Bas 60 and Bas 90, dispersion, alternate runways, ground units and protected command arrangements.
- **SWE_DESIGN**: [Saab, Designed For Easy Support](https://www.saab.com/markets/india/gripen-for-india/technology/designed-for-easy-support). Manufacturer account: 1982 Gripen requirements included road operation and simple dispersed servicing. Later turnaround claims are not backdated to 1970.
- **FIN**: [General Heikki Nikunen, Finnish air-war account](https://www.mannerheim.fi/13_erity/Nikumar.htm). Small fighter force prioritised bombers and avoided unnecessary fighter engagements; forward operating sites supported concentration.
- **VIE**: [National Defense University Press, Adapting to Disruption](https://ndupress.ndu.edu/Media/News/News-Article-View/Article/2076617/adapting-to-disruption-aerial-combat-over-north-vietnam/). Vietnamese ground-controlled, short-pass MiG tactics and adaptations during 1965-1968.
- **OCA**: [US Air Force history, Ideas, Concepts, Doctrine](https://www.dafhistory.af.mil/Portals/16/documents/Studies/101-150/AFD-090529-053.pdf). 1967 Israeli low-level attacks against airfields and the subsequent adaptation of opposing shelters and air defences.
- **ARG**: [Royal Air Force Museum, 1982 timeline](https://www.rafmuseum.org.uk/research/research-enquiries/history-of-aviation-timeline/british-military-aviation/1982-2/). Argentine maritime air attack, Exocet use, British airfield attacks and distant tanker/patrol support.
- **ARG_LOW**: [Military Review, A Sad and Bloody Business](https://www.armyupress.army.mil/Portals/7/military-review/Archives/English/MJ-23/Sad-and-Bloody-Business/Sad-and-Bloody-Business-UA.pdf). Argentine wave-top attacks with conventional bombs as well as missiles; successes and limitations.
- **NOR**: [Norwegian Armed Forces Ombudsman, report to Parliament](https://forsvarsombudet.no/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/2009-Dokument-5-Ombudsmannsnemnda-for-Forsvarets-innberetning.pdf). 333 Squadron P-3 introduction in 1969, maritime surveillance and demanding maintenance. Retrospective institutional source.
- **POR**: [Portuguese Air Force, Base Aerea No. 5 history](https://emfa.pt/unidade-18-base-aerea-n-5). Combat deployments to Guinea and G.91 pilot preparation for African theatres in 1966-1973.
- **POR_CONTEXT**: [Portuguese Defence Ministry, African-war force and logistics study](https://www.defesa.gov.pt/pt/defesa/organizacao/comissoes/cphm/rphm/edicoes/ano1/n12021/recrutmob). Aircraft readiness difficulties, light aircraft under fire and introduction of additional aircraft in 1965-1966.
- **RED**: [Nellis Air Force Base, 414th Combat Training Squadron](https://www.nellis.af.mil/About/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/2605882/414th-combat-training-squadron-red-flag/). Red Flag established in 1975 to supply realistic early combat experience before actual war.
- **FAC**: [National Museum of the USAF, Close Air Support in Korea](https://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Visit/Museum-Exhibits/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/196086/close-air-support-battering-from-above/). Airborne FAC and TACP coordination across USAF, Navy, Marines and ground forces in Korea.
- **LIFT**: [US Naval History and Heritage Command, Berlin Airlift](https://www.history.navy.mil/content/history/nhhc/research/histories/naval-aviation-history/involvement-by-conflict/cold-war/naval-aviation-s-involvement-in-the-berlin-airlift/us-navys-participation-in-the-berlin-airlift.html). Airlift aircraft, scheduling, worldwide resource diversion and the 1948-1949 expansion.
- **LOW**: [Royal Air Force Museum, 1991 timeline](https://www.rafmuseum.org.uk/research/research-enquiries/history-of-aviation-timeline/british-military-aviation/1991-2/). Low-level airfield attacks, aircraft losses, transition to medium-level attacks and Buccaneer laser-designation support.
- **SECTION**: [US Naval History and Heritage Command, Thach biography](https://www.history.navy.mil/content/history/nhhc/our-collections/photography/us-people/t/thach-john-s.html). Mutual-support fighter tactics developed in the early 1940s and used at Midway in 1942.
- **RPV**: [US Army Combat Studies Institute, Eyes of the Army](https://www.armyupress.army.mil/Portals/7/combat-studies-institute/csi-books/OP37.pdf). Ryan reconnaissance RPVs, experimental armed work, Israeli development and the distinction from later operational UCAVs.
- **RPV_DECOY**: [US Air University Review, September-October 1984](https://www.airuniversity.af.edu/Portals/10/ASPJ/journals/1984_Vol35_No1-6/1984_Vol35_No6.pdf). Contemporary professional account of Israeli real-time reconnaissance and RPV decoys in 1982.
