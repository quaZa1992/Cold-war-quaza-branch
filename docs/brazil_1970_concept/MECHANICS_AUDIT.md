## Money units and cost contract

MD `gdp_total` is an annual national GDP measure in billions of internal dollars. The English economy UI at `localisation/english/MD_country_economy_view_l_english.yml:26-28` labels it in billions. `gdp_per_capita` is displayed in thousands of dollars in the same UI. GDP is recomputed from sector output by `calculate_gdp` in native `common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt` around lines 4782-4830 and 5727 onward. Do not use historical-display GDP variables as the treasury input.

For Brazil focus or decision setup charges, compute from Brazil's current GDP at execution using the native treasury path: set `treasury_change` to `gdp_total` multiplied by the negative share, then call `modify_treasury_effect = yes` once. The installed source demonstrates this GDP-relative payment in France, UK, and Iraq native focuses; the project precedent and exact conventions are documented in `tools/france_1970_design/GDP_COST_REFERENCE.md`. For bilateral acceptance, each payer's setup charge uses that payer's GDP in that payer's scope. Show prospective current-GDP amounts before acceptance without executing a payment.

Recurring annual appropriations use current GDP at each weekly finance refresh: `own gdp_total * annual_share / 52`. This prices the active stream from the current economy, not a frozen contract amount. Keep every scripted budget literal to five or fewer fractional digits. Use the existing project hook `COLDWAR_FRA2_add_weekly_expenses` as the structural reference: the native weekly money calculation calls one country expense aggregator in `common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt:4661-4664`; its France implementation is `common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_FRA2_diplomacy_effects.txt:7187+`, which zeros and rebuilds one `COLDWAR_FRA2_cooperation_weekly_expenses` total from eligible signed streams. The documented integration is `tools/france_1970_runtime/integration.py`. Reuse that aggregator shape for Brazil rather than duplicating a native expense-system insertion. Charge a stream once in the active expense aggregation. The main native expense block multiplies earlier `additional_expenses_rate` by inflation at `00_money_system.txt:4064-4066`; later hooks follow that pass. Check the chosen Brazil hook position before applying any second inflation multiplier. `treasury_rate` is displayed income minus displayed expense around `00_money_system.txt:4321`; debt service `debt_rate` is an expense and the native pause rules alter when it enters the displayed budget.

For an active bilateral relationship, current Cuba agreement triggers provide the checked lifecycle pattern in `common/scripted_triggers/COLDWAR_CUB_1970_bilateral.txt`: both countries must exist, remain at peace, impose no native embargo on each other, and have none of the four Imperia sanction classes mirrored in either direction. The eight directional arrays are `imp_dip_sanc_on_us`, `imp_dip_sanc_by_us`, `imp_fin_sanc_on_us`, `imp_fin_sanc_by_us`, `imp_eco_sanc_on_us`, `imp_eco_sanc_by_us`, `imp_mil_sanc_on_us`, `imp_mil_sanc_by_us`. `COLDWAR_CUB_reconcile_bilateral` in `common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_CUB_1970_bilateral.txt` suspends or restores both spirits without setup fees or regranting one-time assets. Recipient consent flows through Cuba's focus offer, recipient country event acceptance/refusal, then bilateral signed flags. The historical 1970 regional framework is ALALC, created by the 1960 Treaty of Montevideo and signed by Brazil, Paraguay, and Uruguay. ALADI replaced ALALC in 1980. The installed MD source also includes a live Mercosur system: `common/scripted_effects/05_MERCOSUR_effects.txt`, `common/national_focus/06_MERCOSUR_shared.txt`, and `common/decisions/mercosur_decisions.txt`. Its joint-focus branch requires `mercosur_member_state_modifier`; accession decisions and country events control membership. Brazil's native history also seeds the Mercosur member modifier, so do not copy that generic/native setup into a 1970 Brazil concept as a historical fact. Use ALALC-era trade coordination for the 1970 route, and reserve ALADI for a 1980 transition. If proposing Mercosur in a later alternate history, require its real establishment, explicit invitation/acceptance, and active membership spirit before member-only rewards. [ALADI, 1960 and 1980 Treaties](https://www.aladi.org/preguntas-frecuentes/); [Brazil Siscomex, ALALC and ALADI](https://www.gov.br/siscomex/pt-br/informacoes/acordos-comerciais/aladi).

## Oil, fuel, and uranium

Oil export income depends on exported oil quantity and the money modifiers above. `fuel_gain_factor` is a separate vanilla fuel economy effect. Do not state that more oil exports automatically produce fuel or that an export bonus adds oil reserves.

MD's Imperia uranium implementation defines uranium as a strategic resource (`common/resources/00_resources.txt`) and provides paid uranium mine construction through the investment triggers/decisions (`common/scripted_triggers/00_investment_scripted_triggers.txt:650+`, `common/decisions/00_investment_decisions.txt` and related helpers). Surveying a deposit is not production. Brazil uranium concepts should not grant resource history production or free uranium mines; raw starts remain zero until a paid development project builds a mine. Treat any prospective uranium effect as unsupported until checked against the actual development trigger, capacity, cost, and production consumer.

## Review risks for Brazil concepts

- Reject invented modifier aliases or explainable-sounding text rewards without a source and consumer. In particular, `literacy_rate_education_modifier` is supported, but a generic `education_gain_modifier` is not the education effect API.
- Avoid permanent `total_workforce_modifier` penalties. Represent specialist scarcity using research/training mechanics and align the remediation and preview.
- Avoid stacking large general research or industrial output bonuses over Brazil's existing ideas, industrial capacity, native focus rewards, or productivity systems without aggregation review.
- Agricultural output, education, and administration are distinct systems with their own costs and workforce uses; do not represent them as interchangeable productivity percentages.
- Treaty preview must show both countries' exact prospective costs, spirit names, signed modifiers, buildings and slots, and other finite rewards even while locked. The display must not charge or grant anything.
- This static review does not validate a final Brazil catalogue, tree reachability, presentation, runtime registration, parser load, or balance. Recheck actual generated focus effects and previews when the JSON catalogue exists.
