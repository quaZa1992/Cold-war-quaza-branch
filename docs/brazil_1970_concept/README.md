IMPERIA COLD WAR

# Brazil: Power, Development and Democracy

**Revision 1.1, 2 November 2026. Unapproved concept. Nothing is installed in the loaded mod.**

The concept contains **224 focuses, 13 political programmes, five shared policy sections, 45 mutually exclusive pairs, eight starting national spirits, eight events, 14 recipient-approved bilateral offers and two domestic decisions**. All names, descriptions, requirements and prospective effect rows are English. IDs begin `COLDWAR_BRA_`.

Open `PREVIEW.html` for the complete local interactive concept. Every focus, including locked party paths, can be selected. The inspector shows resolved coordinates, grouped prerequisites, exclusions, programme effects, decision or event options, and both countries' complete prospective agreement effects. The GDP inputs are illustrative internal MD-dollar values, not observed January 1970 GDP readings.

`PREVIEW_OVERVIEW.png` shows the complete spatial composition. `PREVIEW_POLITICAL_NEIGHBOURS.png` shows the initial military government with its adjacent civilian programmes. The individual `PREVIEW_*.png` branch views use native decoded artwork, 165 x 128 focus cards, a 96 x 130 coordinate stride, and English titles checked against a 147-pixel field at Arial 15. These are external reconstructions, not HOI4 screenshots. Arial, icon placement and connector routing remain approximations to native game presentation.

## Shared development

The five lower sections cover society and institutions, industrial and agricultural development, energy and minerals, the armed services, and Itamaraty.

The economy includes BNDES appraisal, the industrial triangle, Embrapa-style tropical agronomy, competing Cerrado and smallholder models, tooling, Manaus or Northeast industrial development, Embraer, telecommunications and export administration. The energy section covers Petrobras, conservation, fuel storage, Proalcool or conventional refining, domestic hydropower, civilian nuclear knowledge and construction, mineral surveys and Carajas logistics.

Uranium survey and feasibility work produce knowledge only. They do not create raw uranium, free mines or automatic history production. Extraction remains paid development through the actual native investment system.

All 45 building effect records grant one additional shared slot per level in the same state before construction, including infrastructure and the other types that do not normally consume shared capacity. Each has an explicit country scope, state, ownership/control checks, native capacity check and one-time transaction contract. Itaipu capacity and the domestic reactor start construction rather than delivering a finished dam or reactor immediately.

## GDP funding and bilateral agreements

One-time programme prices generally range from 0.12% to 1.5% of the payer's current internal `gdp_total`. Existing native borrowing remains available. There is no new treasury-balance gate. These shares are balance values rather than claimed historical appropriations.

Standing expenditure uses each payer's current annual GDP share divided by 52 and enters the existing weekly MD expense calculation once. Every literal budget share remains within five fractional digits. Subscriptions reprice with current GDP; setup fees occur only at actual completion or valid bilateral acceptance.

The 14 offers cover US training or industrial licences; China trade; Soviet machinery; Itaipu with Paraguay; Japanese Cerrado cooperation or a steel consortium; Angolan technical cooperation; Argentine river consultation, civilian nuclear confidence and industry; German civilian nuclear cooperation; Uruguay port administration; and French Mirage training and technical cooperation. The European offers do not require a Japanese agreement, and Argentine industry does not require a nuclear deal.

The named RP recipient receives separate Accept and Decline decisions. Every locked or unsigned focus still displays both prospective spirits, every signed modifier, both setup charges, both weekly budgets, all assets and slots, and finite research or experience rewards. Completion creates an offer and grants no agreement payout.

Signed agreements have no expiry. Both countries must exist, be at peace with each other, and impose no native embargo or any of the four mirrored Imperia sanction classes in either direction. Invalid conditions suspend both spirits and their weekly streams. Restoration never repeats setup charges or finite assets. Dependent contracts require their actual active parent spirits.

Itaipu has separate own-GDP appropriations and an additional paired financial-support stream: Brazil pays 0.08% of its current annual GDP / 52; Paraguay receives the identical quote. This is an explicitly proposed RP financing term. It is **not** Article XV's historical royalty formula, a physical electricity transfer, or a native electricity tariff.

The regional secretariat is a national administrative facility, not automatic Mercosur membership. ALALC is the period regional framework and ALADI is later. Any future organisation-specific extension must use actual membership spirits and recipient consent.

## Event content

The Officer Council, Constitutional Settlement and Party Reorganisation are tied to substantive national or political focuses. The Labourist Naming Dispute distinguishes the later PTB and PDT. Imported Oil Bill, External Credit Squeeze, Prices/Wages/Credit and Military Aid/Human Rights respond to genuine later pressures or agreements. They do not install a 1973 shock, 1982 default or 1987 moratorium at the January 1970 start.

The inspector shows every option's numeric effects. Crisis policies replace earlier responses and last 365 days. Institutional settlements persist, while the military cabinet programme follows the governing military identity. Native-framed event photographs are reserved for the implementation phase; no event picture or GUI was installed.
