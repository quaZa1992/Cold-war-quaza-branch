# Brazil 1970 leader portrait audit

The audit searched native HOI4 Brazil leader graphics, the installed La Résistance and Trial of Allegiance Brazil portrait folders, the Millennium Dawn workshop leader folders, and this mod's `gfx/leaders/BRA` assets. Exact-person portraits available locally were found for two of the requested names:

- Emilio Garrastazu Medici: existing mod portrait at `gfx/leaders/BRA/BRA_Coldwar_Emilio_Garrastazu_Medici.dds`.
- Luis Carlos Prestes: exact Trial of Allegiance asset at `dlc/dlc040_trial_of_allegiance/gfx/leaders/BRA/portrait_BRA_luis_carlos_prestes.dds`, registered as `GFX_portrait_BRA_luis_carlos_prestes`. This image remains a DLC dependency and was not copied.

No exact local portrait was found for Ernesto Geisel, Sandra Cavalcanti, José Sarney, Paulo Maluf, Ulysses Guimarães, Tancredo Neves, João Goulart, Ivete Vargas, Leonel Brizola, João Amazonas, or Fernando Collor. The JSON record contains person-specific primary archive leads and rights notes for these unresolved portraits.

Several native DLC names are false matches and must not be substituted: Lindolfo Collor is not Fernando Collor; Protógenes Guimarães is not Ulysses Guimarães; Olga Benário Prestes is not Luis Carlos Prestes; and Chilean Tancredo Pinochet is not Tancredo Neves. The mod's Roberto de Guimarães Carvalho is not Ulysses Guimarães either.

The Câmara dos Deputados states its 1960-2002 historical photo archive may be reused under CC BY with credit. It also provides official image records for people including [Ulysses Guimarães](https://www2.camara.leg.br/a-camara/visiteacamara/cultura-na-camara/imagens/exposicoes-historicas-e-artisticas-2016/ulysses-guimaraes/view), [Tancredo Neves](https://www.camara.leg.br/historia-e-arquivo/), [Paulo Maluf](https://www.camara.leg.br/deputados/132056/biografia), [João Amazonas](https://www.camara.leg.br/deputados/4866/biografia), and [Fernando Collor](https://arquivohistorico.camara.leg.br/index.php/mello-fernando-collor-de). However, not every official profile or exhibition photo carries a verified reusable licence. The Câmara's specific [photo archive terms](https://www2.camara.leg.br/a-camara/documentos-e-pesquisa/arquivo/acervo-por-tipo/fotos) are recorded in the portrait mapping.

The [Museu da República's José Sarney collection](https://atom-museurepublica.museus.gov.br/downloads/jose-sarney.pdf) and [João Goulart collection](https://atom-museurepublica.museus.gov.br/downloads/joao-goulart-5.pdf), FGV CPDOC's [Ernesto Geisel and João Goulart archive index](https://cpdoc.fgv.br/acervo/arquivos-pessoais/base-dados-accessus), and [Brizola biography](https://cpdoc.fgv.br/biografias/leonel-brizola) are useful provenance leads. Their image-specific reuse permissions were not established here. The Biblioteca Nacional's Hemeroteca also holds period press photographs of Ivete Vargas, but publication and photographer rights remain unverified.

No external photograph was downloaded, modified, or added. The audit does not use a proxy face or a generated portrait. Original author, credit, or licence for the existing Medici portrait is unknown; the Prestes asset remains under the game's DLC terms. See [leader_portraits.json](../../tools/brazil_1970_runtime/leader_portraits.json) for file paths, sprite names, and person-level notes.
