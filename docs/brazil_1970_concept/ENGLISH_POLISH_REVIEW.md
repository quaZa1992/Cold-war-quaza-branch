# IMPERIA COLD WAR — Brazil 1970 English copy review

## Scope

Prepared `tools/brazil_1970_runtime/english_polish.json` as a strict JSON override package for the two emitted English localisation files. It contains 192 replacements, all checked against existing localisation keys. It does not alter the loaded localisation files, runtime code, focus design, numerical effects, availability, focus titles, or event choices. One party registration decision label is clarified as “Register Ivete Vargas’s PTB.” The root compiler can apply this package after canonical localisation generation.

## Changes

- Rewrote all eight domestic event descriptions to remove internal scenario checks, party-registration implementation language, and event replacement instructions. Oil text responds to surging world prices without hard-coding 1973; the US military dispute refers to human-rights conditions without assuming who governs the United States. The text now describes the cabinet's political or economic decision.
- Replaced the identical standing-agreement notice in all fourteen proposal events with a recipient-facing invitation specific to the proposal, then changed the review button to “Review the terms.” The fourteen agreement notice descriptions plus fourteen review labels account for 28 keys.
- Revised fourteen treaty and cooperation descriptions, including their partner-specific aliases, to keep historical precedents while removing “RP agreement,” “free,” and other implementation language. The wording keeps the important boundaries: Itaipu terms remain distinct from the historical royalty formula and do not guarantee electricity deliveries; river consultations are explicitly framed as Argentine-Brazilian bilateral diplomacy; and historical precedents provide context without presenting the game proposals as the original treaties.
- Added specific policy descriptions for all fifteen domestic event-option spirits, describing the oil and debt trade-offs in terms of inflation-related fiscal costs rather than promising changes to price or inflation rates. Rewrote both domestic decision descriptions for the service review and uranium survey. Clarified twelve congress nomination descriptions, including party-specific wording, and twelve party-identity spirit descriptions. The Lacerdist description covers Lacerda’s living opposition movement at the start and its possible posthumous continuation. Present-tense PMDB, PSDB, PFL and PTB identity text preserves institutional lineage without requiring its historical year of formation, allowing the approved early-reform alternative. The PRN’s 1989 date remains because its runtime formation is gated to that historical period. In particular, the copy distinguishes ARENA/PDS from the military command, MDB/PMDB, the historic pre-1965 PTB from Ivete Vargas’s later PTB, PDT, PT, and the 1989 PRN.
- Cleaned selected domestic programme copy where it described mechanics rather than policy, including oil exposure, energy planning, Embrapa, Embraer, Carajás logistics, the Rapid Competition programme, and the CNBB/public-service choices.

## Historical basis

The copy follows the dates and distinctions in [HISTORICAL_RESEARCH.md](HISTORICAL_RESEARCH.md), including the January 1970 ARENA-MDB system, the 1973 oil shock, the 1982 debt crisis, the 1979 party reorganisation, and the dated diplomatic precedents. Primary institutional sources in that research include the [Banco Central do Brasil](https://www.bcb.gov.br/htms/infecon/dividarevisada/prefacio_introducao.pdf), [TSE](https://www.tse.jus.br/institucional/catalogo-de-publicacoes/arquivos/portfolio-da-exposicao-o-voto-no-brasil/%40%40download/file/Expo-Voto-Brasil-Portifolio2.pdf), [Itamaraty](https://concordia.itamaraty.gov.br/ApiConcordia/Acordo/integra-acordo-html/3848), [Embrapa](https://www.embrapa.br/pt/visao-de-futuro/trajetoria-do-agro), and [CNBB](https://www.cnbb.org.br/ao-celebrar-30-anos-a-semana-social-brasileira-reafirma-sua-luta-pelos-direitos-humanos/).
