Politburo ImageGen prompt and source

Tool: built-in image_gen. Source: the user-supplied voting photograph. Native frame: main MD political_deal.dds, outer frame pixels only. Colour restoration was generated; resizing and native frame packaging were exported with FFmpeg.

Use case: identity-preserve historical photograph restoration. Edit target: the supplied archival photograph of Soviet officials voting with raised hands in rows of dark wooden seats. Restore and naturally colorize this EXACT photograph to clear, realistic modern-camera photographic quality. Preserve every visible person's identity, facial structure, expression, exact position, raised hands and fingers, attire, medals, and the original arrangement. Preserve the recognizable central front-row face and the man on the front right. Remove low-resolution blockiness and color noise; recover believable facial, hair, wool suit and wood detail without painterly smoothing, beauty retouching or invented people. Natural pale skin, charcoal and muted olive suits, white shirts, restrained burgundy ties, warm walnut chamber. Restrained realistic color and balanced indoor light. Composition: final image 6:5 landscape aspect ratio, extend the chamber naturally a little above and below the source as needed so ALL existing people and raised hands remain in frame; do not squeeze or stretch the source. This will be reduced to a small 180x150 photo inset in a Millennium Dawn HOI4 event, so maintain crisp readable faces and clear hands. Output ONLY the restored photo, fully filled edge to edge; no frame, no border, no padding, no text, no watermarks. The game's exact native frame is added separately during asset packaging.

The exporter verifies exact native frame pixels, DDS/PNG equality, sprite registration and event bindings. No fresh in-game screenshot or multiplayer test is claimed. The parser skipped one unrelated vanilla power-balance registry; no event picture in this inventory depends on it.
