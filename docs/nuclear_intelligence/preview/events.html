COLDWAR_NI.1

Nuclear Fuel Activity in Iran

SECRET — INTELLIGENCE ASSESSMENT

Industrial reporting indicates construction and specialised procurement consistent with an expansion of uranium enrichment capacity in Iran. The scale and purpose of the undertaking remain uncertain.

Assessment confidence: Moderate.

The available evidence does not establish a nuclear weapons programme.

Continue discreet observation.
COLDWAR_NI.2

Enrichment Activity Corroborated

SECRET — CORROBORATED ASSESSMENT

Independent reporting corroborates the expansion of uranium enrichment capacity in Iran. Our service assesses the underlying industrial activity with high confidence.

Assessment confidence: High.

Enrichment can support civilian fuel production. These findings alone do not establish a decision to develop nuclear weapons.

Add the findings to the national assessment.
COLDWAR_NI.3

Possible Nuclear Weapons Programme

SECRET — INTELLIGENCE ASSESSMENT

Restricted research activity and procurement reporting indicate that Iran may have undertaken an organised effort to develop nuclear weapons. Independent corroboration remains incomplete.

Assessment confidence: Moderate.

We cannot confirm possession of a usable device or establish how far the programme has progressed.

Seek independent corroboration.
COLDWAR_NI.4

Nuclear Weapons Development Corroborated

SECRET — CORROBORATED ASSESSMENT

Independent reporting supports our earlier assessment that Iran has undertaken organised nuclear weapons development. Our service assesses the military purpose of the work with high confidence.

Assessment confidence: High.

The evidence does not establish a deployable arsenal, the present operational status of the programme or a reliable date for completion.

Refer the assessment to the government.
