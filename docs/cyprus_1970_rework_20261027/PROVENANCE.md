IMPERIA COLD WAR: Cyprus compact concept provenance

Created 27 October 2026 for Enes' requested January 1970 to 1990 multiplayer redesign. This concept supersedes the older 295/296-focus design as a proposal. It is not approval to install the new content.

The concept data, research synthesis, proposed balance, diagrams and viewer were authored in this Codex session. Historical evidence is linked in CONCEPT.md and concept.json. Research used primary IPU, UN, World Bank, Council of the European Union and US historical-document sources, with party history explicitly identified as a participant's account. No historical source establishes the proposed numeric balance values.

Layout and mechanics references: installed Millennium Dawn at E:/SteamLibrary/steamapps/workshop/content/394360/2777392649, especially 05_israel.txt, 05_russia.txt, 00_generic.txt, native party triggers, modifier definitions, fiscal readers and the dynamic-modifier authoring guide. Millennium Dawn contributors retain their authorship. Individual original focus-art authors are unknown. Separate redistribution licence or permission for the reused artwork is unknown. The project marker does not confer ownership or establish a licence.

The preview embeds resized PNG conversions of existing local Cold War focus art. It does not modify the source textures or add watermarks. Exact source paths are recorded per focus in concept.json. The images are provisional family-level icons, not final per-focus art approval. The native focus card dimensions were inspected in interface/nationalfocusview.gui. The external font, grid pixel conversion and connector routing are approximations; this is not a live HOI4 screenshot.

The four treaty parties, three full political programmes, institutional minority penalties and bounded crisis system follow the user's current request. The latest steering requires monthly periodic reconciliation. Native weekly monetary bookkeeping is retained as an existing economy service and performs no new relationship polling in the proposal.

No loaded mod scripts, history, localisation or sprites were edited by concept generation. Existing archives and native profiles are not copied into this design directory. Future retirement archives belong on E:/MDColdWar_Backups/Codex_Test_Archives and native test runs on E:/HOI4_Mod_Tools/mdcoldwar/runtime.

The subsequent requested extension adds a Turkish intervention/refusal crisis, four finite Turkey-only crisis decisions, and Greek volunteer/direct-war focus alternatives. Simultaneous full Greek/Turkish NATO exit and automatic return are requested alternate-history rules. Primary historical and exact native API references are recorded in RESEARCH_INTERVENTION.md, RESEARCH_NATO_RUNTIME.md and REVIEW_INTERVENTION_MECHANICS.md.
