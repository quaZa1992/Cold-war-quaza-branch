# Independent historical review

Rechecked the rebuilt `concept.json` and `CONCEPT.md` on 27 October 2026. The four findings from the first review are resolved in the current concept. No concept or runtime files were changed during this recheck.

## Resolved findings

- **Yugoslavia tag mapping is documented.** The native project registers Yugoslavia under `SER`; the concept now says this is a game-tag mapping, not a claim that Serbia existed as a sovereign state in 1970. Player-facing previews use the historical country name Yugoslavia. The named recipient must remain Yugoslavia. The underlying historical link is NAM participation, while the technical/commercial agreement is clearly a proposed balance arrangement. [NAM founding history](https://www.nam-pn.org/history)
- **EEC and Czechoslovak offers disclose their chronology.** The concept now labels access before the historical 1972/73 EEC association agreement and pre-1972 inspected Czechoslovak supply as negotiated alternate-history paths. It records the real dates: signed 19 December 1972 and effective 1 June 1973 for the EEC agreement; 1972 for the Czech-arms inspection precedent. Six fixed partner response events are explicitly a multiplayer abstraction, not the treaty's documented signing process. This is a deliberate game-model choice rather than a historical error, provided future player text preserves the distinction. [Council of the EU treaty record](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/documents/treaties-agreements/agreement/?docLanguage=en&id=1972002); [UNFICYP arms-import chronology](https://unficyp.unmissions.org/en/1967-crisis); [UN Secretary-General's 1972 report on the inspections](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/490712/files/S_10564-EN.pdf)
- **EDEK arrangement is framed as new RP negotiation.** The presidential branch now calls it a proposed cooperation agreement and expressly distinguishes it from the documented AKEL-EDEK electoral pact of 1970. [University of Cyprus history chapter](https://kypseli.ouc.ac.cy/bitstream/handle/11128/1131/Introduction%20to%20the%20History%20of%20Cyprus-Chapter%2008.pdf); [IPU 1970 election report](https://data.ipu.org/election-summary/PDF/CYPRUS_1970_E.PDF)

## Historical framing checks

- **The party timeline remains sound.** Makarios is an independent president at the 1970 start, not a DIKO ruler. DIKO was founded by Spyros Kyprianou in 1976. Eniaion preceded DISY, which Clerides founded in 1976. EDEK existed in 1970 and remains represented as a coalition actor. The three full programmes are defensible when the presidential current becomes DIKO only later. [Cyprus Parliament 1970 election history](https://www.parliament.cy/easyconsole.cfm/id/249/); [Cyprus Tech University biography of Clerides](https://apsida.cut.ac.cy/items/show/46007); [DIKO party history](https://democraticparty.org.cy/profile/)
- **Turkish Cypriot offices and the non-player response model are transparent.** Fazıl Küçük was Republic Vice-President and head of the de facto Transitional Administration; Denktash was its deputy and an intercommunal negotiator from 1968, before becoming Vice-President/administration head in 1973. The rebuilt concept says confidence-based outcomes are simulated institutional consent and not another human player's decision. Keep that distinction visible in any eventual UI; confidence values are balance rules, not historical measurements or proof of consent. [US Library of Congress Country Study](https://countrystudies.us/cyprus/14.htm); [US FRUS on Denktash's negotiating role](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76v29/d386)
- **Treaty framing is accurate.** The concept distinguishes the 1960 Treaty of Alliance and UK base arrangements, describes consultation under Article IV, and makes no automatic legal verdict on Turkish action in 1974. Consultation under Article IV is among Greece, Turkey and the UK; Cyprus being invited to respond is a useful RP layer, not a Treaty requirement. [1960 Treaty of Guarantee, UN Treaty Series](https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/UNTS/Volume%20382/v382.pdf)
- **Coup attribution and agency are acceptable with the current wording.** The actual 15 July coup was staged by the Cyprus National Guard under Greek officers, against Makarios. The concept gives Greece an explicit player choice to attempt the coup and describes this as a player-driven crisis. Any later text should continue to distinguish the Greek junta's direction/support from the National Guard operation itself. [UNFICYP chronology](https://unficyp.unmissions.org/en/events-summer-1974)
- **Post-1974 effects are conditional.** There is no 1974 refugee starting penalty; displacement and tourism losses depend on actual campaign outcomes. EOKA B is absent at start and introduced after 1971. A 1983 separate-state declaration remains a player-driven choice, consistent with UNSCR 541's finding that the declaration was legally invalid. [UNFICYP chronology](https://unficyp.unmissions.org/en/events-summer-1974); [UNSC Resolution 541](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/58970?v=pdf)
- **The rebuilt presentation is internally clearer.** It removes native decision menus and unlock rewards, names foreign country-event recipients by historical country identity, and describes the coarse map and lack of a separate Turkish Cypriot human seat openly.

## Remaining historical caveats

The concept correctly marks its numeric costs and modifiers as balance proposals. Confidence, officer penetration, starting institutional penalties, and the precise gains from agreements or reforms are not historical measurements. Sources establish institutions and precedents, not each scripted causal value. Preserve this label. The Treaty of Alliance's Greek and Turkish contingents and tripartite command trained the Cypriot Army; that precedent does not itself establish a new Greek military mission in 1970. [FRUS account of the Treaty of Alliance](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1958-60v10p1/d310)

No factual problem remains in the four previously flagged items. No 1974 refugee start, sovereign NCY in 1970, or automatic Treaty-triggered war is claimed. The coarse geographic representation is disclosed.
