# IMPERIA COLD WAR: Israel 2.0 independent content review

Design review dated 28 October 2026. No runtime files were changed. Proposed gameplay values and hypothetical policies are balance/design choices, not historical statistics or verified native effects.

## Why the current programme feels interchangeable

The current canonical `tools/israel_1970/english_design.json` has twenty visible party groups. Eighteen of them have exactly ten focuses. The repeated economic fork, constitutional fork, matching extensions and finale turn different political organisations into the same sequence with different names. The 24-slot presentation source also defines several invented institutions to fill outlook slots. Some extreme successor-state slots are disabled in the live design; their presence in the source is not a claim that they currently load.

The visible problems are more specific than low reward numbers. Mafdal and Agudat share almost identical research/education and social-cost/administrative packages. HaOlam HaZeh receives a generic debureaucratisation/commerce identity. Gahal, Independent Liberals and the State List repeat investment-cost and market-exposure bundles. A territorial policy often produces broad military bonuses that do not arise from its actual local installation. Increasing all those percentages would preserve the underlying monotony.

Use a shared national economy and a small set of distinct governing programmes. Keep other historically real parties inspectable through shorter, meaningful continuations. Asymmetric branch lengths are useful when they reflect different institutions, rather than a quota.

## Eight major programme identities

1. **Alignment: the negotiated production state.** Histadrut enterprises, Kupat Holim, public credit and the actual January 1970 tripartite wage package should form its identity. Choose an employers-union production compact with higher industrial efficiency and less strike disruption, but employer contributions and earmarked bond obligations; or commercial management of state enterprises with stronger output and paid capital investment, but union opposition. The economic gain should come from a tangible contract or enterprise, not a free global research modifier. Mapam begins within the Alignment, with a clearly labelled alternative independent continuation.

2. **Gahal: Herut and the Liberals governing together.** Use the productive tension between nationalist social protection and commercial liberalism. One cabinet can prioritise independent employers, export credit and competition with established party enterprises. Another can use national infrastructure and reserve procurement to attract the development towns. The first pays for guarantees and gives up public-enterprise returns; the second commits money and construction capacity to protected domestic suppliers. Constitutional opposition experience can support scrutiny, without reducing the party to an automatic authoritarian path. Likud is a later optional organisational development.

3. **Mafdal: religious Zionist institutions and the younger generation.** Separate educational and communal investment from the security obligations of sponsored settlements. A funded study/service programme can supply training and reserve preparation, with continuing educational costs. A settlement programme should deliver the actual local infrastructure, farming or protected access that is funded, with local resistance and recurring security expenditure. The older coalition bargain and the younger territorial activists are competing priorities. Do not start with a fully formed 1974 Gush Emunim identity.

4. **Agudat Israel and Poalei Agudat: negotiated autonomy and community provision.** Make separate community education, welfare and vocational/cooperative institutions central. Funding those institutions can relieve selected state services and support a durable coalition, but ties money to protected appropriations and a less uniform public system. Poalei Agudat's productive/cooperative variant should differ from Agudat's educational autonomy. This programme should not automatically receive Mafdal's reserve or territorial-nationalist identity.

5. **Independent Liberals: commercial mobility and a civilian economy.** Tourism, port services, smaller firms and civil liberties provide a distinct civilian route. Credit guarantees can produce offices and export/service income at a real guarantee or interest cost. A tourism/service investment belongs in an actual coast or port state, with its building slots and operating expense. Civil reforms can produce institutional access and conflict with religious coalition partners. Avoid treating this as Gahal with a smaller number.

6. **HaOlam HaZeh - Koah Hadash: public scrutiny and equal citizenship.** Use its real name, Uri Avnery's press/public campaigning and a parliamentary civil-rights identity. Public inquiries can expose patronage and improve the terms of a specific procurement or development programme, but temporarily delay that programme and provoke the affected institution. Municipal procurement and qualifications can widen productive access for citizens. A free press should not mechanically mean poor espionage defence; that is an unsupported shortcut. Do not transplant the later Ratz or Meretz party into the January 1970 start.

7. **Rakah: a Jewish-Arab workers' programme.** Organised workplace representation, municipal equality and a different diplomatic orientation should matter economically. Cooperative/public industrial investment can generate output with wages and operating commitments. Soviet technical or trade agreements remain actual recipient-approved offers, rather than free factories from Moscow. Territorial or diplomatic proposals require RP negotiation, not automatic annexation, peace or border transfer. Maki must remain a separate political option: the 1965 split and Moshe Sneh's stance make it historically misleading to treat the two as identical Soviet parties.

8. **State List: the state above the party apparatus.** The Ben-Gurion holdouts offer an institutional programme of professional administration, defence expertise and public-enterprise accountability. A concentrated defence engineering programme can supply limited research bonuses or production capacity at a genuine capital and secrecy/coordination cost. Alternatively, professional accounts and contract review improve specific enterprises at the expense of political appointments. Rafi already largely merged into Labor before 1970: do not simply move the entire Peres/Dayan camp into the State List. Free Center remains a separate, shorter right-wing continuation.

These families are not a justification for deleting the two actual Alignment-affiliated Arab lists, Maki or Free Center. Their shorter programmes should appear where they interact with municipal investment, coalition terms or competition within the left and right. An invented Islamic municipal party is not an adequate replacement for the actual Arab parliamentary lists.

## National institutions that provide the charm

- **The Reserve Society:** choose peacetime reserve preparation or an expensive, time-limited high-readiness posture. The latter can improve mobilisation, organisation and preparation while raising personnel expenditure and temporarily reducing civilian output. Do not remove workforce or force a war.
- **From Ulpan to Workshop:** absorption centres, language/qualification programmes and housing produce staged, bounded productive labour gains. The complete compatible route has a +15% workforce ceiling. Existing people must not be counted again as new population, and the 1990s Soviet immigration wave must not appear in January 1970.
- **Bonds and charitable transfers:** a sovereign loan produces capital and debt; a charitable programme finances specified civilian absorption, education or health commitments. Both should feel financially powerful, but neither is a perpetual free treasury multiplier.
- **Mekorot and Hatzerim:** improving an already existing national water system and scaling early drip irrigation can create a compelling engineering/export route. Choose urban supply, domestic agriculture or export-oriented agricultural equipment as the scarce funding priority. Treat later desalination megaplants and mature global subsidiaries as future developments.
- **A state beneath every settlement:** controlled-area projects deliver real local assets and increase their actual security bill. Uncontrolled-area encroachment remains unilateral, imposes negative local/host consequences and gives Israel a bounded funded foothold. It must not create factories in land Israel does not control, grant host benefits or request host consent. Effects should track actual control and end or change when control changes.
- **The foreign-exchange constraint:** imported aircraft, fuel and industrial inputs compete with domestic civilian investment. Use actual project bills, debt and trade exposure rather than a generic bureaucracy malus attached to everything.

## Primary evidence and chronology

- [Inter-Parliamentary Union, Israel 1969 election report](https://data.ipu.org/election-summary/PDF/ISRAEL_1969_E.PDF), especially pages 2-3: contemporary party relationships and election representation, including Alignment, Gahal, State List, religious parties, separate communist lists, HaOlam HaZeh and Free Center. It establishes the roster and context, not the hypothetical reward packages above.
- [Bank of Israel, Annual Report 1970, chapter IX](https://www.boi.org.il/media/fj5diyww/pe_9.pdf): full employment, participation and the negotiated January 1970 wage package involving government, Histadrut and employers. Pages 13-15 discuss the wage/bond arrangement and increased employer obligations. The report was published in 1971; its findings are historical evidence, not a requirement that the player reproduce those events.
- [Bank of Israel, Annual Report 1970, chapter I](https://www.boi.org.il/media/e50ggphb/pe_1.pdf): strong growth alongside defence-import, foreign-exchange and debt pressure, with allocation conflicts involving exports and immigrant housing. These support a powerful but constrained economy.
- [Bank of Israel, state loans and bonds](https://www.boi.org.il/information/state-loans-bonds/): sovereign external bonds are borrowing instruments. A direct treasury reward must therefore be paired with the corresponding debt, rather than labelled a donation.
- [Knesset research paper, Methods of Immigrant Absorption in Israel](https://m.knesset.gov.il/EN/activity/mmm/me02775.pdf): the Absorption Ministry was established in 1968, with language, housing and employment responsibilities shared with the Jewish Agency and other institutions. The document is a later official retrospective; modern workforce statistics in it must not be transplanted into 1970.
- [Mekorot institutional history](https://www.mekorot-int.com/our-history/) and [its visitor-centre history](https://www.mekorot-int.com/visitor-center/): the carrier and pumping system already operated in the 1960s. A 1970 focus should extend or improve the system, not claim to invent it.
- [Netafim institutional timeline](https://www.netafimlegacy.com/): Hatzerim/Blass agreement in 1965, early commercial drippers in 1966 and Arava applications in 1967. It supports early irrigation engineering and manufacturing; later technologies and subsidiaries require later development.
- [Knesset commemoration of Uri Avnery](https://main.knesset.gov.il/en/News/PressReleases/Pages/Pr14001_pg.aspx): verifies his HaOlam HaZeh - Koah Hadash service in the sixth and seventh Knessets and journalistic role. The search index supplies the text; direct Knesset page access currently redirects to a geographic maintenance page.

Additional institutional background, distinguished from contemporary primary evidence: [Israel Democracy Institute, 1969](https://en.idi.org.il/israeli-elections-and-parties/elections/1969/), [Gahal](https://en.idi.org.il/israeli-elections-and-parties/parties/gachal/) and [Mafdal](https://en.idi.org.il/israeli-elections-and-parties/parties/national-religious-party/). The 1969 page links party platforms; its party history describes a unity coalition, not a permanent ban on RP alternatives.

## Main-MD presentation comparison

The supplied MD 2.0.3 `common/national_focus/05_israel.txt` uses short focus names, native ruling-party triggers, real party art and multiple prerequisite-relative subsections. It is a mechanical/presentation comparator only: its modern parties, automatic territory/state conversions and world-2000 chronology must not be copied as 1970 facts or MP consent rules. The proposed version needs native-style concise numeric effect fields, substantive descriptions and distinct institutional consequences rather than a catalogue of invented explanatory summaries.

## Concrete catalogue review: 136-focus proposal

Reviewed the generated `tools/israel_v2/design.json` and its authoring source after the two Arab-list municipal additions and the removal of the informational Palestinian-pressure focus. This review is of the proposal before the root agent's subsequent corrections, not the installed 399-focus tree. No design or runtime file was edited by the reviewer.

### Improvements that should be retained

The eleven actual political families now have visibly different lengths and institutions. Absorption, the reserve economy, public debt, water engineering, actual controlled-area construction and the consequences of foreign footholds are stronger identities than the previous repeated ten-focus programmes. The actual Arab lists now receive municipal/coalition continuations rather than being represented by a fabricated Islamist government. The eight bilateral offers now show signed prospective numeric rows for both countries. The absorption programme explicitly removes the corresponding starting spirit.

An exhaustive selection over the proposal's mutual-exclusion pairs, separately allowing the national programme and each single governing party, found no focus that was impossible to reach in every graph selection. This checks prerequisite/exclusion structure only, not native governing-party triggers, state capacity or political accession. The three workforce additions total exactly +15%; no other proposal modifier adds workforce.

### Findings and smallest corrections

1. **Material dependency mismatch: `ISR2_tal_tank`, `ISR2_ambiguity`.** Tank development requires the Nesher fighter programme, and strategic ambiguity then requires that tank programme. Neither institutional dependency follows from the descriptions or the history. Reparent the tank project to the overhaul/industrial foundation or the armoured school; give the nuclear-policy continuation an existing research-establishment entry. The proposal must not force a player to commission a fighter to maintain a nuclear policy.

2. **An economically empty finale: `ISR2_national_bargain`.** Selecting the appropriation costs 1% GDP and saves exactly 0.5% on each of two later projects. The complete funded route has no incremental financial or asset benefit, despite an additional focus and decision. Lower the appropriation contribution to 0.5% for the 1% discount entitlement, or fund a separately visible asset/service. Retain the mutually competing civilian/settlement priorities.

3. **Misleading agricultural scope: `ISR2_controlled_sites`, `ISR2_mafdal_survey`.** Local agricultural productivity is not supplied merely by writing `agricolture_productivity_modifier` into a state modifier. In the installed MD, `common/modifier_definitions/money_modifier_definitions.txt` registers it as country scope, while `common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt` reads it against the country's aggregate agricultural districts. Either make the proposed local benefit an explicit new weighted state-income integration, or rely on the delivered agricultural buildings until such an integration exists. Do not label a country modifier attached to a state as a working local output reward.

4. **Wrong reward family: `ISR2_after_action`, `ISR2_hesder`.** The former promises a research bonus for Land Doctrine, which is not a registered research category. Current MD uses `add_doctrine_cost_reduction`, for example in `05_russia.txt` around line 26240. Show a doctrine-cost reduction rather than a research bonus. Hesder's own description rejects automatic scientific benefit but grants a 50% Infantry Weapons research bonus. Keep its experience and use a bounded training or doctrine benefit instead of weapons research.

5. **An inappropriate charity dependency: `ISR2_bonds_plant`.** Borrowed-capital industrial workshops require the overseas absorption appeal. That humanitarian appropriation is neither an industrial prerequisite nor the source described by this focus. Reparent the workshops directly to `ISR2_bond_drive`. More generally, consider allowing the diamond/citrus commercial branches without forcing the full 8%-GDP sovereign borrowing decision, so commercial development is not equivalent to accepting that loan.

6. **Contract pricing does not match its asset: `ISR2_arms_exports`.** A fixed 250-unit infantry-equipment order costs 1% of whichever buyer's GDP is selected. The same model and quantity therefore have radically different prices depending on buyer, with potentially enormous transfers from large countries. Real consent does not make that pricing credible. Quote a bounded invoice from the equipment model/quantity and production commitment, with the exact same money credited to Israel. The irrigation contract's national +10% agriculture reward for a buyer-scaled bill also needs an explicit scale decision: a pilot state is more credible than a 1970 supplier immediately modifying every farm of a continental state.

7. **Guarantee expiry is not settled: `ISR2_credit_guarantees`, `ISR2_workshop_guarantees`.** Their provisions last 52 weeks but the productivity/investment modifiers have no stated corresponding expiry or renewal lifecycle. Define whether these represent funded temporary programmes or a one-time investment that creates durable capacity. In the former case, expire/suspend the benefit with its appropriation and provide a paid renewal. In the latter, explain the specific durable investment and distinguish it from an indefinitely renewed credit guarantee. Do not let a short budget accidentally buy an indefinite free guarantee.

8. **Party overlap remains: `ISR2_competitive_credit`, `ISR2_household_savings`, `ISR2_workshop_guarantees`, `ISR2_export_services`.** Gahal gets +16% office productivity from its two programmes; Independent Liberals get +14% from two others. Both also rely on cheaper incoming investment and market exposure. Those leading economic outcomes remain remarkably similar. A smaller correction is to make Gahal's household-savings outcome a financing/debt-service benefit, while the Independent Liberals' commercial benefit is delivered through tourism and smaller exporters in named domestic states. This preserves substantial gains while making the choices play differently.

9. **An expensive branch needs a more distinctive return: `ISR2_study_guarantees`, `ISR2_communal_welfare`.** The school guarantee adds a permanent +6% education cost for one-time PP and an opinion change; its follow-up gives only -5% social cost while adding another continuing relief bill. This is not proven strictly dominated because religious influence and political outcomes also matter, but its continuing economic cost is much more obvious than its institutional gain. Make the funded communal service replace a meaningful portion of an existing state service bill, or provide a concrete productive community outcome. Do not compensate with generic weapons research or the Mafdal service package.

10. **Party-history labels overstate the source: many party nodes default to `Historical basis` with only the `election` citation.** The IPU report verifies parties and elections, not the exact hypothetical programmes of a sovereign one-seat-party cabinet. Label these as historically grounded alternative governing policies and cite institutional evidence where a special programme is claimed. This is especially important for Hesder, the Independent Liberals' tourism ministry and the distinctive Maki programme.

11. **Minor preview precision: `ISR2_canal_works`, `ISR2_eastern_credit`.** Shared building slots belong to the Sinai state, while land forts target the selected province; label them separately. For the Soviet loan, show public-debt principal reduction alongside the weekly treasury repayment. The proposed separate lender ledger must be integrated with MD's aggregate interest charge, not merely add a second interest stream. The catalogue already acknowledges that this integration is new work.

### Reachable stacking, without treating it as measured GDP

Enumerating compatible national choices with one party at a time gives the following proposed modifier totals before temporary foreign agreements, starting spirits, foreign footholds or native caps:

- Gahal: office productivity +32% to +37%, incoming-investment cost -27% and world-market exposure +25%.
- Independent Liberals: office productivity +30% to +35%, incoming-investment cost -20% to -32%, depending on the currency choice.
- All parties: agricultural productivity +28% to +36% from the national route alone. Two funded foreign footholds can add another +6% under their proposed base terms.
- The national education-cost burden is +22% to +28%; the Orthodox route reaches +36% to +42% in the current catalogue. This is why a repeated small PP/opinion benefit can feel weak even when its number is positive.

These are source-level additive totals, not a claimed change in actual tax income or GDP. Workforce, employment fulfilment, debt interest, native caps, investments and the current economy affect the final result. The user requested strong financial advantages; a +15% workforce ceiling is not a universal +15% cap on all economic modifiers. The concern is concentration and party differentiation, not an invented blanket cap.

### Native feasibility boundary

The main economic modifiers used by the proposal have real installed consumers: civilian and office productivity in `00_money_system.txt`, workforce in that file's employment calculation, and interest additions in its sovereign-rate calculation. The interest modifier is a number in percentage points, not a multiplicative percentage. `productivity_growth_modifier` is a number read by `00_productivity_effects.txt`; the preview's `+0.06` is therefore more accurate than `+6%`.

New invoice/refund ledgers, earmarked charitable appropriations, local agricultural-income integration, funding-dependent programme renewals and finite sovereign bilateral debt are proposed implementations, not existing native effect calls. The proposal should continue to label that development boundary plainly outside player-facing Effects. The unrelated MD-update crash and current Israel state-scope defects are not fixed or validated by this design review.

### Resolution check: 138-focus catalogue

A subsequent source read confirms these corrections: `ISR2_bonds_plant` now follows the bond subscription directly; a distinct armour-repair root precedes the tank project; an atomic-commission root precedes ambiguity; both military education focuses show doctrine-cost reductions; the unsupported local agricultural modifiers were removed in favour of actual farms/slots; and Gahal's matched appropriation has a 0.5%-GDP public contribution and a genuine required 0.5%-GDP private match. The informational Palestinian focus remains removed, and the controlled/foreign settlement offer previews now directly display the consequences for each affected country/state.

Those corrections resolve the main factual/dependency and native-effect-family issues above. The old findings remain as a record of the reviewed draft, not allegations that they persist. The remaining substantive review items are the buyer-GDP pricing of a fixed arms order, guarantee benefit expiry/renewal, the similar Gahal/Independent-Liberal office-productivity packages, and the strength of the Orthodox route's return for continuing appropriations. Current proposal status is appropriate for an interactive design review. It is not approval of an implementation or a claim of native stability.

### Final design closure

Rechecked the 138-focus catalogue with SHA256 `c46b48eb7e5ae34512407482a53715f5309ca00b055fd8041caec80dfce75a3a`. The remaining substantive findings above are resolved in this reviewed proposal:

- The arms contract delivers 1,000 real stockpiled equipment sets, spares and training against the same fixed $0.01 billion invoice for both parties, with $0.004 billion Israeli setup expense. The figure is explicitly proposed balance, rather than a claimed historical arms price. The buyer-GDP mismatch is removed.
- Both guarantee benefits and provisions are explicitly 52-week funded programmes. Their benefits expire with the provision; renewal requires a fresh funded term.
- Gahal's second office-productivity bundle is replaced by a 2%-GDP domestic loan with matching debt, a 0.4%-GDP setup payment and a -0.15-percentage-point borrowing-rate programme. This creates a financing identity distinct from the Independent Liberals' commercial-services route. Structured modifiers and displayed effect rows agree.
- The Orthodox school branch funds pooled purchasing rather than imposing an additional research-free permanent education surcharge. The root's +8% education cost and purchasing reform's -6% are separately visible. The communal network now substitutes meaningful healthcare/social provision for a funded continuing grant, rather than merely providing PP and opinions.
- The Soviet loan explicitly previews both treasury repayment and reduction of outstanding public-debt principal. Its new integration and rounding-remainder requirement are recorded. The Rakah finale now delivers a paid municipal office/infrastructure project instead of duplicating a general diplomatic unlock.

No material content-review blocker remains for presenting this catalogue as a researched interactive proposal. All new agreement ledgers, programme lifecycle rules, exact native category mappings and execution/preview guards still require implementation and validation. This closure does not approve the installed tree, resolve the separate native-game crash, or certify live multiplayer behavior.
