# IMPERIA COLD WAR: Israel V2 territorial runtime

The territory emitter is `tools/israel_v2/runtime_territories.py`. It returns 33 native focus actions and three provider files to the root compiler, without independently writing the game files. No decision category, decision transaction, host establishment offer, core, annexation or population transfer is emitted.

The West Bank, Gaza, Sinai and Golan use state IDs 208, 209, 213 and 587. Their occupied-state resistance and Israeli police, personnel and administration commitments are separate native modifiers. Every state-only enable condition is inside an explicit absolute state wrapper. This addresses the demonstrated engine error in which a modifier attached with `scope = ISR` evaluated its enable conditions in country scope.

Each Nahal tier buys one agricultural district, one infrastructure level and one fort, after granting three state shared slots. A real state controller, legal owner and fixed eligible fort province are checked separately. Agriculture, offices and infrastructure use actual native physical level limits; forts check their province capacity. National and party projects share the three-tier regional counter. Control loss suspends Israeli marginal costs and returns; recapture restores them without a new building or payment. An election cannot remove site-bound commitments.

Each of the four uncontrolled foothold actions activates the Israeli benefit, actual-host penalty and state disruption together. At most two funded areas are signed, including temporarily suspended areas, so a change of control cannot circumvent the cap. A controller change transfers the host modifiers to the new controller and clears stale host modifiers. A funding closure can end only a non-building foreign foothold. It cannot strip the obligations of a productive controlled settlement.

Generic and Mafdal freezes share the real new-project restriction. Reopening removes the corresponding savings before enabling new starts. The funded support network raises actual per-area expense and office output together. The committee's savings reduce its existing site's actual security expense, while its own administration has a separate funded 52-week term.

Public focus costs use the actual native `modify_treasury_effect` deduction. Under the project decision confirmed during implementation, these public costs may leave a negative Treasury, following native MD focus spending. They are not waived, clamped away or treated as free assets. New foreign-investment commitments still require real, accepted, usable capital before consumption or construction. Invalid control, capacity, tier, commitment and private-capital conditions cannot consume an escrow or grant buildings.

The political-risk book holds its 0.2%-GDP reserve and spends its separate administration charge. Two insured project claims are capped at 0.8% each; the full 1.6% includes the held reserve, so the maximum additional Treasury call is 1.4%. The one-resort book similarly has a 0.1% reserve, 0.8% gross claim and 0.7% maximum additional call. Original USA, West German and Romanian contribution amounts are captured at the actual project commit. A loss sends the full gross claim and original investor shares to the bilateral claim provider, which credits investors and retires their affected original portfolio principal. The territory provider debits the reserve/Treasury once and never charges a second claim on repeated refresh. Only an unused cash reserve returns after cover ends.

The real private-capital provider is the bilateral module. Restricted settlement appropriations use the domestic module's actual non-consuming quote and consuming commit helpers. All project inputs, including procurement type zero, are explicitly set. Focus tooltips quote the net public price without consuming a contribution. Numeric modifiers use native `$RIGHT|...$` formatting, with scope headings and signed rows rather than implementation paragraphs.

All 33 action records include `visible_available`. Their displayed control, ownership, staffing, capacity, area cap, freeze, active programme, investment and cooldown requirements use real native conditions. Internal scenario, GDP-routing and one-time receipt guards remain in the complete execution condition instead of appearing as debug text. Optional insured co-financing is a price alternative, not a mandatory gate that would silently remove the normal public option. A late invalidation reopens the focus without uncompleting its children or refunding political power, and issues no assets or charges.

Palestinian pressure is computed only when Palestine exists and the affected state is its core. Each area has at most two effective pressure tiers, with four in total. Raw counters are exported for the voluntary service agreement's deterministic area selection. Relief caps the selected area's effective pressure at one without authorising a settlement, removing host penalties or changing ownership.

The module exports `init`, `refresh` and `daily`. Refresh recomputes quotes, control, modifiers, pressure and weekly streams without advancing a day. Native timed flags expire on the game calendar. Claims, reserve refunds and one-time assets have separate persistent receipts. Repeated initialization cannot reseed resistance after the initial 1970 opening.
