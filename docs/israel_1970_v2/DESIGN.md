# IMPERIA COLD WAR: Israel 1970 Version 2.2, policies through national focuses

A new design proposal, requested on 28 October 2026. This is not installed gameplay code. The current ISR70 tree and the unfinished native MD-update diagnostics remain unchanged. The proposal does not claim to repair the earlier engine crash.

## Intended identity

A highly capable development economy and reserve society, sustained by unusual institutions and external capital, with expensive commitments. The player converts capital into workshops, skills, irrigation, contracts or readiness. Governing parties change the instruments of policy rather than supplying the same ladder of percentage bonuses.

The expanded catalogue has 213 focuses across 19 programmes. The first expansion added 54 policies to the 138-focus proposal. The focus-only revision converts all 53 former decision unlocks in 42 focuses into direct completion effects or explicit focus choices. Alignment has 24 focuses, Gahal 24 and Mafdal 21. Eleven real party options remain inspectable. The two real Arab parliamentary lists receive distinct municipal/coalition projects; Mapam starts within the Alignment, while Maki and Rakah remain separate.

Positive domestic faction-opinion rewards have been removed throughout the catalogue. Existing weak entries now offer funded production, assets, fiscal changes or service provision. Bilateral diplomatic opinion is supplementary to concrete contract outcomes. Negative faction reactions are secondary consequences and are never treated as the full price of a programme.

## Focus-only actions

There are no decision unlocks in this design. Mafdal chooses its Negev, West Bank or Gaza appropriation through three exclusive national focuses. Gahal has separate civilian and territorial matching appropriations. Sapir has two named factory tenders. Exercise, emergency call-up, early release, controlled regional settlement development, uncontrolled regional footholds, their financial closure and the funding freeze are explicit focus cards. Existing productive controlled sites retain their security and Palestinian-pressure consequences while the relevant presence and Israeli control continue.

All bilateral offers originate at the completion of a country-specific focus. The named recipient accepts or refuses through a country event. The preview shows both sides' complete prospective numbers before acceptance. No domestic policy or recipient selection is moved into an event menu. Technical missions, commercial workshops, equipment orders and signals cooperation have fixed country cards based on recorded relations, without prescribing later wars, leaders or diplomatic breaks.

Renewable funded programmes return through their own focus after their actual term/cooldown. The design references MD's existing uncomplete_national_focus provider, with no child reset or political-power refund. The future implementation must retain persistent asset and financing receipts, charge full new term costs, prevent pending/active duplicates and honour shared regional caps. The reset provider exists in MD; these proposed lifecycle handlers have not been implemented or engine-tested.

## Signature choices

- **Capital with obligations:** Israel Bonds grants 8% of annual GDP in treasury and adds exactly the same debt. The civilian absorption appeal finances up to 4% of annual GDP in specific civilian programmes rather than unrestricted military cash. Gahal can auction one commercial enterprise for 3% of GDP, losing its recurring public dividend; domestic development notes add both 2% GDP cash and 2% GDP debt.

- **A real wage bargain:** the Alignment can use a 365-day wage-bond settlement with +8% factory output and a 1.5% GDP debt obligation, or pay 1.5% GDP for a cash settlement with +6% stability and +4% office productivity. Subsequent Koor, Solel Boneh and development-tender routes differ. The historical January 1970 agreement motivates the choice; the numerical effects are authored balance.

- **Three productive participation reforms:** ulpan-to-employment, ORT workshops and day-care/shift provision each add +5% workforce. The whole compatible catalogue adds exactly +15%, with zero negative workforce-size effects. These are participation and qualification gains, not automatic population imports or losses imposed on another RP country.

- **A reserve army with a civilian bill:** a limited exercise gives experience for money and temporary lost civilian output. A national call-up offers +25% mobilisation speed and +8% organisation for up to 70 days, with weekly expenses and temporary civilian/office productivity losses. No workforce-size penalty. A real war or authorised security emergency is required; historical wars are not forced.

- **Water and practical engineering:** improve an already existing water carrier, expand paid southern districts, or concentrate on metering and productivity. Hatzerim supplies the irrigation-engineering identity. Actual agricultural districts provide real income; country-only agricultural productivity is not incorrectly placed in a state modifier.

- **Repair before national weapons:** aircraft, armour and nuclear policy have separate institutional roots. Tank development no longer requires the fighter project. Technology bonuses and funded development do not grant an operational Merkava or a fleet in January 1970. Nuclear work uses the existing uranium and research systems, without free mines, raw production or warheads.

- **A sale is an actual sale:** the proposed standard order transfers 1,000 actual equipment sets of an agreed accessible model for a fixed $0.01 billion invoice seen by both parties. The seller pays $0.004 billion setup and carries a bounded production commitment. No buyer-GDP-based price distortion and no equipment created without stock. The figures are a design quote, not an asserted historical unit price.

- **Sensible territorial separation:** controlled sites buy one farm, one infrastructure level and one fort, with three shared slots and a real security bill. Uncontrolled footholds create no buildings or cores: they give Israel bounded funded commercial benefits and the host negative production, administrative and supply consequences. They are explicitly an RP abstraction, not an asserted ordinary historical practice. No host establishment veto or positive host reward is introduced. Palestinian institutional costs apply automatically if the affected core/state conditions exist.

- **Government rather than a timetable:** all party programmes remain visible. Future execution uses the actual native exact-party triggers identified in the catalogue. Negotiated ceasefires, territory, loans and technical missions require real recipient agreement where appropriate. No compulsory Rogers response, peace, war, alliance or border is prescribed.

## Duration, costs and ownership

Prices expressed as GDP shares must become current or quoted dollar values through the native MD treasury formatter at implementation. Weekly streams are actual weekly amounts. Loan principal and interest are shown separately, rounded to five fractional digits. Both final principal and final interest instalments must correct their rounding remainder. The proposed bilateral loan needs a real lender/borrower ledger and must not double-charge MD anonymous interest.

Party-funded modifier programmes run only under the exact governing party. National assets and completed one-time projects remain after government changes. Fixed-term wage bargains and guarantees expire on their stated calendar; their bonuses cannot outlive funding. Returning to government, loading a save or restoring a treaty must not replay loans, factories or grants. The 52-week guarantee books renew only with a newly paid provision.

Every building level has its matching extra shared state slot before the construction effect. Infrastructure and forts are included. State ownership, controller and native building/province capacity remain separate implementation guards. The preview is a proposal and does not itself execute those transactions.

Bilateral focuses show both countries' full prospective numeric results in Effects, under explicit acceptance headings, even though neither side has yet agreed. Political explanations, lifecycle conditions and research provenance remain outside Effects. Settlement presence is unilateral and therefore has host consequences rather than an acceptance offer.

## Review and evidence

The independent content review identified the former template repetition and reviewed the new programmes. Its corrections led to independent tank/nuclear roots, actual doctrine discounts, proper local farm assets, matched guarantee duration, a fixed equipment invoice and a stronger communal-service programme. Static checks prove reference and prerequisite/exclusion consistency, not economic causality or runtime safety.

FOCUS_ONLY_CONTENT_REVIEW.md records the separately inspected 213-focus content hash. Final layout integration changes coordinates and group origins only. Five card names were subsequently shortened to fit the native title field. A semantic comparison, normalising just those five names and excluding coordinates/origins, confirmed the reviewed policy, effect and prerequisite data remained unchanged.

The current browser report records all 213 focus inspections and all 19 branches at three viewport widths, including real downward navigation to the final rows of long programmes. The preview uses native icon art, orthogonal prerequisites and visible exclusions. Native MD was measured at a 165 x 128 focus container, 96 x 130 grid and 147 x 20 title field. The concept uses a 147 x 142 multiline card and an 82 x 178 external grid with Georgia font approximation. This is a composed preview, not a live game screenshot or an exact native pixel match.

The policy expansion includes contested territorial investment guarantees, Dayan-style military administration, binding arbitration in essential services, later currency liberalisation and its inflation costs, religious certification, study deferments and the tension between parliamentary control and settler activism. Every new focus identifies a contemporary policy/proposal, later historical precedent or plausible alternative. No later organisation, law or completed project is silently backdated into the 1970 start.

Political-risk and hotel guarantees require actual private project co-financing. Insurance is not free construction money. Cash reserves are held once, weekly administration charges are expenses, and gross claims include the held reserve. At full reserves, the Yanai-inspired book has a maximum extra call of 1.4% GDP on a 1.6% gross claim, and the hotel book 0.7% on a 0.8% gross claim. Unused cash returns only after cover and claims end. Elections close new quotes without cancelling existing cover. The underlying asset and security obligations follow actual control.

Civilian negotiating offers do not require previous settlement construction. National tax and procurement reviews are independent of the social-versus-territorial funding fork. Accepted Gahal and HaOlam HaZeh civil-service arrangements share one replacement family; their distinct administration/commerce and engineering/repair benefits cannot stack as duplicate agreements.
