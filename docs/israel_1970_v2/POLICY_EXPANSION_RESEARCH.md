# IMPERIA COLD WAR: Israel 2.0 policy expansion research

Independent research and content review, 28 October 2026. Scope: extending the important actual Israeli political programmes in the 138-focus design proposal. This document does not implement a focus, certify an effect, or change the installed mod. Proposed amounts below are design examples, not historical measurements.

## Chronology and evidence convention

- **A: available historical foundation.** An institution, policy or proposal existed before the January 1970 start. This does not mean every proposed game reward existed historically.
- **L: later historical precedent.** A dated policy, announcement or conflict after the start. The tree can develop a corresponding policy earlier through RP if its description explicitly presents that as an alternative government initiative. A named later organisation must not silently exist in January 1970.
- **R: proposed RP implementation.** An original game mechanism based on the cited institutions. Neither its numbers nor its consequences should be attributed to the historical source.

An announced bill is not an enacted law. A minister's favourable description is evidence of his position, rather than proof that occupation, investment or tourism had no adverse consequences. Primary records and contemporary reporting are distinguished from later institutional histories and unexamined archive leads below.

## Reward correction in the existing 138-focus proposal

Opinions may remain secondary political flavour. The main result should survive ordinary opinion decay through an enacted programme, an actual funded asset, a defined financial instrument or a bounded research/training result. Stability also becomes a weak principal reward when already capped. The following are the clearest candidates, rather than a requirement to delete every opinion row.

| Current ID | Current principal problem | Smallest useful replacement or extension |
|---|---|---|
| `ISR2_package_deal` | Unlock and Labor Unions Opinion +15, without a concrete compact outcome. | State the production/budget terms of the negotiated compact itself. A funded 52-week civilian production programme could provide +3% civilian-factory productivity with +3% social-spending cost. Subsequent cash/bond choices modify that same settlement rather than stack a second settlement. These figures are R. |
| `ISR2_cash_wages` | A 1.5%-GDP payment buys stability and opinion, while its bond alternative has a large productive result. | Make it a household-demand and municipal-services alternative: paid domestic service capacity or a temporary office-productivity programme with a defined budget. Preserve a reason to choose immediate consumption over deferred industrial finance. Replace the existing outcome instead of adding another permanent national +8% bonus. |
| `ISR2_herut_charter` | Social cost +6% primarily buys stability +5% and business opinion +10. | Attach a genuine development-town housing or rental-construction appropriation. Its descendants below give concrete construction, matching finance and social-service outcomes. A payment must purchase a project, not merely be renamed as one. |
| `ISR2_mafdal_budget` | Education cost +6% primarily buys PP gain +6% and religious opinion +15. | Fund a concrete religious municipal-services or school-workshop project. Keep the religious education budget, but make a bounded technical education reward or actual paid infrastructure the main return. No generic weapons research from religious affiliation. |
| `ISR2_communal_budget` | Education cost +8% primarily buys stability +5% and religious opinion +20. | Give the funding agreement an actual independent-school procurement and building-services outcome. The existing pooled-purchasing continuation can then improve this programme, rather than being the first concrete benefit. Preserve a distinct Poalei productive route. |
| `ISR2_community_clinics` | Healthcare cost +6% and setup mainly buy stability/opinion. | Connect the funded clinics to a bounded service-delivery programme and paid town infrastructure. A new referral or procurement efficiency should affect an actual healthcare budget consumer. Do not invent a native hospital building or a local health modifier that has no reader. |
| `ISR2_mashav` | Israel's direct gain is +10 bilateral opinion for setup and 52 weeks of expense. | Keep the recipient's real agronomy benefit, and give the sponsor a bounded practical-training or agricultural research result if supported by the mission's work. One agriculture research bonus is a clearer substantive return than decaying friendship. This is R, and the complete two-country numeric preview must change with it. |

The remaining opinions in nodes such as state enterprises or competitive credit already accompany substantive outcomes. They do not all require new gifts. The objective is to replace weak principal rewards and deepen institutions, not to inflate every focus.

## Alignment: bargaining power, development priorities and territorial administration

The useful distinction is not simply more state spending. An Alignment government controls an extensive labour and public-enterprise network, but must reconcile wages, investment, defence imports, immigrant housing and a serious social gap. Open bridges, planned settlement and investment insurance introduce a second conflict over how occupied areas are administered.

**Historical basis.** The Bank of Israel records an actual government, Histadrut and employers' package at the end of January 1970, including cash for low-paid workers, government bonds for part of other wage increments, and higher employer obligations. It also records full employment and external-resource constraints. That compact is a policy the January player can negotiate, not something already completed on day one. [BOI 1970, wages](https://www.boi.org.il/media/fj5diyww/pe_9.pdf), [BOI 1970, economic overview](https://www.boi.org.il/media/e50ggphb/pe_1.pdf).

| Proposed downward continuation | Status and practical identity | Meaningful benefit, cost and alternative |
|---|---|---|
| **A Tripartite Production Schedule** | L January 1970 compact; R implementation. Follows the repaired package-deal entry. | A defined 52-week productive compact, its employer/social contribution and its cash/bond settlement are one institution. An extension can renew it at a real budget cost. Do not grant an indefinite strike-free economy from one signature. |
| **Neighbourhoods before Prestige Projects** | L 1971 welfare response; R earlier prevention. Follows domestic development. | Move a limited development appropriation toward poor urban neighbourhoods, paid core-state infrastructure and service capacity. Compete with an industrial or occupied-area appropriation using the same scarce funds. Do not force a Black Panther riot before the player can fund services. |
| **Training Places for School Leavers** | L 1971 youth programme. | Fund vocational instruction, a bounded engineering/industry research bonus and job-linked workshops. Weekly education/social costs replace a generic opinion reward. No extra workforce percentage beyond the three existing participation reforms. |
| **The Allon Security Map** | A 1967-1968 ministerial proposal, not formally adopted national doctrine. | Select limited controlled Jordan Valley sites for paid farms, roads and defensive works. Give the actual assets plus slots, with security expenditure and resistance consequences. This is a spatial development priority, not an automatic core or border settlement for the whole West Bank. |
| **Dayan's Open Bridges** | A operational policy by 1969; R border administration and possible agreement. | Fund crossing terminals and inspection administration. Trade access to Jordan requires the real Jordanian player's agreement, while Israeli permit administration in controlled territory is a unilateral domestic decision. Commercial income must come from an implemented transaction or actual service capacity, with inspection/security expense. |
| **A Political-Risk Guarantee Reserve** | A guarantees and company proposals 1967-1969; L Yanai board in 1971. | Insurance enables a defined real private contribution to a controlled-area workshop, farm or hotel. Israel pays setup and a funded reserve, receives stated premiums and carries a capped contingent liability. A later actual loss of control or negotiated withdrawal can trigger the stated payout. No withdrawal, war or peace is forced. |
| **A Cabinet Settlement Allocation** | L Galili initiative 1973; R earlier cabinet programme. | Consolidate planned controlled-area projects under an annual appropriation, with competing domestic housing priority. Industrial or tourism sites may be eligible, rather than every settlement giving the same army buff. It does not legitimise settlement, remove Palestinian costs or make all Alignment factions agree. |
| **Extend Wage and Social Protection to Permit Workers** | L June 1972 announced government proposal, enactment not established here. | Alternative to cheap subcontracting: higher payroll/personnel cost for a more reliable funded civilian production programme. Use an actual paid employment contract and enforceable programme modifier. Do not model a population transfer, confer citizenship automatically, or add a fourth workforce bonus. |

The first, social and territorial threads can have distinct downward continuations. They should not become eight mandatory prerequisite steps before any useful benefit. Allon and Dayan are different emphases, not necessarily two wholly incompatible personalities or an automatic war/peace fork.

## Gahal: liberal capital with a Herut social constituency

Gahal is the correct 1970 electoral identity. Likud's formation and 1977 programme are later precedents. The Liberal economic wing and Begin's social constituency offer more interesting tension than repeating the Independent Liberals' office-productivity package.

**Historical basis.** Begin's January 1977 convention address advocated private enterprise and rental building alongside housing entitlements, large-family help, wider health coverage, pensions, equal employment conditions and a minimum-wage bill. It also proposed essential-service judicial arbitration. These are announced proposals, not proof that all were enacted. The archive's short abstract mischaracterises the economic formulation; the reproduced speech itself explicitly rejects the phrase capitalistic socialism. [Begin, 18 January 1977](https://db.begincenter.org.il/en/article/likud-govts-vision-for-israel/).

| Proposed downward continuation | Status and practical identity | Meaningful benefit, cost and alternative |
|---|---|---|
| **Homes for Rent, Capital for Builders** | L 1977 announced investment policy; R earlier liberal cabinet. | A funded domestic rental-construction programme with a real private/public match. Deliver paid core-state infrastructure/service assets and a temporary construction benefit. The private contribution must come from an implemented investment, rather than being spawned because a speech mentions investors. |
| **A Development-Town Housing Entitlement** | L 1977 announced social policy. | Replace the weak Herut social-appeal result with a housing appropriation and a limited development project. Its competing route can prioritise enterprise credit. Family assistance means an actual continuing social budget, not a generic productivity bonus merely for having a large family. |
| **Longer School Days in Poor Districts** | L 1977 announced policy. | A funded education service with bounded civilian technical learning or training returns, not a permanent extra research slot. Charge education costs and an initial equipment/programme bill. Distinguish it from Mafdal's religious school autonomy. |
| **Essential-Service Arbitration** | L 1977 announced judicial approach. | An alternative labour institution to voluntary wage bargaining: a funded, time-bounded production-reliability programme with court/administration cost and secondary labour opposition. Do not grant all-industry production immunity or force strikes. |
| **Erlich's Foreign-Exchange Reform** | L October 1977 actual reform. | A substantial financial reform competing with gradual control relaxation. Replace the existing exchange regime rather than stack both. Benefits concern access to capital and financial services; market exposure, borrowing risk and transition expense carry the cost. Neither growth nor hyperinflation should be scripted as inevitable. |
| **A Neighbourhood Renewal Partnership** | L late-1970s precedent; R early initiative. | Offer a diaspora-supported municipal programme. A real foreign country or RP sponsor must accept its contribution. Paid assets and multi-year service expenditure create the lasting result. Keep it distinct from sovereign bond borrowing and restrict humanitarian funds to their stated purpose. |

A full national health-insurance programme is an additional viable social direction, but should use the actual MD health-law and funding system. It must not be represented as free universal care plus a permanent cost reduction. A short policy name alone does not establish a working coverage mechanic.

## Mafdal: institutions, religious status and control over settlement activism

The 1970 party should contain its older coalition leadership, religious schooling, religious workers and younger settlement activists. A finished Gush Emunim apparatus would be an anachronism. The state can support controlled-area settlements while also disputing unauthorised initiatives.

| Proposed downward continuation | Status and practical identity | Meaningful benefit, cost and alternative |
|---|---|---|
| **Religious Councils and Municipal Services** | A institutional foundation; R funded programme. | Repair the entry by funding a concrete municipal-services package, paid infrastructure and limited school/workshop equipment. Keep administrative/religious service expense visible. Religious affiliation itself grants no industrial science or army attack bonus. |
| **A Conversion and Registration Compromise** | L March 1970 law and conversion dispute. | A funded registration/court service that protects a workable immigration process while compromising with coalition partners. Its actual practical benefit must be administrative service or the existing absorption programme's efficiency, without another workforce modifier. |
| **Require Halakhic Conversion** | L 1970 Orthodox demand; R alternate legislative outcome. | A genuine alternative: fund religious courts and training/registration capacity, with additional bureaucracy and a harder coalition bargain. Distinguish recognition as Jewish from eligibility for immigration/citizenship under the Law of Return. Do not expel people or automatically erase citizenship. |
| **Keep Settlement Projects under the Cabinet** | A cabinet administration; L documented later confrontation. | A lower-risk settlement appropriation with bounded approved sites, paid farms/roads and less contingent insurance exposure. It competes with greater discretionary support to activist committees, not with the right to play any settlement path at all. |
| **Back the Young Settlement Committees** | A younger activists; L Gush Emunim from 1974. | More useful selected-site construction or matching support, with greater policing/security expense, local resistance and capped liability. The named movement requires its actual later formation or an explicitly alternate early formation. Do not compel the cabinet to approve every unauthorised outpost. |
| **Religious Vocational Colleges** | A religious education and workers' institutional foundation; R specific expansion. | A paid civilian workshop project and one bounded technical research/training result. Its alternative is protected traditional study funding. No negative workforce modifier and no automatic military technology from theological education. |

The March 1970 amendment recognised wider immigrant-family eligibility while its religious definition and conversion language remained contested. Both Agudat and Poalei opposed its permissiveness according to contemporary reporting. [JTA, 12 March 1970](https://www.jta.org/archive/law-of-return-amendment-adopted-by-51-14-vote-passage-stirs-stormy-debate). Later government resistance to Gush initiatives is directly visible in [JTA, 10 October 1974](https://www.jta.org/archive/government-thwarts-massive-effort-by-israelis-to-settle-on-west-bank). Neither report supports treating every religious party, settler or minister as one faction.

Poalei's Orthodox labour movement, villages and cooperation with productive institutions justify a different continuation from Agudat's independent schools and communal autonomy. The later joint Torah electoral list does not establish a single January 1970 organisation.

## Independent Liberals: the tourism ministry and a civil-law challenge

Moshe Kol's tourism portfolio is a distinct strong identity. It also provides a controversial occupied-area policy which cannot be reduced to a generic secular liberal economic buff. Civil marriage is a separate institutional confrontation with the religious status quo.

|---|---|---|

The instrument gives an important financial advantage but is not a diaspora donation. Avoid a new national office-productivity bonus stacked on the existing two commercial programmes. |
| **Build on the Domestic Coast** | R development priority based on the actual tourism ministry. | Paid service capacity and access infrastructure in an owned, controlled domestic coastal state. This is the less exposed alternative to occupied-Sinai tourism, using the same loan/appropriation rather than two free parallel asset packages. |

Select this or faster subsidised expansion. Its main reward is an actual project or bounded technical result, not foreign opinion. |

## Design and review conditions

- Extend the historically important programmes asymmetrically. Do not impose the same number of descendants on all eleven political groups. Keep the minor parties' distinctive continuations meaningful without manufacturing a mass governing history.
- Every major new reward must have an actual supported effect or an explicitly identified required integration. A programme title, announced invoice or opinion gain is not execution evidence.
- No additional workforce modifiers are proposed. Retain the three existing +5% participation reforms and route-wide +15% ceiling. No programme imposes negative workforce.
- All new financial instruments need exact principal, debt, weekly service, duration, suspension/removal and final settlement. Quoted GDP should be fixed consistently when the instrument is contracted. Private matches must be real contributions. A reserve is not income available for unrestricted spending.
- Portfolio choices should replace overlapping programmes, particularly currency regimes, loan guarantees, housing appropriations and public-service discounts. Compare complete compatible routes before adding another permanent productivity bonus.
- Building and infrastructure grants require the extra shared slot per level in the same selected state before construction. Separate actual ownership, control and native capacity checks. No core, annexation or population change should be smuggled into a development reward.
- All party content remains visible. Use exact native ruling-party checks for execution and programme activation. Real coalition-partner requirements need actual identity, not an invented broad governing-current label.
- Ordinary cross-country loans, investment and trade require recipient RP approval, with the full prospective signed Effects for both countries visible while locked. Settlement establishment remains the specifically requested unilateral exception. The host receives the relevant adverse consequences, not a positive consent package.
- Open bridges, negotiations, insurance claims and political succession must respond to actual game/RP choices. Do not force Rogers, Camp David, the October war, a particular border settlement, deportation or Palestinian consent.

## Independent review of the expanded 192-focus prototype

Reviewed all 54 additions in `tools/israel_v2/policy_expansion.py` and their emitted rows in `tools/israel_v2/design.json`. Initial catalogue hash: `afc2202886ce191602696f62e25125925063fb6249e48a3ea505adec67e6fc3c`. The later read with hash `6677a3513247e9e38d4e68ca07b49433445daa8381be7358141b2b8a98251ad1` includes the clinic correction. This is a design review, not an installed-mod or execution review. Findings describe these snapshots; subsequent corrections need a separate closure read.

### Verified improvements

The major programmes now have unequal, substantive continuations: Alignment 23 focuses, Gahal 23 and Mafdal 19, with shorter Orthodox, Independent Liberal and HaOlam programmes. Positive internal-faction opinion rows are removed. Cash wages have a service-economy result, Herut social policy has actual infrastructure, Mafdal's entry purchases a farm and factory, and Israel receives a bounded agronomy research result from MASHAV. The corrected clinic gives healthcare procurement savings against a continuing clinic grant, rather than an unrelated industrial-repair bonus.

The three new offers, `ISR2_labor_jordanian_option`, `ISR2_gahal_local_autonomy` and `ISR2_haolam_joint_services`, display both parties' signed setup and weekly costs, durations, named spirits and every numerical modifier directly in Effects. Their stated execution requires the real recipient's response. The latter two are voluntary service contracts, not permission for unilateral settlements. None of the new settlement projects gives the host a positive reward or requires Palestinian approval.

Classification generally distinguishes 1970 proposals, later precedents and plausible earlier alternatives. It does not place a finished Gush Emunim organisation in the January start. The Agudat visible gate is corrected, and Poalei-specific branches require actual governing representation; implementing that representation remains explicit work.

### Material findings and smallest corrections

1. **Diplomatic access requires preceding settlement construction.** `ISR2_labor_jordanian_option` follows procurement review, which follows the fortified Jordan Valley project, permits and political-risk insurance. `ISR2_gahal_local_autonomy` requires both a paid settlement entitlement and pensions. These impose unrelated, controversial policies before the player can even make a civil RP offer. Reparent Jordanian/functional policy to an independent Alignment governing or administrative foundation. Let Gahal's civil-services offer follow its governing/administrative programme without requiring settlement construction or a pension act. Keeping actual consent does not repair an inappropriate prerequisite.

2. **The Alignment allocation fork locks unrelated national reforms.** `ISR2_labor_tax_base` requires longer school days and the domestic appropriation, while `ISR2_labor_procurement_ceiling` requires an occupied-area farm/fort project. As a result, funding territorial guarantees permanently prevents tax, family-payment and unemployment reform, and defence procurement review cannot happen without building settlements. Preserve a meaningful first-appropriation choice, but detach independent tax, savings, insurance and procurement institutions from its exclusive asset packages. Similarly consider separating `ISR2_gahal_health_bill` from compulsory arbitration and `ISR2_gahal_ministries` from the forced second asset sale. These are distinct political decisions, rather than technical prerequisites.

3. **The settlement committee can buy a net loss with no defined base benefit.** `ISR2_mafdal_land_committee` charges 0.01% GDP weekly for a 20% maintenance discount. Its immediate predecessor has no monetary maintenance stream. Even a 0.015%-GDP site saves only 0.003% weekly, and a 0.02% site saves 0.004%, both below the committee's charge. Define the selected site's exact bill and show the resulting signed saving. Lower the establishment charge below that saving or fold the committee into the funded next project. A prerequisite that only pays more for a smaller bill is accounting filler. Existing `ISR2_cap_projects` also needs its maintenance base resolved at implementation.

4. **Guarantees need a capital source and a coherent reserve balance.** `ISR2_labor_yanai` reduces two project bills by 0.5% GDP each, for setup of 0.2% and a 52-week provision totalling 0.52%. `ISR2_liberal_hotel_credit` reduces one resort's bill by 0.6%, for setup of 0.1% and 104 weekly provisions totalling 0.312%. Guaranteeing risk does not itself provide the missing capital. Require a real accepted investment or a stated guaranteed project loan, with actual matching finance and debt-service consequences as applicable. Do not substitute an unexplained discount for that source. Specify coverage end, outstanding claims, party-change treatment and the fate of unused reserves. If 1.6% and 0.8% are gross maximum claims, fully funded reserves leave maximum additional treasury needs of 1.08% and 0.488%, respectively. Do not subtract the reserve and then charge the same gross claim again. If weekly payments are irrecoverable insurance premiums, name and account for them as premiums instead of claiming an owned cash reserve. All figures here are proposed balance, not historical rates.

5. **Two new institutional benefits do not follow from the policy.** `ISR2_mafdal_strict_certification` turns conversion certification into a continuing 0.26%-GDP annual fee stream plus PP, without evidence for that fiscal scale. Reduce fees to a costed service levy or replace the windfall with a concrete funded institutional outcome; do not turn a restrictive status policy into an unexplained national revenue engine. `ISR2_orthodox_deferments` grants education cost -4% when its described action protects additional full-time study. Remove that ungrounded saving, or tie a genuine procurement saving to the funded study programme and explain its source. The existing independent-school procurement discount and the new protected-study discount also warrant explicit replacement or differentiated scope, rather than repeatedly discounting the same services.

6. **Indexed borrowing has no distinct financing advantage.** `ISR2_labor_indexed_savings` grants treasury and debt of 2% GDP and adds 0.15 percentage points to the national interest modifier. This is not demonstrated to be preferable to ordinary MD borrowing and prices all public debt rather than just the note. Add a specific funded project, private subscription or favourable instrument term; alternatively merge it into the tax/development finance programme. Do not describe a national interest modifier as a precisely calculated coupon on only this 2%-GDP instrument.

7. **Persistent assets require persistent site obligations.** In particular `ISR2_liberal_sinai_resort` labels its country security modifiers as a governing programme, although its buildings remain after a government change. Costs for that resort and the actual sites delivered by `ISR2_labor_valley`, `ISR2_gahal_settler_entitlement` and `ISR2_mafdal_regularise_sites` must follow the funded project and real control. Switching parties must not retain the productive assets while deleting the obligation. Issued guarantees and settled debt are contractual commitments, also distinct from a new cabinet's optional programme.
