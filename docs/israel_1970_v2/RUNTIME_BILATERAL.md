Offers reach the named recipient through a real country event. Both offer and acceptance recheck the correct native Israeli party where relevant, existence, bilateral peace, embargoes and every mirrored diplomatic, financial, economic and military sanction array. Actual setup funds are rechecked immediately before acceptance. Preview quotes use separate temporary variables; a pending or signed offer retains its frozen price. Refusal, insufficient funds or a changed government cannot pay setup fees or grant rewards. An unanswered offer expires after thirty days, with a fourteen-day diplomatic cooldown. Country AI refuses these new RP offers by default.

Ongoing contracts suspend their paired spirits and weekly contributions together when their eligibility fails. Normal paid terms still expire on their accepted calendar date. Reconciliation resets every participant's bilateral income/expense contributions before adding actual active amounts; it never repeats setup payments, research, experience or equipment. The global equipment-order lock begins when the offer is sent, not only when it is accepted. Each order transfers 1,000 real infantry-equipment units, pays experience only on delivery and refunds the fixed buyer invoice if undelivered after twenty-six weeks. The per-buyer fifty-two-week cooldown begins on delivery. An old event or duplicated acceptance cannot alter a newer order.

The irrigation invoice is $0.00600 billion, setup is $0.00200 billion, and maintenance is $0.00002 billion weekly for 104 weeks. Its full-term operating margin is $0.00192 billion before the supplied stock/research economy, independent of either country's GDP. The invoice is a proposed balance value, not a measured historical price.

Project capital is paid once by the consenting investor. The payment is two percent of the quoted Israeli GDP; the investor receives the same amount of native `int_investments`, and Israel receives restricted escrow only. `ISR2_private_available`, `ISR2_private_required`, `ISR2_private_ready`, `ISR2_private_commit` and `ISR2_private_paid` are the shared interface. Commit recalculates current eligibility, consumes the oldest eligible unallocated subscription first and never charges the investor again. Failed or insufficient funding returns a zero paid amount. Expired unused funds are returned once and removed from the native investment asset; deployed assets remain in the normal portfolio. A temporarily absent investor retains its unused return claim until restoration. The current native portfolio return is shown prospectively with the actual native ROI formula; there is no invented Israeli dividend expense or guaranteed future return.

The Soviet credit creates matched cash and native debt equal to four percent of quoted Israeli GDP. Principal is repaid in 104 successful weekly instalments; total contracted simple interest is eight percent of the original principal. Each weekly settlement atomically debits Israel, credits the Soviet Union and reduces native debt by the principal portion. The final settlement uses the exact remaining ledger, avoiding a residual principal from repeated division. War, sanctions or insufficient cash pause settlement without forgiving principal. Optional early repayment reduces both principal and remaining interest proportionally. These atomic loan payments are deliberately absent from the additional weekly income/expense streams.

The parent integration must exclude `ISR2_bilateral_loan_outstanding` from the ordinary native debt-interest consumer and prevent anonymous auto-repayment of that protected principal. Its regular domestic principal can still be repaid. The parent must add each returned bilateral income/expense stream exactly once, including the fixed partners, and call territory pressure reconciliation before selecting or updating funded Palestinian relief. Relief snapshots the more burdened Palestinian area at acceptance; it does not remove host penalties or Israel's settlement-site costs. The Gahal and HaOlam municipal agreement family replaces its existing paired programme rather than stacking both variants.
