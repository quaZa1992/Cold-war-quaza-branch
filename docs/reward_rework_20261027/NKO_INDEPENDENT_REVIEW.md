# DPRK reward overlay independent review

## Scope and evidence

Reviewed the emitted DPRK tree compiled from `tools/north_korea_1970/reward_revision.py`, its emitted scripted effects, triggers, dynamic modifiers and foreign agreement effects. This review does not cover KOR or JAP emissions, which are pending. The current NKO compile also temporarily lacks the reciprocal KOR bridge; the parent integration pipeline will restore it. I ran no source model tests or native gameplay tests.

## Finding: bilateral building preview does not follow the accepted site fallback

**P2, confirmed source mismatch.** The new or changed China farm supply, Soviet energy project and Japan fisheries previews place the building at state 602 or 601 unconditionally. Their acceptance effects instead choose the first eligible site from two states: China 602/603, USSR project 602/1200, and Japan 601/1199. For example, the Soviet preview grants both assets in 602 at [`COLDWAR_NKO_1970_foreign.txt:3638`](../../common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_foreign.txt:3638), while acceptance can put both in 1200 at [`COLDWAR_NKO_1970_foreign.txt:3745`](../../common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_foreign.txt:3745). The China and Japan equivalents are at [`:3272`](../../common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_foreign.txt:3272) versus [`:3371`](../../common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_foreign.txt:3371), and [`:2712`](../../common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_foreign.txt:2712) versus [`:2803`](../../common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_foreign.txt:2803).

The acceptance trigger rechecks that at least one suitable state is still owned and controlled by NKO, below the building level limit, and coastal for the dockyard: [`COLDWAR_NKO_1970_foreign.txt:1791`](../../common/scripted_triggers/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_foreign.txt:1791), [`:1922`](../../common/scripted_triggers/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_foreign.txt:1922), [`:2005`](../../common/scripted_triggers/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_foreign.txt:2005). So the transaction does not appear to charge for a package with no valid site. However, when the fallback is selected, the focus's prospective building location differs from the accepted building location. Use one shared site-selection contract for preview and execution, or describe the eligible state alternatives without claiming a fixed state. Native acceptance hover still needs checking.

## Mechanics checked

- **Equipment bonuses:** the overlay adds twelve target bonuses across eleven native `add_equipment_bonus` grants. They appear in both focus `effect_tooltip` and one-time completion helpers, target named equipment families, and use negative `build_cost_ic` values, which lower production cost. For example, heavy priority displays artillery -0.05 and medium tank chassis -0.04 in [`COLDWAR_NKO_1970_focus.txt:669`](../../common/national_focus/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_focus.txt:669) and applies the same values in [`COLDWAR_NKO_1970_focus_effects.txt:283`](../../common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_focus_effects.txt:283). The intended compatible artillery path totals -0.19 production cost; the medium-tank path totals -0.16. These are production-cost reductions, not direct treasury savings. Existing `equipment_cost_multiplier_modifier` increases on some military routes feed the separate MD recurring defense budget calculation in [`00_money_system.txt:3226`](../../common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt:3226), so the two effects must not be reported as a single net discount. I found no bonus-duration expiry; grants are protected by each focus's earned flag. Native designer UI and save persistence remain unverified.
- **Research:** the eight overlay grants use one use with bonus 0.5 in `CAT_industry`, `CAT_renewable_energy` or `CAT_electronics`. The native focus previews match the completion helpers. These are bounded research bonuses, not permanent research-speed modifiers.
- **Construction:** inspected domestic multi-level examples preserve the combined level and slot counts. Steelworks emits two arms-factory levels and two shared slots in the same chosen, owned and controlled state; the corresponding eligibility and completion checks are in [`COLDWAR_NKO_1970_focus.txt:811`](../../common/national_focus/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_focus.txt:811) and [`COLDWAR_NKO_1970_focus_effects.txt:368`](../../common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_focus_effects.txt:368). Food district and naval workshops likewise grant two levels and two slots, with ownership/control checks and the coastal check for the dockyard. Their `<9` agriculture, `<19` dockyard and `<34` arms limits leave room for the respective two-level package. The added single-level domestic buildings I sampled use one slot and recheck state ownership/control. Foreign grants use one slot for one level, or two slots for the one factory plus one power plant. No extra uranium production or weapon stock is introduced.
- **Costs and repeat protection:** the GDP-share costs display in the focus preview and are executed in the completion helpers. Changed domestic charges use a positive-GDP gate and an `*_earned` guard before charging or granting assets; e.g. steelworks charges 2.25% of GDP once at [`COLDWAR_NKO_1970_focus_effects.txt:368`](../../common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_focus_effects.txt:368). The shared MD `modify_treasury_effect` adds the signed amount directly to treasury and has no liquid-cash affordability check ([`00_budget_effects.txt:1487`](../../common/scripted_effects/00_budget_effects.txt:1487)). This means the focus may take treasury below zero; it follows the project rule against adding treasury prerequisites by default, so I did not classify it as a defect. I did not test the resulting balance in game.
- **Party programmes:** the revised Christian health-cost values match their dynamic modifier outputs: clinic service is `health_cost_multiplier_modifier = -0.02`; welfare is `+0.04`. The MD health-cost calculation multiplies expenditure by `1 + modifier@health_cost_multiplier_modifier` in [`00_money_system.txt:3519`](../../common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt:3519), so the negative value reduces that cost and the positive value increases it. Their ideas require the Christian party's slot and refresh removes the active modifier when that party leaves while the earned flag persists, allowing restoration without replaying the one-time charge. Definitions are [`COLDWAR_NKO_1970_programmes.txt:2825`](../../common/dynamic_modifiers/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_programmes.txt:2825) and [`:1517`](../../common/dynamic_modifiers/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_programmes.txt:1517); focus completion uses earned guards in [`COLDWAR_NKO_1970_focus_effects.txt:4263`](../../common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_focus_effects.txt:4263) and [`:2229`](../../common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_focus_effects.txt:2229). Native party-change and return behavior was not exercised.
- **Balance and presentation:** the heavy, light and balanced priorities now offer distinct military, agricultural and civilian packages with retained trade-offs. Added descriptions track the corresponding modifier and building effects; for example, heavy priority retains lower food productivity and higher grid use, while light priority sacrifices arms-factory output and raises social costs. Their costs and new buildings are shown in the focus effects. The new equipment rewards improve targeted production economics rather than adding blanket combat stats. These values appear worthwhile as branch alternatives; the artillery package is the strongest stack and carries its GDP fees plus the separate recurring equipment-budget burden noted above. Historical rationale and quantitative comparisons are recorded in [`NKO_REWARD_REVIEW.md`](NKO_REWARD_REVIEW.md).

## Verification limits

The emitted NKO source was reviewed, but neither its native hovers nor acceptance flow were run. Source parity cannot prove HOI4 modifier interpretation, AI behavior, save/rehost behavior, MP synchronization, or balance over a campaign. The East Asia native run and remaining country compiles are separate pending work.

## Final integration resolution

The finding above records the review-time output. It is resolved in the canonical agreement writer: preview and execution now share the ordered owned, controlled, capacity-checked site selector, including the coastal requirement and valid fallback. Locked or site-blocked focus previews retain complete prospective numeric effects. Domestic repeated building types are coalesced before checking aggregate capacity and granting slots. The current NKO model suite passes 1,185 cases. The final native probe also confirms the actual two-level steelworks transaction, GDP debit, zero-GDP rejection and replay guard. Manual tooltip readability and multi-client behavior remain unverified.
