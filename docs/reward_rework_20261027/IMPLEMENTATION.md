# Korea and Japan: substantive focus rewards

27 October 2026. User-authorized reward revision of the reviewed quaZa content. The existing 360 DPRK, 300 South Korean and 425 Japanese focus IDs, coordinates, prerequisites, exclusions, research/party requirements, historical leaders and succession systems remain in place. This changes important programme outcomes and financing; it does not multiply every modifier or make every focus a major capital project.

## Concrete examples

- DPRK Steelworks: two military factories with two shared slots, paid once at 2.25% of current GDP, in an owned and controlled eligible industrial state. The heavy, light and balanced priorities remain mutually exclusive and retain their existing different production, food, construction and spending consequences.
- South Korean Pohang: two civilian factories with two shared slots, at 1.75% of current GDP. The initial 0.75% allocation replaces the old $3B domestic charge and the expanded project adds 1%. At the scenario's $9.085B GDP target this is approximately $159M, not one third of national annual output. This is a gameplay investment scale, not a historical POSCO invoice.
- South Korean specialist procurement: fighter, frigate, tank-chassis and artillery programmes add family-specific production-cost reductions with finite technical bonuses. Original output/readiness advantages and their personnel, training or operating burdens remain.
- Japanese microelectronics: two Kanto microchip plants with two slots, +6% research speed and one 50% industry research bonus, at 2.5% GDP, with the real technology, ownership and native-capacity requirements retained. The plants also add their ordinary native employment and electricity requirements.
- Japanese LDP industrial standards: one Kanto civilian factory and slot, a single 50% industry research bonus, and the programme's civilian productivity/research changes, at the preserved $2B fee plus 0.75% GDP. The rival market follow-up instead adds two Kansai offices at 1% GDP. DSP, JSP, JCP and Komeito receive their own capital and policy packages.
- Japan's Seoul project: two recipient-selected civilian-factory levels and a rail terminal, three slots, and the specified five-year Japanese benefit, financed by Japan only after the recipient's actual acceptance/site selection. Other regional packages have different assets, costs and benefits; proposals do not grant assets merely for completing their sending focus.

## Correctness and lifecycle

All added levels receive a same-state shared slot before construction, including infrastructure and other types that do not consume shared slots. Eligibility checks native per-type and shared-group headroom separately from ownership and control. Coalesced multi-level projects cannot charge for an amount that will not fit. A state at 49 of 50 civilian factories rejects a two-level project; 48 accepts it. Invalid or missing GDP blocks a GDP-priced transaction in full.

Real focus previews show native costs, named signed modifiers, assets, technology/doctrine bonuses and weapon-family rebates. Equipment types/categories are resolved against the current MD definitions and modular aircraft roles. Ordinary capital assets and acquired technical capabilities are durable; active political programmes remain conditional on their real party/leader. Programme refresh cannot reissue factories, research awards or payments. Japanese programme grants now force a current-country modifier refresh after their variable changes, eliminating a stale first-day modifier snapshot. This adds no recurring world scan.

New diplomacy retains recipient authority and both sides' numeric prospective terms. The revised DPRK building previews select the same eligible state as acceptance and remain numerically inspectable while locked. Japan's existing timed agreement architecture and original recipient-selection/fee authority remain; Cuba's standing agreement rules are not silently substituted.

No raw uranium, uranium mine, nuclear weapon, new religious party, arbitrary focus date barrier or negative available-workforce-size modifier was added. Existing Kim Il Sung portraits and the 1994 death/caretaker/Kim Jong Il succession remain unchanged.
