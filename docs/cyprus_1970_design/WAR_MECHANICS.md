# Cyprus 1970–1974: causal crisis and war mechanics

Design and source audit only; no runtime files changed. Read alongside [WAR_HISTORY.md](WAR_HISTORY.md) for primary evidence and [EFFECTS_REVIEW.md](EFFECTS_REVIEW.md) for modifier units and the latest balance review. Current integrated candidate: **295 focuses**, including **22 road-to-war focuses**, six24-focus political routes and39 Turkish Cypriot focuses; nine starting spirits plus two crisis-only definitions. Existing APIs below were read from the local Cold War mod and, where specified, the supplied workshop MD snapshot. Every `COLDWAR_CYP_1970_` crisis variable, flag, event and adapter proposed here is **new content to implement**, not a pre-existing MD helper.

## Findings that determine the implementation

1. The coup and Turkish intervention need **causal entries outside the already-ruling coup branch**. Requiring the coup government to be in office before any coup event can fire creates a circular route. Greek-officer influence, hostile networks and GRE's decision must be able to produce a contested coup while Makarios or another civilian government still governs. The p22 branch then opens only after the coup actually succeeds.
2. A1970 start is not an unchanged1964 siege. The historian's UN S/9521 finding shows broad Turkish Cypriot travel had resumed by late1969, with military-area exceptions and continuing restrictions on Greek Cypriot access in Turkish Cypriot-controlled areas. Interpret existing startup modifiers as **legacy displacement, fragmented services, separate economic institutions, armed positions and contested access**. Renewed blanket restrictions must be a selectable escalation, not an invented universal January1970 condition. See [UN S/9521, para54](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/504435/files/S_9521-FR.pdf); access/index limitations are recorded in WAR_HISTORY.md.
3. The map has only **one province in each CYP state**:145→11984 and146→7193. State812→11128 remains British. A Turkish foothold near Kyrenia in July and the much larger August-controlled northern zone **cannot be separate land positions on this map**. Province7193 is the whole state146 abstraction, not an exact Kyrenia beach. Do not claim otherwise.
4. January1970 CYP, GRE and TUR histories explicitly defer land/naval/air OOBs. TUR's current1970 infantry profile does not grant a naval landing-craft package. A scripted declaration can technically occur, but a credible naval invasion cannot be certified with empty armed forces. Do not hide this by spawning a few modern template divisions.
5. The existing conditional-peace “ceasefire” helper changes legal game ownership, may peace whole factions and sets long truces. It must not be called unexamined for22July1974. A ceasefire flag alone also does **not** stop HOI combat.
6. Both145 and146 already have CYP **and NCY** cores. A blanket `release = NCY` from CYP risks releasing both owned core states, rather than only a later northern administration. No separateNCY at the1970 start.

### Existing Cold War assistance should remain budgeted diplomacy

`mdcw_cw_start_pending_program` at `mdcw_cold_war_effects.txt:3225` validates donor capacity, size1–3, type1–8, enemy status and existing programme before dispatching the accepted offer. Construction assistance is programme1, technical mission2, industrial equipment3, consumer supply credits5, military training6 (`:2618`, `:2693`, `:2768`, `:2918`, `:2993`). Public political support is `mdcw_cw_start_pps` around`:3476`.

These helpers use **sender scope with PREV=recipient**, bilateral indexed variables and Cold War link arrays; calling them in arbitrary root scope corrupts the relationship. Use the existing accepted diplomatic-action path or a deliberately scoped adapter, not an unsolicited `start_program_6 = yes` from a CYP focus. Public political support is overt influence, not a coup-success API. Consume the normal budget/upkeep and capacity rules; a focus can unlock/improve access rather than mint aid.

`mdcw_cw_on_war_relation` at`:4788` already counts interbloc war shocks once per30days and suspends programmes between enemies. `mdcw_cw_on_volunteers` and `mdcw_cw_on_coup` follow. Their hooks are registered in `common/on_actions/mdcw_cold_war_on_actions.txt:43–74`. A custom coup that changes a subparty manually must explicitly handle its own once-only Cold War reaction if the engine hook is not emitted; do not also emit the same shock through a second path. A regional crisis1–3point tension proposal is distinct from existing4/8point interbloc shocks and should be tested together, not blindly added on every event.

### Coercive options can exist without an ethnicity-to-buff conversion

Offer selectable **tightened military-zone checks**, **blockade/collective pressure**, **loyal auxiliary patronage**, **emergency policing**, and **unilateral centralisation**, with attributable institutions. A defensible initial short-term effect might be25–40PP or a temporary regime-control/command benefit already within the economic/organization ceilings. A targeted arms-control action can suppress an armed network if it succeeds; it cannot legitimately erase a whole community's rights or give free research/GDP because civilians were expelled.

Renewed restrictions should add an explicit **new restrictive-policy stage**, costs, loss of trust, diplomatic reaction and risk of civilian deprivation. Counterpart security can also impose restrictions or retain arms, with the same civilian-access rules. The historian identifies atrocities and displacement by different armed actors; record perpetrators/episodes and retain property/missing-person consequences rather than assign collective guilt. Do not encode operational details of harming civilians or create a “remove minority” efficiency reward.

## Two-province map, invasion readiness and ceasefire implementation choice

### What is actually supported

The local `tech_landing_craft_1` at `common/technologies/naval.txt:1791` grants naval invasion capacity100, one plan and division cap4 (plus its other bonuses). It is a real technology ID; this does not establish that granting that whole modern package to1970TUR is historically appropriate. `naval_invasion_planning_bonus_speed` and `amphibious_invasion` are real modifiers (`common/ideas/generic_ideas.txt:517–518`). They improve a real engine invasion; they do not create naval superiority, ships, troops, a plan or a beachhead.

Local `MD_can_reach_for_invasion` (`common/scripted_triggers/zzz_AI_naval_triggers.txt:1`) is a **coarse AI reach heuristic**, using neighbour/coastal/continent/navy counts. It is not an invasion-readiness certificate. `MD_naval_invasion_crossing_escort` at `common/ai_strategy/naval.txt:135` adds a real naval-invasion dominance strategy only when an invasion front exists. No verified local scripted effect was found that can faithfully issue a complete historical amphibious invasion plan with transports, battle execution and exact landing coordinates. Do not invent `naval_invade = yes` or infer that a war declaration creates a plan.

Readiness must inspect initialized1970 ships/convoys, actual land units, transport technology/capacity, an accessible launch port, operational fuel and route superiority/air cover as relevant to the engine. The prior user-deferred OOB work remains a separate implementation prerequisite. A forecast can show political intent and preparation while an actual military option remains unavailable until forces exist; do not pay free dividends for waiting behind an invisible prerequisite.

### Honest implementation alternatives

**Current-map compromise:** abstract the small first landing and July ceasefire/conference through explicit operation/negotiation state, leaving whole-state ownership unchanged. Describe the first operation as a limited **abstract foothold**, with limited costs/risks and verified external choices, not as a factual transfer of all146. A later actual war or an agreed/defeated final settlement may produce whole-state control. Clearly label that abstract operation as a simulation, separate from a real engine land war; do not display a fake active engine ceasefire.

**Full engine-war version:** authorize and implement finer northern/coastal/inland geography and the relevant historical armed forces first. Then first landing control, the Kyrenia–Nicosia connection, July ceasefire lines and August expansion can be determined by actual province battles. This is a map/OOB task outside the current design files.

**Coarse engine-war version:** a real TUR–CYP war can run on the existing map, but capturing7193 captures the whole northern-state abstraction. The UI must explicitly acknowledge that military compression; it cannot claim to reproduce the historical July/August territorial sequence. August escalation should not be scripted as mandatory conquest of145 merely because146 is already occupied. Preserve diplomatic offramps and let a human choose wider, explicitly alternate objectives.

The design must select one of these versions before runtime coding promises an accurate two-phase land campaign. All can share the political lead-up; they cannot share a pretend exact historical frontline.

### Why the generic conditional-peace helper is unsafe here

`CPD_sign_peace_deal` near `common/scripted_effects/02_peace_deal_effects.txt:807` turns controlled enemy states into **owned** states on the ceasefire path, then calls `CPD_execute_white_peace`. The latter at`:853` uses720day ceasefire truces, can leave factions, and can peace faction allies/subjects. This is a useful general deal system with a different contract; calling it for22July would risk legalizing the entire coarse northern state, ending unrelated wars, ejecting a NATO member and blocking the August phase.

A scenario-specific ceasefire adapter requires actual engine testing of war participants, ownership/control restoration, unit positions, supplies, access and redeclaration. `white_peace` alone can reset occupations; `set_truce` alone is not a real ceasefire inside an active war. Do not assert that restoring a controller at peace freezes a line until tested. An abstract current-map first phase avoids inventing a nonexistent temporary armistice API. Final accepted settlements can use real peace/transfer tools, with actors and the complete war context checked.

## NATO, NCY and modern-content integration traps

Local1970 alignment setup adds **GRE, TUR andENG to the same NATO faction** (`common/scripted_effects/IMP_1970_world_politics_effects.txt:1198–1199`). Greek officers assisting the Guard are not equivalent to sovereignGRE formally joining every CYP war. Model historical support via the budgeted mission/arms/diplomatic channels; do not call `add_to_war` against a same-factionTUR and assume the engine permits it. An explicit alternate GRE–TUR war needs a valid alliance-state transition chosen by the affected player. Greece's1974 withdrawal from NATO's integrated military command is not automatically the same as `leave_faction = yes`.

Keep British state812 outside automatic conquest/release/annexation effects. British base defence, humanitarian use and mediation are separate actor choices, not forced Turkish or Greek access to British land. A third party requesting ENG intervention cannot choose that option for a humanENG player.

For a laterNCY administration, **do not use blanket `CYP = { release = NCY }`**. All145/146 and812 containNCY cores in the source; release based on cores can have unintended scope. A tightly guarded `NCY = { transfer_state =146 set_capital = { state =146 } }` is supported by analogous named-tag creation syntax, but remains a whole-state postwar action requiring explicit political/economic/leader/OOB setup and a justified phase/result. KeepCYP cores/claims distinct from game ownership; game ownership is not universal international recognition. Use a1974/1975 administration cosmetic and dated leadership if created, not an1983TRNC name/state claim at the July landing. Do not give all later2000 history/ideas/technology to the revived country.

Existing `events/Greece.txt:4278–4368` has older Cyprus/Enosis war, equipment and state-transfer content; it includes large free weapons grants and annex-style declarations. Treat it as syntax evidence, not as the new balance/agency template. Gate incompatible modern events/decisions away from the1970 chain. `CYP_NCY_diplomatic_category` also exposes modern offshore-gas/EU decisions without a scenario gate, as recorded in EFFECTS_REVIEW.md.

## AI and verification contract

- **Updated multiplayer policy:** a genuine Cyprus AI slot always selects the historical Cypriot policy and eligible historical response for the current government/phase. This replaces the earlier suggestion of a mere historical bias. Historical policy selection does not select Greek sponsorship, Turkish intervention, British participation or the outcome of combat. When another player's choices remove a prerequisite, follow the defined historical-policy continuation rather than fabricate the missing foreign action. A human-controlled Cyprus retains its choices.
- Ordinary event `ai_chance` and focus `ai_will_do` can express AI preferences, but a strict Cyprus history profile must exclude eligible alternate political routes and define deterministic responses/priority where weights alone do not guarantee selection. Human responses are never selected by AI weighting. Reserved human country slots must not lose their pending political choices merely because the engine temporarily reports AI control after a disconnect; slot reservation and recovery are new session-management contracts, not a claimed native disconnect callback. See [MULTIPLAYER_ROLEPLAY.md](MULTIPLAYER_ROLEPLAY.md) for the future integration design.
- IndependentTC mandate can favour security/autonomy under direct threat, or shared institutions when credible safeguards and access exist; it is not a synonym forTUR's current ruling party. Same civilian-protection/abuse consequence rules apply to armed actors in either community.
- Ordinary war outcomes may defeat a player. Do not require every affected player to consent to a hostile act, but provide visible triggers, costs, threats and counterplay. Conversely, no player can force another human to pay aid, accept an ultimatum, ratify a constitution, join a war or approve a ceasefire.
- Exercise peaceful success, failed coup, successful coup then reversal, ultimatum compromise, actual intervention, rejected ceasefire, Geneva success, Geneva failure with renewed attack refused, and wider alternate war. Include repeated/stale event, actor death/regime change, occupied/annexedCYP, absentGRE/TUR/ENG and save/reload cases.
- Test with emptyOOBs as a known unavailable-operation state, then with actual approved1970forces. Inspect all maps/control/ownership after every peace/release effect. Check that civilian damage/refugees are neither generated twice nor restored as free manpower; existing `imp_refugees_calc_refs` only handles contested states and is not a generic incident callback.
- Re-run the existing shared+active-party reward caps after new war modifiers. Crisis pressure should supply decisions and consequences, not accidentally add another permanent+20%organization package outside the already audited18%ceiling.

The mechanics research identifies real reusable language/API shapes and important map/peace constraints. It does not declare a working invasion plan, a tested temporary-ceasefire adapter or authored event network. Those remain concrete implementation tasks after the final campaign abstraction is selected.

## Audit of integrated `cyprus_crisis.py` / 295-focus JSON

The integrated22-node branch correctly distinguishes the1970 NationalFront from the laterEOKA-B stage and separates bounded `COLDWAR_CYP_1970_coup_pressure` from communal tension. Coup pressure is initialized45 once and every emitted delta is clamped0–100. This is political-pressure tuning, not a45% historical coup probability. The new **Armed Enosis Underground** starting spirit uses stability-0.03/PPfactor-0.05, both real keys. Alongside constitutional breakdown and Makarios's authority, the visible authored combination is stability **-10points** and PPfactor **-9%** before other laws/ideas. These are the final candidate's values; the earlier optional-network example in this document is a recommendation, not an additional spirit to stack.

Only two new crisis-focus modifier payloads exist: mainland-command dependence **foreign influence defence-4%**, and a **temporary180day defence+3%** preparedness outcome. There is no new permanent positive organization, research or construction package, so the previously audited permanent ceilings do not increase merely by adding this branch. Every new modifier key already exists. Every referenced war-contract label is declared in the JSON, but the contracts remain prose and new dispatchers/events must still be authored.

Three important executable gate sketches are present:

- `war_guarantors`: successful-coup OR unilateralEnosis OR grave-renewed-communal-violence flags; routine constitutional disagreement is insufficient.
- `war_july_ceasefire`: actual/explicitly abstract first-intervention-phase flag.
- `war_august`: Geneva-failed flag AND no implemented final-settlement flag.

Other sponsor, date, formation, military-readiness, civilian-transition and actor-state conditions remain in requirements/notes rather than executable gate code. In particular, the plot/putsch path cannot yet be described as protecting a Makarios player from selecting a coup against their own current presidency; it explicitly delegates that distinction to opposition/foreign event logic. `war_loyal_guard` must wait for an implemented command transition before applying its last pressure reduction/removing a pressure spirit. An unanswered Greek letter is not a completed withdrawal. This is a partial design sketch, not a runtime defect in files that have not yet been implemented.

### Required pacing adapter for July/August

The graph currently assigns10–35day durations to several reactive nodes. The historical15July coup→20July landing→22July ceasefire→Geneva→14August sequence cannot wait for those focuses to complete sequentially. Long focuses can prepare command reform and policy before the crisis; **actor responses and short conference deadlines must execute as events/short decisions independently of focus progress**. Corresponding tree nodes can display an unlocked option or record a resolved milestone.

There is a real supported milestone-completion effect: `complete_national_focus = ID` (`events/05_armenia.txt:10170`); block form with `focus`, `use_side_message`, `originator_name` occurs in `events/05_australia_events.txt:5354`. No local example was verified for a hypothetical `reward = no` parameter, so do not invent that option. Use a single payload owner and once-only outcome-applied flags: resolving the actor event records/applies the result once, and completing the milestone must not pay a second cost, deliver a second benefit or redispatch the same ultimatum. Guard callbacks against stale crisis instances and a changed outcome. This is a new adapter to author using a real engine completion effect, not an existing Cyprus framework.

### Authorable now versus military prerequisites

## Multiplayer roleplay integration policy

The follow-on design in `MULTIPLAYER_ROLEPLAY.md` takes precedence over earlier generic historical-AI suggestions. Turkey and Greece focus trees/events are deferred; this update specifies their future interface only. Assume the other sovereign countries are human-controlled, and distinguish a permanent AI slot from a reserved human country under temporary AI control.

The interface records **historical Cypriot policy**, **a foreign actor's chosen action**, and **the resulting world state** separately. A historical Cyprus AI is deterministic about its own eligible policy; it cannot create another player's sponsorship, war declaration, signature or payment. Lack of a historical prerequisite produces a historically consistent continuation, not a fabricated intervention.

`war_guarantors` should record the first substantive eligible GRE/TUR/ENG response in the live crisis. Receipt, opening the dossier, acknowledgements, asking for more time and silence do not satisfy that condition. Other guarantor channels stay open after completion. This is a historical milestone badge, not a three-country unanimity requirement or a new veto over independent foreign actions.

The badge completes once in the campaign. Subsequent crises keep their own actor replies, proposal versions and outcomes; they never reset the focus for another reward and never read an old completed focus as proof of a current agreement. Actual diplomacy and ordinary engine wars can continue without waiting for a focus timer or an unanswered third participant.

Permanent focus exclusions between conference/refusal and Geneva/remobilisation need review before implementation: negotiations can precede rejection or renewed hostilities. The same applies to an externally imposed coup after attempted officer negotiations. Record actual outcomes as independent milestones with explicit live-state conditions; retain irreversible exclusivity for genuine strategic programmes. Do not assume that forced completion safely bypasses all prerequisites/exclusions without engine tests.

The new event/decision adapter owns costs and effects once, then records the focus through the verified `complete_national_focus` syntax. A response timeout can expire an offer or mark no reply; it never creates a human's signature, funds, coup authorization or war. Reserved human slots hold binding replies during absence under an explicit session rule; no native disconnect-detection hook is claimed.
