# North Korea 1970 — implemented programme

Implemented after the user's instruction to code the approved v2 design, the screenshot corrections and the substantial national expansion. The user explicitly waived the previous focus-count limit. The original v2 approval documents retain their original concept-stage wording; this document records the installed result.

## Installed content

- **360 focuses** in 16 sections, including 162 additions to the previous 198-focus build. There are 415 prerequisite edges and 55 reciprocal exclusion pairs. Original IDs, rewards, icons, relative branch structure and dependencies are preserved. Lower political roots have moved down by different amounts to fit the extended upper sections; their internal offsets remain intact.
- **Six starting national spirits**, with the monolithic system tied to the governing WPK: The Monolithic Ideological System; Economic and Defence Construction; The Extended Seven-Year Plan; Power and Imported Inputs; An Uneven Civilian Supply; Juche and the Two Patrons.
- Political programmes for WPK (MD slot 4), Korean Democratic Party (5), Chondoist Chongu Party (7), cabinet/planning administration (13), military emergency government (22), Yan'an reform communists (2), industrial labour (3), and the Christian Federation (14). Kim Jong Il's seventeen-focus programme additionally requires the actual ruling leader. Party-bound spirits leave office with their party and return from the preserved earned receipt when it returns.
- Fifteen bilateral agreements across eight partners, with nine negotiated upgrades. The six new packages cover Chinese industrial exchange, agricultural supply and staff/maintenance training, and Soviet power engineering, fuel/transport and air-defence/staff training. They complement the existing nine agreements.
- Recipient accept/refuse decisions, 90-day offers and fresh submissions after refusal or expiry. Ninety-six country-event definitions provide stage-specific incoming, acceptance, refusal and closure notices. Accepted agreements have no arbitrary expiry.
- GDP-based expenditure, current-GDP weekly commitments, external-credit review and repayment choices, investment access, construction planning, supply-disruption mitigation and safeguards withdrawal.
- Historical death news on **8 July 1994**, with the approved restoration in a registered MD news frame. Kim Jong Il succeeds only when slot 4 and the actual ruling Kim Il Sung are still present. Alternative governments and unrelated replacement leaders remain in office. The death is announced once even if NKO has ceased to exist.
- English-only localisation; `COLDWAR_NKO_` identifiers and marked custom additions.

Main game files are `common/national_focus/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_focus.txt`, `common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_*`, the corresponding triggers/dynamic modifiers/decisions, and `events/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_*`. `tools/north_korea_1970/emission_manifest.json` lists all 31 installed outputs, including the six shared integrations, with hashes. There are 20 new script/interface files, four new English localisation files and one news texture.

## Mechanics and presentation

All 240 priced focus nodes calculate expenditure from current GDP through MD's native treasury helper. The screenshot follow-up increases original one-off GDP shares by approximately 25%, rounded to five decimal places; original recurring commitments are unchanged. The expansion has separately budgeted GDP prices. Focus duration remains the native focus `cost`, independently of the monetary reward effect. There is no cash-balance affordability gate. On an agreement proposal, each country's GDP price is saved separately; acceptance charges each saved amount exactly once, including when treasury is negative. The six new contract-proposal focuses have no separate upfront charge before consent. Previously saved offers and machinery principal are not repriced retroactively. Weekly commitments use current GDP × annual share ÷ 52 in the native expense aggregator. No long fractional budget literal or separate recurring treasury deduction is used.

Every one of the eighteen construction records pairs the building levels with the same number of extra slots in the same selected state: fifteen focus projects and three industrial-agreement factories. Ownership, control, coastal requirements where applicable, and building-type maxima are checked before execution. A valid secondary state is selected if the primary state is unavailable. No starting OOB, uranium production, mines or completed nuclear weapons are granted by this implementation.

Artillery programmes use `army_artillery_attack_factor`. Rocket artillery has its separately registered `modifier_army_sub_unit_SP_R_arty_Bat_attack_factor`. The armed-reunification route grants the existing `annex_everything` war goal against an independent South Korea; declaration remains the player's choice.

Agreement eligibility checks both countries, mutual peace and all four Imperia sanction classes in both directions. War or sanctions suspend both endpoint spirits and weekly charges. Refresh restores eligible spirits without repeating setup payments, buildings or slots. Upgrades replace the base spirit with the combined base-plus-upgrade values once. Withdrawing safeguards removes the domestic safeguards programme and both safeguarded-upgrade spirits, leaving only the original civilian cooperation package when otherwise eligible.

Actual disruption of an accepted Soviet industrial or Chinese border agreement causes one non-stacking 365-day production/research penalty. Replacement supplies now remove it for 0.50% of current GDP. Repeated refresh calls do not extend or recreate that same disruption.

New machinery accounts now record 1.875% of GDP as debt and equal proceeds, then spend those proceeds on procurement. Repayment uses the saved principal, capped by remaining aggregate debt. The two-year review also supports rescheduling for a 0.25%-GDP fee or suspension of repayment, with the approved credit penalties. Settling the account clears its exposure and temporary arrears penalties. The Credit Reckoning focus can open review early. The unsupported `clear_temp_variable` call reported by the user's last game log has been removed; the model now rejects that effect in loaded NKO scripts.

The planning unlock uses optional **advance-funding decisions for the fifteen construction focuses**. Each invokes the same guarded executor, price, grant and receipt as its focus. The player must still finish the focus to unlock its descendants, and completion cannot charge or grant the project a second time. These decisions change delivery timing; they add no extra buildings or modifier values.

The 1970 Kim Il Sung history expiry is held until 9 July so the scripted 8 July transfer can identify the real incumbent before the engine replaces him. The event's eligibility is a bounded calendar-day window using native relational date checks, plus a global once-only receipt. This avoids relying on ambiguous date equality or on a stale incumbent snapshot.

Two display-unit corrections preserve the approved effect values: `productivity_growth_modifier` is numeric, so foreign terms show values such as +0.06 without a percent sign. Flat `experience_gain_army` is daily experience, so the shared MD dynamic-tooltip formatter now uses vanilla's numeric `+=2` instead of `%1`. The percentage field `experience_gain_army_factor` is unaffected.

Normal focus descriptions use institutional text, with revised country- and programme-specific diplomatic descriptions. Technical routing, scenario checks and redundant predecessor receipts are hidden, while party, leadership, agreement and construction requirements have meaningful tooltips. Recipient decisions preview numeric charges and native spirits before their hidden guarded executor runs. The redundant GDP expenditure sentence is removed; monetary previews show the native Treasury effect. Both numbers in each building/slot reward are explicitly green.

The renewal interval is **180 days from each successful outgoing proposal**, including the original focus proposal. It is enforced in the shared sender trigger and executor by a per-stage timed flag, with native `days_re_enable = 180` on the renewal decision. The reply window remains 90 days. Early refusal, expiry, war or sanctions cannot bypass the remaining interval. Receiving countries may still answer valid pending proposals during that interval.

## Successor extension and historical grounding

The seventeen-focus successor section retains its original four-focus structure. The Supply Commission leads to a choice between Public Distribution and Tolerate Local Markets. Each has a distinct continuation, Priority Farm Inputs or Barter Channels, before joining at Industrial Repairs. Prioritize the KPA and Fund Civilian Recovery form a second exclusive choice, followed by Recovery Accounts. The national expansion adds Enterprise Returns, Local Discretion versus Central Allocations, and A Ministry Compact. All thirteen additions require WPK rule and the actual Kim Jong Il incumbent. They grant conditional programme modifiers, with no new construction grants or automatic foreign assistance.

The agricultural, machinery and procurement problems draw on contemporary observations: household plots, cross-border barter, faltering public distribution and shortages of fuel, fertilizer and spare parts are documented in the [FAO/WFP June 1998 mission](https://www.fao.org/4/w9066e/w9066e00.htm) and [November 1998 assessment](https://www.fao.org/4/X0449E/X0449E00.htm). The competing policies and numerical outcomes are gameplay proposals, not claims that each was an implemented historical reform. The recovery programme does not force the historical disasters or guarantee imported aid.

The public-distribution route offers agricultural output and stability at higher social expenditure and foregone revenue; its follow-up prioritizes farms over civilian construction. Local markets improve productivity and tax revenue at the cost of political direction and policing, followed by barter access with greater export commitments and external exposure. KPA priority improves organization and supply efficiency while raising personnel costs and delaying civilian construction. Civilian recovery improves agriculture and productivity while reducing immediate army organization. Industrial rehabilitation increases output and efficiency but consumes more fuel; recovery accounts improve efficiency and research with a larger administrative burden. Social spending uses MD's registered `social_cost_multiplier_modifier` and its native budget consumer.

The current full overview and all sixteen normal-scale branches are in `expansion/`, generated by `tools/north_korea_1970/preview.py` from emitted coordinates and the native bitmap title font. Connector routing is approximate. The earlier `followup/` images and audit record the previous screenshot-fix build, not the expanded tree.

## National expansion

The WPK programme now has 36 focuses, the economy 49, defence 40, Korean diplomacy 24, atomic policy 16, KDP 22, Chondo 22, cabinet 18, KPA government 18, Yan'an 16, labour 16, Christian civic government 16, general foreign policy 18, successor leadership 17, China 16 and the Soviet Union 16.

The economic continuation covers coal, chemicals, civilian workshops, mine safety, fertilizer, industrial water, electric versus diesel traction, freight, ports, distribution, housing, reserves versus rapid reinvestment, apprenticeships and public maintenance. It follows the existing Joint Ventures focus and therefore retains that predecessor's historical timing; it is a later industrial programme rather than a new 1970 entry. The WPK adds inspection, cadre rotation versus specialist appointments, ministerial training, cultural direction versus professional institutions, and service delivery. Each alternative governing programme receives eight more institution-specific focuses and a new exclusive policy choice.

Defence adds four independently investable service programmes, each with an internal exclusive choice and follow-through. Artillery contrasts massed gunfire with rocket batteries; vehicles contrast recovery battalions with common parts; air defence contrasts fighter instruction with dispersed bases; coastal readiness contrasts patrol refits with littoral exercises. Civilian nuclear continuation requires both an active civilian cooperation agreement and continuing safeguards. Research rewards are single-use bonuses in native categories, with no materials or finished technology grants.

China and the USSR can be cultivated in parallel. Their 16-focus programmes deepen economic cooperation before offering consent-based military instruction and exercises. Domestic continuations that depend on a contract require both its signed-active condition and its actual membership spirit; war or sanctions also remove those dependent programme modifiers, and eligible resumption restores them without repeating purchases. The sovereign Soviet standards option preserves its earned domestic policy independently of a suspended training contract. The exact recurring exercise programmes are negotiated alternate-history proposals, not claims of documented historical joint manoeuvres.

Primary/archival precedents and explicit historical boundaries are recorded in `expansion/HISTORICAL_SOURCES.md`, `PARTY_PRECEDENTS.md`, `DEFENCE_PRECEDENTS.md` and `DOMESTIC_PRECEDENTS.md`. Independent emitted-source reviews are `expansion/MECHANICS_REVIEW.md` and `LAYOUT_REVIEW.md`.
