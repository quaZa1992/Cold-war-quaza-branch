# North Korea Screenshot Follow-up Review

## Scope and evidence

Reviewed the emitted national focus tree, scripted triggers/effects, bilateral decisions and localization; `tools/north_korea_1970/current_design.json`; `docs/COLDWAR_NKO_1970_concept/followup/preview_manifest.json`; and both supplied PNG previews. This is a static source and preview review. The manifest identifies the native font (`hoi_16mbs`), a 147 by 20 title field and 165 by 128 card bounds on a 96 by 130 grid, but also records approximate connector routing and no in-engine render.

## Findings

- **Layout:** The overview shows the 13-focus Jong-il branch in the intended lower-right programme area. The normal-scale branch image is balanced and legible; the leader entry continues through a fork, a central recovery path and a second policy fork. The drawn joins correspond to the emitted focus prerequisites. Resolving all 198 manifest coordinates found 198 unique positions and no pair of card bounds intersecting at the stated grid and card dimensions. A check against the installed `hoi_16mbs.fnt` found no focus title wider than 147 units. The preview is a faithful coordinate/font review, while engine connector routing remains unverified.

- **Prerequisites and choice logic:** The four original succession focuses and nine new focuses total 13. `COLDWAR_NKO_jongil_settlement` has one prerequisite block with both cadres and cabinet fields, so either parent satisfies the entry. The new `COLDWAR_NKO_jongil_industrial_repair` likewise accepts either farm inputs or barter channels; `COLDWAR_NKO_jongil_recovery_accounts` accepts either the KPA or civilian recovery choice. The distribution/local-markets and KPA/civilian-recovery pairs are reciprocal exclusions. The emitted triggers preserve these OR joins and exclusions.

- **Party and leader ownership:** All 13 Jong-il nodes are marked with the WPK party slot and Kim Jong-il leader requirement. The display shows those meaningful requirements; `original_tag`, scenario and routing checks remain in hidden trigger logic. The focus completion helpers also recheck the actual leader and party before paying. New permanent programme modifiers are enabled only while the relevant earned receipt, WPK government and Kim Jong-il are present, so the policy effect deactivates when its institutional owner leaves office.

- **Once-only effects and cost preview:** Each focus completion helper checks its earned receipt, sets it before applying the effect, and exposes material costs and modifiers in `effect_tooltip` while the real helper runs in `hidden_effect`. The generated focus localization contains no `Expenditure:` rows. The nine new focus costs are 0.40–1.00% of GDP, emitted to five decimal places; their preview multipliers match the corresponding guarded execution helpers. The pre-existing focus one-off shares are increased by 25% within the validator’s stated five-decimal rounding tolerance. The machinery principal is 1.875% of GDP, rescheduling costs 0.25%, and mitigation costs 0.50%.

- **Bilateral proposal reuse:** The emitted foreign triggers, send helpers and resubmit decisions each contain 18 per-stage cooldown entries. Each `send_live` trigger rejects its matching timed cooldown flag; each successful send sets that flag for 180 days while setting the pending flags for 90 days; and all 18 renewal decisions specify `days_re_enable = 180`. This applies to initial focus proposals and renewed proposals. No weekly commitment inputs or weekly calculation were changed in the inspected generated paths.

- **Bilateral preview and settlement:** The proposer preview states the proposal and conditional acceptance, then previews both countries’ current-GDP effects; the recipient acceptance preview uses the saved GDP quotes. Acceptance settlement uses those stored quote variables and immediately calls the reconciliation helper, which replaces superseded base modifiers when an upgrade is accepted. The former long bilateral effects paragraph is no longer included. Building fallback selection prose remains, with both building quantities marked green (`§G...§!`) and no duplicated prose count for factory effects.

- **Modifier keys and scripts:** Most new dynamic modifier keys were confirmed in the installed MD 1.1.2 common files. The canonical agriculture spelling is `agricolture_productivity_modifier`; the salary/personnel, police-cost, trade and production modifiers have donor definitions/usages. The initial emitted version used the unsupported `welfare_cost_multiplier_modifier` on three focuses. The emitter and current design now use `social_cost_multiplier_modifier`, which the native MD money modifier definitions and `00_money_system.txt` consume. The rebuilt programme file contains the corrected values on `COLDWAR_NKO_jongil_supply_commission_programme` (0.04), `COLDWAR_NKO_jongil_distribution_programme` (0.06) and `COLDWAR_NKO_jongil_priority_inputs_programme` (0.04); the unsupported key no longer appears in the emitter or current design. Separately, the reported legacy `clear_temp_variable` cleanup has been removed from the domestic generator and emitted domestic effects/decisions. The current validator explicitly rejects that unsupported effect. Relevant generated script files are UTF-8 without BOM; the generated English localization files use a BOM.

## Limits

The unsupported welfare modifier key was corrected and re-emitted; the obsolete temporary-variable cleanup is absent from the generated domestic files. The refreshed `implementation_validation.json` records 273 source transaction cases, structural and encoding passes, and a byte-identical rebuild. HOI4 was not tested. The screenshots establish the supplied preview rendering, not HOI4 rendering; native Treasury and dynamic-modifier tooltip colors, actual daily cooldown behavior, decision cooldown interaction and fresh-game parser acceptance still require an engine run.
