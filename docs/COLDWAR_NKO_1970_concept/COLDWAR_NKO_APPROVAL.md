WPK is the historical ruling party. KDP and Chondoists are real controlled satellite parties; their independent governments are alternate history. Cabinet, army, unions and religious federation are institutions, not historically autonomous competing parties. The Yan'an route is an explicitly counterfactual exile-return/reform programme and uses no dead or purged leader. These distinctions are retained in the preview and research record.

The existing outlook system determines actual accession. A party does not need a ceremonial unlock focus after entering office. Its policies deactivate when it loses government and reactivate when it returns. Earned factories, debt, research bonuses already used and signed interstate obligations do not replay or vanish. The alternative programmes occupy separate coordinates; no hidden cards overlap one another.

## January 1970

The six proposed spirits represent the monolithic political order, Byungjin's defence burden, the extended Seven-Year Plan, power/import bottlenecks, uneven civilian supply, and Juche's balance between external patrons. Each has a defined improvement or replacement route. Their exact values are in the main catalogue and starting-spirit inspector.

Do not project the 1990s famine, a mature Songun system, or an operational nuclear arsenal into 1970. Kim Il Sung remains leader. Kim Jong Il's succession apparatus develops from 1974 and becomes public at the 1980 congress; those focuses build party authority rather than replacing Kim Il Sung early. The historical debt crisis is conditional on the player's external-credit policy. Late-period support disruption follows an actual cancelled/changed cooperation agreement, not a forced decision by another player.

## Accounting and foreign-player control

- All 67 nonzero focus-price entries are full one-time country expenditure. Charge once on successful completion. Treasury may become negative and native MD weekly borrowing may cover it; there is no added cash affordability requirement.
- Focus-funded buildings use full current MD project prices, real candidate state IDs and current ownership, control, capacity and coastal checks. The explicit price is the complete price; do not also call a purchase helper that charges a second time.
- The machinery loan books debt +7.5, treasury +7.5, and project expenditure −7.5 once. Net immediate project cash is zero. Its repayment debits cash and reduces only recorded outstanding principal, once. The incident offers repayment, a $2.000bn rescheduling with +1.00 rate point/−10% trade opinion for 730 days, or a moratorium with +2.00 rate points/−15% trade opinion/−3% stability for 730 days. Principal is never erased by refusal.
- Every interstate offer has a 90-day pending period, explicit acceptance/refusal, one-time signed receipts on both countries and sender feedback. The recipient sees numeric effects. No foreign player's territory, government, money, alliance or aid policy is changed unilaterally.
- Recurring costs are modifiers of native weekly MD budget components. Research projects retain the existing scaled weekly nuclear budget and material requirements. No free mine, uranium resource, reactor, fissile material or nuclear weapon is granted.

## Verification and approval boundary

Three economical agents independently researched history, measured MD geometry, and traced the supported effects. The concept graph has 167 nodes, 189 prerequisite edges and 30 pairwise exclusion links. Independent geometry checks covered the eight proposed government states, full 165×128 item bounds, 147×20 name boxes, resolved relative anchors and the completed overview/branch renders. All 167 short names fit the installed native font field; the shortened names have at most 140px advance.

The interactive preview uses genuine MD art and the measured 96×130 grid. Browser typography and connector routing approximate the game. Static renders have been visually reviewed. A browser-runtime check could not run on this host: no local Chromium executable was available and the in-app browser could not reach the local preview server. JavaScript syntax is checked separately; HOI4 loading, hovers, save/load, live branch refresh and multiplayer balance remain untested.

This is a source-backed concept. New NKO agreement, incident, state-selection and party-reconciliation adapters are designed but unimplemented. The effective start's universal MD economic/law modifiers still need a final in-engine baseline capture before installing the six new country-specific spirits; the native 2000 add_ideas block is not a 1970 baseline.

No loaded game file has been changed. Approval is requested for this complete design and numerical proposal before implementation.
