# Final v2 mechanics audit

**Result:** source-backed concept; new adapters remain unimplemented. The generated structural check reports 189 nodes, 212 edges, 33 exclusions, 10 building records and no data errors, but explicitly says `engine_tested: false` (`structural_validation_v2.json`). This audit validates the concept contract against the checked-in MD/Imperia sources; it does not certify script parsing or runtime behavior.

## Verified against the current concept

- **GDP prices:** all 75 priced focus nodes use `cost_gdp_share` from 0.20% to 2.00%; no zero-priced node advertises an expenditure line. Their `cost`/`days` focus fields are not cash prices. `budget_contract` specifies current payer GDP, saves payer-specific quotes on an offer, accepts MD automatic borrowing, and does not add an affordability gate. This has a native precedent: `common/national_focus/00_generic.txt:624-626` calculates a treasury change from `gdp_total` and calls `modify_treasury_effect`.
- **Credit ledger:** the 1.50%-GDP machinery loan books equal recorded principal and treasury proceeds, then spends that same quote on the factory; net cash from the financing and procurement lines is zero. Repayment is limited to saved outstanding principal. That is consistent with the native paired treasury/debt helper at `common/scripted_effects/mdcw_cold_war_effects.txt:4672-4679`. Rescheduling is separately priced at current GDP. The delayed incident must settle the same saved principal once, without charging the separate project price again. Its 1.25-point risk premium applies only while principal is outstanding and clears after full repayment.
- **Buildings and slots:** all 10 building records (nine focus rewards plus the Soviet agreement grant) pair each level with one shared slot in the same explicit state set. The state lists are NKO states 601, 602, 603, 1199, 1200 or 1201, and every focus reward says to test state ownership/control and placement/type caps before charging. Both building construction and `add_extra_state_shared_building_slots` are native primitives; examples include `common/national_focus/00_generic.txt:1755-1761` and `common/decisions/05_GRE_decisions.txt:1641-1647`.
- **Specialist military effects:** the artillery programme uses `army_artillery_attack_factor` for towed and self-propelled artillery and the registered `modifier_army_sub_unit_SP_R_arty_Bat_attack_factor` for rocket artillery. MD classifies `Arty_Bat` and `SP_Arty_Bat` as artillery, while `SP_R_arty_Bat` is a distinct rocket battalion (`common/units/MD_land_units.txt:1256-1376`); the rocket battalion key is registered in `common/units/unit_modifiers/unit_modifiers.txt:112-115`. The audited set of 51 numeric modifier keys across focus, spirit and agreement data all have a checked-in source occurrence. No artillery focus relies on a general army-attack bonus in place of the artillery-specific effect.
- **Korean war route:** `armed_reunification` now requires KOR to exist and be independent and NKO to be at peace with KOR. `confederation_offer` carries the same current-state guards plus the signed exchange agreement. `annex_everything` is a defined MD wargoal type (`common/wargoals/00_invasion.txt:107`); it is awarded as a goal, not an automatic declaration or annexation.
- **Treaties:** all nine agreement records have recipient accept/decline decisions, 90-day pending terms, shared accepted/pending/initial-grant receipts, fresh-quote resubmission after refusal or expiry, and stage-specific upgrade receipts. The written active predicate covers mutual war and each of diplomatic, financial, economic and military sanctions in either direction. That matches the paired sanction arrays written by Imperia (`common/scripted_effects/IMP_sanctions_support_effects.txt:6-49`). The specification correctly requires reconciliation at both endpoints and on changes, monthly and load; a dynamic modifier `enable` condition alone cannot be assumed to reattach an engine-removed spirit. The native weekly expense aggregator is available at `common/scripted_effects/mdcw_cold_war_effects.txt:3996-4008`; agreement-specific GDP conversion and its single active-only contribution still need a new adapter.
- **Agreement anti-replay:** saved quotes are paid only on recipient acceptance; refusal, expiry and invalidation clear pending state; grant receipts prevent replaying money, factories, slots or research bonuses; upgrades require an active base and a recipient decision. Sender resubmission is documented for declined/expired stages without replaying completed-focus rewards. The concept appropriately does not require current treasury cash before GDP-priced expenditure.
- **Nuclear safety boundary:** no focus grants uranium output, a mine, or a finished weapon. Nuclear research bonuses are one-use bonuses for reactor/weapon categories. `COLDWAR_nuc_economy_allows` is an existing scripted trigger (`common/scripted_triggers/COLDWAR_nuclear_power_triggers.txt:4-12`) that checks the developed/emerging Imperia economy modifiers. Keep actual resource development behind the paid survey/development system and the existing nuclear programme and material checks.
- **Leadership and safeguards guards:** the regenerated design now places the exact native `Kim Jong-il` ruling-leader check in all four Jong Il focus `eligibility_notes`. The safeguards focus explicitly produces the `COLDWAR_NKO_safeguards_valid` receipt and the NPT stage requires it, the bilateral civil-atomic receipt on both tags, and an active base predicate. Kim Jong-il availability is documented as a proposed country-lifecycle receipt, not an existing API. These are coherent concept contracts; their producers and periodic reconciliation still need implementation.
- **1970 bootstrap:** `bootstrap_contract` now explicitly attaches the NKO tree and six starting spirit packages only under the 1970 NKO startup guards, assigns the monolithic spirit to WPK, reconciles without replaying rewards, and excludes starting OOB and uranium. This is necessary because NKO history has no `add_ideas` in its `1970.1.1` block (lines 5-57); the later `2000.1.1` block begins at line 59 and contains historical 2000 ideas at line 898. The bootstrap contract correctly does not treat those as 1970 starting spirits.
- **Succession:** the requested Kim Il Sung death date remains 8 July 1994. The news contract says to transfer only when the Kim regime held power immediately before death and to snapshot the incumbent before the current leader expiry. `Cabinet Continuity` is an intentional political policy, not a competing ruler path; I have not treated its change from the former settlement idea as a defect.

The Western-credit narrative and structured data agree on a 1.50%-GDP principal and matching procurement outlay; there is no separate cash-price field on the focus, so the ledger does not count a second project payment.
