# IMPERIA COLD WAR: Unilateral settlement encroachment

Corrected on 28 October 2026 following the user's rejection of the negotiated
foreign-presence design. The current Israel module has 399 focuses, 182 decisions
and 77 events. The four regional settlement focuses retain their stable IDs and
positions but now establish funded unilateral footholds. No Palestinian or other
host-country acceptance, refusal or termination decision is emitted for them.

## Current effects per foreign-controlled area

| Recipient | Programme and numeric effects |
| --- | --- |
| Israel | Israeli Settlement Holdings: office productivity +4%, agricultural productivity +3%, social costs +1%, bureaucracy costs +1%, police costs +2%, personnel costs +1% |
| Actual controller | Settlement Encroachment: agricultural productivity -3%, bureaucracy costs +2.5%, police costs +2%, stability -1% |
| State | Israeli Settlement Encroachment: infrastructure construction speed -10%, local factory access -5%, local supplies -5% |
| Initial funding | Israel treasury -1% of annual GDP, once per area. The affected country receives no payment. |

The affected country receives exclusively adverse modifiers. The additional
Palestinian West Bank/Gaza programme remains separate and cumulative when
Palestine exists and retains ownership or a core. Its precise two-tier effects
and selected funded compensation relief are in `PALESTINIAN_PRESSURE.md`.
For example, a Palestinian-controlled foreign foothold initially combines the
generic host's -1% stability with the Palestinian programme's -2%, for -3% in
that area. The totals in the Palestinian document are that separate programme's
cap, not a cap on these additional generic host effects.

Bureaucracy, police, social and personnel costs modify native weekly MD spending
components. Agricultural and office productivity have actual economy readers.
The state's factory-access modifier changes native industry access; it does not
rewrite MD's raw-building GDP formula. No workforce-size or population change is
introduced. The shared Israeli workforce route remains capped at +15% added.

## Execution and continuity

Completing a regional foreign foothold focus finances and activates the presence
directly if a different existing country controls that state. The execution
recomputes 1% of current Israeli GDP and checks actual funds and bankruptcy before
charging. It never transfers money to the controller. The paid receipt prevents
repeated initial investment on duplicate execution or later reactivation.

The actual current controller bears the country modifier, even if legal ownership
differs. Control changes disable the old controller's modifier immediately; the
daily reconciliation removes it and installs the burden on the new controller.
Israeli control suspends this foreign footprint, leaving the separate controlled
construction and occupation systems applicable. An already funded foothold still
counts as initial Palestinian settlement pressure during Israeli control, so
that burden does not disappear merely because control changed. Returning to foreign control
restores the funded footprint without a fresh payment. War, embargoes and
sanctions do not grant a settlement-specific permission veto or erase its costs.

Israel can end its own foreign settlement funding and later restore it through
the retained Israeli decision. Host governments cannot remove this programme
through an invented acceptance or termination menu. Existing ordinary diplomacy,
war and territory systems remain available. No automatic war, land transfer,
core or timed uprising is introduced. Optional settlement-freeze and compensation
negotiations are separate mitigation policies; they never authorize establishment.

Old paid active compacts retain their paid/active receipts and take the new
unilateral terms without replaying transfers. Old unanswered proposals are
cleared, remain unfunded and can be financed through Israel's re-establishment
decision. Former host decision files are emitted as empty, disabled compatibility
stubs; their old acceptance helper grants nothing. No old host grant is replayed.

The focus and Israeli re-establishment decision show Israel, controller and state
effects, plus the separate Palestinian effects where relevant, before execution.
Their prospective arithmetic uses dummy and temporary variables only. Native
grant and treasury providers occur only inside the guarded actual transaction.

## Period basis and explicit RP extension

[UN Special Committee report A/8389/Add.1, 9 December 1971](https://unispal.un.org/pdfs/0E7ECB64C366F509852569F400684BF3.pdf),
paragraphs 11-16, records statements and press reports of government-backed
settlement expansion, fencing near Deir el-Balah and Kiryat Arba/Hebron. It also
records conflicting Israeli and other representations. These are precedents
for unilateral land commitments, local opposition and administrative costs,
not measured modifier percentages or an account of host permission.

[ICRC, June 1968](https://international-review.icrc.org/articles/application-geneva-conventions-particularly-fourth-territory-occupied-israel)
documents the contemporary occupied-territory humanitarian and administrative
setting. The historical settlement examples concern Israeli-occupied areas.
Representing a funded foothold while another country effectively controls the
whole HOI4 state is an explicit multiplayer RP abstraction requested by the user,
not a claim that this exact mechanism existed under restored sovereignty in 1970.
All numeric weights are design proposals. Period settlement departments and the
Settlement Division provide institutional flavour; no 1974 movement is invented
as an existing 1970 institution.
