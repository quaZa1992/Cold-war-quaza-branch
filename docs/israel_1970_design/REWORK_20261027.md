# IMPERIA COLD WAR: Israel 1970 policy and focus-tree rework

Implemented on 27 October 2026 for multiplayer roleplay. The loaded module has
389 focuses, 170 decisions and 73 events. Numerical values below are deliberate
game-balance proposals, not estimates of the causal effects of historical policy.

## Player choices

The Suez military branch and the civilian territorial programme now open
independently. A cabinet can initiate ceasefire negotiations before choosing an
escalatory or defensive posture, renew a rejected proposal, and implement a later
accepted ceasefire after having prepared forward defences. No American initiative
is invented as a mandatory diplomatic event. The legacy `ISR70_rogers` ID is kept
for save references; its visible name is **Open Ceasefire Negotiations**.

Egypt retains the actual acceptance/refusal option. Its response does not select
Israel's broader territorial programme. The settlement survey and civil-status
review also open directly from the
territorial agenda. A negotiated settlement freeze or equal civil status does
not require first choosing permanent maximalist retention.

The Alignment leadership conference and
development-town hearing can be chosen without waiting for 1974 or 1971. Existing
party checks, named alternative leaders and separately formed later parties remain.

## Economic strength

The six compatible shared programmes add **+15% available workforce in total**:

| Programme | Additional workforce |
| --- | ---: |
| Wages, Prices and the Histadrut | +2% |
| Vocational training | +3% |
| Development towns | +2% |
| Immigration absorption | +4% |
| Universities | +2% |
| Public health | +2% |

This changes the native MD employable-workforce pool without creating population.
No new workforce-size penalty is introduced. Native laws and the overall native
+50% workforce-modifier cap still apply.

The shared industrial route adds +18% civilian-factory productivity. A compatible
shared export route adds +36% office productivity; factory equipment output and
electronics productivity growth also have distinct improvements. These effects
are consumed by MD's tax and GDP calculations, rather than being cosmetic labels.
Programmes retain appropriate education, welfare, personnel, financing or
external-market trade-offs. The authored reward layer revises 158 existing focus
specifications, including 80 party choices, 23 specialised follow-ups and 55 shared
programmes. Territorial projects and the roleplay corrections are additional.

## Settlement programmes

The survey selects one actual Palestinian-owned state controlled by Israel:
the West Bank or Gaza. It changes neither ownership nor cores. Three approaches
remain available as explicit alternatives:

| Programme | Main benefit | Main commitment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Negotiated freeze | Municipal factory and infrastructure, local supply, a separately funded administrative compact | Palestinian consent, public-service spending and a ceiling on expansion |
| Limited Nahal sites | Agricultural district, +6% national agricultural productivity, water and road supply, perimeter fort and repair capacity | Capital cost, administration, police cost, local opposition and a binding perimeter |
| Civilian settlement network | Agriculture and workshops, +10% agricultural productivity, +3% factory output, connected roads, forts and repair capacity | Subsidised services, bureaucracy, security spending and a choice between compensation/limits and unilateral continuation |

After unilateral continuation, the selected area can receive up to **three paid
extensions**. Each grants one agricultural district, one infrastructure level,
one land-fort level and three shared building slots, together with +4% national
agricultural productivity, +1% factory output and +5% local supply. Each also adds
+2% social costs, +2% police costs, +5% local garrison requirement and four points
of local resistance target. The phases cost 1.5%, 1.8% and 2.1% of current annual
GDP, take 270, 300 and 330 days, and each costs 25 political power.

Only the selected area can receive the phases. Each phase requires the previous
one to have actually completed. A funded binding ceiling prevents further starts
and invalidates outstanding extensions. Loss of ownership/control or another
required delivery condition refunds the stored reservation. Country operating
ideas suspend with loss of the relevant territory and restore without repeating
construction or payments. The administrative compact's national saving now follows
the same territorial condition as its local benefit.

The compensation ceiling is an Israeli-funded domestic property-compensation
programme. It does not transfer money to the Palestinian government's treasury.
The separate administrative compact does transfer its agreed 0.3% GDP payment.
The three revised settlement offers show complete prospective numerical terms in
their focus and acceptance-option displays. These displays use isolated tooltip
rows and temporary display arithmetic; they call no treasury/debt provider and
grant no actual idea, state modifier, resistance effect or building.

All policy project construction now receives one shared slot per physical building
level, including infrastructure and province forts, before construction. Explicit
slot-only grants remain additional land capacity. Native infrastructure/province
capacity, staffing, legal ownership and actual control are checked separately.

The operating costs are native MD spending modifiers applied to its weekly
budget. GDP-share setup payments are one-time annual-GDP-based capital amounts.
No annual cash amount is inserted as a weekly budget literal.

## Historical and mechanical references

- [Bank of Israel, Annual Report 1970](https://www.boi.org.il/22165?culture=en),
  published 31 May 1971: contemporaneous public finances, investment and employment.
- [Public sector chapter](https://www.boi.org.il/media/qt3dypbe/pe_7.pdf):
  compulsory loans, restrictive fiscal measures and defence-related spending.
- [Domestic investment chapter](https://www.boi.org.il/media/mmqpmnq5/pe_5.pdf):
  infrastructure, industrial investment and competing development demands.
- [FRUS, Dayan discussions, 14 November 1972](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76v23/d319):
  open bridges, procurement and the cost/technology trade-offs of domestic aircraft.
- [Yigal Allon Centre biography](https://en.yigal-allon-centre.org.il/biography/):
  the Allon Plan influenced policy without becoming an officially adopted whole.
- [Mekorot institutional history](https://www.mekorot-int.com/our-history/):
  carrier and irrigation infrastructure as the basis for later expansion.
- [Technion computer-science history](https://www.technion.ac.il/en/blog/2019/11/50-years-for-technions-faculty-of-computer-science/):
  a faculty founded in 1969 supports a period electronics/technical-training route.
- [IAI institutional history](https://www.iai.co.il/about/history/): ELTA, Gabriel
  and Arava support distinct air, naval and electronics programmes.

Settlement geography, agricultural-security institutions and public investment
motivate the programme design. A settlement alone is not treated as a national
combat bonus. National agriculture benefits represent the associated technical,
marketing and cooperative networks; local roads and security remain state-scoped.
Nahal/Mafdal-era institutions are used for 1970 rather than treating Gush Emunim,
founded in 1974, as an existing 1970 movement.

Main MD 2.0.3 Generic, Russia and Israel were inspected as mechanical references.
The native `ISR_ariel_industrial_zone` combines slots, Treasury -2 billion and
small-business opinion +5 at focus cost 5. The 1970 projects adapt this mixed
asset/cost/constituency grammar using GDP-scaled costs and period geography.
Native variable-delta tooltips follow Russia's programme presentation. Actual
reader evidence is recorded in `rework_validation.json` and the authored helper.

## Layout, validation and provenance

Layout revision 8 repositions all 389 focuses in national, political and policy
terraces. Party branches remain visible. The preview consumes the compiled
coordinates, icons, titles and prerequisite groups; independent parsing checks
all 389 installed positions and 432 visible prerequisite connections. The
interactive inspector includes locked alternatives, branch navigation and zoom.
It is a source-derived external approximation, not a live HOI4 screenshot.

Checks passed: content/reference validation, reward validation, full layout,
950 policy checks, 46 policy-map checks, 302 bounded rework checks and an idempotent
rebuild. The rework evaluator interprets emitted transaction AST with explicit
fixtures for native staffing, capacity, province control and fiscal providers.
It covers duplicate starts/callbacks, changed GDP, exact refunds, phase bounds,
control loss and one-time finance. Browser results are in `preview_validation.json`.
No native HOI4 hover, save/reload or multiplayer/rehost test was performed.

An old canal-restoration check attempted to restore obsolete building history.
The compiler now preserves the independently authored native 1970 economy while
still comparing every non-building history/geography field to the retained
snapshot. The approval/source snapshot itself was not changed. No state boundary
or population allocation was added by this rework.
