IMPERIA COLD WAR
Rhodesian commando field uniforms and FN FAL material variant

The two skinned meshes use the project's DDR_1970_Kampfgruppen infantry bind pose, native selection hull, face, hands and locator set. The DDR and Millennium Dawn contributors retain their existing credits. Individual mesh and texture authors and any separate permissions are unknown. See IMPERIA_COLD_WAR_NOTICE.md at the mod root.

The trunk and limbs are adapted from the MakeHuman Community CC0 basemesh. The male young and muscular macro targets and young_lightskinned_male_diffuse2.png are MakeHuman system assets under CC0 1.0. Credits include Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier. The anatomy was retargeted, cropped, texture-baked, reskinned and reduced for the MD infantry animation rig. Sources: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/tree/master/src/mpfb/data and https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html . Licence: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/blob/master/LICENSE.md .

The field boots, olive socks, shorts, field cap, shirt treatment, brushstroke pattern, skin shading and FAL paint treatment were made for this project using the supplied photographs as visual references. Neither photograph is included. The unpainted FAL, its unchanged geometry, original albedo detail and its normal and specular maps come from the installed Millennium Dawn-derived gfx/models/units/weapons assets. Authorship of the individual FAL assets and separate permission are unknown. Map-scale textures use DirectXTex block compression and a full mip chain. Existing underlying rights remain applicable.
