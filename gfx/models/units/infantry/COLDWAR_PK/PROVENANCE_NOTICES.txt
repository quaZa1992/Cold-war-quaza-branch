IMPERIA COLD WAR

Blue helmet and uniform for the Peacekeeping Contingent.

Model, skin, base texture and animations are adapted or reused from the bundled Millennium Dawn
generic rifle infantry actor (geo_generic_soidler.mesh, geo_uniform_generic_diffuse.dds,
geo_gear_generic_diffuse.dds, infantry.gfx and units_infantry.asset). Individual artist credit
and any separate licence for the donor art are unknown. See the mod and upstream notices.

The project recoloured the clothing, helmet straps, UN lettering and armband, and made
light, medium and deep skin-tone variants while preserving the same shared geometry.
The donor mesh, weights, skeleton, attachment nodes, normal maps, specular maps and
animations are reused. Contemporary UN reference, for colour and markings only:
https://ask.un.org/faq/209615
https://peacekeeping.un.org/en/news/un-peacekeeper-helmet-returns-to-south-lebanon-40-years-after-the-establishment
