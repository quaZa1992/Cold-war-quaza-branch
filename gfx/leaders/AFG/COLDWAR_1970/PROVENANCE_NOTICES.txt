IMPERIA COLD WAR

Afghan photographic leader portraits, 14 November 2026

The user requested downloaded historical photographs restored/colorized with the
clarity of a modern smartphone photograph. These five DDS files are AI-assisted
photographic derivatives, not unmodified archival photographs. Source identity,
age, pose and period clothing were retained; the background was simplified.
Originally monochrome images were colorized; the two color sources were restored.
No visible watermarks or project marks were added to the portraits.

Restoration: OpenAI ImageGen, directed and integrated with OpenAI Codex for this
project at the user's request. Export: centered proportional crop, Lanczos resize
to the native MD 156 x 210 dimensions, opaque DXT5/BC3 DDS without mipmaps.
Exact prompts, per-file sources, dates, licences, credits, bindings and SHA-256
hashes are recorded in the adjacent asset_bindings.json. Original downloads,
restored PNG masters and export code are retained in the external development
workspace named in that record. The usual E: development drive was unavailable.
