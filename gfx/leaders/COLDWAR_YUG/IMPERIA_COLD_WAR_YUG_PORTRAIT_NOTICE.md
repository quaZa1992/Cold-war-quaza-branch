IMPERIA COLD WAR

# Yugoslav leadership photo provenance

These images are used in the federal government and successor formation scripts.
Institutional photographs represent their regional seats or a relevant federal institution.
The Herceg-Bosna image is a Mostar civic landmark. The federal defence image shows JNA generals Veljko Kadijević and Blagoje Adžić.
Documented sources with unknown permissions are tracked separately from clearly licensed works. This record does not grant republication rights.
