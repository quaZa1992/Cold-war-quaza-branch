IMPERIA COLD WAR
Czechoslovak source-restored leader portraits

Historical source photographs retain their original authorship, licences and any other rights. A project marker is not a claim of ownership. Sources with unknown or reserved rights are not claimed to be freely redistributable. No publication is requested or performed.
AI restorations reconstruct photographic detail and color; they are not unmodified archival originals. The source age is preserved. Classic fallback files remain source-based monochrome unless the original is in color. All source URLs, credits, rights, exact prompts, local operations, dates and hashes are in asset_bindings.json.
