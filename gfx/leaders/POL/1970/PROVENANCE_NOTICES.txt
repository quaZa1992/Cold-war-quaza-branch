IMPERIA COLD WAR

Poland historical leader portraits, 15 November 2026

The following 33 portraits were restored from identified historical photographs using the built-in image_gen tool. The edited images use natural modern photographic colour and clarity. Face identity, source age, historical clothing and pose guided the restoration. Fine detail, inferred colours, simplified backgrounds and outpainted areas are synthetic reconstructions. The sources have different dates; these are not photographs all taken in 1970.

Technical output: 156 x 210 pixels, uncompressed 32-bit A8R8G8B8 DDS with opaque alpha, matching the existing Gomulka and Gierek texture format.

Original photo rights remain with their authors or rights holders. A project marker or AI restoration establishes no ownership of the source. The source catalogue licence is recorded per image. For CC BY-SA sources, the restored derivative is distributed under that same licence, including its attribution and share-alike terms. Where separate permission or licensing could not be established, it is expressly recorded as unknown; this record does not grant such permission.
