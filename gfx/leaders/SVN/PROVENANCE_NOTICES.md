# South Vietnam portrait provenance

24 DDS textures, 156 x 210 pixels, DXT5 / BC3. Created with built-in ImageGen from the referenced photographs, except the two user-approved representative stand-ins. Original Thieu and Tran Van Huong textures are unchanged.

Colourisation, reconstructed detail and inferred colours are interpretive AI modifications. These are not untouched archival photographs. The project identifier does not establish ownership or replace source licences. Unknown information is identified explicitly.

Nguyen Gia Hien and Vo Van Tai use fictional representative stand-ins approved by the user because authenticated photographs could not be located. They must not be presented as authenticated likenesses.

Tran Ngoc Lieng uses the 1966 family photograph credited to Fkhuong at English Wikipedia (photographer described by the uploader as their father). The derivative is distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ . The attribution, source, secondary reference and all modifications are retained in PORTRAIT_PROVENANCE.json.

Known period limits: Trinh Dinh Thao uses a photograph dated approximately 1945 to 1950, retained rather than the still earlier 1929 alternative. Nguyen Ngoc Loan uses the identified 29 April 1976 photograph. No exact 1970 capture date is claimed for these sources.
