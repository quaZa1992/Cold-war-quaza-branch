# IMPERIA COLD WAR — Cabinet briefing artwork

The procedural briefing sheets `briefing_territory.dds`, `briefing_internal.dds` and `briefing_contacts.dds` are generated by `tools/colonial_affairs/security.py` with the existing paper, typography and DDS-writing helpers in `tools/colonial_affairs/ui.py`. Their PNG counterparts in `tools/colonial_affairs/ui_preview/` are previews of the same artwork.

Source: the local Security Affairs interface and its procedural paper generator. Layout, headings and English ministerial prose were revised by Codex at the user's request. Original author or credit for the pre-existing paper generator: unknown. Applicable licence or redistribution permission for the pre-existing material: unknown. No external photograph, insignia or newly downloaded artwork is used. Windows Arial Narrow and Impact fonts are rendered through the existing local font helper; redistribution permission for font-derived artwork has not been independently established.

Modifications: dedicated cabinet briefing sheets, a tinted introductory panel with a muted red filing margin, three ruled sections with political and institutional headings, and spacing for native game text and adjacent controls. The artwork contains no ownership watermark. Native organisation, territory and support views retain their existing artwork.
