Production
Each transparent PNG was framed to the native visible footprint, reduced with Lanczos resampling and exported into a 72 x 88 straight-alpha BGRA8 DDS. The right portrait was mirrored to face the gauge. The black-and-white portraits and metal rims were generated together.
