IMPERIA COLD WAR: Greek candidate portrait icons.

These 33 x 30 DDS tiles are technical crops of the corresponding Greek portrait assets. The donor pattern and dimensions were inspected in Sheep's existing US Republican and German CDU candidate decisions, `interface/imp_leader_decision.gfx`, and `gfx/interface/decisions/leaders/decision_merkel.dds`. The donor artwork itself is not copied into the Greek tiles. Original donor artist and licence are unknown.

Per-person photograph sources, original authors, licences or unknown permissions, restoration prompts and modifications are recorded in `tools/greece_1970_leaders/ASSET_NOTICE.md`, `generation.json`, `source_inventory.json`, and the earlier `tools/eastern_mediterranean_1970/SOURCES.md`. The project marker establishes no rights over third-party photographs. The new photographic restorations use the built-in image_gen tool; these icon exports only crop and resize the completed masters.
