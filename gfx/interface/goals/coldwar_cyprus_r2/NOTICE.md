IMPERIA COLD WAR: Cyprus focus emblems

Three original raster emblems were generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool for this project: the Republic of Cyprus, peacekeeping and communal safeguards, and civilian command of the National Guard. No third-party frame pixels were copied into the generated images.

Original author/source: OpenAI image generation for this project. No separate third-party licence or ownership claim is made. Exact prompts, original output locations, workspace sources, hashes and mechanical conversion steps are recorded in `tools/cyprus_1970_rework/art_sources/sources.json`.

Modifications: proportionally downscaled inside a transparent 100 x 78 native symbol canvas and converted to uncompressed RGBA DDS. Transparency is retained. No visual repainting, background substitution or watermark was added. The local `native/` copies similarly fit existing MD icons without changing their original files; source paths, hashes, modifications and unknown original authors/permissions are recorded in `tools/cyprus_1970_rework/NATIVE_ART_COPIES.json`. Their original rights and credits remain applicable.
