# IMPERIA COLD WAR: Israel 2.2 native focus artwork packaging

These are technical copies of existing registered focus artwork. Each DDS has a companion `.provenance.json` recording its exact source registration, source file and pixel hashes, dimensions, alpha bounds, crop, proportional downscale, padding and output hashes. No source image is overwritten and no motif is repainted.

Original individual artists and separate asset licences or redistribution permissions are unknown where the inherited source records do not identify them. Existing Millennium Dawn, Imperia, Cold War and applicable native asset credits and rights remain separate. See the root `IMPERIA_COLD_WAR_NOTICE.md`. Project identification establishes no new rights.

The GFX packaging follows the existing Israel focus-art generator and native MD shine pattern, including its `gfx/FX/buttonstate.lua` native shader alias. The two opposing shine animations reuse `gfx/interface/goals/shine_overlay.dds`. Runtime rendering and shine behaviour still require an engine check.
