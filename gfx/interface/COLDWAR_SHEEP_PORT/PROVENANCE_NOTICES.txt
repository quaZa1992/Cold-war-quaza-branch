IMPERIA COLD WAR
Artwork for the 1970 British, Indian, South African and Rhodesian focus programmes

The source is AnimeMinecraftSheep's Sheep-Work branch of Imperia-MD/mdcoldwar at c518aed537fcee0abce893beded3cab1c609d0f8.
ASSET_PROVENANCE.json names each image, source path, SHA-256 and dimensions. The associated national focus, idea, event and decision sprite definitions also reuse existing Millennium Dawn and game assets by reference. AnimeMinecraftSheep supplied the programme integration. Underlying photographers, artists and third-party rights are those credited by the original records. For assets without a recorded artist, the original author is unknown. Where no asset-specific licence or permission is recorded, it is unknown. The project marker identifies this integration.

Original texture bytes are preserved except for the country-event variants named in the manifest. Those variants place the supplied picture inside the 180 x 150 photo inset of the existing Millennium Dawn 217 x 163 political_deal.dds frame, preserving the frame and alpha pixels. The numbered flag plates are scaled proportionally to retain all choices. Original national focus icons, leaders, flags, national spirit images, news images and decision illustrations retain their source treatment.
