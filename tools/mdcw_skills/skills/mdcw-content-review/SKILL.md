# IMPERIA COLD WAR — Historical content and reward review

Read `AGENTS.md` and [references/content-contract.md](references/content-contract.md). Resolve the requested country, mechanic, design and runtime sources. Trace current generators and emitted content. Respect an approved design and distinguish review findings from new balance proposals.

Establish the January 1970 institutions, parties, economy, conflicts and international position. Research and record precedents for new or substantially changed national policies and selectable spirits. Mark alternate history and game balance as such. Do not transplant modern parties, organisations, borders or religious paths into a country to fill MD categories.

Compare complete reachable alternatives: timing, setup and recurring costs, requirements, benefits, drawbacks and compatible stacking. Identify strictly dominated routes, repeated small gifts, bureaucracy-only filler and bonuses lost at a cap. Reward variety is a judgment grounded in the programme, not a focus-count quota or a mandatory penalty on every focus.

Use relevant normal main MD trees as mechanical and presentation benchmarks, adapting scale to 1970 and the country. Review emitted English descriptions alongside actual effect previews. Political narrative belongs in descriptions; Effects show concrete native signed costs, modifiers, spirits, assets and unlocks. A named spirit or later event reference does not replace full prospective bilateral terms.

Check treasury helpers for internal charges before adding another payment. For every building level, require the extra shared slot in the same state and transaction, including infrastructure and forts. Verify ownership, control and native capacity independently. Check actual research/doctrine categories, dynamic-modifier lifecycle and full cross-country consent.

Keep party branches visible with exact native ruling-party execution gates. Require membership spirits for organisation branches, including their entry nodes, and preserve reachable admission. Review actual icon/texture classes and complete native event frames, not registration alone.

Report supported findings with examples and current path/line evidence. Do not enforce upstream's generic-tree minimum, ten-event quota, regional commander stereotypes or modern start-date conventions. Use the existing focus/modifier/layout skills when those specific tasks are involved.
