# IMPERIA COLD WAR — Historical commanders and advisers

Read the active checkout's `AGENTS.md`. Establish the real tag, January 1970 identity, character provider, history file, OOB, recruitment effects, and any owning generator. Cosmetic names do not establish native tags. Preserve other bookmarks and existing succession systems.

Research who held or could credibly hold the requested posts at the start date. Record historical sources, service, rank, appointment dates and uncertain identities. Distinguish an actual commander from a plausible alternative. Do not recruit a later celebrity into a 1970 command merely because modern MD contains their portrait.

Check existing character IDs and recruitment before adding anything. Count active divisions and ships in the loaded 1970 OOB, rather than all units in every bookmark. Upstream leader-count formulas and regional skill ranges are calibration examples, not quotas or a substitute for historical ability. Do not add ships, advisers, leaders or traits outside the requested scope to satisfy a template.

Use current native character and adviser schemas. Trace trait modifiers to actual providers. Keep IDs stable, use the appropriate `original_tag` eligibility convention where identity should survive a civil war, and recruit each character in the correct dated history or event. Follow the existing project leader system for political office holders.

Resolve portrait sprites, textures, dimensions and rights against a live donor. Keep required project markers and script UTF-8 without BOM. New English display names and descriptions follow project standards; historical proper names may retain their original spelling.

Verify definition-to-recruitment closure, duplicate IDs, post eligibility and 1970 recruitment. Report real people and dates, skill/trait choices, files changed, missing portraits and the checks actually run. Source validation cannot prove portrait rendering or native recruitment.
