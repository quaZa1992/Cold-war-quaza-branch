# Asian and Middle Eastern starting research: January 1970

This register accompanies `asia_profiles.json` and covers 97 country-history tags. Country-level caps are original, conservative game-design mappings, not historical statistics or a uniform equipment year. The cutoff is 1 January 1970, including capabilities demonstrably established by the end of 1969. A year printed on a technology node is not sufficient evidence of national possession.

Imported aircraft, tanks and ships can support a bounded equipment-family ceiling when an established national institution operated and maintained them. They do not copy their supplier's machining, electronics, strategic weapons or complete research catalogue. Isolated research facilities and prototypes do not establish nationwide industrial adoption. Local regional-release profiles are explicitly low-confidence scenario estimates; central government research institutes, nuclear facilities, foreign garrisons and later insurgent arsenals are not mechanically inherited.

## Specific conservative decisions

- Japan qualifies for industrial robotics because domestic licensed robot production began in 1969. F-104J and Type 61 remain separate from the later F-4EJ and Type 74. Ohsumi launched in February 1970, so no successful orbital capability is awarded at the January cutoff.
- China has a real nuclear and medium-range missile programme, but no January 1970 satellite, operational nuclear submarine, civilian power station or mature ICBM. Its older conventional equipment and uneven industry do not receive the whole 1965 catalogue.
- South Korea's ADD and licensed M16-production agreement are later than the cutoff. Its early research reactor and imported modern aircraft are assessed separately from industrial self-reliance.
- India has licensed aircraft and missile work and operating BWR power reactors at Tarapur in late 1969. The game has no corresponding BWR node, so that achievement is recorded through nuclear foundations, experimental-power and licensing knowledge without falsely awarding the Magnox/PWR families. No 1974 nuclear test or later orbital satellite is backdated.
- Research-reactor states receive at most the compressed `nuclear_technology` foundation unless a distinct power capability is supported. This node unlocks enrichment-facility construction in the mod, but grants no building, stockpile, weapon, warhead, money, enrichment operation or completed special project. TRIGA and IRT operation alone do not justify `reactor1`.
- Israel's advanced but opaque 1969 nuclear position is recorded from contemporary declassified sources. No public arsenal, thermonuclear capability, commercial nuclear-power station, completed weapon project or warhead stock is asserted. WEIZAC/GOLEM establish scientific computing, not domestic integrated-circuit manufacture.
- South Vietnam's Dalat reactor entered extended shutdown in 1968. Its scientific knowledge is not erased, but the North Vietnamese tag does not inherit the facility or operating experience before reunification.
- Basic gun antiaircraft ceilings stop at 1950. The game's 1965 antiaircraft node is MANPADS and is not awarded merely because a country owned towed guns. Ordinary F-104/MiG-21/Mirage III families use the local 1955 early-supersonic tier; third-generation F-4-level families are reserved for evidenced F-4 operators.

## Consulted sources

Retrieved using the web tool on 30 October 2026. Links identify the consulted primary institution or first-hand research publication. Summaries below are paraphrases; no source document, image or long quotation is redistributed. Individual domain thresholds, exclusions and low-confidence regional estimates remain project judgements. Later publication dates are used only for retrospective facts about pre-cutoff events.

- **jap_nuclear**: [JAPC: overview and nuclear commissioning chronology](https://www.japc.co.jp/english/corp_data/an_overview.html). Tokai commercial operation began 25 July 1966; Tsuruga began only 14 March 1970.

- **jap_air**: [Japan MOD Miho: displayed aircraft histories](https://www.mod.go.jp/asdf/miho/about/tenjiki/index.html). F-104J introduction in 1962 is eligible; F-4EJ deliveries in 1971 are outside the cutoff.

- **jap_tank**: [Japan GSDF 7th Division tank museum](https://www.mod.go.jp/gsdf/nae/7d/siryoukan.html). Type 61 accepted April 1961; Type 74 remained a prototype before its September 1974 acceptance.

- **jap_robot**: [Kawasaki: robot division history](https://global.kawasaki.com/en/corp/profile/division/robot/history.html). Domestic licensed Unimate robot production began in 1969.

- **jap_elec**: [Toshiba: corporate chronology](https://www.global.toshiba/ww/outline/corporate/history/chronology.html). 1959 transistor television, 1963 nuclear turbine generators and 1967 automatic postal reading demonstrate engineering depth.

- **jap_space**: [JAXA ISAS: OHSUMI mission](https://www.isas.jaxa.jp/en/missions/spacecraft/past/ohsumi.html). First successful Japanese satellite launch was 11 February 1970, after the cutoff.

- **chi_arms**: [China NDRC: seventy years of national defence industry](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/xwdt/gdzt/cl70zn/201909/t20190930_1197756.html). Official chronology dates local jet manufacture to 1956 and tanks to 1959.

- **chi_nuclear**: [China Atomic Energy Authority: nuclear industry chronology](https://www.caea.gov.cn/n6760341/n6760359/c6827876/content.html). Fission test 1964 and thermonuclear test 1967; nuclear submarine launch December 1970 and commission August 1974 are later.

- **chi_space**: [IDCPC: Two Bombs, One Satellite chronology](https://www.idcpc.gov.cn/english2023/dzwk/zgkx/202307/P020230717028605489828.pdf). Nuclear missile test October 1966; first satellite April 1970, outside the cutoff.

- **kor_add**: [Korean Agency for Defense Development: history](https://www.add.re.kr/board?menuId=MENU02989&siteId=). ADD was founded 6 August 1970; prototype weapons and the Lightning project followed in 1971.

- **kor_rifle**: [National Archives of Korea: M16 production agreement](https://www.archives.go.kr/next/newsearch/showDetailPopup.do?rc_code=1310377&rc_rfile_no=200302907993&rc_ritem_no=&sitePage=0-0-0). The bilateral M16 manufacturing agreement belongs to 1971, not January 1970.

- **kor_nuclear**: [KAERI: history and research achievements](https://www.kaeri.re.kr/board?menuId=MENU00734). TRIGA Mark II completed March 1962; Mark III completed May 1972.

- **nko_nuclear**: [IAEA Director General: safeguards in the DPRK](https://www.iaea.org/sites/default/files/gc/gc66-16.pdf). Soviet IRT research reactor completed in 1965; the later reprocessing and power-reactor programme is excluded.

- **tai_air**: [Taiwan Air Force museum: F-104 service history](https://air.mnd.gov.tw/EN/News/News_Detail.aspx?CID=89&ID=13587). US-supplied F-104A/B delivery began May 1960; operation does not establish local fighter manufacture.

- **raj_hal**: [Hindustan Aeronautics: official history](https://hal-india.co.in/our-history). MiG-21 licence-manufacturing organisation established 1963 and HAL amalgamation 1964; later Ajeet, Jaguar and Cheetah expansions are excluded.

- **raj_mig**: [HAL: Nasik aircraft manufacturing division](https://hal-india.co.in/production-unit-details/aircraft-manufacturing-division-nasik). Nasik established 1964 for licensed MiG-21FL and K-13 manufacture.

- **raj_nuclear**: [BARC: research reactors](https://www.barc.gov.in/reactor/index.html?tab=4). CIRUS research reactor commissioned July 1960; nuclear research does not prove a January 1970 weapon.

- **raj_power**: [NPCIL: operating power plant chronology](https://npcil.nic.in/content/302_1_RAPP.aspx). Tarapur Units 1 and 2 BWR commercial operation began 28 October 1969.

- **raj_space**: [ISRO: official timeline](https://www.isro.gov.in/ISRO_EN/Timeline.html). Sounding rocket in 1963 and ISRO formation August 1969 precede first satellite Aryabhata in 1975.

- **pak_nuclear**: [Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission: PARR-I golden jubilee](https://paec.gov.pk/Documents/PakAtom/P%201-8%20Nov-Dec%202015.pdf). PARR-I first criticality was 21 December 1965.

- **pak_power**: [Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission: nuclear power](https://paec.gov.pk/NuclearPower/). KANUPP first criticality was August 1971, after the cutoff.

- **pak_arsenal**: [Senate of Pakistan: POF industry briefing](https://www.senate.gov.pk/en/news_content.php?id=Njk1OQ%3D%3D). POF established in 1951 to supply military ammunition; this supports a limited local ordnance base.

- **afg**: [ILO: Remuneration in Afghan industry, 1969](https://researchrepository.ilo.org/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Remuneration-in-Afghan-industry/995219345202676). Contemporary industrial survey supports distinguishing a small modern sector from nationwide advanced industrial capacity.

- **nep**: [Nepal Third Plan, 1965-70](https://nepalindata.com/media/resources/bulkuploaded/Third_plan_1962-65_eng_july17.pdf). Contemporary planning document supports basic development ceilings, not mature high-technology industry.

- **bhu**: [Bhutan Ministry of Infrastructure: departmental history](https://moit.gov.bt/about-us-1/). Road project began in 1959; Bhutan Engineering Services organised in 1961 around the first major highway.

- **sri_air**: [Sri Lanka Air Force: first jets arrive](https://www.airforce.lk/tamil/history_pages.php?pages=the_first_jets_arrive). Jet Provost handover was in 1959; this is a trainer capability, not a supersonic combat-aircraft industry.

- **ind_arsenal**: [Pindad: corporate profile](https://pindad.com/profil-perusahaan). Local army industrial organisation Pindad dates from 1962 and has earlier ordnance roots.

- **ind_reactor**: [Indonesia BRIN: first research reactor retrospective](https://www.brin.go.id/news/99874/kilas-balik-reaktor-pertama-di-indonesia). Bandung TRIGA inaugurated February 1965; its 1 MW uprating came only December 1971.

- **ind_imports**: [US State FRUS: procurement of Soviet materiel from Indonesia](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76v20/d328). Retrospective official record documents Soviet aircraft, missiles and naval equipment inherited from the Sukarno period and a later shortage of Soviet replacements.

- **sin_army**: [Singapore Army: official history](https://www.army.gov.sg/about-the-army/history/). Artillery and additional infantry formations in 1967, armour in 1968 and commandos in December 1969.

- **sin_navy**: [Singapore Navy: early years](https://www.navy.gov.sg/about-us/history/early-years/). Initial 1967 force used two wooden ex-Royal Navy boats; first Independence patrol craft commissioned only December 1970.

- **sin_econ**: [Singapore EDB: aerospace industry origins](https://www.edb.gov.sg/en/business-insights/insights/how-singapores-aerospace-industry-took-flight.html). Air Defence Command founded in 1968; initial industry concentrated on infrastructure and servicing.

- **may_air**: [Royal Australian Air Force: historical journey](https://www.airforce.gov.au/about-us/history/our-journey). Ten Sabres handed to Malaysia on 1 October 1969; this is a recent imported subsonic fighter capability.

- **bru**: [Brunei Ministry of Defence: RBAF profile](https://www.mindef.gov.bn/rbaf-profile/). Brunei Malay Regiment formed May 1961; sovereign independence came January 1984.

- **phi_arsenal**: [Philippines DBM: Department of National Defense organisational mandate](https://www.dbm.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/OPCCB/opif_2008/OPIF/dnd-FINAL.pdf). Government Arsenal legally created 1957; a legal mandate alone is not evidence of completed January 1970 modern production lines.

- **phi_nuclear**: [PNRI: nuclear science milestones](https://www.pnri.dost.gov.ph/images/publications/201-New-Milestones-Flyer.pdf). PRR-1 research reactor reached criticality in 1963.

- **sia_nuclear**: [Thailand Office of Atoms for Peace: official overview](https://www.oap.go.th/en/overview-of-office-of-atoms-for-peace/). National atomic institutions and imported research-reactor project existed by the early 1960s.

- **svn_nuclear**: [Dalat Nuclear Research Institute: official history](https://nri.gov.vn/en/history.html). TRIGA critical February 1963; extended shutdown from 1968 to 1975. The site was in South Vietnam, not North Vietnam.

- **mon**: [IMF: Mongolian political and economic history](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/display/book/9781557752079/ch001.xml). Industrial expansion in the 1960s relied heavily on Soviet and CMEA connections; later mining projects must not be backdated.

- **brm**: [World Bank Archives: Burma economic conditions, 1966](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/077011226823305122-0560011966/original/WorldBankGroupArchivesfolder1076809.pdf). Contemporary record describes weak growth, low investment and nationalisation constraints.

- **hkg**: [Hong Kong LCSD Memory: electronics industrial history](https://www.hkmemory.hk/en/collections-postwar_industries-manufacturing_industries-electronics-industry_focus.html). Electronics factories began in 1959 and radio/component assembly grew in the 1960s; local integrated-circuit production is dated 1979.

- **mac**: [Macau Virtual Library: manufacturing development](https://www.macaudata.mo/macaubook/book219/html/06701.htm). 1960s manufacturing concentrated on light export goods; later electronics growth is not backdated.

- **mld**: [UK Parliament Hansard: Maldivian economy, 26 March 1964](https://hansard.parliament.uk/Commons/1964-03-26/debates/e7024ae2-8e3f-4fdc-b5cf-0f90f2b6df9c/Economy). Fishing, imported engines, a boatyard and a Male power supply existed; they do not imply a national industrial grid.

- **isr_air**: [Israel Aerospace Industries: official history](https://www.iai.co.il/about/history/). Arava first flight in 1969; the firm's chronology dates Nesher first flight to 1970, so it does not justify an operational Nesher at the cutoff.

- **isr_compute**: [Weizmann Institute: WEIZAC history](https://www.wisdom.weizmann.ac.il/weizac/weizac.html). WEIZAC first calculation October 1955 and successor GOLEM launch in 1963 establish domestic scientific-computing experience.

- **isr_nuclear**: [US State FRUS: Israeli nuclear programme, July 1969](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76v23/d38). Contemporary declassified assessment establishes an advanced opaque programme; no automatic public arsenal, thermonuclear weapon or warhead stock is inferred.

- **isr_reactor**: [UN Secretary-General report: Israeli nuclear armament](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-184756/). Soreq research reactor operated from 1960 and Dimona from 1963; neither is a commercial electricity station.

- **per_nuclear**: [UN Treaty Series: Iranian research-reactor assistance, 1967](https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/UNTS/Volume%20614/volume-614-I-8865-English.pdf). 1967 agreement specifies a five-megawatt training and research reactor at Tehran University.

- **per_air**: [US State FRUS: Iran military sales, December 1968](https://history.state.gov//historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v22/d322). Discussion of existing and additional F-4 purchases supports imported Phantom operations, not domestic fighter production.

- **irq_nuclear**: [IAEA: research reactors in Arab countries](https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/Publications/PDF/P1575_CD_web/datasets/papers/A11%20Takriti.pdf). Iraqi IRT research reactor entry is dated 1967.

- **tur**: [Turkish Presidency of Defence Industries: institutional history](https://www.ssb.gov.tr/en/who-are-we). MKE reorganisation in 1950 and later emphasis on import dependence and licensed manufacture justify a limited ordnance sector, not the later aerospace/electronics complex.

- **jor_armor**: [US State FRUS: Jordan arms sales, 1965](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v18/d139). Basic M48 supply was discussed for the 1965-69 programme; newer variants were expressly unavailable.

- **gulf_air**: [UK National Archives via AGDA: aircraft and missiles, 1968](https://agda.ae/en/archives/TNA/FCO/8/664). Contemporary UK correspondence documents Lightning delivery arrangements for Kuwait and Saudi Arabia.

- **oma**: [Oman Ministry of Defence: Royal Air Force history](https://mod.gov.om/Home/RAFO). Provosts and Pioneers in 1959 and Strikemasters ordered in 1967; 1970 helicopter and transport purchases and 1975 Hunters are excluded.

- **sipri**: [SIPRI Yearbook 1968-69: arms-transfer register and charts](https://www.sipri.org/sites/default/files/SIPRI%20Yearbook%201968%E2%80%9369.pdf). Contemporary arms-transfer research confirms differentiated imported aircraft, armour and missile holdings, not the transfer of complete manufacturing industries. Used chiefly for comparative cross-checks.

- **rice**: [IRRI: breeding history of IR8](https://ricetoday.irri.org/changing-the-world-with-seeds-the-breeding-history-of-ir8/). IR8 released November 1966, with earlier trials and subsequent regional adoption; India and Philippines are eligible early adopters.

- **pak_rice**: [World Bank: agricultural research and IR8 diffusion](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/568651468162563497/pdf/multi-page.pdf). West Pakistan planted IR8 in 1967 and expanded rapidly by 1969.

- **ham**: [US Treasury: Hamas historical background](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/js672). Hamas was formed in 1987; its later organisation and weapons cannot be backdated to 1970.

- **nus**: [UN Security Council: Al-Qaida in Iraq entity narrative](https://main.un.org/securitycouncil/es/sanctions/1267/aq_sanctions_list/summaries/entity/al-qaida-in-iraq). Al-Nusra emerged publicly in January 2012; modern-group military capacity is outside the scenario cutoff.

- **pkk**: [Japan Ministry of Justice: PKK historical country information](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/930005666.pdf). The PKK was formed in 1978; its later organisation must not be backdated.

## Coverage and uncertainty

- **raj_carrier**: [India Ministry of Defence, PIB: Vikrant history](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=98029) confirms the first Vikrant's British acquisition and commission on 4 March 1961 and its catapult/angled-deck operations. The existing early carrier hull therefore includes the basic `aircraft_carrier_engineering` flight-deck module foundation. This is bounded operating and maintenance knowledge, not a claim that India could independently construct an aircraft carrier in January 1970.

- **jap_nuclear_foundations**: [JAEA Nuclear Science Research Institute: historical facilities](https://www.jaea.go.jp/english/04/ntokai/decommissioning/index_01.html) records JPDR power operation from 1963, a nuclear-fuel metallurgy facility established in 1957, and ceramic fuel research including uranium-dioxide fabrication at a facility established in 1959. [JAEA Tokai: MOX fuel development](https://www.jaea.go.jp/04/ztokai/summary/center/plutonium/) records the first fuel-development facility opening in 1965 and plutonium-fuel research beginning in January 1966. These support `nuclear_technology`, `mdcw_experimental_power_reactors_1954` and `mdcw_reactor_fuel_fabrication_1953` as the foundations behind the already evidenced Magnox node. They do not imply completed commercial fuel self-sufficiency: the later MOX production facility started in 1972 and reprocessing operations were later still. No building, enrichment operation, stockpile or completed special project is granted.

- **jap_licence**: [Mitsubishi Heavy Industries Technical Review: aircraft and rockets](https://www.mhi.com/jp/technology/review/sites/g/files/jwhtju2326/files/tr/pdf/e401/e401036.pdf). The manufacturer identifies licensed F-86F and F-104J production in the early postwar aerospace sector. A narrow early-supersonic airframe and J79-era engine allowance is used, rather than a full later aircraft-module package.
- **jap_mat**: [Japan MOD: Type 64 anti-tank guided missile](https://www.mod.go.jp/pco/niigata/HP/01-data/mokei/mokei-e/mokei-e23.pdf). Wire-guided Type 64 MAT supports the early heavy guided-antitank family, not a generic later light-antitank package.
- **tai_nuclear**: [National Tsing Hua University: institutional history](https://archives.lib.nthu.edu.tw/history/history04.html). Completion of the research reactor in 1961 justifies the nuclear-foundation node only.
- **chi_electronics**: [Chinese Academy of Sciences: first integrated circuit](https://english.cas.cn/Special_Reports/CAS_75th_anniversary/Key_National_Needs/202410/t20241014_691705.shtml). Domestic integrated-circuit work is documented in 1965; the profile grants the foundation without pretending nationwide modern computer-industry adoption.
- **redeye_restrictions**: [US State FRUS: Redeye proliferation review](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76ve04/d215). The later review still records Israeli denials and Iranian, Saudi and Turkish requests or restrictions. These are not January 1970 operators merely because later equipment lists include Redeye.

- **chi_df3**: [US Army ODIN: DF-3](https://odin.t2com.army.mil/WEG/Asset/DF-3_%28Dongfeng_3%29_Chinese_Intermediate-Range_Ballistic_Missile). Operational deployment is dated 1971. The profile therefore retains the earlier missile foundation and excludes the DF-3-generation IRBM1.
- **pak_hangor**: [Associated Press of Pakistan: Navy War College Hangor commemoration](https://www.app.com.pk/domestic/navy-war-college-celebrates-53rd-hangor-day/). The official naval event identifies Hangor as the first French Daphne submarine, commissioned in 1969. This supports bounded service knowledge, not a granted boat or domestic submarine production line.

The detailed core records cover 44 sovereign states, colonial/protected territories or explicitly identified 1970 predecessor settings. The remaining 53 profiles are regional, conditional scenario, alternate or later-organisation release tags. Their modest local baseline is intentionally below the parent national research system unless local industrial evidence warrants more. No claim is made that each hypothetical polity had its own historical 1970 research establishment. Low-confidence cases are conservative and open to replacement by more specific evidence.

Asian former Soviet republics, Soviet regional tags, Cyprus and Northern Cyprus are assigned to the European/Soviet companion register. Australia, New Zealand and Pacific tags are assigned to the root coordinator's Americas/Oceania register. Turkey and the Iranian, Arab, Anatolian and South Asian conditional release tags are included here.

## Source and compiler verification

The 97 Asian profiles were evaluated read-only through the live `build.selections` function for all eight combinations of No Step Back, By Blood Alone and Gotterdammerung, for 776 country/DLC cases. After the documented Japanese nuclear-foundation correction, no selected technology was missing an explicit `dependencies` entry and no selected technology contradicted its declared DLC `allow_branch`. The later Indian carrier-deck foundation uses the already selected naval-engineering prerequisite. Exact technology IDs, cutoff years, duplicate grants and UTF-8 without BOM checks also passed. This is source/model verification, not a live-engine or multiplayer test.
