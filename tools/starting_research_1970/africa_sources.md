# Africa: January 1970 starting research evidence

Cutoff: 1 January 1970. Accessed 30 October 2026. This is a source register and a record of conservative game-design inference, not a claim that a nation's entire population or every unit shared one technical standard.

The machine-readable country records are in `africa_profiles.json`. Its 89 African country and regional/faction tags are evaluated separately. Modern non-state tags are marked as absent at the cutoff; their local release floors are deliberately modest. The files do not change borders, political histories, research slots, armed forces, equipment stocks or building levels.

## Interpretation

Domain years are selected technical ceilings. They are not a permission to research every node dated earlier than that year. An imported fighter permits a narrow operating/support baseline at most; it does not automatically confer an engine, radar, missile or aerospace production industry. The same rule applies to foreign-managed mining enclaves, ships, research reactors, computers and colonial garrisons.

Where no well-dated country-specific equipment or institutional evidence was found, the profile uses a low-confidence 1940s/1950s floor and explicitly withholds specialist branches. A regional release cannot add together the full research catalogues of several parents. The numbers are design judgments constrained by evidence, not historical measurements.

The two nuclear exceptions are Egypt and South Africa. Their operating research reactors support the existing mod's compressed `nuclear_technology` foundation only. This does not grant a plant, a bomb, enrichment production, a completed nuclear special project, or the power-reactor technologies. Ghana's early nuclear institutions and abandoned reactor plans do not establish an operating reactor.

## Primary and institutional sources

- [US Congressional Record, 29 October 1969, pages 32094-32095](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GPO-CRECB-1969-pt24/pdf/GPO-CRECB-1969-pt24-1-1.pdf). A period public record reproducing equipment assessments for 1968-69. It supports Algeria's and Egypt's Soviet-supplied armor, air and maritime operating capabilities. These inventories are not evidence of local manufacture. Procurement quantities are not copied into the mod.
- [US Air University Review, January-February 1968](https://www.airuniversity.af.edu/Portals/10/ASPJ/journals/1968_Vol19_No1-6/1968_Vol19_No2.pdf). Period analysis of African air power and logistics. Its Nigeria/Biafra examples distinguish imported aircraft from the very small local service establishments. The discussion of poor ground communications informs cautious support baselines.
- [R. K. Baker, Tropical Africa's Nascent Navies, US Naval Institute Proceedings, January 1969](https://www.usni.org/magazines/proceedings/1969/january/tropical-africas-nascent-navies). Contemporary original analysis used to distinguish small coast/river patrol services from larger navies, and to withhold ocean-going research from inland states. OCR contains errors; the technology mapping stays broad and conservative rather than reproducing questionable ship counts.
- [IMF Staff Papers, Economic Integration in Central and West Africa, January 1969](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/024/1969/001/article-A005-en.xml). Period regional analysis supports the distinction between small processing sectors, foreign-supported technical enclaves and diversified industrial economies. It does not establish a specific modern machine-tool or electronics capability for every state.
- [World Bank, Statistical Indicators of Levels of Industrial Development, 31 May 1969](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/481941468765888079). Period industrial comparison used as a general cross-check. The conservative country ceilings remain an inference; this is not a source for military-system research.
- [United Nations: former Trust and Non-Self-Governing Territories](https://www.un.org/dppa/decolonization/history/former-trust-and-nsgts). Institutional chronology for territorial status and independence. It prevents Angola, Mozambique, Cape Verde, Sao Tome, Guinea-Bissau, Namibia and other later-independent states from being described as independent January 1970 research actors. No inherited metropolitan technology bundle follows from colonial status.
- [United Nations: current Non-Self-Governing Territories](https://www.un.org/dppa/decolonization/en/nsgt). Used for the distinct St Helena and Western Sahara administrative context.

## Country-specific checks

- [IAEA-hosted Egyptian Atomic Energy Authority presentation on ETRR-1](https://conferences.iaea.org/event/330/contributions/28311/). ETRR-1 first criticality is placed in 1961. This supports a research foundation only.
- [CIA, Foreign Computer Capabilities, 1969, declassified original hosted by the National Security Archive](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/media/22627/ocr). The UAR section describes imported machines and no known domestic computer development. Egypt receives the basic-computing foundation, without advanced integrated-circuit or computer-industry grants.
- [South African government, Defence Related Industries White Paper, 1999](https://www.gov.za/documents/white-papers/south-african-defence-related-industries-white-paper-01-dec-1999). The historical section identifies the 1968 development/production organization and the earlier domestic production network. Later localization and strategic programs are excluded from the cutoff.
- [Necsa: SAFARI-1](https://www.necsa.co.za/safari-1/). The operator dates commissioning to March 1965, supporting research-reactor knowledge without a bomb or power-reactor grant.
- [CSIR, The First 40 Years, D. G. Kingwill, 1990](https://researchspace.csir.co.za/bitstream/10204/6789/1/Kingwill_1990.pdf). Institutional history dates the CSIR ZEBRA computer to 1958. Later numerical-control/software discussion is not backdated wholesale.
- [Eskom heritage: 1963-1972](https://www.eskom.co.za/heritage/history-in-decades/escom-1963-1972/). Institutional history distinguishes initial long-distance links and a 1969 Cape connection from central integration in 1972 and the completed national grid in 1973. South Africa therefore retains regional-grid knowledge without automatic 1965 dispatch mastery. The same source dates imported-power links and prevents later Swazi/Mozambican connections being backdated.
- [Ditsong National Museum of Military History: Atlas Impala](https://ditsong.org.za/en/military-equipment-named-after-animals-the-atlas-aermacchi-impala-aircraft/). Local licensed production began before the cutoff. The Mk II is later, so only the early jet/airframe foundation is used.
- [Arms Procurement Commission, Armscor witness statement](https://www.justice.gov.za/comm-sdpp/hearings/witness-statements/ws-debeer-p001-048.pdf). Government inquiry evidence confirms the licensed Impala line from 1966. Later Oryx and Mirage F1 examples do not justify January 1970 grants.
- [A. Wessels, The South African Navy during the years of conflict, University of the Free State journal article](https://www.ufs.ac.za/docs/librariesprovider20/history-documents/articles-documents/the-san-during-the-years-of-conflict-1095-eng.pdf?sfvrsn=9c56fb21_0). Original archival historical research dates the first Daphne commissioning to 24 July 1970. The country receives no operational submarine branch at the cutoff.
- [University of the Free State, history of South African frigates](https://journals.ufs.ac.za/index.php/jch/article/download/398/382/757). Original historical research supports the pre-cutoff surface-escort exception; it does not justify later strike craft or submarines.
- [Volta River Authority: Akosombo Hydro Plant](https://vra.com/our_mandate/akosombo_hydro_plant.php). The operator places original construction in 1961-1965. Ghana receives a selective large-hydro exception, without a complete advanced-industrial package.
- [IAEA safeguards symposium: Ghana's historical experience](https://conferences.iaea.org/event/150/contributions/5356/). Ghana had no research reactor when signing the NPT in 1968; later acquisition must not be projected backwards.
- [Zambezi River Authority: Kariba Hydro-Electric Scheme](https://www.zambezira.org/hydro-electric-schemes/kariba-hes). The operator dates the original scheme to 1956-1960.
- [Zambezi River Authority: institutional history](https://www.zambezira.org/about-us). The common pre-cutoff power institutions support selective Zambia/Rhodesia operating knowledge. The northern power station and later Zambian schemes are not granted as January 1970 assets.
- [Kenyan Ministry of Defence: Kenya Air Force at 50](https://www.mod.go.ke/kenya-air-force-at-50/). The air force's 1964 establishment and inherited support facilities are distinct from later combat aircraft or domestic production.
- [University of Nairobi ICT: historical projects](https://ict.uonbi.ac.ke/basic-page/historical-projects). Its description begins university computing services in the 1970s. Without better pre-cutoff evidence this does not justify a January 1970 research grant.
- [Nigerian Air Force: official history](https://airforce.mil.ng/index.php/history). The civil-war acquisition of older Soviet jets supports a limited early-jet operating baseline.
- [Library of Congress Federal Research Division: Nigeria, a Country Study](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/frd/frdcstdy/ni/nigeriacountryst00metz_0/nigeriacountryst00metz_0.pdf). The military chapter separates the fledgling 1960s air force from later expansion and indigenous aircraft development. Post-1970 systems are not backdated.
- [US diplomatic telegram, Addis Ababa, 1 March 1967](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v24/d331). The period record discusses aircraft, vehicles and helicopters through American assistance. It supports an Ethiopian imported-equipment exception, not an autonomous industrial catalogue.
- [US Secretary of State memorandum, 5 August 1970: Soviet Military Deliveries to Libya](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76ve05p2/d64). The Soviet medium-tank/APC deliveries occurred in July 1970, so they are excluded from 1 January.
- [US North Africa memorandum, 1970, document 63](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76ve05p2/d63). The French Mirage program was announced in January 1970 with future deliveries. It must not become an already-operating January 1 fleet or a domestic production technology.
- [Botswana Government Gazette, Defence Force Act, 1977](https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/bw/1977/bw-government-gazette-dated-1977-03-23-no-20.pdf). The act establishes the later defense force. Botswana's 1970 floor remains police/mobile-unit scale.
- [Lesotho Defence Force: institutional history](https://ldf.gov.ls/ldf/about). The later air wing is dated to 1978. No air-force technology is inferred for 1970.
- [World Bank, Gabon Country Economic Brief, 1 April 1968](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/088051425723620302-0560011968/original/WorldBankGroupArchivesfolder30279673.pdf). The period brief identifies the foreign-managed resource economy and contrasts it with the local agricultural sector; income from raw materials does not establish diversified high-technology capabilities.
- [World Bank, Senegal economic report, 8 June 1970](https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/636701468104941050). Retrospective evidence on the 1960s supports Dakar's stronger civilian workshops/processing role. Proposals and developments after January 1970 are not granted.

## Modern and hypothetical tags

The status classification for modern militias is separate from their deliberately low hypothetical release-floor technology.

- [UN Commission of Inquiry on the Central African Republic, 2014](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/774767/files/S_2014_373-EN.pdf). The modern Seleka/anti-Balaka context is many decades after 1970.
- [US Department of State, Country Reports on Terrorism 2016: Al-Shabaab, hosted by UNHCR Refworld](https://www.refworld.org/reference/annualreport/usdos/2017/117895). The modern Somali organization belongs to the 2000s, so its later equipment is excluded.
- [Sierra Leone government records: AFRC, 25 May 1997 to February 1998](https://www.sierra-leone.org/government-AFRC.html). Primary governmental statements establish that the modern AFRC tag is not a 1970 formation.
- Other later political/faction and hypothetical regional tags are identified by their names and their retained later country history. Their low-confidence local ceilings are explicitly an inference, not a claim that a distinct 1970 laboratory, state or military institution has been verified.

## Verification limits and provenance

No source imagery, full publication, proprietary database dump or equipment list is redistributed. External sources retain their own copyright, author credits, conditions and permissions. The project marker establishes no ownership of those sources. Original authors or separate permissions are unknown where the linked source does not identify them. The profiles and mapping judgments were authored with Codex for this project.

The 89 records cover the repository's Africa tags including modern and hypothetical releases. Canary Islands is left to the Europe/Spain profile owner because its institutional context is Spanish; any other geographic allocation collision must be resolved by the coordinator. No live game validation was performed by this research worker.
