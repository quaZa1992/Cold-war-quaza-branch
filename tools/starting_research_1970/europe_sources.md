# European and Soviet January 1970 research evidence

Cutoff: 1 January 1970. Historical sources were consulted on30October2026. Country-specific ceilings are implementation judgments; local/released entities are explicitly conservative inferences, not claims of independent1970 programmes. Sources linked for these entities support the parent technical context rather than every local-domain assignment. Existing separately curated infantry records were reviewed and retained where no contrary evidence was established.

Technology semantics: nominal1955 jet2 includes F104/MiG21/MirageIII. Gen3 is reserved for demonstrated later airframe/BVR capability. Computing1945 may include computers in service; integrated_circuit1955 requires actual IC-production evidence. Domestic transistor-computer production by itself does not justify it. Industrial robotics is evidenced specifically for Sweden and Norway, not a rich-country blanket.

Nuclear mapping: reactor1=Magnox; reactor2=PWR. Do not auto-grant every incoming prerequisite. Researchreactors receive nuclear_technology only. Dutch BWR and Swedish PHWR have science/experimental generation/licensing, avoiding the wrong reactor type and forced mutually-exclusive1971 programmes. This documents a limit of the available tree, not a claim these countries lacked nuclear generation.

SER represents federal Yugoslavia and CZE federal Czechoslovakia. Soviet/Yugoslav/local releases get reduced local institutional skills, not the whole parent strategic stock. Foreign bases do not transfer national army/air/nuclear research.
