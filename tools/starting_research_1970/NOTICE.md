- `MD_plane_modules.txt` credits Kanthier and TheGeneral. It supplies the existing early gun, first cannon and conventional bomb-role structures and numerical values used by the historical armament equivalents.
- `MD_tank_modules.txt` credits Crocomoth, Doolitle, Traiano, Cyrus and TheGeneral. The historical 90 mm module retains the native small-cannon numerical values and ammunition rules while permitting documented medium-tank installation, instead of forcing a 105 mm weapon onto M47/M48.
- `MD_ship_modules.txt` credits Londinium, Bluehunter and Bird, with updates from DC83, TheGeneral and Simone. The light steam module preserves the conventional steam engine's numerical values and uses the native light-surface category for steam frigates. The Mk 112 ASROC equivalent uses the first native ASW support module's category and numerical balance tier, with a historically correct eight-round trainable box-launcher identity. This category abstraction neither installs a mortar on the historical ship nor represents a later VLS or a nuclear warhead.
- `MD_arty_modules.txt` supplies native first-tier artillery structure and numerical values. Its available file header does not identify an individual author. The 175 mm and 203 mm equivalents provide correct heavy weapon identities and conventional ammunition compatibility within that existing balance tier. They do not claim measured real-world firing rates or ballistic simulation.
- `mdcw_historical_air_naval_equipment.txt` supplies the existing early static aircraft and utility-helicopter baselines. Individual original author is unknown in the available header. Added attack/bomber/carrier fallbacks use bounded early-era game values and correct native roles. The extra modular utility-helicopter frame is retired. ## Engine conventions and limits

The compiler follows [native MD variant conventions](https://github.com/MillenniumDawn/Millennium-Dawn/blob/main/.claude/docs/oob-variants-reference.md). It preserves the researched bare-hull ordering described in [Paradox's January 2023 developer notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=394360&enddate=1675435046&feed=steam_community_announcements). It does not use a blanket modular `allow_without_tech` bypass.

Precise service-knowledge bundles are hidden, non-researchable native technologies with only documented fitted modules and, exceptionally, the exact native MD frame of an evidenced pre1970 operated model, without promoting its operator to the generic frame's later national research generation. Shared static models can use a common exact-type bundle when an ordinary unlock does not cover every DLC configuration in which that same design is created. Repeating an already available bare-type unlock in the alternative configuration creates no additional equipment or effect. They grant no industry modifiers, scientific bonuses, projects, buildings, money, ammunition stockpiles or deployed formations. Their role is to represent already known service equipment without copying an exporting country's entire research tree. Explicit nominal-year exceptions require evidence for the actual pre-1970 hardware and do not justify a future weapon or electronics package.

All new variant names are proper historical names or concise English descriptions. No images or model binaries are created by this task. No new military OOB, equipment quantities, fleets or air wings are supplied. Validation records distinguish source/model checks, a native startup observation, and unperformed save/reload or multiplayer checks.
