Staged Japan 1970 Foreign Affairs outputs

All outputs remain in staging/foreign and are not installed. The focus tree can call COLDWAR_JAP70_FOREIGN_<focus-id>_effect for domestic rewards or COLDWAR_JAP70_FOREIGN_<focus-id>_offer for bilateral consent flows. Expenses use the existing modify_treasury_effect hook with treasury_change in billions.

Domestic coverage is 41 focuses: 38 five-year temporary rewards, +5% maximum fuel capacity, an oil-shock contingency that follows imp_oil_shock after completion, and one cost-only supplier-dialogue preparation.

Consent offers cover 19 fixed-recipient proposals, two independent responses to korea_reconciliation_offer, the KOR industrial-cooperation offer, and 15 player-selected partner proposals. Each ordinary acceptance event has a unique +20 opinion modifier, an agreement-specific title/description, GFX_treaty event picture, and native effect_tooltip matching the acceptance effects. Per-recipient timed ideas use unique IDs, including separate Korea reconciliation ideas. The Seoul project acceptance opens a KOR state-target decision; it requires a state KOR owns and controls with an available industrial_complex slot. The decision builds one factory instantly and only then charges Japan $2B, grants Japan +2% office productivity for five years, and awards mutual +20 opinion. ASEAN infrastructure support accumulates +2% Japan office productivity per completed partner project; each contribution expires after 1825 days.

Recipient-selected projects cover Seoul, ASEAN infrastructure, Mekong development, and Indochina reconstruction. ASEAN invitations go only to current ASEAN_Member spirit holders. Mekong invitations target existing countries with capitals in strategic regions 142 (Indochina) or 145 (Burma); Indochina reconstruction uses regions 142, 145, 147, 158, 159, and 160. These region names were checked in map/strategicregions. Acceptance creates a recipient-only state-target decision. The regional/capital checks select invitee countries only; recipients may select any state they own and control with capacity, and no capital-state construction is automatic. Payment occurs only after the valid building is constructed.

Native references checked: treasury effect in common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt; modifier definitions in common/modifier_definitions; max_fuel_factor in native ideas; GFX_treaty in interface/MD_eventpictures.gfx; JAP_imperial_influence_communist in interface/MD_ideas.gfx. State-target and free_building_slots grammar follows current decisions, including common/decisions/05_japan.txt.

The event root is the responding recipient; JAP scopes the proposer. No event changes recognition, ownership, basing or political alignment. Static checks do not replace an in-engine multiplayer test.
Opinion modifiers have agreement-specific IDs. Timed opinion modifiers follow the agreed 3- or 5-year package durations. Proposals whose terms do not specify an opinion duration remain permanent. Coast-guard exchange now uses its own recipient-consent flow and three-year modifier.



The remaining 15 consent-based offers use Japanese player-selectable targeted decisions over `global.countries`, followed by an independent response from each invited government. ASEAN offers require the current `ASEAN_Member` spirit; LNG candidates must own an oil-producing state; coastal and Pacific filters use owned coastal states or selected Pacific/Southeast Asian capital regions. Pacific invitations use political capital regions named East Asia, Southeast Asia, Australia, and New Zealand; global science exchange can invite any non-Japanese country. Developing-market invitations require a gdp_per_capita value below 20; they do not impose a population minimum. These eligibility filters are scope assumptions, not historical claims. Japan's benefits use per-agreement cumulative variables and individually scheduled expiry events; recipient timed ideas and distinct opinion modifiers apply only after acceptance. Scientific exchange charges each signatory $1.000 billion. Coast-guard training lasts three years.

The targeted-decision category metadata is staged separately under `categories/`. To integrate, copy staged files into their corresponding common, events, and localization paths and wire each focus reward to the mapped offer helper. Focus previews describe opt-in proposal terms independently of availability; expiry events are hidden.

Machine-readable mapping for all 80 foreign-affairs focuses is in `foreign_helper_registry.json`; it includes helper ID, status, preview body, and player-facing localization key. The underlying coverage sources remain `manifest.json` (41 domestic) and `bilateral_manifest.jsonl` (39 bilateral/project offers).

New eligibility references: the market-access decision checks the existing `gdp_per_capita` variable and requires a value below 20; state oil production uses `has_resources_amount` as in `common/decisions/00_resources_expansion_decisions.txt`. The 10 Pacific invitation capital regions map to East Asia (143/155), Indochina (142), Burma (145), Malaya (147), Australia (156), New Zealand (157), Sunda Islands (158), Borneo (159), and Philippines (160). The dynamic modifier icon `GFX_idea_JAP_japanese_economy` is defined in `interface/MD_ideas.gfx`.
