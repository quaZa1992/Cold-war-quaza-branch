## Architectural sources

- Western Wall Heritage Foundation, [Measurements and masonry](https://thekotel.org/en/wailing-wall-western-wall/): 19 m exposed height; prayer section approximately 57 m long; the entire western retaining wall, mostly outside the prayer view, is 488 m long. The Foundation gives both 28 and 29 exposed stone courses in different paragraphs; use this as an approximation, not an exact measured elevation. Drafted margins distinguish the large Herodian lower stones; many upper courses come from later Muslim restorations and the smallest top stones were added in the early twentieth century.
- Israel Antiquities Authority, [Western Wall Plaza excavations 2005–2009](https://hadashot.iaa.org.il/report_detail_eng.aspx?id=1219&mag_id=115): published 2009; archaeological reconstruction and historic plaza discussion. The broad plaza was created in 1967; its pavement slopes gently from west to east. Avoid attributing all modern plaza fixtures to 1970.

## Modeling interpretation

The primary photographs verify the flat, uncrenellated wall; large irregular rectangular lower blocks with weathered drafted borders; much smaller upper stone courses; sparse plants in joints; a rough masonry building at the north with a small ground-level pointed doorway and small windows; and a simple waist-high prayer divider. Intermediate dimensions, support depth, courtyard footprint and individual block lengths are visual estimates rather than a surveyed reconstruction. Retain the 1970 earthen access bank south of the prayer face instead of the later wooden Mughrabi bridge.
