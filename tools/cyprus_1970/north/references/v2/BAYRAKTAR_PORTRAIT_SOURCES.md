The original university-hosted PDF was downloaded successfully as `ayhan_cankut_2006_tmt_firat_189636.pdf` (8,835,845 bytes).

- Author: Ayhan Cankut.
- Title: Kibris Turkunun Varolus Mucadelesi (Turk Mukavemet Teskilati ve Faaliyetleri).
- Fırat University, 2006, **master's thesis**, thesis number 189636. It is not a doctoral dissertation.
- Institutional handle: https://hdl.handle.net/11508/16205
- Repository item: https://openaccess.firat.edu.tr/items/9c4aa216-4589-446b-84cb-561515b5c96c

- Figure: **Resim 6: Bayraktarlarin Fotograflari**, printed page **101**, PDF page **122** (zero-based index 121).
- Figure attribution: Ayhan Cankut, personal photographs, Kibris Turk Mucahitler Dernegi.
- Extracted original image: `bayraktars_sheet_original_R649.jpg`, 1088 x 762 pixels.

The PDF photograph shows a museum-style row of framed portraits above a map. Those individual portraits are very small. It corroborates the provenance and visible photographic types, while the downloaded Veryansin plate gives much clearer individual labels and faces.
