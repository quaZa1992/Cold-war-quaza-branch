# SSR equipment specialization target audit

## Conclusion for “small aircraft airframes”

`small_plane_airframe` is the Light Fighter equipment archetype. It is the class or family of light aircraft designs, not the hull of one particular plane and not a deployed wing. The MD archetype index labels it “Light Fighter” (`E:\SteamLibrary\steamapps\workshop\content\394360\2777392649\common\units\equipment\equipment_archetypes.md:17`). The designer chassis itself is non-buildable (`MD_plane_airframes.txt:6-11`); the first concrete small airframe is `small_plane_airframe_1`, year 1965 (`MD_plane_airframes.txt:269-292`), enabled in the BBA aircraft tree (`BBA_aircraft.txt:115`).

For a player preview, use a short scope label such as “Light fighter airframe production cost: -10%”. “Aircraft hull” can be mistaken for one chassis model. A reduction applies to qualifying future production, not to existing aircraft or wing count.

## Modifier and reversibility evidence

Most importantly, `continuous_air_production` explicitly lists the base and duplicate plane targets separately and gives each `instant = yes` (`common/ideas/generic_ideas.txt:1991-2032`). This directly supports the revised seven aviation keys and confirms why they should remain explicit. The project implementation contract specifies `instant = yes` for these target effects.

## Design corrections for the current concept

- The revised `specialisations.py` retains the global output/capacity/dockyard rewards and adds base equipment costs for the six military/electronics programmes. The seven aviation, three artillery, and three light-warship targets all have exact native IDs and direct precedent evidence above. No unsupported target ID was found.
- Base equipment costs are aviation -5% on seven explicit categories, medium tank chassis -6%, artillery -8% on three categories, small arms and utility vehicles -5%, patrol boats/corvettes/frigates -5%, and C&C equipment -4%. Including route rewards, aircraft production is -15%, shipbuilding is -15%, and defence electronics is -14% on their named equipment scopes.
- Parent plus route arithmetic: advanced armour is `-0.06 + 0.05 = -1%` equipment cost; serial armour is `-0.06 - 0.12 = -18%`. Precision artillery is `-0.08 + 0.06 = -2%`; volume artillery is `-0.08 - 0.14 = -22%`. Thus both premium routes still lower cost overall while keeping their stated premium. The legacy scaling rule preserves cost penalties at 75% when independence begins; positive equipment bonuses remain penalties under the existing rule.
- The aviation root now explicitly covers small fighter/strike/CAS, medium fighter/multirole/CAS, and heavy transport categories. It does not target `large_plane_airframe`, so no strategic bomber benefit is implied. C&C now targets the exact `cnc_equipment_type` with a reliability improvement, replacing the misleading infantry small-arms target.
- `corvette` and medium chassis remain subject to DLC and equipment-variant reachability in the live build. Static sources establish exact IDs and native precedents, not the result of all DLC combinations at runtime.

## Remaining runtime checks

Static files establish IDs, target categories, scenario equipment variants, and example syntax. They do not prove the live game applies each category bonus to every DLC branch or removes its benefit when the hosting idea is removed, preserves the correct localization in the SSR preview, or behaves identically in multiplayer. Test those DLC combinations with the bonus active and after removing its spirit before representing the result as runtime-certified.
