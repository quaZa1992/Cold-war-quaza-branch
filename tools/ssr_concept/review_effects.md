Either expose the recurring funded programme explicitly or describe the effect as a higher service burden and provide a separate measurable service reward.

## Specific revisions before implementation

1. Correct any broad “industry productivity/output” prose attached to `eff`: it changes MD's civilian tax/GDP model, not the vanilla production of civilian goods. Say “civilian economic productivity” and explain the economic model's scope in the concept or tooltip. Keep `out` explicitly tied to military production. The Consumer Supply branch's military-output penalty should be presented as deliberate reallocation away from defence industry.
2. Rebalance the repeated `eff` stack. The current maxima are +40% while SSR programmes are active and +45% in the independent phase, including the separately permanent +3 improvement; never add the active SSR sector reward to the independent package. The Armenian +5% native focus is a calibration example, not a hard cap.
3. Revisit the social-cost stack and claims of funded services. +29% SSR and +38% independent are phase-specific upper routes with different starting/replacement conditions. Reduce the stacked increments, or make the player-facing financial/service relationship concrete.
4. Treasury values such as 0.30 and 0.06 are setup costs; agreements use 0.0002-0.0005 weekly at `content.py:208-224, 393-396`. Their conversion to current treasury and GDP scales remains unresolved. Distinguish setup and weekly values, and check overlap with subject subsidies before selecting runtime amounts. This is a mapping/calibration item, not a demonstrated affordability defect.
5. Building rewards request one level and one shared slot (`B` at `content.py:55`), but their target is still the generic “One eligible owned and controlled core state.” State selection, preview location, and capacity/ownership binding remain pending the generator mapping. This is unresolved design scope, not a tested runtime failure.
6. Native modifier caps and their interaction with the proposed stacks, especially stability, remain an implementation verification risk. The concept includes targeted stability cuts. No universal cap is asserted here.

## Not a mechanics blocker

The pre-independence Army XP and one-use land-doctrine discount are a design choice, not unsupported sovereignty. They can represent institutional training under Union command while granting no units, equipment, separate command, or guaranteed deployment. The concept already states this boundary in the defence group note and Combined Exercises description at `content.py:145, 152`.

Other implementation contracts still need native emitted effects: `action` strings such as recovery/preparedness counters and offer actions are design data, not direct modifiers. The eventual generator must expose concrete effects and wire their readers. This is pending integration, not evidence that the content concept is intrinsically invalid.
