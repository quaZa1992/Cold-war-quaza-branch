## Privatization and tax representation

- Russian “privatize state corporations” is not a factory transfer mechanic: `common/national_focus/05_russia.txt:840-865` adds corruption, runs `two_random_industrial_complex`, and improves oligarch opinion. The helper at `common/scripted_effects/00_scripted_effects.txt:278-292` selects two controlled states with free slots, adds one shared slot and one instant civilian industrial complex in each, then charges treasury 15 unless payment is skipped. This is additional construction, not ownership conversion.
- Poland's explicitly named two-year enterprise privatization focus grants `POL_enterprises_privatization_idea` for 730 days (`common/national_focus/05_poland.txt:27281-27312`). The idea at `common/ideas/Polish.txt:2247-2256` gives +10% `production_speed_industrial_complex_factor` and -3% `consumer_goods_factor`. It represents short-term investment/production incentives, not a visible split of privately owned CIV stock.
- The money system's CIV/industry contribution is based on `industrial_complex_total`, factory productivity and manpower fulfillment, for example `common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt:420-455`, `:5882`, plus corporate tax at `:704-712`. The audited source has no broad national-private-ownership factory pool whose ownership can be reassigned. Physically deleting CIVs would destroy productive capacity and lower tax/GDP inputs; it is misleading as a default “privatization” reward/cost. Model ownership reform through temporary investment/productivity, corporate tax or business-influence changes, or an explicit, consequential factory closure if that is truly intended.
- Tax modifiers must be read by the particular revenue line. `tax_gain_multiplier_modifier` is a percentage modifier definition (`common/modifier_definitions/money_modifier_definitions.txt:2-6`) and the corporate office tax formula consumes it at `common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt:704-712`. `corporate_tax_income_multiplier_modifier` is separately applied in the same formula. Do not claim that a general native tax modifier directly changes every tax stream or native overlord share. Treat native tax and overlord transfers as existing baseline revenue, and describe any added programme streams separately to prevent double-counting them as new taxes.

## Military specialization: scope and supported pattern

- Russian focus `SOV_UVZ_expanding` (`common/national_focus/05_russia.txt:32610-32645`) visibly adds `industrial_capacity_factory = +0.10`, `production_factory_max_efficiency_factor = +0.10`, a one-use tank tech bonus, and an equipment bonus for medium tank chassis (`armor_value +0.09`, reliability `+0.09`, build cost IC `-0.08`). This is a strong source precedent for separating industrial capacity/efficiency, a limited research bonus and a named equipment design bonus. Equipment `build_cost_ic` changes a named design's production cost; it does not add equipment or production lines.
- The `add_equipment_bonus` native pattern is used throughout `05_russia.txt` (examples lines 5919, 21724, 24818, 32215). Its nested equipment/chassis stats include `build_cost_ic`, reliability, armor and other design stats. Use exact MD equipment category/item names and the source-style `name`, `bonus`, `instant` structure; do not advertise this as flat factory output.
- Research bonuses use native `add_tech_bonus = { name = ... bonus = 1 uses = 1 category = CAT_tanks }` as in the above tank focus; an aircraft example is `CAT_aircraft` in `05_russia.txt:13301-13305`; armored fighting vehicles also appear as `CAT_armored_fighting_vehicles` at `:21877-21883`; infantry as `CAT_infantry` at `:31712-31718`. For 1970 SSR rewards, choose categories actually relevant to the focus and available techs; the source shows use of `CAT_tanks`, `CAT_aircraft`, `CAT_infantry`, and `CAT_armored_fighting_vehicles`. Do not assume `CAT_armour` spelling; source uses `CAT_tanks` and `CAT_armored_fighting_vehicles`.
- The source uses distinct native construction/building modifiers: `production_speed_buildings_factor` for general building construction; `production_speed_arms_factory_factor` for military-factory construction; `production_speed_air_base_factor` for air-base construction; and `production_speed_naval_base_factor` for naval-base construction. The latter two appear together in `common/continuous_focus/generic.txt:42-43`; Russia's Aeroflot focus adds the air-base key at `05_russia.txt:34945-34972`. None means aircraft/ship equipment output.
- `production_speed_dockyard_factor` is dockyard production speed, not naval-base construction. It has numerous idea providers such as Japanese industry ideas at `common/ideas/05_japan.txt:916,1037`; Russia has no such provider in `05_russia.txt` in this source pass. Do not invent an `aircraft_output` modifier. For actual aircraft/tank/artillery equipment production specialization, use scoped `add_equipment_bonus` on exact equipment identifiers, or a supported factory/dockyard production modifier. Russian examples include `fighter`, `tactical_bomber`, `naval_bomber`, `artillery_equipment`, `infantry_weapons_type`, `medium_tank_chassis`, and `medium_tank_artillery_chassis` in `05_russia.txt:5919-5966`, `:24818-24824`, `:24873-24922`, and `:32626-32642`. Bonuses such as `build_cost_ic = -0.07` reduce that equipment's IC cost; chassis stats change only the named design. They do not grant equipment or create production lines.
- Research categories: Soviet examples verify `CAT_tanks`, `CAT_aircraft`, `CAT_infantry` (`05_russia.txt:13301-13305`, `:21871`, `:31716`, `:32642`); MD focus examples verify `CAT_artillery` (`common/national_focus/01_EU_USoE_shared.txt:2960`, plus `05_afghanistan.txt:676`). Use real `add_tech_bonus` categories and check that a matching unresearched tech exists in the 1970 start. Do not imply an artillery tech bonus from an equipment-cost bonus.
- Caspian naval specialization needs a territory/native map gate: a Caspian fleet is a closed-sea flotilla and does not create open-sea reach. There is no generic “Caspian naval power” modifier established in this source pass. If granting dockyard/naval infrastructure, bind it to a controlled Caspian-coast state, show/guard the extra shared slots per project instructions, and avoid promising blue-water access. Confirm actual coastal state, naval base, ownership/control and unit access in the 1970 map before implementation.

## Evidence limits

Source code proves the MD calculation path and examples, not engine acceptance of every vanilla modifier key or its exact tooltip in a loaded game. In particular, validate engine keys for factory efficiency, production output, building construction, army training, and native shared-building slots in the intended game version. The dynamic productivity APIs and money-system custom keys above have explicit MD definitions/readers; standard HOI modifier spelling should not be inferred from similarly named MD variables.

## Reversible equipment bonuses and closure distinction

- The MD doctrine author note `common/doctrines/grand_doctrines/_documentation.md:83-91` explicitly says to use scoped `equipment_bonus` instead of the `add_equipment_bonus` effect because the latter does not remove the bonus when doctrine changes. Use an equipment bonus inside an active idea/dynamic idea if the bonus must end with SSR status; do not use one-time `add_equipment_bonus` and then promise that 80% of its benefit disappears on independence. The source has idea-scoped examples in `common/ideas/Russian.txt:2236-2240`, `common/ideas/05_bulgaria.txt:89-102`, and timed special-project ideas at `common/ideas/!_special_project_ideas.txt:71-75,148-156`.
- Equipment bonus applies only to the named equipment identifiers (examples in Russia: `fighter`, `tactical_bomber`, `naval_bomber`, `artillery_equipment`, `medium_tank_chassis`). It is separate from a generic combat modifier such as `army_artillery_attack_factor`; the latter changes army combat values and does not make a producer's equipment cheaper or more capable.
- Civilian factory deletion exists as an economic closure/loss effect, not as a native ownership-transfer or sale mechanic. Examples: Japan event `events/05_japan.txt:1821,1843,1874,1904` removes CIV levels in a damage/crisis context; Bosnia event `events/bosnia_events.txt:2934,2954,2988,3008` removes CIV levels as a loss. This is evidence that physical `remove_building = { type = industrial_complex ... }` destroys capacity. It is a bad default abstraction for privatization.
- Research category spellings are exact: `CAT_corvettes` and `CAT_destroyers` are attached to hull techs at `common/technologies/naval.txt:251-517,1224-1438`; Soviet focus uses `CAT_aircraft`, `CAT_tanks`, and `CAT_infantry`; `CAT_artillery` is used in `common/national_focus/01_EU_USoE_shared.txt:2960` and Afghanistan `:676`. These differ from unit equipment identifiers and production speed modifiers.
