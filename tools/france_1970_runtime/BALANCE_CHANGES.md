## Historical grounding and inference

The French National Assembly's [2014 defence-budget report](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/14/budget/plf2014/b1428-tIII-a10.asp) reviews the Jaguar's Cold War origins and identifies difficulties from coordinating national industries, work allocation and changing service specifications. This is a precedent for the Shared Components production-coordination penalty. The chosen -8% modifier is a gameplay abstraction and does not reproduce a historical Jaguar measurement.

The [1975 Franco-Soviet energy cooperation agreement](https://www.elysee.fr/valery-giscard-d-estaing/1975/10/17/accord-entre-le-gouvernement-de-la-republique-francaise-et-le-gouvernement-de-lunion-des-republiques-socialistes-sovietiques-sur-la-cooperation-dans-le-domaine-de-lenergie-moscou-le-17-octobre-1975) provides a period precedent for energy equipment, studies and technical cooperation. Funding specialist training is a design inference from that work, rather than a claim that the treaty imposed the proposed education-cost modifier.

The existing HISTORY.md, ADDITIONAL_SOURCES.md and MECHANICS.md in `tools/france_1970_party_revision` record the other policy and institutional precedents. Personnel, royalty, tax and innovation trade-offs above are explicitly proposed mechanisms for those programmes, not claims of exact historical agreements or national budget figures.

The core save migration rebuilds earned domestic modifier pools using the amended current payloads. It performs no new fee, building, research, doctrine, experience or faction-opinion transaction. Standing treaty modifiers are restored from their current idea definitions under the same signatures and active-relation requirements.
