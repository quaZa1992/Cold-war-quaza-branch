# France 1970 focus and national idea art provenance

The polish overlay references existing Millennium Dawn textures and animations from Workshop item `2777392649`, inspected at `E:/SteamLibrary/steamapps/workshop/content/394360/2777392649`. Focus icons are registered in the upstream `interface/goals.gfx`; selected/available shine sprite blocks are in `interface/goals_shine.gfx`; small national idea icons are registered in `interface/MD_ideas.gfx`.

- Original authors and credits: Millennium Dawn authors; individual texture artists are not identified in the inspected files.
- Applicable licence or permission: unknown from the inspected source files. The overlay does not assert ownership or add a new licence.
- Modifications: no source texture is copied, recoloured, resized or modified; shine animation blocks are copied without change except for the renamed sprite alias. The returned `.gfx` content creates project-prefixed aliases to the original MD paths and clones 36 shine sprite blocks, changing only each registered name. `art_copies` is empty.
- Historical selection: the UDR branch uses the French Republic emblem rather than `FRA_cdg` or later RPR/UMP marks. The Socialist and ecological party programmes use civic or policy art rather than later PS/EELV identity marks. The right-wing 1970 slot uses the Republican emblem, not the later FN mark.
- Idea artwork: idea aliases point to actual small MD `gfx/interface/ideas` assets. The economic focus medallion `economic_prosperity` is not reused as national idea art.
