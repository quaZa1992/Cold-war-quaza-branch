# France 1970 replacement

Current revision adds six recipient-approved procurement ultimatums tied to
active African agreements. French aid can be withheld after refusal; existing
concessions and host-paid fees remain in force. An accepted charter brings
France a weekly host-GDP payment and military access, with a protection
guarantee and French personnel costs. See
`tools/france_1970_pressure/BALANCE_AND_DESIGN.md` for terms, historical scope,
doctrine corrections and the existing-save limitation. All 22 negative
corporate-tax rows in 19 bilateral agreements are removed by the later
`deal_balance_revision.py` overlay; frozen approvals remain unchanged.

The researched foreign-policy extension is installed with
37 additional focuses and 32 additional recipient-approved agreements. Its
compact regional placement follows the user's correction to the original
wide comb. See `tools/france_1970_foreign_policy/README.md` for the new rights,
payments, native budget breakdown and current verification limits.

Earlier follow-up: the user approved the visualized 20-focus expansion
on 30 October 2026. See `tools/france_1970_party_expansion/IMPLEMENTATION.md`
for installation, dated CID migration and current checks; `COST_PRESENTATION.md`
records the native cost-row repair. See `CURRENT_FIXES.md` for the earlier opinion display
repair, three persistent recovery arcs, twenty investment reward revisions,
the shared agency sprite-frame correction, and exact current test limits.
The base focus catalogue has 205 building levels with matching slots, five additional
standalone slots, 247 setup-fee entries and four recovery-stage ideas in addition to the eight
initial spirits. The earlier 437-assertion native build predates these changes.

The approved 213-focus design replaces the retired France tree. The player
starts under Georges Pompidou. Nine governing-party programmes stay visible,
with actual native subparty requirements, alongside shared institutions,
industrial development, defence and recipient-approved foreign cooperation.
The Gaullist branch includes three competing approaches to NATO: independent
alliance policy, selective operational cooperation and a European pillar.

The approved party amendment adds 16 distinct recipient-approved agreements,
bringing the total at that stage to 35, and two optional domestic funding decisions. The foreign-policy extension raises the total to 67. The PCF
can pursue an Italian cooperative programme or a Soviet industrial partnership
with exclusive energy and maritime continuations. Other party programmes have
their own licensing, credit, insurance, computing, training and environmental
packages. The user's later instruction replaces eleven influence penalties
with tangible production, research, personnel, education or tax disadvantages.
See `BALANCE_CHANGES.md` for the exact before/after values and historical basis.

The polish preserves the approved focus IDs, costs, prerequisite groups and
exclusions. Rewards follow the approved party snapshot plus the user's later
eleven-focus tangible-disadvantage amendment. The horizontal card footprint falls from 12,645 to 9,669 pixels
on the native 96 by 130 grid, a 23.54 percent reduction. English descriptions,
short titles and native focus/idea art have been reviewed. All 36 focus art
aliases have their matching native shine definitions; national ideas use small
idea textures. No source image pixels were modified.

## Authoritative inputs and build

`approved_design.json` is an immutable byte-for-byte copy of the approved
concept. `APPROVAL.json` records the implementation instruction and source hash.
Its old proposal status is historical metadata, not the installed status.
`approved_party_revision.json` is the immutable 156-focus amendment, recorded
with its source hash and implementation instruction in
`PARTY_REVISION_APPROVAL.json`. `balance_revision.py` records the later user
instruction as a narrow deterministic overlay; neither snapshot is changed.
`runtime_design.json` is the polished output and `manifest.json` records the
installed file hashes and ownership boundaries.

`approved_expansion.json` is the immutable 176-focus proposal approved by
`EXPANSION_APPROVAL.json`. `expansion_revision.py` merges only its twenty added
focuses, two CID lifecycle changes and the existing Fund's alternate parent
onto the pinned previous runtime. It preserves the original approval files.

`approved_foreign_policy.json` and `FOREIGN_POLICY_APPROVAL.json` record the
researched 37-focus extension and condensed layout instruction.
`foreign_policy_revision.py` preserves every earlier focus and decision while
adding four regional chapters and 32 distinct agreements.

`deal_balance_revision.py` then applies the user's subsequent tax and doctrine
instructions. `pressure_contracts.json` supplies the six-country pressure
configuration, embedded in the compiled design; `pressure.py` emits the
decisions, hidden expiry events, ideas, triggers and weekly payment helpers.
The manifest binds both sources and their generated outputs.

country-history and fallback entry points, native party callback, and native
additional-expense accumulator. It does not regenerate other countries.

## Payment, assets and authority

All 247 setup-fee entries use the paying country's current native `gdp_total`.
No flat Treasury invoice or Treasury-balance gate is introduced. All 134 standing
agreement operating expenses are annual GDP shares divided by 52, recalculated in each
signatory's own expense calculation. There is no historical display-price
conversion in the runtime arithmetic.

The 32 new contracts also have matched service-fee or aid streams. Both sides
read the same seven-day quote based on the named payer's GDP. Budget breakdown
rows expose actual active income and expenditure totals in native MD format.

Each of the 205 building levels includes one extra shared state building slot,
before construction, within its one-time guarded transaction. Ownership, full
control and the building's actual physical cap are separate checks. Explicit
technology requirements remain real requirements. Scripted instant completion
uses the project's native-tested building-grant convention; it does not award
future construction technologies.

Party programme variables survive changes of government. Their dynamic
modifiers detach when the actual subparty loses power and restore on return.
Durable national improvements and one-time assets, fees, research, training and
faction opinion changes do not replay. A one-time migration rebuilds earned
domestic modifier pools from their current payloads and removes retired pools;
it never runs finite transactions. Parent-dependent programme pools also
require their actual active parent spirit.

Each of the 67 diplomatic programmes originates in its own French focus. The
named recipient receives native acceptance and refusal decisions. Both countries'
complete prospective terms remain in the focus's nonexecuting Effects preview.
Signatures have no fixed expiry. Both spirits suspend for bilateral war,
embargo or any of the four mirrored Imperia sanction classes, and restore when
the relationship qualifies again. NATO programmes additionally require actual
membership in both countries. The lifecycle never restores NATO membership.
Party-specific proposals require the actual governing party when sent,
renewed and accepted. Signed state agreements survive subsequent elections.
The FN offer also checks its dated identity at sending and acceptance.

Offers wait 90 days for a response. A declined or expired proposal can be renewed
after its 180-day waiting period from its completed source focus. The same
conditions and recipient consent apply. Accepted families cannot stack rival
programmes. These response windows affect proposals, not standing signatures.

The native party registry gains dated RPR, Republican Party, CDS and Green
identities in the existing slots. It preserves starting identities, leaders,
elections and the distinctions between the liberal, centrist and radical
programmes. See `IDENTITY_SOURCES.md`.
