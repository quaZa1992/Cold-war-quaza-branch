Read-only gameplay review requested by the user. No loaded game file or gameplay

## Findings, ordered by consequence

1. **P1 — Native GCC membership list includes governments that have not joined.**
   Native startup inserts all six founders into `global.gcc_member_state` even
   in 1970, before they have a membership spirit. The new branch checks the
   spirit correctly, but retained native joint-exercise and expulsion-voting
   loops read the stale array. This can include unsigned governments in council
   procedures. Evidence: `common/scripted_effects/00_startup_effects.txt:228`,
   caller `common/on_actions/00_on_actions.txt:1747`, consumers
   `common/decisions/Gulf.txt:971` and `:1136`. Repair should reconcile only the
   six founding entries against actual spirits, preserving legitimate other
   members. See `../gcc_1970/AUDIT_2026_10_19.md`.

2. **P2 — The older UAE charter bypasses common GCC admission conditions.**
   Two admission providers coexist. The older UAE offer can admit protected
   Qatar, whereas the common GCC path rejects it at dispatch and acceptance.
   The older guard also omits the recent-expulsion restriction. Reproduced in
   the emitted-script harness. Canonical guard:
   `tools/uae_1970/diplomacy.py:513`; loaded event `.14001` at
   `events/COLDWAR_UAE_1970_contracts.txt:233348`; older decision at
   `common/decisions/COLDWAR_UAE_1970_decisions.txt:2255`. Unifying the visible
   decision alone is insufficient: already open old popups need equivalent
   acceptance guards. See `DIPLOMACY_EVENT_AUDIT.md`, D2.

3. **P2 — Declining an old arms quotation cancels a new procurement round.**
   Buyer quotation `.90001` has an unconditional decline effect that closes
   the currently pending procurement offer. Reproduced: obtain quotation1,
   let it expire, send round2, decline the old quotation1; round2 is closed and
   a new cooldown starts. Canonical source `tools/uae_1970/diplomacy.py:610`,
   loaded option at `events/COLDWAR_UAE_1970_contracts.txt:199893`. Bind decline
   to its own current round and partner. See `DIPLOMACY_EVENT_AUDIT.md`, D1.

4. **P2 — Cabinet acceptance remains selectable after the crisis is resolved.**
   The displayed option and meeting authority omit a crisis condition required
   by the executed settlement. Resolve mediation while the cabinet dialog is
   open: accepting the old cabinet offer then consumes its pending receipt but
   makes no cabinet settlement. Source `tools/uae_1970/diplomacy.py:343`,
   shared meeting authority at `:292`. Align the live option/round authority
   with execution and use the existing stale-dialog fallback.
   See `POLITICS_EVENT_AUDIT.md`.

5. **P2 content gap — Delegated powers have no mechanical consequence.**
   `COLDWAR_UAE_decision_delegation` costs 50 PP (`common/decisions/
   COLDWAR_UAE_1970_decisions.txt:418`). Acceptance sets two flags and adds an
   empty status idea (`common/ideas/COLDWAR_UAE_1970_contracts.txt:141`). The
   accepted flag only disables repetition; the executive flag only maintains
   that status idea. There is no focus, office, constitutional or modifier
   consumer. The existing source therefore implements roleplay status only,
   despite the described stronger federal executive. A real benefit requires
   an explicitly chosen institutional effect; do not invent a large buff as
   an audit correction. See `DIPLOMACY_EVENT_AUDIT.md`, D4.

## Presentation and design questions

- The Founders' Settlement enables distinct exclusive follow-up focuses, but
  the options have no explicit preview naming the unlocked choice. Mediation
  and property settlements display a debit while their remedy is delivered
  through flags and the general programme reconciler. These are not missing
  gameplay rewards; their outcome presentation needs focused native previews.
- Direct programme-refresh calls in events may expose broad reconciliation
  details rather than the marginal result. Actual native hover output has not
  been observed; this remains a UI concern rather than a proven rendering bug.
- A successful cabinet bargain leaves the constitutional-crisis flag set,
  unlike mediation. Whether these should resolve the same crisis needs the
  intended design clarified, rather than assuming they are interchangeable.
- All authored event instances currently use `GFX_political_deal`. The preview
  retains that exact registered image. Individual historical illustrations
  have not been authored for these events.
