# COLDWAR UAE — direct focus outcomes instead of filler meetings

User-authorized revision, 19 October 2026. The screenshot's **Preparing the British
Handover** offer is removed. The already-announced withdrawal still executes
through the existing historical schedule; its completion requires no petition.

## Removed popup families

- Early Handover, Britain Offers Earlier Release, Agreed Transition Date.
- Founders' Settlement, Federal Dispute, Crown and Property, Delegation of
  Federal Powers, Federal Cabinet Bargain.
- Reopen Status Talks, Amend Withdrawal Terms, British Withdrawal Policy menu,
  Release Reserve Principal menu.
- Islands Settlement, Iran's Abu Musa Counterproposal, Saudi Border Talks.
- The old duplicate UAE Gulf Charter provider; the common GCC admission remains.

These are 16 dialogue types, formerly emitted as 2,048 immutable round instances.
Remaining UAE content: 19 substantive types /2,432 round instances. The two cosmetic GCC acceptance/refusal notices were also removed. With the
common GCC branch:20types /2,624IDs. Retained IDs are unchanged; no removed slot is
reused for a different agreement. Ten obsolete decisions and their PP fees
are removed (40→30). Reserve withdrawal and the British renewal offer remain
as direct zero-PP decisions, each without an intermediate menu.

## Direct focus outcomes

- **Prepare the Handover:** office productivity +5%, bureaucracy cost+2%,
  permanent 3 percentage-point relief to the inherited fragmented-administration
  bureaucracy burden. Existing programme status conditions are retained.
- **The Provisional Charter:** ratifies the founding framework directly.
  **Emirate Guarantees** and **A Stronger Mandate** are selected by completing
  the existing exclusive focuses, rather than paying for a preliminary popup.
- **The Development Fund:** existing real productivity/infrastructure effects
  remain; adds 3 pp relief to inherited unequal-development stability loss and
  +3% bureaucracy cost for administering the works.
- **Executive Authority:** its existing+15%PP/−6ppstability programme directly
  records the enacted delegation. It requires the proclaimed federation.
- **Renew the Compact:** existing +4 pp stability, direct cabinet/premiership
  settlement and up to 3 pp inherited bureaucracy relief. The separate presidency
  remains. No extra +3% cabinet spirit cancels the relief. Requires the proclaimed
  federation; old crisis receipts are cleared.
- **A Citizen Republic:** retains its+12%PP/−6ppstability trade-off and adds
  +5% office productivity for civil administration. No invented 5%-GDP purchase
  of the monarchy. Prior already-earned settlement receipts remain honoured.
- **Gulf Diplomacy:** adds+5%PP and−20%relations-maintenance cost alongside its
  existing+3% bureaucracy cost, representing a professional foreign service.
- **The islands focus:** retains its existing direct economic/political
  effects; no cosmetic arbitration dialog or automatic foreign transfer.

The numbers are game balance. Civil administration, federal powers and
needs-based development spending are grounded in the
[UAE Constitution, Articles47,60,126–127and132](https://uaecabinet.ae/en/the-constitution).
Cabinet/presidency separation follows the
[official cabinet history](https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/the-uae-government/the-uae-cabinet).
Republican content is explicitly alternative history.

## Contracts retained

Actual foreign consent, paid equipment transfer, technical exchange, oil
participation and meaningful Sharjah/oil/Kuwait crisis choices remain.
Declining or expiring an offer does not grant its benefits. Obsolete pending
receipts are cleared without payments or altered treaty dates. The old buyer
quotation decline is now round-guarded, so it cannot cancel a newer purchase.
