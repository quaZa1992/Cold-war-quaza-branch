# COLDWAR UAE: focus-led policy revision,19 October2026

This revision supersedes the older decision and domestic-course inventories.
The user's explicit exceptions are OPEC votes and reserve management. Kuwait
assistance must use focuses. All new localisation is English.

## Player-facing delivery

- Exactly three authored UAE decisions remain: OPEC export restraint, reserve
  deposit and reserve withdrawal. Reserve withdrawals consume recorded
  principal; they create neither interest nor money from nothing.
- Domestic reforms use their existing focus costs and continuing programme
  benefits. Former course invoices and duplicate temporary bonuses are removed.
  Fishing Harbours additionally queues one naval-base level in Dubai province
  3725, at its existing single$0.15bn focus cost. Occupation or a completed
  level-ten target prevents that construction; unrelated ports do not.
- The generic resource expansion category is hidden only for the1970 UAE
  campaign. Paid oil/metals investment belongs to its national tree. Other
  countries and later UAE bookmarks retain that category.
- Britain, Bahrain, Qatar, Egypt, the Soviet Union and Kuwait retain genuine
  recipient-controlled agreement events when the relevant focus issues an
  offer. Domestic confirmation meetings and the Sharjah/oil-slump/Kuwait choice
  menus are removed. Event-number slots are reserved rather than reassigned.
- Three complementary Kuwait focuses require an independent proclaimed
  federation and an actual Kuwaiti war. They offer financial support, transit
  through UAE territory or access for existing UAE forces in Kuwait. They
  neither create units nor declare war.
- The National Council remains consultative and recognises educational
  associations. The separate optional Political Competition focus enables
  elections and legal parties through the existing party system. Historical
  federation/currency development does not force elections.
