## Confirmed player-facing gaps

### Party split event previews omit actual registration effects

The `COLDWAR_BRA.7` option is titled “Recognise distinct PTB and PDT organisations”, but its `effect_tooltip` in `events/COLDWAR_BRA_1970_events.txt` previews only `add_ideas = COLDWAR_BRA_policy_event_party_split_option_0`. The hidden effect calls `COLDWAR_BRA_event_apply_party_split_option_0`; that helper also calls `COLDWAR_BRA_1970_form_ptb_late` and `COLDWAR_BRA_1970_form_pdt` (`common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_BRA_1970_effects.txt:313-327`). Each formation helper registers an organization and adds 0.01 to party popularity plus 0.01 to its outlook (`common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_BRA_1970_politics.txt:124-150`). The native popularity helper previews party popularity, but executes outlook change through a `hidden_effect` and has no matching outlook preview (`common/scripted_effects/00_MD_politicsview_scripted_effects.txt:275-282`). The player sees only the settlement spirit while the option also registers two organizations and changes both party and outlook shares. Expose these material consequences in the option preview, including both organizations and signed percentage changes, without running them during preview.

### Party reorganization previews omit the newly available registration decisions

The two `COLDWAR_BRA.3` options preview their idea and, for the pluralist option, `enable_elections_with_no_leader_change`. Actual effects also call `COLDWAR_BRA_1970_open_party_reform` and `COLDWAR_BRA_open_organisation_stages` (`tools/brazil_1970_runtime/core.py:48-71`; `common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_BRA_1970_effects.txt:96-151`). These changes make the party registration and nomination decisions visible. Their availability is conditional on flags raised by the event. The event preview currently does not show the newly accessible decision route, so the player sees the cabinet spirit/election effect but not that it opens the registration stage. Consider showing the relevant native decision unlock or category effect in the option preview; keep internal flags hidden.

## Chronology consistency risk

The later PTB and PDT can be formed immediately after any party reform. `COLDWAR_BRA_open_organisation_stages` sets `labourist_reorganization_open` whenever `party_reform_open` is present, without a date condition (`common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_BRA_1970_effects.txt:12289-12292`). The monthly action fires the one-shot party-split event after that reform flag is set (`common/on_actions/COLDWAR_BRA_1970_on_actions.txt:34-41`). Neither the event helper nor the `form_ptb_late` and `form_pdt` effects apply a historical date check (`common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_BRA_1970_effects.txt:313-340`; `common/scripted_effects/COLDWAR_BRA_1970_politics.txt:124-150`). Their own English descriptions identify PTB registration as 1979 to 1980 and PDT as a later split; party name descriptions say the later PTB wins registration during the 1979 to 1980 reorganisation. If an early democratic opening is meant to remain counterfactual but historically paced, the current one-shot event can settle the split in 1970 and prevents a later 1979/80 event. Add date gates or make the early resolution an explicit alternate timeline. This is a source-confirmed chronology inconsistency against the current player-facing dates, unless the early formation is an intentional alternate-history rule.
