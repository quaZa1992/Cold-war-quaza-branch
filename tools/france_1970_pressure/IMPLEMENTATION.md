# France: pressure and agreement balance implementation

Implemented on 30 October 2026 in the current 213-focus French tree. No focus
coordinates, prerequisites, exclusions or party gates were changed. The
existing African finance and security entries expose six country-specific
pressure decisions, with Gabon reachable from either entry. The 67 original
bilateral agreements retain their consent and suspension systems.

`BALANCE_AND_DESIGN.md` records the authorized tax and doctrine changes, the
full charter terms, historical limits and existing-save limitation.
`RESEARCH_MECHANICS.md` records primary historical sources and the traced
native providers. Exact configuration is in
`tools/france_1970_runtime/pressure_contracts.json` and embedded in the compiled
`runtime_design.json`. The compiler manifest binds that configuration, its
generator and all six emitted pressure files.

Execution and previews are separate. A French demand costs 35 political power;
the recipient receives an acceptance and refusal decision. Both responses
preview both countries' prospective terms, with the actual transaction hidden
from duplicate display. Scheduled hidden country events resolve an unanswered
offer after 30 days. Invalidated or orphaned proposals clear without punishment.
The pressure decisions have no AI automation.

Acceptance charges each setup fee once and records paired permanent
signatures. The standing levy reads one seven-day French cache valued from
the host's GDP. Its two endpoints enter the native weekly money accumulator
and existing cooperation-budget breakdown exactly once. Refusal filters only
French-paid aid transfers on both ledgers. It does not revoke base resource
rights or cancel host-paid service fees.

Standing ideas, access and guarantees reconcile through the existing bounded
French treaty lifecycle. Owned relations are removed on suspension;
pre-existing relations are preserved. Recipient cleanup after France's
disappearance preserves the signed charter for restoration and does not repeat
the initial charges. A 365-day interval restricts further demands.
