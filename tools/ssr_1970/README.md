## Automatic independence and party changes

Native subject-release and re-entry hooks, plus a cheap daily watcher for the fourteen SSR tags, reconcile actual status. The watcher also compares MD's exact governing-party slot, including changes within the same broad outlook. A communist-party check is never substituted for actual SSR status.

A selected specialisation has one combined idea for its actual completed stage and choices. The generator emits the finite legal states and their independent counterparts. On actual independence it removes the full Union idea and selects the exact national legacy: 20% of each beneficial modifier or equipment bonus, 75% of each adverse modifier, and half of its weekly GDP allocation. Both versions cannot coexist. A subsequent accepted re-entry restores the earned Union version; another exit restores the stored legacy and recovery state. Reconciliation contains no treasury charges, construction, research rewards or experience grants.

Ordinary Union programmes suspend outside SSR status. Independent party programmes require actual sovereignty and their exact current party; departure suspends their earned values and return restores them without another one-time reward. National construction, research bonuses, experience, persistent institutional reforms and completed enterprise settlements survive. Initial planning, patronage and regional problems have explicit reform priorities; completed reforms cannot regress or stack with their obsolete versions.

Supply, accounts and defence transition liabilities use the approved country-specific base, actual preparation receipts frozen at first exit, and three unique repair steps. An active accepted clearing settlement with the real Union state or an explicitly recorded successor applies the approved negotiated factor. The code does not dissolve the Union, force secession, nominate a successor, transfer territories or start a war.

## Agriculture and equipment

The agricultural mandate reduces native agricultural-district and subsistence labour requirements by 10% each. Harvest Machinery adds district -15% and subsistence -10%; Rural Cooperatives instead add -5% each. The complete mechanised route therefore contributes district -25% and subsistence -20%, compared with -15% for both on the diversified route. These are sector requirements, not a reduction in total workforce. Native caps, other sources, employment demand and the subsistence GDP formula still apply. The independent legacy retains only 20% of these labour savings.

Weapon-related programmes retain their approved production/efficiency bonuses and have scoped production-cost reductions. Actual MD equipment identifiers and English names are used, including separate fighter, strike, close-support and heavy-lift aircraft targets, main battle tanks, artillery, light warships, small arms, utility vehicles and C&C equipment. Equipment bonuses are attached to the selected idea with native `equipment_bonus` entries and `instant = yes`; no permanent focus `add_equipment_bonus` bypasses the independent legacy.

## Transactions and diplomacy

Focus setup prices use current national GDP at execution, without an added focus Treasury gate. Each successful guarded completion pays and grants its durable reward once. A building project selects an eligible owned, controlled core state, checks native capacity, grants an equal number of extra shared slots first and adds the construction in the same guarded transaction. This includes infrastructure. No automatic uranium extraction or mine is granted.

Three exclusive sovereign economic courses start their 730-day productive and fiscal windows only once. State changes do not reset that deadline. The separate restructuring burden is relieved through six finite enterprise lots. Sale, worker buyout and public retooling preserve factory levels. Only liquidation closes one actual civilian factory, requiring at least two in the selected state at execution. Each lot has one settlement receipt, only one national transaction can be pending, cancellation charges nothing, and completion rechecks current authority, GDP-priced funds, ownership and control.

Seven diplomatic focuses initiate distinct offers. The origin selects a named eligible country through compact paged events, and the actual recipient receives acceptance/refusal decisions. Focus and decision previews show both countries' full prospective numeric terms before consent. Accepted agreements have no expiry; their paired benefits and dedicated weekly expenses suspend during bilateral war, embargoes, any of the four mirrored Imperia sanction classes, or loss of required sovereignty. The fourteen origin countries check validity daily, with immediate recipient notification after an origin spirit changes; budget calculation also rechecks the current pair. Restoration never repeats setup charges. Recipient programme modifiers and their operating budget are deduplicated by agreement type. Either side can terminate; refusal permits the approved later retry. Last-agreement cleanup reconciles the recipient before retiring its activity flag.

The three weekly cost providers feed one marked insertion in MD's additional-expenses calculation. Each provider clears its own cached contribution and uses current `gdp_total`. Gradual and directed restructuring support continues while the persistent restructuring burden remains, including after its separate 730-day growth window, and ends when the six sector settlements remove that burden. Those course costs belong only to the market provider. The approved fractions are already weekly; no second division by 52 is applied. No parallel money system or subject subsidy is introduced.
