# Air Command source and asset provenance

Scope: the fourteen files emitted by `build.py`, the nineteen generated doctrine pictograms, their original masters, the six style-reference copies and the review compositions in this folder.

The doctrine identities and inherited positive rewards originate in Millennium Dawn and Paradox's native doctrine system. The three complete original MD files and their SHA-256 hashes are retained in `baseline.json`. Individual authors and separate redistribution permissions are unknown where not identified by the upstream project. Existing notices and licences retain their authority. The project marker establishes no ownership of third-party material.

The new balance implementation, tools, English prose and design adapters were written with Codex for the user. Historical source authors and institutional credits are individually linked in the approved design catalogue and `design.py` source register. Historical documents are referenced, not redistributed. The values are game-balance choices informed by those precedents, not quantitative historical measurements.

All nineteen new pictograms were generated using OpenAI's built-in ImageGen tool at the user's request. Exact prompts and original saved generation paths are in `imagegen_sources.json`; originals are copied unchanged into `masters/`. No visible watermark was added. Runtime conversion is limited to alpha-bounds cropping, aspect-preserving downscaling, transparent centering and native BGRA DDS encoding. Each source and runtime SHA-256 is in `icon_manifest.json`.

Style donors are the existing native MD blue-grey aircraft pictograms with black outlines and orange tactical accents. The generation record identifies the exact two supplied reference paths. The other reference copies and contact sheet were used for visual comparison. Original individual artists and separate reference permissions are unknown. Referencing this style does not transfer ownership or establish a new licence over inherited artwork.

The two research-node sprites reference the unchanged native `gfx/interface/technologies/0_modules/plane_builder/support/tech_drone_1.dds`; the two equipment sprites reference the unchanged native `gfx/interface/technologies/USA/AIR/UAV1.dds`. Native fonts and GUI assets remain unchanged. Individual original artists and separate permissions are unknown unless identified upstream.

`native_style_references.png` and `icon_review.png` are asset-review compositions, not screenshots of the live game. They include project labels for review only. No project label is painted into a runtime icon. No source photo, document, font or unrelated game texture is repackaged by this feature.
