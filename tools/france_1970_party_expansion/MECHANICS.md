# France 1970 party expansion: mechanics palette

Scope: design-only mechanics reference for eight non-UDR party programmes. This document does not modify runtime code or approve balance values. Exact effects below are grounded in `tools/france_1970_design/verified_effects.json`, `tools/france_1970_design/mechanics_reference.md`, current MD source, and the loaded France concept at `tools/france_1970_runtime/runtime_design.json`.

## Implementation boundaries

- Keep all eight branches visible and use their exact existing party trigger at execution. January 1970 starts with slot 1 Western Conservatism; slot 2 Liberalism is in the coalition, not the ruling party. The other programme gates are slot 2 `western_liberals_are_in_power`, slot 3 `western_social_democrats_are_in_power`, slot 4 `emerging_communist_state_are_in_power`, slot 14 `neutrality_neutral_conservatism_are_in_power`, slot 16 `neutrality_neutral_libertarians_are_in_power`, slot 17 `neutrality_neutral_green_are_in_power`, slot 18 `neutrality_neutral_social_are_in_power`, and slot 20 `nationalist_right_wing_populists_are_in_power`. CID identity is the January 1970 case; FN identity requires its actual post-1972 date gate. Do not replace these with coalition membership or a custom party label.
- The current design already contains many office, infrastructure, factory, research, energy, tax and service-cost effects. Additions should be narrow and tangible, not another blanket productivity/research modifier. Existing modifiers and signed agreements can stack across a route; calculate the total against the actual reachable graph before finalizing values.
- Any one-time GDP fee uses current payer-country `gdp_total` at payment. A recurring programme uses a persistent annual GDP share converted to MD weekly units by `/52` in the established expense aggregator. Keep both countries' own GDP bases for any recipient-approved offer. Do not invent cash income, resource sales or a second money engine.
- Every granted building level, including infrastructure, receives one extra shared slot in the same guarded one-time transaction before construction. Check live state owner, full control and type-specific native capacity separately. The metropolitan France state files are 54 Hauts-de-France, 55 Normandie, 56 Ile-de-France, 57 Alsace-Lorraine, 58 Bourgogne, 59 Loire, 60 Aquitaine, 61 Midi-Pyrénées, 62 Rhone-Alpes, 63 Provence, 64 Bretagne and 65 Corsica. No asset should be granted to a state based only on its historical January 1970 owner.
- Recipient-approved party agreements must use a distinct France focus as the offer source and the named partner's recipient decision. Preserve the current bilateral runtime rules: fresh recipient consent, pairwise peace, mutual native embargo check, all four mirrored Imperia sanction families, permanent treaty lifetime, suspension and restoration on eligibility changes, and actual parent/member spirit requirements. A government change does not cancel a signed agreement; it does suspend the party-specific programme while that party is out of office. Preview both countries' full native numeric effects before acceptance.

| `add_doctrine_cost_reduction` | Distinct from research bonuses; finite doctrine grant with a doctrine category and one-use receipt. | `operative_slot` | Flat integer agent-slot count, not a percentage. The verified palette is source-supported, not game-balance-certified. A listed provider proves that MD defines and uses a key; it does not prove a proposed value is balanced in a particular route. No campaign-wide cap has been certified.

## Candidate mutually exclusive pairs

These are mechanical frames for historical review, not approved focus names or numbers. Each pair should have two visibly different deliverables and an explicit opportunity cost. Small domestic packages can target roughly 0.10-0.30% of the payer's current GDP once; a continuing staffed service should use a small annual GDP share divided by 52, rather than a flat Treasury debit. Exact rates belong in the proposal and need route-total review.

| Party and exact gate | Candidate course A | Candidate course B | Native palette and overlap guard |
|---|---|---|---|
| RI / slot 2 | SME equipment-credit guarantee: one industrial complex in a verified provincial industrial state, then one finite `CAT_industry` bonus. | Artisan bookkeeping and export-certification office: one office in a separately verified state, then one finite `CAT_electronics` bonus. | Asset + finite research are distinct from another country-wide output modifier. Current RI already has credit expansion, sound-franc and Business rewards; do not add another permanent office/industrial productivity spirit. A credit guarantee is not free income. |

These frames are deliberately conservative about “creative” modifier use: not every institution has a dedicated MD modifier. Use a finite study, actual building, native expense or decision unlock only when its concrete effect previews accurately. If none represents the policy faithfully, keep the reward as a visible decision or narrow asset rather than attaching an unrelated combat, influence or productivity stat.

## Existing decision and agreement integration

The current France design already includes five authored decision records: `COLDWAR_FRA2_energy_audit`, `COLDWAR_FRA2_fund_reactor`, `COLDWAR_FRA2_community_negotiations`, `COLDWAR_FRA2P_participation_workshops`, and `COLDWAR_FRA2P_regional_conversion`. Reuse only where a party route genuinely makes the decision relevant; retain its exact focus, party, ownership/full-control, technology and capacity requirements. These are not generic native upstream decisions and must not be advertised as such.

The main MD France tree has distinct decision-category unlock precedents in `common/national_focus/05_france.txt:618,1376,1938,3985,6601,7745,11110,12148,13173,16993,18298`. These are later or specialized branches; do not imply they are available to the 1970 scenario unless the linked category, decision and prerequisites are loaded and reachable. The new party revision should not add a custom approval flow for domestic policies. Cross-border agreements retain actual recipient consent and the bilateral lifecycle already audited in `mechanics_reference.md`.
