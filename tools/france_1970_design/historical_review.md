# Historical review: France concept FRA2

**Scope.** Historical-fit review of `concept.json` only. No concept, party registry, or game code was changed. The focus count, effect amounts, and balance values remain proposals, not historical statistics.

## Overall assessment

The broad model is historically grounded: the 1970 state begins with Pompidou, Chaban-Delmas and a strong UDR majority; it treats the 1969 franc adjustment and May 1968 settlement as recent background; and it separates the later 1973 oil shock from starting conditions. The nine party programmes are appropriate as seven January 1970 parties plus future ecological and FN accessions. Keeping Michel Debré inside UDR and orthodox Communists inside PCF avoids inventing parliamentary parties. The CIR belongs as a reference within PS's pre-Epinay story, not as a separate governing slot.

The main historical risk is timing. Several later historical precedents are available as soon as their party branch is open, even though the concept itself correctly identifies them as later. Add date/event gates or label those early completions clearly as alternate history. A second risk is the starting package: its stacked national penalties can make a high-growth 1970 France read as already stagnant. A third is the diplomacy branch: many historically signed projects are framed as new offers requiring consent.

## What fits well

- **Starting parties and institutions:** UDR, RI/FNRI, CD, Radical Party, PS under Alain Savary, PCF under Waldeck Rochet, PSU and CID fit a January 1970 frame. CDP cooperation within a CD branch is a possible representation choice, but must say it is a reintegration or Duhamel-style choice: Duhamel's CDP had left the CD and joined Pompidou's majority in 1969. CIR is correctly kept within the PS path until the 1971 Epinay merger. Debré and PCF orthodoxies are better represented as factions than separate party branches.
- **Chaban's programme:** “Contracts for Progress,” “The New Society,” technical training, ORTF autonomy, labour bargaining, regional development and public-enterprise reform all have direct precedents in his September 1969 programme. The presidential-industry alternative also matches the tension between Chaban's reform opening and Pompidou's executive control and industrial strategy.
- **Economic starting point:** Plan Calcul, DATAR-type regional planning, industrial investment, agricultural restructuring and export competitiveness fit the strong post-devaluation economy. The concept correctly identifies an actual technician bottleneck and rural/industrial regional mismatch instead of claiming that France was already deindustrialized.
- **Future social and economic policies:** The PS path correctly allows Epinay/refoundation in 1971, a left common programme in 1972, public ownership and demand expansion, then a 1983 monetary/fiscal turn. The PCF path presents pluralist and Soviet-aligned choices as internal alternatives. The PSU path treats self-management as a developing option rather than a settled 1970 doctrine.
- **Later movements:** The ecology branch correctly calls for an alternative electoral accession. René Dumont's 1974 candidacy was an important entry into national politics, but it was not a 1970 governing party. The FN branch has the right 5 October 1972 foundation guard and does not describe it as a major January 1970 party. CID is the right starting slot.
- **Fifth Republic and defense:** The executive/parliamentary split, independent deterrent, conscription, national procurement and outside-NATO-integrated-command posture fit the period. The concept does not grant actual nuclear assets or technology by focus.

## Corrections and gates before implementation

1. **The UDF/RPR realignment needs to be represented.** UDR becomes RPR in December 1976; the CD and CDP merge into CDS in May 1976; the RI becomes the Parti républicain in 1977; and the UDF is founded on 1 February 1978 as a federation of non-Gaullist parties. RPR-UDF win the 1986 legislative majority and produce the first cohabitation, 1986-88. The current tree mentions dated UDR-to-RPR identity but has no comparable UDF/CDS/federal-coalition path. Add dated identities and make coalition cooperation distinct from the party-in-power trigger. [RPR history](https://www.larousse.fr/encyclopedie/divers/RPR/141728); [CD/CDP to CDS, French National Archives](https://rdf.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/garance/entities/agent/050624/); [UDF history](https://www.larousse.fr/encyclopedie/divers/UDF/147896); [1986 cohabitation, French government](https://www.info.gouv.fr/les-anciens-premiers-et-premieres-ministres-de-la-ve-republique/jacques-chirac-1986-1988).
2. **Date the later party branches and succession.** The concept spans 1970-90 but should make the key transitions readable: Chaban leaves Matignon in July 1972; Pompidou dies in April 1974 and Giscard wins the presidency; Mitterrand wins in 1981; RPR-UDF win in 1986; Mitterrand wins again in 1988. A national tree need not recreate every election focus, but it needs to represent the different governing coalitions and at least the 1986 cohabitation as an institutional contingency. [French National Assembly, political calendar](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/elections/dates.asp).
3. **Gate specifically later policies.** “The Defferre Reforms” refers to laws enacted in 1982-83; make their historical route unavailable before 1981/82, unless early decentralization is deliberately an alternate act. “Elected Regional Councils” also needs a dated distinction: regional public establishments date from 1972, while regions as elected territorial collectivities came with the 1982 reform, with direct elections in 1986. Similarly, keep the 1974-76 Giscard liberal/civil reforms after his election. [French Interior Ministry, decentralization chronology](https://www.interieur.gouv.fr/fr/Archives/Archives-des-dossiers/2012-Dossiers/La-decentralisation-a-30-ans).
4. **Gate the self-management and feminist movements.** “Factories Under Self-Management” should not be an immediately accessible January 1970 historical outcome. PSU made autogestion a defining doctrine in December 1972; Lip's self-managed production was 1973. The tree also omits the Mouvement de libération des femmes (MLF), whose first public meeting was in spring 1970, followed by the 1971 Manifesto of the 343 and later abortion-rights organization. The civil-liberties branch can carry some reforms, but the MLF and reproductive rights are important movements in a 1970-90 society tree. [CNRS-affiliated history of PSU and autogestion](https://histoire-sociale.cnrs.fr/amp/le-psu-et-lautogestion/); [French National Assembly, movement toward the Veil law](https://www2.assemblee-nationale.fr/14/evenements/2015/anniversaire-loi-veil/la-marche-vers-la-loi/vers-la-loi); [Library of Congress, MLF timeline](https://guides.loc.gov/feminism-french-women-history/20th-century).
5. **Clarify the environmental branch's party chronology.** Dumont's 1974 presidential campaign was an early national environmental intervention, not a party accession; the first national movement followed. Les Verts formed in 1984. Keep the current alternate accession path, but provide a 1974 movement/policy stage before a later party can govern. [French ecological movement history](https://commissions.lesecologistes.fr/pages/1ZHfjWztraXEMEG7nlWmt8/histoire-de-l-ecologie-politique); [Ministry archives on Les Verts, 1984](https://www.archives.developpement-durable.gouv.fr/fonds-europe-ecologie-les-verts-secretariat-a48139.html).
6. **Reduce or rebalance the stacked starting maluses.** In 1970 industrial value-added rose 6.9%; average GDP growth for 1970-73 was 5.4%; and unemployment was around 1.6% on the OECD series, despite severe regional/skills mismatch. The six spirits currently imply repeated stability loss, two manufacturing-efficiency penalties, an infrastructure penalty, an education efficiency penalty, elevated bureaucracy, higher energy use and more expensive equipment. Some tension is real, but broad national penalties for urban and regional problems, social settlement and energy exposure may overstate present crisis. “Unfinished Settlement of Grenelle” can model residual bargaining conflict, but the settlement was reached and output was strong. “Uneven Modernisation” should not imply nationwide infrastructure construction was inefficient. “The Price of Independence” can express the deterrent's budget opportunity cost, but a universal equipment-cost modifier does not target that trade-off. Preserve strong growth and low unemployment while locating disadvantages in specific, defensible constraints. [INSEE, 1970 industrial report](https://www.persee.fr/doc/estat_0336-1454_1971_num_28_1_2143); [INSEE, France's 1970-73 external performance](https://www.persee.fr/doc/estat_0336-1454_1977_num_94_1_6262); [OECD, France 1975 unemployment series](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/1975/01/oecd-economic-surveys-france-1975_g1g16f0b/eco_surveys-fra-1975-en.pdf).
7. **Qualify postcolonial recognition and amnesty.** The 1962, 1964 and 1968 amnesties are factual precedents. However, another blanket amnesty in 1970 is a proposed alternate measure, not a routine next step. Similarly, state recognition of the Algerian War and the distinct experiences of harkis, repatriates and Algerian families is a valuable alternate policy, but was politically and legally delayed. Label these as competing future choices with their constituencies and unresolved claims, not as an already pending national consensus. Avoid a blanket stability reward for closing memory disputes.
8. **Correct the diplomacy branch's baseline status.** A300 (29 May 1969), Concorde (1962 treaty; first flight 1969), Jaguar (1965 memorandum), Symphonie (1967 Franco-German agreement) and Franco-Soviet science/technology (1966 agreement, 1970 dialogue protocol) were already actual programmes or frameworks. In January 1970 these are continuation, implementation or renegotiation choices, not proposals for a new historical treaty. Do not require a fictional partner acceptance to validate signed historical commitments. A new RP add-on or changed commercial/security terms can still be an explicitly alternate proposal.
   - The A300 agreement is a sound historical precedent, but Airbus Industrie was not created until December 1970. Title it as implementation/launch if after that date, or alternate early expansion if before.
   - Concorde was already in flight testing; “deepen” or renew with a new acceptance is an alternate extension, not the actual project start.
   - Jaguar cooperation was already agreed; distinguish deployment/procurement from renegotiating its existing MOU.
   - Ariane is properly marked later/alternate, but the historical programme was a multilateral European effort as a European programme approved by the European Space Conference in July 1973 and then managed by ESRO, the ESA predecessor, not a Franco-German bilateral launcher deal. [ESA, Ariane origins and approval](https://www.esa.int/About_Us/50_years_of_ESA/Thirty-five_years_of_Ariane_how_Ariane_was_born). The branch can offer bilateral terms as alternate history, but should not imply that is the historical precedent.
   - France's energy independence ratio was roughly 22% in 1973; oil dependence is a real vulnerability, but the 1973/74 shock and Messmer acceleration are future events from January 1970. The concept's “no automatic 1973 shock” caveat is right. Make the historically accelerated reactor programme contingent on the shock, while allowing earlier civil nuclear investment as a separate national policy.
   - Algeria's 1965 hydrocarbons accord and 1968 migration agreement are real and distinct. The 1965 accord was a joint framework under Algeria's resource sovereignty, not French ownership. A proposed 1970 rework is alternate; later 1971 nationalizations and renegotiation should be dated. The Iranian supply focus is plausible but should identify the proposed commodity/technology basis instead of presenting a generic oil accord as an established precedent.
   - Senegal did not have a 1963 defense treaty. Senegal inherited 1960 cooperation agreements following the Mali Federation's breakup; the formal defense agreement dates to 1974. The concept correctly calls a 1970 renegotiation proposed, but should cite the 1960 framework in the focus basis and present equality/mutual respect as an alternate renegotiation inspired by the later treaty.
   - “The Hague and the Communities” is a post-summit follow-through, not accession: France was already an EEC founding member. The December 1969 summit set the agenda of completion, deepening and enlargement. Its gates should be actual EEC membership and relevant negotiations, not completion of both US and Soviet science treaties.

## Party registry work required before implementation

The concept should wait on a historically corrected France registry, not install around the current `FRA.json` as-is. Slots 8-12 currently describe Iranian movements, transnational Islamist groups and a Paris mosque/student association as major France party slots; remove or replace them with French political, labour, feminist or regional organizations if needed. Add the omitted CIR as part of the 1970 PS history and date its 1971 merger. Keep Debré as an internal UDR faction and PCF orthodoxy as an internal PCF faction. Correct the radical timeline: 1972 split first under a different name, MRG label in 1973. Keep MRG, RPR, CDS, UDF, Les Verts and FN as dated later identities rather than start parties. The 1970 CID identity should not inherit FN content. CD and CDP need separate 1970 identities, with their 1976 merger reflected before CDS/UDF integration.

For scope, add the MLF and union/labour movement (CGT, CFDT, FO) as influential social actors or event/decision strands without pretending they are parties. Regionalist/ecological movements and the 1980s cohabitation/re-alignment also deserve a dated route if the tree claims full coverage to 1990. These are the main gaps; avoid filling slots with foreign religious currents merely to use available outlooks.
