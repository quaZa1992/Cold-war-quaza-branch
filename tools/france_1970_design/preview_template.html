France: A Republic Between Two Eras

January 1970 to 1990 · IMPERIA COLD WAR · Concept for approval

A prosperous country with unresolved conflicts

Pompidou governs with Chaban-Delmas and a strong UDR majority. Rapid growth, advanced public research and major industrial projects coexist with inflation, a narrow technical training system, uneven regional development and unresolved workplace tensions after 1968.

Cheap imported oil supports expansion but exposes transport and industry to external supply. Housing shortages, migrant living conditions, rural change and the legacies of Algeria make the distribution of prosperity a political question. The 1973 oil shock has not happened.

Eight proposed starting spirits. Values are balance proposals. Existing French Franc, religious identity, internal factions and native economic costs remain separate.

Named proposals, real recipient consent

A French focus presents a new stage or revision of cooperation. The named RP recipient receives Accept and Decline decisions. Both sides' complete prospective terms are visible before either government commits.

Signed agreements have no fixed expiry. Bilateral war, a native embargo, or diplomatic, financial, economic or military Imperia sanctions in either direction suspend both spirits and their weekly streams. Valid conditions restore both without repeating setup payments, assets or research. Dependent agreements require the actual active parent spirits.

Each signatory pays its own GDP share. Weekly programme funding is the stated annual GDP appropriation divided by 52, recalculated from that country's current GDP. Every building level includes an extra shared slot in the same state.
