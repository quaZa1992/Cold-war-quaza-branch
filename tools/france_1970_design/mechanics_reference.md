## Verified effect palette and use

The executable-key inventory is in `verified_effects.json`. Key families with a live provider and consumer suitable to France include factory output/productivity and production efficiency growth/cap; civilian and dockyard capacity; industrial research and named technology bonuses; doctrine cost and finite doctrine awards; taxes, treasury and recurring weekly budgets; bureaucracy and education expenditure; productivity growth; agriculture and local resources; energy production/use and nuclear development; investment capacity; army, navy and air operating costs; logistics, fuel and accident rates; stability, war support, unrest/resistance, and organization/reinforcement; diplomacy and foreign influence; and equipment-design bonuses. This does not make every key useful in every campaign. Follow each key's stated base, sign, cap, scope and stacked providers.

Construction-speed factors are supported as exact native keys: `production_speed_buildings_factor`, `production_speed_industrial_complex_factor`, `production_speed_infrastructure_factor`, `production_speed_arms_factory_factor`, `production_speed_dockyard_factor`, and `production_speed_nuclear_reactor_factor`. France's own MD idea file contains providers for each. These affect build time for the named category; they do not grant the building. The distinct `nuclear_energy_generation_modifier` multiplies reactor output in the current energy formula, after the base/reactor/state terms and before fuel-availability and manpower-fulfilment multipliers (`common/scripted_effects/!_energy_effects.txt:474-499`, definition `common/modifier_definitions/energy_modifier_definitions.txt:122-127`). Faster reactor construction and more energy per active reactor are separate policies. The audited military keys include `army_org_factor`, `army_core_defence_factor`, `army_morale_factor`, `supply_consumption_factor`, `equipment_cost_multiplier_modifier`, and the exact army training key `training_time_army_factor`. The guessed unsuffixed `training_time_factor` has no confirmed provider and should not be used.

For scoped research grants, use `add_tech_bonus` with existing technology category tags: `CAT_electronics`, `CAT_industry`, `CAT_armor`, `CAT_aircraft`, `CAT_naval` and `CAT_nuclear_reactors`. Each is present on real technologies in the current tree (`engineering.txt:71`, `industry.txt:68`, `armor.txt:60`, `BBA_aircraft.txt:93`, `naval.txt:67`, `industry.txt:4875`) and registered in `technology_tags/00_technology.txt:25-204`. France directly uses industry, armor and nuclear categories in its native focus tree (`05_france.txt:13237, 15689, 16144`). The standard grant fields are `name`, `bonus`, `uses` and `category`. Do not use research categories as doctrine categories. A `CAT_electronics` bonus is valid against its real technologies, although no France focus using that exact grant tag was found in the inspected tree.

Consumer goods are supported: use the native `consumer_goods_factor` country modifier, not an imagined custom consumer-goods key. The live main MD source has many country and balance-of-power idea providers (for example `common/ideas/AA_law_economics.txt`, `common/bop/CHI.txt`); native engine label and formatter must be checked in the current localisation before presenting a value. It changes the economy's consumer-goods share, so a lower value increases free civilian factories; it is neither direct output nor a cash income stream.

Economy modifiers that sound like generic boosts have distinct consumers. `production_factory_efficiency_gain_factor` changes production-efficiency growth, `production_factory_max_efficiency_factor` changes its ceiling, `industrial_capacity_factory` is factory output, `civilian_factories_productivity` changes civilian-factory productivity, and `country_productivity_growth_modifier` affects the productivity-growth system. They are not interchangeable. `productivity_growth_modifier` is a flat monthly base input and then passes through the country/state calculation. Taxes are read by the Imperia weekly money calculation; `tax_gain_multiplier_modifier` multiplies its computed tax base. Bureaucracy, education, social spending and defence budgets use distinct MD expense formulas, not HOI4 consumer-goods modifiers. `stability_factor` is a native national modifier with French idea providers; `resistance_growth` changes growth in occupied states and is not a domestic unrest measure.

Useful current France policy anchors include the EU focus `FRA_strenght_throught_EU` at `common/national_focus/05_france.txt:1432` and its `FRA_EU_strong` idea in `common/ideas/05_france.txt:3586`; the nuclear/EU focus `FRA_nuclear_eu` at `05_france.txt:2165` and its explicit reactor-construction and nuclear-generation dynamic values at `:2192-2193`; and the arms industry example `FRA_strat_indus` at `05_france.txt:2422`, which grants the named production-efficiency cap spirit. France's aerospace chain includes `FRA_boreal_space` (`05_france.txt:6143`), `FRA_the_space_program` (`:16976`), and `FRA_cnes_budgeting` (`:17162`). The space-agency idea provider `FRA_idea_space_agency_2` (`common/ideas/05_france.txt:171-179`) bundles +1% consumer-goods share with political-power, research and launch-vehicle production trade-offs. These are live upstream code examples, not proof that those later-era focus gates are reachable in the January 1970 scenario.

For ongoing costs, preserve the native weekly unit. The money calculation is in billions per week (`common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt`, expense/tax readers around lines 302-800 and expense aggregation around 3697-3812). Bureaucracy and education multiplier readers occur around 3367 and 3430. Military/service payroll and social-cost modifier definitions have their own readers; only expose a budget key after following that exact formula. A value such as 0.05 on a weekly expense multiplier means a five percent change to that existing bill, not 0.05 billion per week. A direct recurring budget literal must be a weekly amount in MD units; source annual budgets need conversion. Scripted budget literals are limited to five fractional digits.

The France-specific direct recurring stream precedent is NEPAD: `FRA_nepad_sponsor_weekly_cost` is persistent on France, with increments of 0.15 at `common/decisions/France.txt:668,722,776,829`; the weekly expense aggregator adds that variable to `additional_expenses_rate` when `original_tag = FRA` and the NEPAD dynamic modifier is active (`common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt:4431-4435`). The aggregator resets and rebuilds `additional_expenses_rate` at `:3699+`, and the treasury/display calculation consumes it later (`:4714+`). Treat the country's persistent programme variable as the source of truth and add it once to the weekly aggregate under its active-programme guard; do not store recurring cost in the already-reset aggregate. I found no standalone Cuba-specific weekly expense variable/reader in Cuba's current 1970 scripts; Cyprus R2's separate stream is joined at `00_money_system.txt:4605`.

Recommended first-pass scale for a 1970 France focus draft, before route-stack calculation: use +2% to +5% for a narrow one-spirit sector improvement, +5% to +10% only for a major national reform with a concrete cost or opportunity cost, and generally stay at or below the comparable upstream national examples in the JSON. Use integer +1 for investment/assistance slots where a real slot is the intended result. Prefer finite research/doctrine awards with the exact category and uses over large permanent research multipliers. Do not stack values across mutually exclusive ruling parties; do check compatible focuses, starting spirits, permanent agreements and concurrent programs together. No campaign-wide numeric cap is certified by this source audit.

## Limits of this audit

This is source verification, not a HOI4 load, tooltip screenshot, saved-game or multiplayer test. A native modifier declaration or localization proves the key is available and displayable, not a designer's desired gameplay impact. The game binary implements engine-owned formulas; those formulas are not independently proven by text search. The France palette should be cross-checked against the final catalogue and any emitted native rewards, with per-route stacking, one-time/recurring distinction, and the exact 1970 party gate. No loaded mod file or generator was changed for this reference.
