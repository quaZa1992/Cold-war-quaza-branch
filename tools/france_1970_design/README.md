## January 1970: strengths and disadvantages

France begins with an unusually strong presidency and a large parliamentary majority. This provides genuine capacity to act, but leaves parliament, regional institutions and public broadcasting under a concentrated executive. Chaban's New Society is a concrete reformist alternative within the governing movement, not an invented ideology.

Growth and technological ambition must remain visible. INSEE's retrospective reports industrial value-added growth of 6.9% in 1970. This is not an already deindustrialised, mass-unemployment economy. Export orders benefit from the 1969 franc adjustment, while restrictive credit and inflation complicate domestic investment. The official parity fell 11.11%; the inverse francs-per-dollar quotation rose 12.5%. These are two expressions of the same adjustment.

The difficult starting conditions are institutional and distributive:

1. Grenelle settled immediate wage demands without ending conflict over authority and representation at work. CGT, CFDT, FO, employers and the state remain distinct actors.
2. Advanced firms and research centres coexist with an insufficiently broad technical training system and crowded universities. The Faure reform already exists; funding and implementation are the policy questions.
3. Modern exporters, agricultural transformation and major public projects create regional winners and losers. Older coal, textile and steel districts face vulnerabilities before the later industrial crisis.
4. Rapid urbanisation brings housing and service shortages. Migrant labour is central to production, while bidonvilles and poor accommodation reveal the limits of integration policy.
5. Oil-intensive transport and industry expose France to foreign supply and price changes. The actual 1973 shock is a future contingency, not a January 1970 penalty silently imported from later history.
6. The independent deterrent and national procurement preserve strategic choice at a substantial opportunity cost for conventional forces and civilian investment.
7. Algeria, repatriation, harkis and overseas commitments leave different and sometimes conflicting domestic claims on the state.
8. Women face persistent social and economic inequalities; labour, feminist and student movements belong to the political setting without being falsely represented as parliamentary parties.

These conditions are represented by eight proposed starting spirits. Their effects are balance abstractions, not empirical measurements. Starting stability totals -8 percentage points. The temporary devaluation settlement lasts 270 days; the other problems have explicit reform remedies. No new negative workforce-size modifier is proposed. Native monetary, religious and faction ideas remain separate, and native nuclear costs must not be charged a second time.

Primary narrative anchors include [Chaban's September 1969 programme](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/histoire/chaban1969.asp), [INSEE's contemporary industrial assessment](https://www.persee.fr/doc/estat_0336-1454_1971_num_28_1_2143) and the [presidential chronology](https://www.elysee.fr/georges-pompidou). The full report records the remaining historical sources and qualifications.

The historical starting leader remains Georges Pompidou. RI/Giscard, CD/Lecanuet or Poher, PS/Savary and later Mitterrand, PCF/Rochet and Marchais, PSU/Rocard and later ecological and nationalist leaders require the existing election and succession machinery, not automatic focus accessions.

No Islamic party path is designed for France. Debre's orthodox Gaullists and communist orthodox cadres are internal programme alternatives rather than invented mass parties. CIR belongs in the PS refoundation narrative; CD and CDP need accurate distinct 1970 identities before their later merger. The current registry contains France-unrelated Iranian/Islamist entries and synthetic faction slots. These are identified for correction in the implementation proposal, not silently changed in this concept phase.

Later recompositions must preserve actual party identity: RPR and CDS in 1976, PR in 1977, UDF in 1978, and the governing-party distinction during the 1986 cohabitation. FN's content requires its real October 1972 identity; CID receipts never become FN rewards. Ecology is a later electoral alternative. Earlier enactment of elective reforms drawn from 1974 or 1982 is explicitly alternate history, not a claim those reforms occurred in January 1970. Historical succession and actual organisation foundations retain genuine dated conditions.

Earned party modifier pools are active only under their exact native ruling-party trigger, suspend outside government and return on re-entry. Every branch stays visible. Asset, cash, experience and finite research awards never replay. Shared institutions, durable national investments and signed international commitments are separate from the current party programme.

## Shared development and defence

Common national programmes carry real investment: regional infrastructure, industrial plants, technical colleges or mass universities, Plan Calcul, telecommunications, high-speed rail or motorways, agriculture and an energy strategy. The nuclear path unlocks a separately funded reactor project in Loire rather than granting reactors, uranium production or advanced technology for free. Every building level includes a same-state extra shared slot, granted first inside the same one-time transaction. Implementation must separately check ownership, control and native capacity.

Defence offers deterrent priority or conventional balance; trained citizen forces or a professional intervention core; national armour or shared components; Mirage development or consent-dependent Jaguar cooperation; and oceanic fleet capacity or maritime escorts. Research uses verified `CAT_armor`, `CAT_aircraft`, `CAT_industry`, `CAT_electronics` and `CAT_nuclear_reactors`. Existing doctrine, construction, personnel, safety, organisation and logistics keys replace invented military modifiers.

## Bilateral roleplay

Each of the nineteen offers originates in a distinct French focus and names its recipient. Only that recipient receives acceptance/refusal. No country-picker menu replaces French focus choices. Historical programmes such as A300, Concorde, Jaguar, Symphonie and Franco-Soviet cooperation are the basis for negotiated new stages, not unsigned historical treaties retroactively invalidated in 1970. Ariane's actual origin is multilateral; the proposed bilateral launcher package is explicitly alternate history.

Agreement families prevent stacking rival packages. Airbus emphasises industrial productivity; Concorde research. Algeria offers a deeper technical-education follow-up; Iran gives France more immediate energy benefit at lower running cost with reduced influence. Senegal offers civilian or defence cooperation. Both signatories see exact setup, weekly costs, research, assets and modifiers directly in the originating focus's `If they accept` preview, even when the offer is unavailable or unsigned.

Signed spirits have no expiry. Bilateral war, missing signatories, native embargo and any of the four Imperia sanction classes in either direction suspend both sides. Ending the condition restores both spirits and running streams without repeating setup fees, finite research or assets. Algerian technical institutes require both actual active parent agreement spirits. Third-country wars do not invalidate a peaceful bilateral pair.

All Treasury charges are percentages of the payer own current GDP. Standing programme budgets are annual GDP percentages divided by 52 and recalculated weekly in the native `additional_expenses_rate` path. Runtime share literals have at most five fractional digits. The same live-GDP contract applies separately to both signatories. Native expense multipliers remain distinct percentages of their existing bills. Current terms and source checks are in `GDP_REWARD_REVISION.md` and `GDP_COST_REFERENCE.md`; older fixed-dollar envelope totals are superseded.
