# France 1970-1990: reward precedents for differentiated focus outcomes

Research and recommendations only. This note does not change `concept.json`, builder files or live mod content. The proposals identify historically supported tangible outcomes; they do not claim an exact national budget cost or justify a particular game modifier. All numeric costs, magnitudes and modifier values remain balance proposals. Bilateral projects use the historical baseline where one already exists; any new terms, money transfers or partner obligations still require the partner country's consent.

## Recommended policy outcomes

1. **`housing` — serviced housing and migrant-worker accommodation.** In January 1970, SONACOTRA was already an operating national institution, founded in 1956 under Eugène Claudius-Petit, with a record in workers' hostels, transitional housing, rehousing repatriates and clearing bidonvilles. A tangible reward could be a state housing programme plus serviced urban infrastructure, and a dated follow-on that converts temporary accommodation into ordinary social housing. Link its social cost to housing finance and local services, not to workforce-size penalties. The Nanterre bidonvilles make the social need concrete. [FranceArchives, Claudius-Petit and SONACOTRA](https://francearchives.gouv.fr/findingaid/3a20b6a79c047ea24965cf9a9475485178934922); [Hauts-de-Seine Archives, Nanterre bidonvilles](https://archives.hauts-de-seine.fr/n/bidonvilles/n%3A247).

2. **`new_society` / `progress` versus `presidential_industry` — two distinct governing contracts.** The actual Chaban-Delmas programme (1969-72) proposed bargaining, improved technical education, administrative and university reform, regional development and less hierarchical government. Make its tangible outcome an employer-union consultation framework and training capacity, with implementation risk if social partners do not cooperate. The presidential-industry route should instead strengthen planning/procurement and export-oriented industrial investment, accepting weaker labor voice or regional spread. The 1969 programme is direct evidence for the New Society route, not for every later Chaban measure. [French National Assembly, Chaban's 1969 programme](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/histoire/chaban1969.asp).

3. **`participation` — enforce and extend profit-sharing institutions, not universal worker ownership.** Profit-sharing (intéressement) had existed as an optional agreement since 1959. The 17 August 1967 ordinance established mandatory participation for qualifying large firms, with reserves intended to share results and build investment savings. A Gaullist reward can increase coverage, workplace representation or employee savings through those existing instruments. It should not promise binding worker control as the automatic result: the statute shares profits and savings, not managerial sovereignty. Contrast this with PSU self-management and PCF worker-control proposals. [French Government Inspectorate report on participation's 1959/1967 legal basis](https://www.vie-publique.fr/files/rapport/pdf/144000339.pdf).

4. **`mutuals` / `social_market` — improve existing social-insurance institutions.** The 1967 Jeanneney ordinances created distinct health, old-age and family branches and national funds, with organized roles for labor and employer representatives. A plausible outcome is stronger local caisse/hospital capacity or improved pension and family administration. An opposition-centre route should preserve the mutual and associative role while bargaining over state contributions. Do not imply social insurance itself was newly invented by this focus. [French Social Security, chronology](https://www.securite-sociale.fr/la-secu-cest-quoi/histoire/les-grandes-dates); [French Social Security, governance](https://www.securite-sociale.fr/la-secu-en-detail/acteurs/gouvernance).

5. **`technical_colleges` / `mass_universities` — two separate skills pipelines.** The Faure law of 12 November 1968 had already restructured universities, created elected governance, and allowed multidisciplinary and experimental institutions. The next reward is funding and staffing for its implementation, not “university autonomy” granted again. Separately, technical instruction can expand IUT and engineering-technician training; engineering and skilled-worker shortages were identified in the OECD's contemporary assessment. Differentiate broad university access from targeted technician output. [French National Assembly, Faure and the 1968 law](https://www2.assemblee-nationale.fr/sycomore/fiche/2922); [OECD, France 1972 survey](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/1972/02/oecd-economic-surveys-france-1972_g1g16e93/eco_surveys-fra-1972-en.pdf).

6. **`sixth_plan`, `regional_links`, `regional_industry` versus `champion_model` — decentralized investment versus concentration.** The Sixth Plan (1971-75) was approved by law on 9 July 1970. DATAR and regional-development finance already pursued industrial decentralization; by 1975 that policy had generated substantial provincial employment, while investment remained geographically uneven. Make the regional route provide transport links, industrial-site preparation and loans/primes for regional firms. Make the champion route provide concentrated procurement and capital for nationally competitive firms, with gains in scale but continued regional concentration. The Fos industrial-port zone belongs to this national planning history: its development was authorized in the 1960s, with major works and land decisions in 1967-71 and the first large industrial complex maturing later. A dedicated Fos policy node would be historically coherent if the designer wants to represent a port-scale project; otherwise place it under regional industry rather than granting a completed port. [INSEE archive on the Sixth Plan](https://www.bnsp.insee.fr/ark%3A/12148/bc6p070rsvr.pdf); [INSEE, industrial regional development policy](https://www.bnsp.insee.fr/ark%3A/12148/bc6p06xvp21.pdf); [French Senate report, Fos port works](https://www.senat.fr/rap/1969-1970/i1969_1970_0058_09.pdf).

7. **`plan_calcul` / `national_computers` versus `open_computers` — institutional R&D, not instant autonomy.** The Plan Calcul began in 1966-67; IRIA was created in 1967 to develop computing research, and a CII research center opened in Grenoble in 1970 with IMAG. A reward may establish an IRIA/CII research and procurement programme, training posts or a computing lab. The domestic-systems route buys national supply security at sustained public-procurement cost; the compatible-systems route allows international standards and components while preserving specialist French research. Neither path should instantly grant advanced computers or erase foreign competition. [INRIA history of Plan Calcul and IRIA](https://inria.fr/sites/default/files/2022-12/StratTerr_ANG_Def.pdf); [Grenoble computer-history project, CII centre](https://aconit.inria.fr/omeka/exhibits/show/informatique-grenoble/consolidation/cii.html).

8. **`telecoms` — national network modernization through PTT/CNET and domestic suppliers.** The state had PTT engineering, CNET research and French telecommunications manufacturers. The Direction Générale des Télécommunications' 1971-78 records document network-equipment procurement and the CIT-Alcatel/CGCT supplier base. A 1970s outcome can fund exchanges, long-distance lines, switching research and procurement series. A later 1970s telephone-service acceleration is a dated continuation, not a completed January 1970 network. Differentiate public coverage from private supplier autonomy; do not represent a modern digital network as an immediate reward. [FranceArchives, Direction Générale des Télécommunications procurement records, 1971-78](https://francearchives.gouv.fr/findingaid/409ad175bbc6d199719977587eb8ced9b685ae49/).

9. **`transport_choice` → `tgv` or `motorways` — experimental railway versus dispersed road access.** Public authorities approved the Paris-Lyon high-speed study in 1970; SNCF's TGV 001 experimental train emerged in 1971, with commercial service only in 1981. A credible focus grants engineering studies, prototype/development capacity or a later rail-construction project after prerequisites; it should not instantly create a working high-speed rail network. The motorway route can instead add road access to smaller industrial centres, with petroleum exposure as a later cost. [SNCF Patrimoine, TGV chronology and 1970 approval](https://patrimoine.sncf.com/2024/10/31/le-premier-tgv-ne-date-pas-de-1981-mais-de-1971/); [SNCF Archives, conception to 1981 service](https://www.archives.sncf.com/il-etait-une-fois-le-tgv-les-pionniers-racontent-2/).

10. **`nuclear_scale` versus `diverse_energy` — pre-shock preparation, then dated acceleration.** EDF decided in 1970 to shift from the UNGG gas-graphite line toward pressurized-water reactors. This supports a real 1970 design choice: standardize on PWR technology, train engineers and build supply-chain capability. It does not support starting a vast completed power fleet or using the 1973 oil shock as the 1970 justification. The Messmer programme of 1974 was the post-shock acceleration and should be date-gated. An alternative can diversify fuel supply and fund conservation without claiming energy independence. [CEA, French reactor programme and 1970 PWR decision](https://www.cea.fr/Pages/innovation-industrie/transferts-industriels/les-reacteurs-electronucleaires.aspx); [EDF, 1974 Messmer programme](https://www.edf.fr/groupe-edf/le-groupe-edf/histoire).

11. **`conversion` — prepare older basins for transition, not compensate a deindustrialized 1970.** Older coal, textile and steel areas still produced, while French industrial output was growing. The 1970 reward should fund industrial conversion studies, modernization grants and retraining in named exposed basins; actual rescue, closure and conversion packages should be date- and crisis-gated in the late 1970s/1980s. Distinguish transition readiness from national deindustrialization, which was not yet the starting condition. [INSEE, industrial regional policy and later conversion support](https://www.bnsp.insee.fr/ark%3A/12148/bc6p06xvp21.pdf); [existing France design research, 1970 industrial output and Longwy timeline](history_research.md).

12. **`socialist_refoundation` / `common_programme` — CIR integration, then a negotiated programme.** The PS had formed in 1969 under Alain Savary; Mitterrand's CIR remained a distinct organization until he joined the PS with it at the June 1971 Épinay congress. The 1972 Programme commun was a later PS-PCF-left-radical agreement. Represent CIR accession or coalition-building as an organizational change with a tangible campaigning/party-organization outcome, then a negotiated policy package; avoid awarding the whole Common Programme in 1970 or claiming the PS was already Mitterrand's consolidated party. [French National Assembly, Mitterrand, CIR and Épinay chronology](https://www2.assemblee-nationale.fr/sycomore/fiche/5312); [1972 Common Programme text and date](https://www.lemonde.fr/archives/article/1972/06/29/le-programme-commun-de-gouvernement-etabli-par-les-delegations-du-parti-socialiste-et-du-parti-communiste-francais_3141472_1819218.html).

13. **`self_management` — PSU doctrine from late 1972; Lip as 1973 precedent.** PSU made autogestion a defining course at its December 1972 congress. Lip workers' occupation and production of watches in 1973 provided a celebrated practical example, not proof that worker-managed firms can be implemented nationwide without finance or market access. A meaningful focus reward can support a cooperative-credit scheme, worker-led pilot and legal/technical advisory capacity. Keep firm-level experiments distinct from the proposed national socialist ownership transfer. [Centre d'histoire sociale, PSU and autogestion](https://histoire-sociale.cnrs.fr/amp/le-psu-et-lautogestion/); [Fondation Jean-Jaurès, Lip and self-management](https://www.jean-jaures.org/publication/juin-1975-le-parti-socialiste-et-lautogestion/).

14. **`municipal_socialism` / `workers_control` / `public_combine` — PCF municipal services versus national planning.** PCF-run municipalities and local networks are historically credible delivery institutions; for a concrete, cautious award use municipal housing, school, clinic, transport or cultural-facility projects and union representation, not an immediate nationwide “workers' state.” National public combines are a separate later state-led route with capital and administrative costs. The 1972 left programme can date and constrain a shared national platform, while the PCF's 1976 abandonment of “dictatorship of the proletariat” belongs to a later doctrinal branch. [Le Monde archive, Programme commun, June 1972](https://www.lemonde.fr/archives/article/1972/06/29/le-programme-commun-de-gouvernement-etabli-par-les-delegations-du-parti-socialiste-et-du-parti-communiste-francais_3141472_1819218.html); [Le Monde archive, Marchais and 1976 doctrine](https://www.lemonde.fr/archives/article/1976/01/09/les-declarations-du-secretaire-general-du-p-c-f-a-antenne-2-france-m-georges-marchais-renonce-a-la-formule-de-la-dictature-du-proletariat_3123810_1819218.html).

15. **`airbus`, `symphonie`, `ariane` — distinguish existing projects from new offers.** A300 already had its Franco-German launch agreement of 29 May 1969; Airbus Industrie as a consortium was formed 18 December 1970. The historical 1970 focus can fund France's national work share and industrial capacity or pursue an alternate re-negotiation; do not ask Germany to sign the original A300 agreement again. Symphonie was already an equal-funded Franco-German programme under the convention signed 6 June 1967; in 1970 it is under development, with launch access and use restrictions still unresolved. Its branches can fund ground stations, research/telecom applications or negotiate later launch arrangements with all affected states. Ariane is later: European launch policy was settled through the 1973 European Space Conference / ESRO process, so a France-Germany bilateral offer in 1970 should be labeled alternate, and a historical path should wait until 1973. [Airbus, 1969 agreement and 1970 GIE formation](https://www.airbus.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2009-05-airbus-celebrates-the-40th-anniversary-of-its-first-aircraft); [ESA history, Franco-German Symphonie 1967 and launch restrictions](https://www.esa.int/esapub/hsr/HSR_37.pdf); [ESA Archives, 1970-73 European telecommunications and launcher choices](https://historicalarchives.esa.int/highlights-first-esro-telecommunications-studies-1967-1972).

## Reward-writing guardrails

- Use the historical institution or industrial deliverable as the reward: funds, procurement programmes, research centres, construction, equipment capacity, training places, service networks, bargaining institutions or dated party organization. Avoid substituting generic stability, political power or bureaucracy bonuses where a real output fits.
- Make branch contrast explicit in the outcome: concentrated champions versus regional conversion; national computer control versus standards-based procurement; public access versus cost restraint; autonomous French military industry versus pooled projects; union bargaining versus worker-managed experiments.
- Treat the source as support for the historical precedent, not a source of game costs. Do not invent Treasury costs from historical budgets without conversion evidence. Keep any agreed foreign contribution behind recipient approval and show only France's own prospective commitment as its decision's effects.
- Respect the chronology: Faure 1968 is implemented after start; Sixth Plan law is July 1970; A300 project is already agreed in 1969 and Airbus GIE forms December 1970; PSU autogestion is December 1972 and Lip in 1973; Messmer expansion follows the 1973 oil shock; Ariane is a 1973 European multilateral choice; TGV moves from 1970 approval to 1971 prototype and 1981 commercial line.
