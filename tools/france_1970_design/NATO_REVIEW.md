## Findings

- **Membership and command authority are represented consistently.** All 18 nodes require the actual `NATO_member` idea and `western_conservatism_are_in_power = yes`. The three policy strategies are mutually exclusive; every one retains membership and national nuclear decision authority. No new node calls `NATO_join`, `NATO_leave`, changes a faction, grants military access, or assigns French forces to an integrated command. That matches France's 1970 setup and the native separation documented in `NATO_MECHANICS.md`.
- **All 4 proposal focuses check both sides' membership at proposal time.** Rhine and European Land Standards check GER; Atlantic Naval Exercises and European Naval Industry check ENG. The corresponding member-only French followups additionally require the actual French treaty spirit and UDR in both conditions and activation conditions. Offer spirits are separate for France and recipient, each side has an explicit setup payment and weekly expense, and the proposal focus only unlocks a recipient-owned acceptance/refusal decision. The four agreements grant bilateral exercise, training, research, procurement, or industrial modifiers only. Their data does not grant NATO membership, command, basing, access, or a NATO-wide decision right.
- **The refreshed design now specifies recipient consent and NATO-specific suspension.** All four offers carry acceptance-time conditions that recheck France and the named partner's NATO membership, bilateral peace, no native embargo in either direction, no diplomatic/financial/economic/military Imperia sanction in either direction, recipient acceptance, and an unexpired, unapplied proposal. Their transaction rule calls for both setup charges and both signatories' finite rewards to apply atomically once, with signed receipts preventing replay. `rules.nato_lifecycle` now explicitly suspends both spirits and weekly streams if either country loses NATO membership, restores them without replaying setup, XP or research if membership returns, and never restores membership itself. This resolves the two design-specification gaps noted in the prior pass. Runtime implementation and execution testing are still required; these fields are not active game code. The agreement spirits' activation conditions check both signatories' actual membership.
- **Exact party gates are present.** The entry, two nuclear choices, three strategy choices, and all children carry `western_conservatism_are_in_power = yes`; French membership and actual active parent spirits gate every dependent programme. The group stays visible through its group trigger and contains no party-dependent branch visibility rule. The four offer nodes require the partner's actual `NATO_member` idea at proposal time.

## Effect and category audit

All modifier names found in the NATO nodes are valid native modifier keys in the local MD source or have direct native examples. I found no abstract `CAT_*` aliases. `CAT_armor`, `CAT_electronics`, and `CAT_naval` are registered in `common/technology_tags/00_technology.txt` (`:26`, `:205`, `:59`) and occur on real technologies in `common/technologies/armor.txt`, `common/technologies/engineering.txt`, `common/technologies/mdcw_historical_industry.txt`, and `common/technologies/naval.txt`. The tree's limited-use research and doctrine rewards are represented distinctly: technology bonuses use the real categories with one use; doctrine reductions use `land_doctrine` or `naval_doctrine` with explicit reduction and use count. Native examples and implementation notes are in `NATO_MECHANICS.md:36-39`; local France focus examples for the doctrine and technology operations are `common/national_focus/05_france.txt:16209-16217` and `:13235`/`:14733`/`:15242`.

The newer modifier keys are source-supported: `foreign_influence_defense_modifier` is defined in `common/modifier_definitions/influence_modifier_definitions.txt:1-6` and used by France (`common/ideas/05_france.txt:680-703`); `max_planning` has an existing France NATO example at `common/ideas/05_france.txt:706-716`; `industrial_capacity_factory` and `industrial_capacity_dockyard` appear in French ideas at `:2275-2276` and in MD industry technologies (`common/technologies/industry.txt:52-53`); `naval_accidents_chance` has native country-idea examples in the donor ideas cited in `NATO_MECHANICS.md`. `army_org_regain`, `supply_consumption_factor`, `equipment_cost_multiplier_modifier`, `bureaucracy_cost_multiplier_modifier`, and `navy_personnel_cost_multiplier_modifier` are likewise established native keys. Positive expense multipliers in the plan are costs, while negative equipment cost and supply-consumption values are savings. The relevant formulas and definitions are documented in `NATO_MECHANICS.md:26-39`.

The concept records doctrine rewards at 25% for one use, except European Naval Staffs at 50% for one use. It records research at 25% electronics once, and 50% once in the selected armor or naval partnership. These are source-supported reward types, not proof of rendered tooltips or successful runtime application. No native hard cap was found for the additive modifiers in the files reviewed; keep the small increments and do not describe them as capped.

## Reachability, stacking, and choice quality

Each strategy is exclusive and has its own downstream choices. Within Independent, land and sea priority exclude each other and their design children follow only the selected priority. This avoids stacking both arms of the same national allocation. Pragmatic's Rhine and Atlantic offers exclude each other, as do the European land and sea offers. Veto and consultation exclude one another. No strict dominated pair was evident: the choices exchange distinct benefits and expense profiles.

The maximum reached values below add the new branch to the prior static UDR whole-route maxima from `NATO_MECHANICS.md`, which already include the old Atlantic Training agreement. They are per-key ceilings assembled from compatible choices, not a single package and not engine-tested totals:

| Reachable selection | Cross-stack implication |
| --- | --- |
| Independent + veto + land priority | Adds up to +0.07 equipment cost multiplier to prior UDR maximum +0.20, for +0.27 total; adds +0.04 factory capacity. The 0.27 is an increase to the recurring MD equipment bill, not factory IC cost. The branch is deliberately expensive as described. |
| Independent + veto + sea priority | Adds +0.04 equipment cost multiplier, +0.05 dockyard capacity, and +0.05 navy personnel bill. It does not take the land equipment research reward. |
| Pragmatic + Rhine + Conditional War Plans | Adds +0.02 army org regain to the prior UDR maximum +0.09, for +0.11; also adds +0.03 max planning and +0.03 army personnel expense. France's modern `FRA_NATO_support` has +0.05 max planning (`common/ideas/05_france.txt:706-716`), so if that idea is also active this modifier would total +0.08. I found no local history/startup grant of that modern idea in the 1970 setup scan, so do not assume it is active in January 1970. |
| Pragmatic + Atlantic Fleet + Compatible Fleet Support | Adds -0.05 supply consumption to the earlier UDR maximum -0.06, for -0.11; adds +0.03 navy personnel cost and -0.05 naval accident chance. This is the strongest logistics stack in the proposed military choices, but it avoids blanket combat statistics. |
| European Pillar + European Land Standards | Adds +0.25 electronics research once and +0.50 armor research once; equipment cost falls by 0.04 for France and Germany. |
| European Pillar + European Naval Industry | Adds +0.25 electronics research once and +0.50 naval research once; each signatory gets +0.04 dockyard capacity. |

Old shared-defence rewards and the old Atlantic Training spirit are not erased or duplicated by the new nodes. Add the selected branch's modifier only where the type overlaps; the new branch does not add broad army attack, defence, morale, or organization bonuses. Recipient offers also impose their own setup and weekly charges, so compare full family costs in acceptance previews. In particular, each European agreement charges both sides 0.12b at setup and 0.002b weekly; the pragmatic bilateral offers charge 0.08b setup per side and 0.001/0.0015b weekly depending on signatory.

## Implementation boundary and source status

This concept is not a runtime implementation. The custom UDR programme, recipient response decisions, separate bilateral spirits, treaty family limit, NATO-specific suspension and restoration, and stream persistence all require new project code. The native membership array and `NATO_member` lifecycle are source-supported (`common/ideas/Factions.txt:6-60`); the 1970 initializer grants France its membership (`common/scripted_effects/IMP_1970_world_politics_effects.txt:1205-1253`). The native NATO leader request addresses one leader, whereas accession's ratification route records approval from every current member (`NATO_MECHANICS.md:19-22`). This tree grants no membership or command change, so it correctly does not use either accession mechanism. If a later branch proposes alliance-wide command integration, one USA response must not stand in for all-member consent.

The concept is internally coherent on focus prerequisites, exclusivity, and explicit partner preview effects. Before implementation, resolve the NATO membership-exit condition for already signed pacts, implement acceptance-time validation and atomic dual-country accounting, and independently verify generated native tooltips and live suspension/restoration behavior. Parent-reported browser checks are not attributed to this review.
