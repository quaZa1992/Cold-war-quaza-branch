IMPERIA COLD WAR

Annual Union commissions in the existing Events and Decisions screen. Implementation of the user's approved decision-only order design, 25 October 2026.

The Soviet player can issue a binding commission, while a human SSR can submit a proposed quantity and financial assistance for Soviet confirmation. Actual SSR status is required; governing ideology is not. The fourteen original republics are supported. The first valid reservation wins. An unsigned proposal occupies its equipment family for up to 30 days without a payment or production baseline. Approval opens a full 365 days. Rejection, withdrawal and unanswered-proposal expiry have no penalty.

The three native categories are Annual Union Orders, Republican Submissions and Annual Obligations. Preparation decisions reveal only the current selection step: republic, obligation, sector, family, quantity or assistance. Quantity uses increments of 1, 10, 100 and 1,000, with a one-unit minimum and a native-safe one-million ceiling. There is no new GUI, numeric-input window or event-based configuration menu. Co-op controllers share their country's draft; the authoritative transaction checks its current terms and reservation again.

Selecting a republic in Republican Submissions records the identities of its current proposals. Approve and Reject require that reviewed identity. If the republic withdraws a proposal and submits another, Moscow must use Review the Latest Submitted Terms before accepting or rejecting the replacement. The same identity check protects production certification: Examine Production Return selects the actual annual commission before Certify or Return for Revision becomes available. A delayed click cannot certify the next year's replacement commission.

The catalogue has 44 commission families. It includes small arms, C&C equipment, utility and heavy vehicles, MBTs, light tanks, APCs, IFVs, towed and self-propelled artillery, MLRS, anti-aircraft and anti-tank weapons, the actual MD aircraft and helicopter archetypes, warships, submarines, convoys and three missile families. Construction targets cover civilian and military factories, agriculture, cotton/textile capacity, thermal generation, infrastructure and dockyards. Cotton can be fulfilled by the required agricultural increase or the required civilian-processing increase, rather than falsely treating a CIV as raw cotton production. Naval orders require an owned, controlled coast and dockyard capacity. The register itself grants no free buildings.

Production and delivery are distinct. Native cumulative counters measure honestly named broad artillery, convoy and missile classes from their opening baseline. Narrow equipment families require the SSR to submit a production return and the Soviet player to certify it. Imports and old reserves cannot automatically certify new production. The decision descriptions expose that measurement method. Delivery orders instead dispatch real equipment with native `send_equipment`; the actual increase in Soviet inventory is added to the order's delivery record, allowing partial consignments and preventing an invented recipient grant. Warships are not offered as stockpile deliveries.

Success and failure programmes both last 180 days. Named spirits such as Successful Programme: Main Battle Tanks identify the result and affected industry. The normal weapon programme gives -8% production cost for its exact MD target; a shortfall gives +8%. Construction success uses the listed sector productivity/generation/construction modifier. A separate shared +3% Consumer Goods Factor burden lasts 180 days after the latest valid failure. It is one spirit, not one stacking copy per failed order. The actual equipment-target channels also aggregate overlapping broad/narrow programmes: one target has one active cost result, failure takes precedence while it remains active, and each result retains only its original remaining duration. Separate duplicate archetypes are explicitly targeted, following MD's own equipment-bonus patterns.

Early fulfilment does not release the equipment-family reservation. The original annual clock continues to its 365-day end, preventing repeated setup payments and bonuses. Production and delivery share the same equipment-family reservation. Overlapping artillery programmes and cotton/construction alternatives cannot reuse the same opening capacity within one republic. Country variables, native missions and receipts survive save/rehost; opening the menu does not charge money or grant a reward.

AI handling is deliberately separate from ordinary AI weights. An AI SSR receives only a non-punitive advisory commission. A registered human-to-AI transition neutralises an unfinished binding commission for that annual window. A later human does not inherit its deadline or reactivate it. The original annual reservation remains until its scheduled end, with no new failure, success award or refund of assistance already delivered. Every success, failure and delivery transaction checks current membership, responsibility and status. Previously settled human outcomes are not retrospectively rewritten by changing control. AI/neutral commissions are excluded from collective scoring; an empty set cannot earn a collective success. Pausing a session does not consume game days.

Financial assistance is optional and expressed as 0%, 0.025%, 0.05%, 0.1% or 0.2% of current Soviet GDP. The stored percentage is applied at actual commitment; a proposal does not lock an obsolete dollar amount. Both treasury changes use MD's `modify_treasury_effect` in the same guarded transaction. The final affordability check recalculates the current quote; a stale preview cannot cause a partial payment. This is a one-time transfer, with no additional recurring money stream. Timed bonuses and policy effects do not transfer further money on restoration.

Integration with the retained Union tree

The old automatic 300-321-day targets and random AI-floor completion are retired when the new register is enabled. A one-time migration cancels unfinished legacy missions without a new failure. Entry points, queued old events and punitive option effects are guarded; the 180-day expiry events for already settled historical legacy effects remain valid so those costs cannot become permanent. The retained Soviet generator applies the same adapter on regeneration. Existing shared-focus coordinates, prerequisites, completed-focus receipts and ordinary PP rewards are preserved.

Mission-dependent joint focuses have usable successors instead of unavailable former actions:

Institutional spirits require their actual earned focus flags and Union membership. Temporary actions need an active human commission and cannot reset their 180-day clocks through exit/re-entry. Existing reserve/investigation and emulation features retain their other native consumers. The yearly collective summary uses settled human commissions, preserving good-year counters for the existing five-year-plan capstones. An actual collective success grants the existing plan variant for 180 days. It never resets a still-running 365-day individual order at January settlement.

Historical and native references

The numerical policy and order modifiers above are gameplay calibration, not historical statistics. The inherited joint-tree design and this [contemporary CIA study of Soviet distribution and planning](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP71T00730R000100020029-9.pdf) describe informal procurement agents and the concentrated end-of-period production effort known as shturmovshchina. They support procurement relief with administrative costs and additional shifts with disruption elsewhere. The [1973 CIA assessment of 1972 economic results](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP85T00875R001700050022-1.pdf) links delayed commissioning of industrial capacity and machinery/output problems. These are contemporary outside assessments, not neutral official Soviet accounts or numerical balance instructions. The inherited People's Control and control-commission institutions supply the audit/enforcement setting; the tax, administration and political trade-offs are design inferences.

Exact equipment names, archetypes and duplicates come from `common/units/equipment/MD_*.txt`, `MD_x_tank_chassis.txt` and the approved SSR equipment labels. MD's `_land_policies.txt` explicitly lists duplicate archetypes separately, rather than proving automatic parent-bonus inheritance. Native `documentation/dynamic_variables_documentation.md` provides production and mission-time variables; `effects_documentation.md` provides `send_equipment`, timed ideas and mission adjustments. `common/synchronized_dynamic_tokens/COLDWAR_UNION_orders.txt` registers every mission token referenced by a mission-time variable. Existing small MD/Cold War idea images are referenced unchanged through the SSR sprite catalogue. Original individual image authors and applicable permissions are unknown where the inherited records do not identify them; no ownership is asserted. See `IMPERIA_COLD_WAR_NOTICE.md` and `tools/ssr_1970/art_catalogue.json`.

Regeneration and verification

Run `python -B tools/union_orders_1970/compile.py`, then `python -B tools/union_orders_1970/validate.py` and `python -B tools/union_orders_1970/test_orders.py`. The narrow compiler owns the new order files and explicitly marked legacy integrations. It does not run the broad Soviet generator or change the money-system file. `manifest.json` records its outputs; the SSR manifest receives only expected hashes for shared donor integrations.

`script_harness.py` executes the actual emitted script subset and fails on unsupported operations. Its tests cover scopes, payments, concurrent reservations, stale approvals and certification, proposal expiry, physical transfers, AI takeover, independent legacy clocks, overlapping outcomes, construction alternatives and model save/rehost. These are source regression tests. `check_generator.py` checks the retained donor without running its broad writer and records `GENERATOR_PARITY.json`; hidden bookkeeping wrappers are normalised for semantic comparison.

`engine_probe.py` uses a unique private profile on E: with copied runtime inputs. It has an explicitly recorded fixture-only authority override for marked AI countries so transaction paths can be exercised without a second client; removing that mark tests actual AI handling. Selected private timers are shortened after activation to exercise native expiry, while shipped durations stay 365/180. `ENGINE_REPORT.json` retains all executed assertions, source hashes, diagnostic records and these limitations. Earlier attempts are separate records. Read the current report rather than treating the presence of a probe as a passing result. A native startup/fixture run is not a live multiplayer, native save/rehost, production-cost inspector or manual-tooltip rehearsal.

The final native fixture passed all 29 assertions, including real transfers, both treasury entries, proposal replacement, stale certification, AI neutralisation, recipient construction totals and native spirit expiry. The source regression suite passed 30 tests. After that native run, four trailing spaces in two legacy integration files were removed. `summarize.py` compares the final runtime against the immutable native snapshot and records `IMPLEMENTATION_RESULT.json`; only the documented fixture authority override is removed for that comparison. This keeps the observed native evidence and final-source parity distinct.
