IMPERIA COLD WAR: COMECON interface artwork and provenance
=======================================================

The user supplied and explicitly selected `user-emblem.png` in this conversation. Its SHA-256 is `5d41ce4b893e600fc5d7d1c354fd92bf34b753dfbe88815081c6befb09c03b4b`. The original temporary attachment was `codex-clipboard-e7e9470a-0315-4378-9e48-47a8790c3679.png`. Original historical designer, image author and applicable licence are unknown. User authorization covers this requested project use; it does not establish third-party ownership or a general redistribution licence.

The circular toolbar donor is the existing mod texture `gfx/interface/help_gui/EU_help_button_custom.dds`. Original individual texture author and applicable licence are unknown. Existing native game and MD artwork is attributable to Paradox Interactive, the Millennium Dawn team and project contributors as applicable. The original EU window records AngriestBird and Doolittle as interface authors; that does not identify the texture artist. The generated COMECON button keeps the donor's outer circular rim and alpha pixels. Its inner disc and stars are replaced by a red field and the white interior symbol from the supplied emblem, proportionally resized. `art-record.json` records source hashes and transformations.

The specialist marks in `source-art` are lossless PNG conversions of existing mod DDS artwork already used in the approved concept. `source-art/provenance.json` retains their exact original mod paths, hashes and known credits. Unknown individual authors and rights remain unknown. The generator juxtaposes these marks with the rectangular COMECON emblem in a 22-frame native sprite sheet, matching the approved dossier. Only the toolbar uses the circular variant. These are interface compositions, not claimed historical specialist organisation logos.

The paper dossier and button surfaces are original procedural interface assets created by the accompanying project generator. The compact cover adds deterministic paper grain and fibres, staple impressions, pressure creases, a bound spine, light edge wear, stacked sheets and a folded corner. No downloaded paper texture is used. Local bitmap font atlases are rendered from installed Windows Segoe UI, Georgia and Courier New, matching the approved concept. Font source paths, hashes and embedded credit/licence records are preserved in `font-provenance.json`. The font software is not copied into the mod; the atlases contain rendered glyphs. A separate redistribution permission for the generated atlases has not been established and is recorded as unknown. Country flags are never embedded in these assets. The interface uses each actual country's current native `GetFlag`, including cosmetic and ideological changes.

The PNG files in `docs/design/comecon` are diagnostic views or previews of these same assets unless explicitly identified as native game screenshots. An asset preview or geometric composition is not a live game screenshot. This notice and the project marker do not replace any original licence or credit. See the repository's `IMPERIA_COLD_WAR_NOTICE.md` for the wider project notice.

The annual Council office adds original procedural ballot cards, red selection borders and ruled correspondence rows from `assets.py`. They contain no national-flag pixels. Electoral and header flags continue to use native current-country flag images.
