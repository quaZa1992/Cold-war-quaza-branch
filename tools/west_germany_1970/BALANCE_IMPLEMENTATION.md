# IMPERIA COLD WAR: German multiplayer balance implementation

The approved follow-up implements the five corrections from the user-facing review, plus a symmetric offensive-order limit. The user's final timing instruction is **two months per crisis round**, not two months for an entire crisis. Existing maximum rounds, brink countdowns, treaty chapters, normal action cooldowns and human-response requirements remain separate.

## Delivered behavior

* Crisis rounds resolve on their second monthly pulse. A missing human instruction holds the counter at month one and the dossier says that instructions are missing. The displayed remaining time is clamped to 0 through 2 for older saves. Three/four regular rounds therefore take six/eight months, unless resolved earlier, stalled by a human, or entering a separate brink countdown.
* Swing and credit proposals pay their existing PP/initiative preparation only. Climate, leverage, border concessions, political consequences and the native credit debit apply once after a valid DDR acceptance. Refusal and expired or duplicate replies grant no concessions. Swing recalculates real Imperia capacity and requires a free type-5 partner link and two free capacity before granting anything. Its real programme provider executes inside the guarded transaction. Capacity failure leaves refusal possible. Old-save rewards already granted before this patch are not inferred or reversed.
* Raid, counterspy, vetting and double-agent actions share a **180-day intelligence-operation cooldown**, in addition to their existing individual cooldowns. Their permanent counterintelligence awards are respectively +3, +4, +3 and 0. Raid still removes 10 penetration, counterspy 6, vetting 3, and the double-agent action retains its distinct effects. DDR's +5 HVA operation per 180 days remains available. This constrains defensive stacking without inventing a large permanent national research penalty.
* Both German governments have a **60-day offensive-order lock**. It covers Bonn's media, church aid, delivery freeze, escape helpers and exercises; DDR's compulsory exchange, transit checks, border tightening, HVA operation and transit closure. De-escalation, domestic policies and treaty responses do not consume this lock. Native decisions and dossier buttons recheck the same country-owned cooldown before charging.
* A Recognition Crisis freezes the actual recognising country in a one-entry GER array. Its special action now imposes real **Imperia diplomatic sanctions for 35 PP**, matching the native diplomatic-action price. It updates both mirrored sanction ledgers, the actual opinion modifier, the Cold War callback and both countries' sanction calculations. It also applies the existing crisis reduction (-20) and climate cost (-5). It neither invents a diplomatic-relations engine command nor removes recognition of either German state. Native Imperia diplomatic actions remain the way to lift these restrictions.
* Each distinct recognising government can generate the existing native tier PP award **once per German state**. Separate GER and DDR paid arrays prevent withdrawal/regrant and duplicate-response farming. Historical and old-save baselines pay nothing retroactively. Native recognition ledgers are never changed merely to seed PP receipts. The non-1970/non-German native reward behavior is preserved. An unseeded hot-load callback safely treats its current recognitions as the baseline and may forgo that first award.
* Capitulation messages open a settlement menu and no longer annex DDR, change Bonn's government/faction or make peace with Moscow by themselves. Either German player can propose a return to the status quo. Following Bonn's capitulation, West Berlin can be transferred to DDR by agreement. Following DDR's capitulation, a separate agreed reparations package transfers **$0.20 billion from DDR to GER**, with live affordability checks. These fixed counterfactual terms are balance choices, not historical events or compulsory victory outcomes.
* Every country actually at war with GER or DDR when a proposal is made becomes a signatory, including a human USSR if it is a belligerent. The proposer signs its own proposal. Every other participant receives a framed country event and can accept, refuse or leave it pending, with persistent response decisions. Silence never accepts. Changed participants, missing countries, changed territorial/funding conditions or an ended German war invalidate the proposal. Acceptance executes no peace until every signature exists. Only wars involving GER or DDR are explicitly ended; wars between third countries remain intact. Alliance membership and sovereign governments are preserved. A new scenario war clears the old proposal.

All new player text is English. New and changed custom files/blocks carry the exact project marker. Script files use UTF-8 without BOM; localisation uses UTF-8 with BOM. Changed/new country events reuse the current native MD `GFX_political_deal` frame. No global GUI sizing or new bitmap assets were introduced.

## Source ownership and bounded installation

Canonical country sources: `rivalry.py`, `events_rivalry.py`, `events_ddr.py`, `security.py`, `gui.py`, `ddr_rivalry_ui.py`, `ddr_relations.py`, `systems.py`, `compile.py`; new transactions live in `balance_patch.py` and `balance_runtime.py`. Recognition PP and claimant capture are authored in `tools/diplomacy_1970/build.py` with the bounded native patch in `native_hooks.py`.

`compile_balance.py` renders the country generator in memory and installs only nodes changed against `balance_audit/before_generated.json`. Unrelated divergent live nodes are rejected, and a subsequent update to already-installed nodes requires the full previous installed file hash. It never runs a broad live-tree overwrite. `balance_audit/integration.json` retains installed runtime hashes and `plan.json` records subsequent dry runs. The original files are under `rollback_backups/german_balance_20261023`.

The existing sole GER monthly callback maintains a pending settlement. It performs no world scan when no proposal is pending. Explicit proposal/signature validation scans the existing countries to detect changed belligerents. No new daily simulation or recurring recognition scan was added.

`engine_balance_probe.py` uses an isolated muted profile and an actual GER event ROOT to exercise native credit consent/debits, defensive-operation cooldown, crisis clock, real recognising-country capture, mirrored sanctions, recognition regrant and a Soviet-signature-gated peace. Its report is `balance_audit/engine_report.json`; consult that result before claiming native success. It does not certify manual hovers, save/reload, a multiplayer session, the complete Imperia assistance economy or every hypothetical capitulation outcome. No user playset or saves are changed.

It also exposed an already-empty Taiwan ledger removal in the diplomacy initializer; that removal is now guarded, and the rerun no longer reports it. The full profile still reports general engine assertions, including an event-command assertion after the concentrated direct-handler fixture. Their cause is not established by this test, so the result is not a clean-log certificate for the whole mod. Raw logs remain in the private profile, with earlier attempts preserved separately. `balance_audit/final_verification.json` records current runtime hashes, encodings, project markers, source-model totals, native results and these limits.
