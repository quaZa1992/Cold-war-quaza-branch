# American Statecraft: installed Kissinger programme

The USA 1970 tree contains 53 additional focuses and a continuing Kurdish regional
system for Iraq, Iran, Turkey and Syria. The complete tree has 354 registered IDs,
353 visible cards. Its foreign-policy shortcut opens the new statecraft entry;
the existing Gulf and naval shortcuts remain available.

The original 41-focus concept is frozen in `approved_design.json`, SHA256
`54d93f67e7cd0be84ce4cf865d1dbc844b87a2f7be8372cc67c703d1de9f5cf3`.
The earlier interactive visualization is design evidence. The installed scripts,
native layout review and implementation reports describe the delivered behaviour.

## Programme and choices

- Strategic flexibility, triangular diplomacy, consultations with Beijing and
  Moscow, political assistance in Chile and military liaison in Angola.
- Limited commitments permit two concurrent regional campaigns. Expanded
  mandates permit four and carry higher bureaucracy costs. Both provide
  distinct intelligence and diplomatic benefits.
- The Kurdish chapter contains eight Iraqi, six Iranian, seven Turkish and six
  Syrian focuses, plus its common entry. Regional rights, armed organisation and
  recognition are different choices with different political conditions.
- Nine regional diplomatic arrangements and four additional Chinese programmes require the named recipient's real
  acceptance. Soviet programmes use the standing superpower treaty paper. Both countries receive the shown spirits only when their actual
  conditions hold. War, native embargoes and any of the four mirrored Imperia
  sanction classes suspend cooperation. Restoration does not repeat setup assets.
- Material acceptance, political liaison, participation in an uprising and
  requesting recognition each have their own local-player decision.

Every bilateral focus exposes the prospective named spirits, signed modifiers,
equipment and weekly balances for both countries before acceptance, including
while locked. Preview helpers use native non-executing effect tooltips.
AI recipients retain real acceptance and refusal. Routine AI actions do not
withdraw standing agreements, liaison or funded programmes at random, and the
host AI does not alternate paid security/investment setup fees. These AI weights
leave every human withdrawal and policy switch available. Financial crisis guards
on new host spending use the actual native bankruptcy mission.

## A continuing regional campaign

The host and the USA see a native decision-category panel with one unrest bar,
a short stabilisation status and a warning. The defending government has three
substantive responses: a field security operation, investment, or negotiated
regional rights. Existing local governments approve their own political terms.
The defending government does not approve hostile American covert sponsorship.

Two real divisions in the eligible region make a paid security operation
effective. Its effect applies while the forces remain there. Each successful
week adds lasting stabilisation, capped at 100. Troop withdrawal ends the direct
deployment benefit; accumulated stabilisation remains and declines gradually.
Investment develops more slowly and can also build lasting calm. Operations and
investment are alternative spending programmes. An accepted political settlement
is a separate remedy and remains available during escalation.

There is no 30-day deployment expiry and no automatic end to the campaign.
The regional ledger survives containment, withdrawal, war and reconquest.
Recurring commitments continue until changed or suspended. Containment does not
give permanent immunity: sustained renewed pressure can eventually revive unrest.
Four divisions provide additional direct containment. The calculation uses actual
division count and position, not a simulated measure of their combat strength.

An armed rising requires funded access, committed material, armed organisation,
unrest above 79, qualifying mobilisation and a subsequent warning. A weekly tick
advances readiness; the USA must then choose **Support an armed mobilisation**.
The final effect rechecks all conditions and fully controlled eligible territory.
Existing local RP governments must separately agree to participation. Recognition
requires a regional authority actually holding relevant land and asking for it.

The simple panel aggregates unrest at host level. Iraq must still have at least
two eligible fully controlled northern states before a new KUR rising. This
replaces the old separate state-resistance display. The mobilisation and warning
intervals are escalation safeguards, not a troop-duration requirement.

## Costs and bounded geography

The later [regional-control specification](../../docs/design/usa_kissinger_20261028/REGIONAL_CONTROL_20261029.md)
adds long-term reconciliation, four exceptionally expensive reforms that reduce
the minimum from ten to six years, paid American escalation and two coercive
host choices with actual state population losses and separate player events.
These extend the continuing system; containment alone does not close its ledger.

All runtime currency uses MD billions; all recurring values are weekly. The
[GDP financing revision](../../docs/design/usa_kissinger_20261028/GDP_COSTS_20261029.md)
replaces nominal charges throughout this chapter with current-GDP percentages.

| Commitment | Setup | Continuing expense |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Host security operation | 2% of current GDP | 0.004% of GDP/week and +5% army personnel cost |
| Host regional investment | 1% of current GDP | 0.002% of GDP/week and +1% bureaucracy cost |
| Host political settlement | 1% of current GDP, 75 PP | 0.001% of GDP/week, subject to actual local consent |
| New funded US regional programme | Focus-specific GDP share and actual committed equipment | 0.00002% of US GDP/week at funded stage, 0.00005% at armed stage |
| Optional covert air route | 0.0002% of US GDP, 25 PP | An additional 0.00004% of US GDP/week while funded |
| Gulf wartime package | 0.025% of US GDP, 25 PP and 1,000 actual weapons | 0.005% of US GDP/week |

New policy figures are gameplay choices informed by the historical programme;
the later user instruction indexes the Gulf programme against US GDP too. The air
route requires five actual transport aircraft and a controlled American airbase.
Weapons are debited from the real US stockpile once, held in a finite cache and
delivered only through the recipient's acceptance. The same cache cannot pay both
KUR and PUK twice. Political-only programmes do not promise uncommitted weapons.

| Theatre | Eligible territory and identity |
| --- | --- |
| Iraq | States 164, 633, 630 and conditionally 1078. Existing KUR and PUK identities are preserved; PUK requires its actual formation condition. |
| Iran | State 408, Kordestan. New identity KPI: Iranian Kurdish Council. |
| Turkey | State 162, Turkish Kurdistan. New identity KPT: Anatolian Kurdish Council. |
| Syria | State 193, Hasakah. New identity KPS: Jazira Regional Authority. |

These are deliberately bounded game regions, including mixed-population areas;
they are not a claim that all residents support separatism. No Iranian, Turkish
or Syrian campaign repurposes the Iraqi KUR or PUK player country. New actors do
not copy their former government's treasury, debt, equipment or armed forces.
They begin with a small local formation and require real reinforcement and aid.
An undated, owned helper grants the same eight 1940-1945 technical fundamentals
and DLC-specific early anti-aircraft technology used by the existing KUR/PUK
baseline. It works for later births and the three new tags; it grants no weapons
or 1965 research. Actual aid explicitly uses the USA producer, so an authority
without its own 1965 rifle design can receive the committed foreign equipment.

The Kuwait intervention remains independent of the new chapter. An unresolved
Iraqi invasion can include occupation after Kuwaiti capitulation. It unlocks the
existing American liberation war goal rather than declaring war automatically.

## Historical scope

Iraqi Kurdish assistance through Iran is the historical core: the Nixon-Kissinger
programme began in 1972, sought to occupy Baghdad internally and ended after the
1975 Algiers agreement. The 1991 Kurdish rising followed the Gulf War, rather than
being an automatic prewar creation of a US-controlled country. Starting a hostile
campaign against the Shah or NATO Turkey is an explicit alternative policy with
a deliberate rupture and credibility consequences. The Syrian branch is an
alternative regional pressure programme, not a claimed historical Kissinger
operation. No PKK, PYD, YPG or SDF identity is placed into January 1970.

Sources and the distinction between historical precedent and balance proposals
are recorded in
[the historical review](../../docs/design/usa_kissinger_20261028/HISTORICAL_MECHANICS_REVIEW.md)
and [the implementation mechanics review](../../docs/design/usa_kissinger_20261028/IMPLEMENTATION_MECHANICS_REVIEW.md).

## Ownership and regeneration

Run `python tools/usa_kissinger_implementation/compile.py` from the repository root
for this bounded integration. It writes its owned runtime providers and art,
replaces only its marked focus section, updates the existing Gulf armed-support
reward and adds narrow marked weekly-income/expense hooks. Repeating it must
produce `changed: []`.

The general USA compiler calls the same module. The owning Iraqi US-content
generator calls `gulf_bridge.py`, so later regeneration retains one Iraqi regional
ledger and does not restore the retired 30/90-day packages. No complete Iraq or
world generator is needed to install this programme.

Already-paid legacy Iraqi assistance and security receipts migrate once, without
charging the setup invoice again. Existing delivered material and the legacy
weekly contract are retained. Queued obsolete grant events are guarded after
migration. Existing saves should be backed up by the player; fresh native loading
and scripted migration checks are distinct from a full old-save rehearsal.

  regional containment, recurrence, migration and territorial guards.

  complete USA tree and its existing programme providers.
