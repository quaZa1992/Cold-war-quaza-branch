# IMPERIA COLD WAR

## Cuba 1970 revision layout and runtime review

This review covers `revision_design.json` and the emitted 120-focus tree after the DDR and Guantánamo revision. The earlier foreign-policy concept and approval remain intact as the parent snapshot.

The revised tree has 120 focuses: 27 foreign-policy nodes, 23 of them recipient-consented offers, and the original 13-node Fidel/PCC branch. The DDR island agreements are independent roots at `[44,0]` and `[48,0]`, beside Soviet assistance at `[36,0]`. Berlin and Havana at `[46,2]` accepts either island agreement; Citrus for Machinery and Industrial Training follow it at `[44,4]` and `[48,4]`. Both follow-up deals can coexist. The emitted tree now declares each relative-position anchor before its dependent focus, and the validator checks that ordering to catch the engine’s forward-anchor parser failure.

I checked every same-row focus center against the native 165-pixel card width on the 96-pixel grid. The closest same-row centers are four grid units apart, so the revised card layout has no horizontal overlap. The DDR subtree is symmetric and its prerequisite edges move downward. The defence branch also remains collision-free: Coastal Patrols sits at `[32,1]`, between Territorial Defence and Air Defence, and Coastal Strongpoints continues below it at `[32,3]`.

The Island Diplomacy root at `[56,0]` avoids the new DDR roots. It has longer direct prerequisite edges to Canadian agreements at `[62,4]` and `[66,4]`, Overseas Service at `[64,6]`, and the Algerian accord at `[70,5]`. The longest is 14 columns and five rows. In the updated browser preview, the long foreign-policy routes remain legible around the Mexico, Spain and Canada rows; the DDR and defence branch connectors are also clear in their focused previews. Browser QA inspected all 120 focus cards across 24 view/width combinations (320, 736 and 1024 pixels), with no overflow, and confirmed zoom, pan, click interaction and native asset loading. This verifies the browser preview only: a native HOI4 hover/screenshot pass and in-game connector rendering have not been tested.

The reused Guantánamo Perimeter and Coastal Strongpoints focuses retain their earlier rewards and modifiers while adding six province fort levels across Cuban-controlled State 855 provinces 7590 and 9603. Their availability checks require Cuban ownership/control of State 855 and per-province free building capacity; the native fort definitions set `province_max = 7`. The emitted effects grant exactly one extra shared building slot per fort level, matching the project’s building-grant rule.

The foreign-contract validator confirms 23 offer events, each delivered to the named partner with pending/open receipts, a delivery log, accept/refuse options and a display-only paired-agreement preview. The current pictures resolve to registered GFX names, including `GFX_news_us_cuba`, `GFX_soviet_union_flag`, and `GFX_treaty`.

Validation passed against the final emitted revision: `validate.py` reports 6,217 checks and `validate_bilateral.py` reports 1,777 checks. The bilateral validator compares every accepted research grant across the concept, both player previews and the actual acceptance helper, and checks that each research localization key appears exactly once. These are static AST/reference checks; native engine testing is tracked separately by the root implementation owner.
