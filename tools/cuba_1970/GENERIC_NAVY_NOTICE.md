# IMPERIA COLD WAR: MD generic naval branch integration

The user requested the complete generic MD navy tree in Cuba's national focus tree. This adds all 20 focuses between `GENERIC_naval_reform` and the Air Force Reform section of the installed main Millennium Dawn `common/national_focus/00_generic.txt`. Six original prerequisite focuses are also copied: Light Industry Investments, Heavy Industry Investments, Refitting the Military Industry, Military Industry Investment Projects, Air Force Reform and Fighter Planes. Omitting those would leave four naval routes blocked by focuses outside Cuba's loaded tree.

The source and English localisation are frozen in `GENERIC_NAVY_SOURCE.json`, with original source hashes. Credit belongs to Millennium Dawn contributors; individual authors and individual asset permissions are unknown here. The user authorized this local adaptation. This notice neither claims ownership of upstream content nor grants redistribution permission. Existing game icons, national spirits, projects, modifiers and economic providers are referenced in place. See the repository `IMPERIA_COLD_WAR_NOTICE.md` and upstream notices for further attribution.

All costs, durations, AI weights, research bonuses, doctrine mastery, special-project progress, national spirits, factory thresholds, power-rank conditions, internal prerequisites and exclusions remain as in MD. IDs, references and English keys receive the `COLDWAR_CUB_GEN_` namespace. The naval root is at (24, 6), its industry feeder root at (5, 6), and its air feeder root at (32, 6). The entire 20-focus naval branch retains its original relative positions. Both feeder chains retain their original relative positions, and every prerequisite is above its continuation. A naval shortcut is included.

There are three construction adaptations required by the project's existing slot rule. Naval Bases grants one extra shared slot in its selected state before its one naval-base level. Light Industry Investments grants two slots for its civilian industry and queued fossil power plant, rather than the donor's one slot. A Cuba-owned copy of the composite-plant helper grants the donor's existing slot before construction. Costs, building levels, construction timing, target selection and other conditions are preserved. The shared MD helpers are unchanged.

The original 120 Cuban focuses remain. The explicit additional-copy request raises the installed total to 146, including the six required feeders. No party programme, Fidel focus, foreign agreement or Guantánamo fortification is removed to finance this addition.

This is a faithful MD progression, with its original limits. Advanced ships still require the actual Naval Vessel Project, sufficient dockyards and, where applicable, regional or major-power status. Project progress is not project completion and does not grant a research facility or scientist. Maritime and defence-industry opinion rewards still require those native internal factions. The import does not create them, grant a fleet, waive ship technology or redesign Cuba's 1970 government. A complete campaign through every special-project gate is outside the current test coverage.

`generic_navy.py` is the reproducible import provider used by `compile.py`. `validate_generic_navy.py` independently reverses the namespace and checks the emitted AST against the original MD blocks, allowing only the root placements and listed slot adaptations. `GENERIC_NAVY_VALIDATION.json` records source checks; `GENERIC_NAVY_ENGINE_REPORT.json` records the separate native-engine reward probe. Forced native completion tests reward execution, not elapsed focus time or the entire advanced-ship campaign.

Final checks: 6,246 Cuban core checks, 1,777 bilateral checks and 1,462 donor/integration checks passed. The native probe passed all eight assertions with no Cuba-specific log errors: the authored 1970 tree loads, naval reform gives its 75 XP, the naval-base focus charges $0.50 billion, a real dockyard is constructed, the industry prerequisite executes, and either health-policy parent independently unlocks the repaired continuation. The first probe's same-tick country factory-count assertion failed; the repeat checked the constructed state building directly and logged one dockyard in Santa Clara, with no gameplay modification. Both attempts remain available. All ten compiler-owned runtime files are identical after repeat compilation. Manual native focus-window routing, an actual save/reload cycle, the full advanced-ship campaign and multiplayer remain untested.

The native reward tests preceded those two coordinate-only edits. The report preserves the tested hashes and records this follow-up separately; reward, availability and prerequisite ASTs are unchanged.
