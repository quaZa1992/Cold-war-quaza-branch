Completed receipts retain earned national development. One-time Treasury/debt changes, construction, research/doctrine uses, experience and budget-tier changes are executed only by their guarded application helper. If a government or state-control change prevents completion-day execution, daily reconciliation retries a completed, unapplied reward through the same guard; descendants require the actual predecessor receipt.

The four shared programmes remain national. Thirteen political programmes activate only for the exact current `ruling_party` slot. Native leader refresh and government hooks synchronize them, with daily reconciliation as a fallback. Departure does not erase receipts; return rebuilds programme variables without repeating assets or transactions. CMEA-only contributions additionally require the actual membership idea.

The initial harvest and the temporary mobilisation programme expire at the July 1970 settlement. The mobilisation focus requires an ongoing harvest; after its end the lasting sector-reallocation route remains available. Historical later policies can be introduced earlier as the approved alternate-history choices, except the explicitly dated 1972 island diplomacy. The 120-focus layout is unchanged.

Signed agreements have no one-year expiry. Their paired spirits operate only while both countries exist, are at peace with each other, and neither applies a native embargo or an Imperia diplomatic, financial, economic or military sanction against the other. The undertaking grants no resources, subsidy or automatic trade route.
