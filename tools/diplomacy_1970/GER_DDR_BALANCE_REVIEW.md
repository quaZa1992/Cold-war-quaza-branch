# IMPERIA COLD WAR: German rivalry and recognition review

Review date: 23 October 2026. Target: live January 1970 multiplayer scenario. This is a source-level review with an independent economical-agent cross-check. It is not a multiplayer playtest, economy calibration or historical simulation. Numeric recommendations below are design proposals, not installed changes.

Follow-up: the user approved the five user-facing corrections and explicitly selected two-month crisis rounds. Those changes are now implemented; [BALANCE_IMPLEMENTATION.md](../west_germany_1970/BALANCE_IMPLEMENTATION.md) records current behavior, exact values and verification. The assessment below is preserved as the pre-change review. Its six-month timing, early concession and repeat PP findings describe the earlier version.

## Implemented recognition integration

The native recognising country's `recognised_countries` array remains authoritative. Each actual new DDR recognition updates `Granted_Recognition`, which both German dossiers already display, and sends Bonn a country-specific notification. Withdrawals also update the count and notify Bonn. Native MD `GFX_political_deal` supplies the existing 217 by 163 RGBA event frame. No art or global GUI was replaced.

A genuinely first-observed new third-country recognition can start the existing Recognition Crisis, with its existing intensity 30, three-round limit and pressure +10. It requires initialized Bonn, the Hallstein legacy, no Basic Treaty, legal leverage above 1, no active crisis or crisis cooldown, no pending strike/countdown dialogue, peace and no completed unity. Sending a campaign request alone no longer starts that crisis; the unrelated random crisis pool no longer invents a recognition.

All recognitions still count and notify when those crisis gates are closed. There is intentionally no queue that starts a deferred recognition crisis months later. Regranting recognition produces a new truthful notice, but the permanent per-recogniser observation flag prevents another crisis opportunity from the same country. A recognition during another crisis never overwrites that crisis or resets its intensity. A third-country grant does not automatically sanction that country or remove its recognition of Bonn.

Historical seeding is silent even if the startup recount ran before the roster was assembled. Existing saves establish their current ledger as the baseline on first recount; subsequent transactions are observed. There is no added daily/monthly world scan or simulation hook. The existing explicit recount performs the observation in its existing world pass.

The native pressure-response event `recognition.8` now writes the ledger before counting it. `recognition.31.b` recounts immediately rather than waiting for its reply event. Existing native direct buttons, campaign acceptances, AI grants, accepted DDR national-focus offers and the mutual-recognition helper reach the same recount. Recognition of Bonn remains independent.

## Assessment

The framework suits multiplayer RP: separate player governments, real diplomatic recognition, explicit signatures for nine treaties, controllable crisis orders and several alternatives to war. It has both confrontation and lasting two-state cooperation. It currently favors Bonn in sustained economic coercion and lacks a comparably material DDR covert benefit. Equal raw strength is unnecessary; both players need agency, counterplay, meaningful victories and attractive cooperation.

There is no automatic human-DDR collapse or fixed 1989 removal in the audited shared sandbox. High exit pressure or crisis can open embassy/uprising crises, which are different from annexation. Negotiated unity requires both German signatures and the Four Powers. War remains a distinct explicit route. Preserve these boundaries.

## Recommended corrections, in priority order

1. **Move negotiated concessions to acceptance.** Confirmed defect: `rv_act_swing` immediately gives climate +5 and Bonn currency leverage +1; `rv_act_credit` immediately gives climate +10, Bonn currency leverage +1 and DDR border leverage -1. The recipients' refusal options in `COLDWAR_GER_DDR.2/.3` do not reverse these. Bonn thus receives concessions even when East Berlin refuses. Keep preparation costs separate; apply agreed payments and concessions once inside a current, guarded recipient acceptance. Review pending flags, stale responses and one-time receipts alongside the change. The acceptance currently contains the actual treasury charge, so the advance concessions are especially problematic.

2. **Review the entire intelligence exchange rather than simply increasing HVA research damage.** Two HVA operations add +10 penetration for 50 PP and 4 initiative over roughly a year; one Bonn raid removes 10 and adds 5 counterintelligence for 50 PP and 2 initiative, before Bonn's additional security buttons and beneficial counterspy events. Passive penetration rises +1.2/year, or +3/year when Ostpolitik exceeds 50; counterintelligence decays 1.8/year. Audit the raid, counterspy, vetting and double-agent options together. Prefer distinct, bounded espionage choices with exposure risk and a useful intelligence/technology outcome, or consolidate overlapping defenses. A universal large permanent research penalty would be poor RP balance.

3. **Give both governments reversible policy and bargaining outcomes.** DDR shortage is a pressure index, not cash reserves: higher is worse. Its `>40` credit-request condition is therefore correct. Passive shortage grows 2.04/year initially and 3/year after mid-1979; high Swing offsets 0.72/year. Intershops (-4 shortage/365 days) and KoKo (-6/540 days) can counter it, with media/opposition or dependence drawbacks. Preserve difficult economic starts but let durable reforms address the slope rather than requiring endless repeat clicks. Accepted financial offers should use real treasury/debt or Imperia projects; a shortage reduction alone is not a monetary transfer.

4. **Keep reconnaissance and pressure as meaningful alternatives to crisis spam.** Initiative caps at 6 and replenishes monthly. DDR's offensive cooldowns are separate, allowing several actions on one day. This is not unlimited spam, but coordinated bursts are possible. Consider a shared offensive instruction period for both sides if actual RP sessions show bursts overwhelming responses. Do not automatically introduce a new daily system or apply a DDR-only restriction. Recognition gains should remain diplomacy achievements, not repeated permanent Bonn stability losses.

5. **Set crisis pacing for the campaign's speed.** A round takes six game months. Three/four-round crises can occupy 18/24 months if their intensity does not settle earlier, and a missing human order keeps a round pending. That protects player choice but can stall RP for a long time. A prospective 90-day round is a playtest candidate, with an explicit defer/withdrawal route and no automatic acceptance. Do not infer that the old AI Monte Carlo script validates the current sandbox: `simulate_rivalry.py` still models retired leader/Moscow scores, dated assumptions and AI-only choices.

6. **Replace labels that promise foreign actions with real target-aware actions or accurate local labels.** The existing Recognition Crisis special action called "Break Off Relations" reduces crisis intensity by 20 and climate by 5, but its executed provider does not target any third country's diplomatic status. Now that a real recogniser is known, a future targeted diplomatic protest/restriction could use actual MD/Imperia systems. Until designed, do not claim that pressing this button breaks native relations or sanctions the recogniser. Several other crisis special actions are also local abstractions and deserve a presentation/consent pass.

7. **Make war settlement and success conditions equally legible.** `COLDWAR_GGR.40` is a Bonn victory after DDR capitulation; it offers postponement or forceful absorption. `COLDWAR_GGR.41` is Bonn's capitulation; it offers postponement or a settlement transferring West Berlin to DDR, peace, Bonn's NATO/faction exit and stability -15 percentage points. The defeated Bonn player currently selects that settlement. A later symmetric, recipient-controlled peace negotiation is preferable for human players and any affected allies. Winning the RP as DDR should include durable sovereignty, accepted recognition and advantageous cooperation, not require conquering Bonn.

8. **Close recognition PP farming separately.** The generic native `recognition_grant_increment` still pays power-tier PP after recounting German recognitions. The native withdrawal button has a 25-PP eligibility requirement but no actual PP debit. Recognition toggles or duplicate stale native responses can therefore yield repeat recipient PP even though the new observer correctly deduplicates crises. Guard a recognition reward by a separate one-time receipt per recogniser, without preventing later legitimate withdrawal/regrant notices. This pre-existing shared-MD reward behavior was not changed by the notification patch.

## Historical grounding

The Hallstein claim treated third-country diplomatic recognition of DDR as an unfriendly act; the new Ostpolitik and Basic Treaty superseded that approach. This justifies ending recognition-triggered confrontation when Bonn has adopted the relevant policy, rather than penalising normal recognised coexistence forever. Historical relations and full international recognition are not interchangeable; the existing mutual-recognition gameplay helper remains an abstraction. [Bundestag, Hallstein doctrine and new Ostpolitik](https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/1954-04-07-ddr-996486), [Federal Foundation, Basic Treaty background](https://www.bundesstiftung-aufarbeitung.de/de/recherche/dossiers/die-innerdeutschen-beziehungen-und-der-grundlagenvertrag/historischer-hintergrund).

The 1983 credit and accompanying border concessions provide a historical basis for negotiated relief with obligations, rather than unilateral benefits on proposal. Those historical precedents do not set game amounts or authorize a dated automatic outcome. [Federal Archives, Strauß and Schalck-Golodkowski](https://www.bundesarchiv.de/themen-entdecken/online-entdecken/themenbeitraege/von-strauss-und-schalck-golodkowski-eingefaedelt/).
