# France party revision: additional source audit

Research note for `proposal.json` and `CHANGES.json`. The deal packages below are counterfactual governing programmes. A documented precedent supports the theme only; it does not establish that the proposed bilateral contract, its terms, or its game effects actually existed. No reviewed proposal guarantees energy delivery or changes NATO status/command. Two offer descriptions state intended industrial outcomes too certainly: PCF shipping says French yards “gain work” and Soviet services “gain capacity”; the PS monetary offer says France “gains room for monetary discipline.” Keep these contingent on acceptance and performance.

## Additional sources supplied and checked

1. **International Sociological Association, RC10, “History” (published in ISA Bulletin No. 30, 1982; prepared by Kurt Jonassohn).** The ISA history records the first international conference on participation and self-management at Dubrovnik, Yugoslavia, on 13–17 December 1972 and the second in Paris on 7–10 September 1977. This is good evidence for real French-Yugoslav intellectual and professional contact on worker participation. It does not document a state treaty, government-funded industrial exchange, or the specific PSU package. Use it as a precedent for a proposed exchange, not a claim of an existing bilateral programme. [Source](https://www.isa-sociology.org/research-networks/research-committees/rc10-participation-organizational-democracy-and-self-management/history).

2. **Dassault Aviation, “Super-Étendard.”** The manufacturer’s history says Argentina ordered 14 Super-Étendards in July 1979; it also describes the 1973 French Navy contract and 1974 maiden flight. This supports an actual French naval aviation export precedent to Argentina. The proposed training, technical support, reciprocal funding and party programme are new terms, and should be presented as an alternate-history agreement. The order is later than the 1970 start. Dassault is the source author/publisher; no separate page author or reuse licence is stated. [Source](https://www.dassault-aviation.com/en/passion/aircraft/military-dassault-aircraft/super-etendard/).

3. **Archives nationales du monde du travail, inventory 2012 007, Jean-Pierre Brulé / CII records.** The inventory lists Unidata subagreements and an agreement among CII, Philips and Siemens dated 4 July 1973, alongside 1972 preparatory records. This confirms a real tripartite European computing cooperation precedent. Do not describe the proposed France-Germany computing consortium as that exact agreement: Philips was a partner, and the consortium was established in 1973, not at the January 1970 start. Credit: Archives nationales du monde du travail; the inventory identifies the fonds and record creator as CII / Jean-Pierre Brulé. Specific page author and reuse licence: unknown. [Finding aid PDF](https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/camt/fr/egf/donnees_efg/2012_007/2012_007_INV.pdf).

4. **Deutsche Bundesbank, historical timeline.** The Bundesbank dates the Werner Report to 1970, the European exchange-rate arrangement (“snake”) to 24 April 1972, and the European Monetary System to 13 March 1979. This is the date check for the PS monetary proposal: coordination was debated in 1970, but EMS/ECU mechanisms must not be represented as already operating then. Credit: Deutsche Bundesbank. Page licence: unknown. [Source](https://www.bundesbank.de/en/bundesbank/history).

## Party and chronology checks

- The proposals are party-government alternatives. Do not say that PCF, PSU, PS, ecology or nationalist-right governments actually led France in this period or signed these packages.
- `SER` is the scenario tag for Yugoslavia in 1970; `YUG` is not the correct recipient tag here.
- Nothing in the reviewed new party offers should grant or remove NATO membership or place forces under NATO command. The two PCF Soviet offers are economic/commercial and civilian technical programmes, not alliance changes.
- The monetary offer must not refer to the euro or imply that the EMS/ECU existed in 1970. The 1970 Werner Report is the relevant early planning precedent; the EMS dates from 1979.
- Direct agreement records and public-sector feasibility references were not found for the exact proposed UDR investment, RI licensing/export-credit, Centre mutual-insurance/naval-school, PS-Algeria credit, ecology, PCF-Italy, or PSU treaty packages. Keep these expressly described as plausible or counterfactual cooperation, with actual precedents named only as inspiration.
