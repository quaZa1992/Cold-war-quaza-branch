## PCF example

Soviet Partnership becomes a real recipient-approved industrial offer. Each country pays 0.45% of its own current GDP at signature and funds 0.06% of its own annual GDP per year, recalculated as weekly expenditure divided by 52. France gains one civilian factory in Rhone-Alpes, its slot, and +6% civilian factory construction speed, with -3% foreign influence defence. The Soviet Union gains one civilian factory in Tatarstan, its slot, and +3% civilian factory productivity, with -2% corporate tax income.

The energy continuation and shipping continuation exclude each other and require the actual active industrial partnership in both countries. Energy offers France two +50% energy research uses and -4% civilian factory energy demand, with -4% foreign influence defence. The Soviet side receives two +50% energy research uses and +6% energy generation, with +3% education costs. Each pays another 0.20% of its own GDP; annual programme shares are 0.08% for France and 0.05% for the Soviet Union, divided by 52 weekly.

Shipping offers a French dockyard and Soviet commercial office, their slots, finite naval research and specialist output bonuses, with tax concessions and separate GDP funding. The autonomous PCF course has its own Italian cooperative partnership rather than merely foregoing Soviet rewards. The final PCF settlement follows one selected agreement through a single OR prerequisite group.

## Shared contract

Party branches stay visible. Native party-in-power predicates gate proposing a deal and domestic party-programme execution. Domestic programme modifiers deactivate outside office and restore earned values on return without replaying finite awards. Signed state agreements survive elections. Both standing spirits and funding suspend during bilateral war, native embargo, any mirrored Imperia sanction class, or loss of an actual active parent or required membership. Restoration does not repeat setup fees, buildings, experience or finite research.

Each named recipient receives the real acceptance or refusal decision. The focus inspector shows both prospective packages before signature, including while locked. Acceptance must recheck the complete transaction before either country pays. Every asset grants its shared slots before construction in the same one-time transaction; ownership, full control and native physical capacity remain separate requirements.

All Treasury setup shares use the payer's own current GDP. Recurring commitments are annual GDP shares divided by 52 for weekly MD expenditure. There is no flat-price or Treasury-balance requirement. Runtime money literals have at most five fractional digits.
