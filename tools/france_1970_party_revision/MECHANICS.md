# France 1970 party programmes and bilateral effects

## Scope

Read-only design and preview notes for the current `tools/france_1970_runtime/runtime_design.json` party groups. This does not edit the approved concept, builder, emitted runtime, or loaded mod. Effect availability and meanings below are supported by current MD or Imperia source providers and consumers. The proposed party applications are design options, not approved rewards or balance values. Static source evidence does not establish final in-game balance or tooltip behavior.

## Current party focus findings

The party groups use native ruling-party requirements. The source mapping is in `tools/france_1970_design/verified_effects.json` and `tools/france_1970_design/mechanics_reference.md:11-16`: `ri` is slot 2 Liberal, `centre` slot 14 Neutral Conservative, `radical` slot 16 Neutral Libertarian, `ps` slot 3 Social Democrat, `pcf` slot 4 Communist, `psu` slot 18 Neutral Social, `ecology` slot 17 Neutral Green, and `right` slot 20 Nationalist Right Wing Populist. Keep these requirements visible and use those exact predicates. Do not make a branch disappear when its party is out of office.

The current PCF `soviet_friendship` node (`runtime_design.json:3559-3612`) is a follow-up, not an offer origin. It gives `land_doctrine_cost_factor = -0.06` and `foreign_influence_modifier = -0.05`; its conditions include both the actual Communist ruling-party trigger and `has_idea = COLDWAR_FRA2_soviet_science_france`. That spirit is currently awarded by the separate general `soviet_science` focus (`runtime_design.json:7959-8033`), whose offer is not party-gated. If the intended gameplay is a PCF-originated Soviet agreement, either route that existing offer through this party focus or make a new, distinct offer with a new identity. Do not keep the old general offer and add a second Soviet deal that repeats its research, modifiers or GDP stream. The focus-originating focus should retain its exact party guard, while the named Soviet player retains real acceptance. Once signed, keep the accepted standing agreement durable; use a PCF active-party check only for a party-specific follow-up programme, not to erase a completed bilateral signature.

Other existing party nodes already spend many common modifier slots. Examples include PS `political_power_factor` and `stability_factor`, PCF `industrial_capacity_factory` and factory-efficiency modifiers, PSU `army_core_defence_factor` and `training_time_army_factor`, Ecology `energy_gain_multiplier` and civilian energy demand, and Radical `research_speed_factor`. Replace an overlapping generic modifier when adding a new outcome. Do not stack another small stability, political-power, bureaucracy, broad productivity or research-speed bonus onto a party route without recomputing the compatible route total.

These are package directions for the design owner to select and cost. They do not constitute a proposed value table. Existing accepted deals are permanent agreements whose spirits suspend or restore under their lifecycle predicate; a party's active programme is separate and should follow its actual ruling-party provider. This lets a treaty survive a government change without falsely claiming that its programme remains staffed by an out-of-office party.

## Compatible stacking review

The current `GDP_REWARD_REVIEW.md:60-76` provides static whole-route upper bounds for the current focus payloads before starting spirits and bilateral spirits. Notable current ceilings are: PS `social_cost_multiplier_modifier +0.37`, Radical `education_cost_multiplier_modifier +0.26`, PS `bureaucracy_cost_multiplier_modifier +0.28`, UDR plus NATO `production_factory_efficiency_gain_factor +0.30`, UDR plus NATO `equipment_cost_multiplier_modifier +0.27`, UDR `civilian_factories_productivity +0.16`, PS `political_power_factor +0.25`, PSU `training_time_army_factor +0.13`, and National Right `army_core_defence_factor +0.14`. These bounds combine compatible nodes; they are not one simultaneous cross-party build. The original report explicitly excludes standing agreement spirits. New bilateral packages must be added to their own party route and checked against any already signed compatible agreement, not treated as a separate mutually exclusive party.

The project design tree's exclusive siblings matter. For example, PS `redistribution` and `competitiveness_ps`, Liberal `sound_franc` and `credit_expansion`, and multiple PCF alternatives cannot all be selected together where their exclusion is specified. New output cannot be calibrated by adding every node in a party group. Conversely, `soviet_friendship` currently follows `planned_targets`; a new deal that is tied to that route would stack with the compatible PCF route unless its existing modifiers are replaced.
