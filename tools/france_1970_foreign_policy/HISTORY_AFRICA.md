# France and African cooperation, 1970-1990

This record supports an extension of the installed French tree. Historical institutions and precedents are separated from alternative policy packages. All costs, modifier values and GDP shares in the design are game-balance choices, not measurements of historical contracts.

## Monetary institutions and competing settlements

The franc-zone arrangements combined fixed parities, reserve pooling, transfer freedom and a French convertibility guarantee. Agreements in November 1972 and December 1973 increased African responsibility in the central and western unions; the BEAC and BCEAO headquarters subsequently moved to Yaounde and Dakar. The alternative packages therefore concern the terms and governance of an existing financial relationship. They do not pretend France founded the CFA system in 1970, turn reserve deposits into French-owned money, or model the whole currency regime with a spirit. [Banque de France, historical monetary cooperation](https://www.banque-france.fr/fr/banque-de-france/partenariats-afrique-france).

**Design:** a settlement-service package can give France paid banking work and the partner financing institutions, with fiscal concessions and French service commitments. A more African-led package offers larger local services and technical training, while France finances a greater share and receives fewer direct fees. Fees are proposed bilateral services, not a claim that historical reserve pooling was a tax paid to France.

## Defence, basing and advisory obligations

The defence agreement of 24 April 1961 covered France, Cote d'Ivoire, Dahomey and Niger. The later official impact study distinguishes military instruction and equipment assistance from paid logistical support, including a 1965 support convention. This supports separately negotiated training, access and logistics choices. No package automatically controls a partner's government, puppets the country or commits it to an unrelated war. [1961 ratification law](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000000509033), [French government impact study](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/contenu/Media/Files/autour-de-la-loi/legislatif-et-reglementaire/etudes-d-impact-des-lois/ei_art_53_2012/ei_partenariat_defense_cote_d_ivoire_cm_21.11.2012.pdf.pdf).

France's recurring interventions in Chad included Limousin, Tacaud and Manta. The official military history describes Manta's deployment and its support from Bangui and Libreville. These were costly expeditionary commitments, not free permanent combat bonuses. [French Ministry of Armed Forces, OPEX history](https://www.defense.gouv.fr/chemins-memoire/histoire-memoires/ressources-historiques/operations-exterieures/memoire-opex/1963-2011-50-ans).

**Design:** host-approved access and airfield support improve actual deployment possibilities and partner capabilities, while France funds personnel and logistics. A competing national-cadre programme gives the host stronger domestic military institutions and gives France bounded doctrine/training rewards without foreign access. These are alternative priorities; they do not recreate a particular intervention or generate an automatic war.

## Gabon: extraction, processing and negotiated returns

The Senate's bilateral history records the defence relationship, French investment and the sectors involved: petroleum, timber, uranium, manganese, finance and transport. It identifies French construction of the Transgabonais, inaugurated in 1986. The later report's 1990s aid figures are not reused as 1970 budget values. [French Senate, Gabon bilateral relations and investment history](https://www.senat.fr/rap/l97-002/l97-002.html).

**Design:** an oil-service concession can favour French contractual revenue and a smaller recipient investment package. A processing compact creates more production and training in Gabon, with a greater French financing commitment and less direct rent. Neither package reserves oil for France: native export modifiers change the exporter's realized market earnings. The Gabon basing/advisory choice is a distinct negotiated defence relationship.

## Niger: investment rights and real mine development

SOMAIR was constituted in 1968 following the 1967 France-Niger agreement; its first yellowcake was produced in January 1971. The contemporary industrial record reports later COMINAK production beginning in 1978. These provide a chronology for investment and operating agreements, without making later production a January 1970 fact. [SOMAIR anniversary report, Orano](https://www.orano.group/docs/default-source/orano-doc/expertises/tour-des-implantations/niger-mines/somair_rapport2017_2018_haute_re_solution-pdf-compress%C3%A9-min.pdf?sfvrsn=b803693d_2), [UNIDO mining-sector report](https://downloads.unido.org/ot/48/10/4810358/15001-20000_15709.pdf).

**Design:** the concession option offers French funding, infrastructure and recipient-approved uranium resource rights through the native investment system. Its counterpart keeps its own resources and receives more local industrial training and processing investment. Neither gives a uranium mine, raw production, a fixed delivery or guaranteed reactor fuel. The loaded deposit registry places the investable Niger deposit in state 329; the content uses that actual provider and does not silently redraw geological data or claim its native map label precisely locates Arlit.

## Cameroon and institutional cooperation

The 1974 cooperation agreements included economic/financial and personnel assistance. The official French treaty inventory dates the technical-personnel cooperation agreement to 21 February 1974. This supports administrative and technical institutions, rather than treating a later named convention as already signed in January 1970. [French Senate, 1974-1975 treaty deposits](https://www.senat.fr/dossiers-legislatifs/depots/depots-1974.html), [Senate bilateral defence history](https://www.senat.fr/rap/l01-228/l01-2286.html).

**Design:** central-African monetary services and local treasury capacity are prospective renegotiations grounded in the broader relationship. Their exact contracts are counterfactual selectable packages, not verbatim reproductions of a treaty.

## The 1990 horizon

Mitterrand's La Baule speech explicitly linked French support with political opening. It is evidence for a later alternative to purely executive relationships, rather than proof that client networks disappeared in 1990. The proposed more transparent and locally governed options can anticipate this direction under an alternative government; they are not labelled as completed La Baule policy in 1970. [Elysee, speech of 20 June 1990](https://www.elysee.fr/francois-mitterrand/1990/06/20/allocution-de-m-francois-mitterrand-president-de-la-republique-sur-la-situation-economique-de-lafrique-les-possibilites-daide-des-pays-les-plus-riches-et-la-position-francaise-en-matiere-de-cooperation-et-daide-financiere-la-baule-le-20-juin-1990).
