IMPERIA COLD WAR — France v5 mechanics and recipient-owned agreement design

Status: design and reference audit only. No loaded game file is changed by this proposal. The companion mechanics_catalog.json contains 44 individually sourced mechanics: 19 native country modifiers, 17 registered MD/project modifiers with actual readers, and eight native or MD effect APIs. Installed main MD examples are calibration references, not a promise that their magnitude, sign, historical context or late-game economic scale fits France in 1970.

1. Concrete distinctions for the redesign

Use outcomes that affect different decisions instead of giving every military and foreign focus organisation/research speed. Suitable families are:

| Family | Durable bilateral provider | Distinct finite/domestic outcomes to consider separately |
|---|---|---|
| Land industrial cooperation | idea equipment_bonus to infantry/utility/tank archetypes; factory output or efficiency; weekly land-equipment upkeep trade-off | Named domestic equipment design gain, finite armour research, paid arms works |
| Aviation | idea equipment_bonus to actual airframe classes; aircraft production/repair theme with paid maintenance obligations | Eligible airframe/module research, aviation school XP or doctrine uses |
| Maritime | dockyard output, repair/logistics, port and yard construction effects | Named naval doctrine bonus, paid yard/port asset, verified hull research |
| Civil investment and institutions | investment inflow slots, investment reward terms, agriculture/civilian-factory productivity, education/admin weekly expenditure | Paid infrastructure or extractive project, finite civil technology exchange |
| Trade, energy and diplomacy | resource extraction, civilian industrial energy efficiency, renewable/general generation, trade opinion or influence efficiency | Directed influence change, paid resource development, finite treasury settlement |
| Training, intelligence and assistance | training time, supply consumption, reinforcement chance, counter-intelligence points, operative capacity, Cold War aid capacity/efficiency | Army XP, limited doctrine reductions, intelligence institution establishment |

These are six nonstacking contractual families, not six identical reward bundles. A French government could maintain one agreement in each family simultaneously. There is no additional arbitrary global three-compact ceiling. The resulting maximum is six active family agreements, and compatibility with France's domestic military/economic programmes must be audited before selecting final magnitudes. A partner/family pair has a canonical receipt and canonical spirit provider shared across party variants; a new party cannot stack another copy by using a differently named offer.

The catalogue includes substantive civilian differences. Agricultural productivity enters agriculture's contribution/tax calculation; civilian-factory productivity enters its own economic contribution/output path; base productivity growth is a monthly growth input and country productivity growth is its multiplier; energy demand efficiency is not energy generation; investment inflow capacity is a flat project-slot limit; investment reward is a delivered-segment monetary return, not construction speed. Those distinctions should produce different policy purposes, benefits and actual budget obligations.

2. Unit and native API conclusions

- equipment_cost_multiplier_modifier is weekly land-equipment upkeep. Its reader in common/scripted_effects/00_money_system.txt:3207 constructs 1+modifier and applies it to equipment_operative_cost (:3216). It cannot be described as cheaper production. Negative saves upkeep; positive is an expense.
- Genuine production IC changes are equipment_bonus child fields such as build_cost_ic. German.txt:581 provides a reversible idea equipment_bonus for train/utility classes with -0.10 and instant=yes; 05_bulgaria.txt:99 uses infantry_weapons_type -0.05; France:1481 uses small_plane_airframe -0.10. Removing the active spirit retracts its provider. A free-standing add_equipment_bonus is a separate named equipment gain; no verified removal API was found, so it must not be smuggled into an agreement advertised as fully retractable.
- experience_gain_army is flat Army XP per day, distinct from experience_gain_army_factor. Vanilla experience_gain_army_tt formats +=2, while installed MD overrides it with percent formatting. intelligence_agency_defense likewise represents flat counter-intelligence points with a conflicting MD percentage formatter. Use meaningful flat values and verify the live tooltip rather than labelling 0.075 as 7.5%.
- land_reinforce_rate is additive hourly reinforcement probability: +0.02 means +2 percentage points to the hourly chance. It is not a 2% relative increase.
- common/doctrines/_documentation.md:29–37 explicitly documents [folder_name]_doctrine_cost_factor: -0.15 is a 15% cost reduction to grand doctrines and subdoctrines in that folder. Native finite add_doctrine_cost_reduction has actual MD examples, including Australia:955 with cost_reduction=0.5, uses=2, category=land_doctrine. These are doctrine benefits, not technology research bonuses. Source documents do not replace an engine check of legacy finite bonus behavior in the current doctrine system.
- productivity_growth_modifier is a flat base monthly growth input. The reader multiplies it by 0.5 and then the country growth factor; +0.05 is not +5% immediate output. country_productivity_growth_modifier affects the multiplier, with a 0–100 clamp on 1+modifier. State catch-up and state growth apply subsequently.
- energy_use_modifier_civs is a fractional demand multiplier despite the potentially misleading name. The consumer at !_energy_effects.txt:254 computes industrial_complex_total × (1+modifier) ×0.5 ×display_multiplier. Thus -0.10 reduces that demand base by 10%; it does not grant flat energy or power plants.
- imp_investment_reward reads the recipient controller's modifier but credits a proportional delivered-segment return to the investor's int_investments balance. Do not label it a recipient treasury gift. imp_investment_in_extra adds integer incoming project slots to a GDP-tier baseline.
- imp_sanction_resistance feeds 1-resistance into sanctions penalties. It does not erase actual sanctions-array membership: a compact still ends when the two signatories sanction each other, even if either can absorb sanctions economically.
- mdcw_cw_assistance_capacity is flat capacity; assistance_effectiveness multiplies existing aid efficiency. These are separate Cold War system levers, not general national research or flat foreign influence.

Each JSON calibration retains its signed observed amount and explicitly labels whether it is a benefit or a penalty/cost. For example, a negative trade-opinion or resource-extraction entry is a cost-side reference, not a proposed positive reward. No universal rule says a French modifier must be smaller than one arbitrarily selected upstream spirit; final values require compatible-route and family sums.

3. Equipment classes, DLC and 1970 research

The native folder gates are common/technology_tags/00_technology.txt:232–239: armour_folder without No Step Back, nsb_armour_folder with it; fixed_wing_folder without By Blood Alone, bba_aircraft_folder with it. The JSON includes a DLC matrix with actual technology categories and project guards.

For armour, mbt_tech_1 (NSB_armor.txt:237) is a 1975 technology requiring sp_armoured_vehicle_project and enabling medium_tank_chassis variants. Legacy MBT_2 (armor.txt:92) is 1975 and its actual equipment definition MD_x_tank_chassis.txt:1274 declares archetype=medium_tank_chassis. Both technologies include CAT_main_battle_tanks and CAT_tanks, but only the designer route has that project prerequisite. A shared archetype bonus therefore does not justify bypassing different research eligibility.

For aircraft, BBA gen_4_light (BBA_aircraft.txt:226) is 1980, requires sp_aircraft_project, enables small_plane_airframe_2, and uses CAT_light_aircraft. Legacy AS_Fighter2 (fixed_wing.txt:86) is 1985 and uses CAT_medium_aircraft; MD_x_plane_airframes.txt:329 declares archetype=medium_plane_fighter_airframe. These cannot be treated as category-equivalent light-airframe targets simply because both are aircraft. A Mirage or interceptor programme must choose actual relevant classes for each DLC configuration, including earlier 1960s/1970s technologies where appropriate. CAT_aircraft is broad and meaningful, but a broad category should not accidentally let a narrowly described programme subsidize every aircraft class.

Research awards must list actual unresearched technology eligibility, use the active DLC folder and enforce separate required-project guards. A future technology may receive an eligible finite research bonus; it is never instantly unlocked by this proposal. Project availability in the selected 1970/DLC configuration must be tested before promising a designer research route. No uranium state-history production or free mine is proposed: surveyed uranium requires paid development under the existing project contract.

4. Recipient-owned acceptance decisions

A focus named for a fixed country sends a fixed invitation at completion, not a France target-picker decision. For example, a focus concerning an Anglo-French aviation programme writes the France/ENG/aviation pending receipt and creates no foreign benefit. ENG then owns two ordinary decisions: accept the named proposal, or decline it. The decisions' ROOT is the recipient, and France is entered by an explicit FRA scope. There is no FROM dependency, recipient event reward, automatic alliance, economic punishment or automatic acceptance.

Native ordinary decisions are feasible without targeted-decision machinery: allowed/visible constrain the owning country, available holds the quote's meaningful requirements, complete_effect performs the choice. Current native MD common/decisions/00_research_slot_decisions.txt:1–27 demonstrates a country decision's repeat enable/timing/effect fields; country-flag age predicates are used in 05_CHI_decisions.txt:4487 and the existing project. A dated pending flag is sufficient to make both recipient choices visible for at most 90 days. Acceptance availability rechecks the date, counterpart existence, sovereignty, peace, all eight mutual sanctions memberships, sender mandate and both countries' current funds/PP. Its transaction repeats those checks before charging or granting either side.

Use separate, fixed pair/family quote receipts. A second offer must not overwrite the first pair's government epoch snapshot. Eligibility can be displayed while the decision is disabled for funds; hidden exact scenario/date/receipt guards must not drown the visible cost and requirement list. Prospective native effect_tooltip rows remain outside execution guards, with actual side effects hidden and guarded. Both countries see named numeric costs/spirits, with native MD treasury units and weekly expenditure modifiers.

Expiry need not schedule a world update. At age >89 days, the pending flag becomes inert: recipient choices hide, acceptance fails, no money was charged, and a new fixed reissue may clear/replace the old receipt. Accepted state is distinct from pending state. A single optional timeout mission can clear cosmetic receipts, but it must not be implemented as days_remove=90 on an 'accept' action: days_remove applies an activated decision's duration and is not itself a recipient response deadline.

No repeatable=yes national-focus pattern was found in the current national_focus source corpus. Do not promise that syntax or assume a repeatable focus continues to satisfy has_completed_focus semantics. To permit reoffers after refusal, expiry, withdrawal, sanctions or war, use a plain fixed-partner French reissue decision unlocked by the completed country-specific focus. It has no target selector and grants no partner effects. It only restamps the same fixed invitation after eligibility/cooldown checks. This is compatible with replacing the current generic targeted-offer UI. If the user forbids even fixed reissue decisions, fresh reoffers require another explicitly approved sender interaction; endless recipient reconsideration without renewed sender intent is not equivalent to fresh bilateral consent.

5. Permanent bilateral state without world scans

Use canonical family/pair identifiers and fixed tags, for example France/ENG/aviation, with distinct France and partner idea IDs if their approved effects differ. On acceptance, stamp both active receipts and the family lock before granting the two unlimited add_ideas spirits. No add_timed_idea is used. An ordinary government change does not remove accepted receipts or spirits. Domestic party programmes remain separately owned; no party-dependent allow_branch may hide other party branches. Party-specific availability/completion guards can prevent execution while preserving inspection.

A maximum-one-per-family France lock prevents duplicate stacking across parties and different partners. Each receipt is keyed by family and bilateral pair, with one-use technology/XP/project receipts separately retained across expiry, withdrawal and government changes. A new party may renew or replace a treaty only through fresh recipient consent and approved setup costs; it does not replay consumed finite grants. Automatic replacement is not acceptance.

For sanctions, in the fixed partner scope use eight explicit is_in_array tests: imp_<dip|fin|eco|mil>_sanc_on_us and imp_<type>_sanc_by_us, each with value=FRA. Runtime diplomatic UI complete_effect writes exactly these mirrored arrays (imp_sanctions_diplomatic_actions.txt:34–49,126–141,226–241,326–341); the historical support helpers use the same contract. No opinion threshold or invented sanction flag is needed.

Native idea cancel guards can directly test the pair's fixed counterpart, bilateral war, sanctions and active receipt. Weekly cleanup then reconciles only France's six family locks and their saved/known fixed counterpart, clearing both ideas and receipts on withdrawal, war, sanctions, lost counterpart or broken linkage. Either country gets a fixed withdrawal decision with an actual current pair identity guard. A stale receipt on a restored former country cannot withdraw another country's current deal.

The cleanest initial implementation is six explicit family dispatches into fixed pair helpers, gated before any counterpart work. If arrays are preferred, keep only active compact partners (bounded at six family entries), use verified for_each_scope_loop scope semantics from imp_sanctions_effects.txt:136 onward, and defer removal from the iterated array until after iteration. Adding/removing arrays exists natively, but safe mutation while iterating was not established. Never add an every_country daily loop. A fixed counterpart helper also handles disappearance without relying on every_country, which excludes deleted countries. Native idea cancellation protects orphan copies; weekly counterpart cleanup clears sender locks so a deleted/annexed recipient cannot block the family permanently.

Accepted modifiers may be retracted on termination; already completed knowledge, XP and physical infrastructure remain only if the approved design explicitly classifies them as durable one-time gains. Preserve their receipts. Sanctions lifting or a returning party must never auto-regrant a terminated agreement. These lifecycle rules are contract design, not a claim of observed engine ordering.

6. Outstanding verification before implementation

Source checks establish usable providers, script consumers, current labels and reference magnitudes. They do not prove engine rendering, simultaneous multiplayer decisions, DLC project reachability, native idea-cancel timing or save migration. Before runtime work, independently review the final named-country design for historical institutions, recipient incentives, specific class/category matches and whole-route stacking; then model refusal, expiry, insufficient funds, simultaneous accept/decline, each sanction direction, bilateral war, government alternation, annexation/restoration, repeated reissue and withdrawal. Render hovers with real acceptance gates false as well as true. Resolve the two upstream MD flat-modifier formatter conflicts without inventing percent units.

Provenance: source code and native labels come from the local project and installed main MD paths recorded in mechanics_catalog.json. Original source notices remain authoritative; authors/licences beyond those notices are unknown here. This proposal contains no new third-party binary assets. See IMPERIA_COLD_WAR_NOTICE.md.
