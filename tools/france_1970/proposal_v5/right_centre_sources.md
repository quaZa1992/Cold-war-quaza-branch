IMPERIA COLD WAR — France right-centre foreign and military proposal sources

Research notes for `research_right_centre.json`. The proposal separates documented positions and agreements from counterfactual offer/event content. Offer names and reciprocal spirits are design proposals; their historical anchors do not claim that those exact treaties existed.

## Sources and supported claims

- **S01 — Élysée, Georges Pompidou biography and presidency chronology.** [https://www.elysee.fr/georges-pompidou](https://www.elysee.fr/georges-pompidou) Records Pompidou's December 1969 Hague summit, his February-March 1970 US visit, and repeated meetings with Soviet leaders. Supports the start-era diplomatic context; not a party manifesto.
- **S02 — French Senate, 2 December 1971 defense debate.** [https://www.senat.fr/comptes-rendus-seances/5eme/pdf/1971/12/s19711202_2593_2647.pdf](https://www.senat.fr/comptes-rendus-seances/5eme/pdf/1971/12/s19711202_2593_2647.pdf) UDR speaker Maurice Bokanowski identifies national independence, nuclear deterrence, territorial defense, reserves and service-national reform as defense concerns. This is a parliamentary position, not proof of unanimous UDR doctrine.
- **S03 — Élysée, Jacques Chirac biography.** [https://www.elysee.fr/jacques-chirac](https://www.elysee.fr/jacques-chirac) Places Chirac in UDR through 1975 and records his election as RPR president on 5 December 1976. This supports the date-aware UDR-to-RPR identity distinction.
- **S04 — Airbus, industrial history.** [https://www.airbus.com/en/newsroom/stories/2022-10-28-october-50th-anniversary-of-the-first-flight-of-the-airbus-a300](https://www.airbus.com/en/newsroom/stories/2022-10-28-october-50th-anniversary-of-the-first-flight-of-the-airbus-a300) Airbus Industrie was established as a French-law GIE on 18 December 1970, initially with SNIAS and West German Deutsche Airbus as equal shareholders; Spanish CASA joined in 1971 and British Aerospace entered the consortium in 1979. The UK supplied A300 wings as an associate earlier. This supports industrial workshare offers, not shared national military command.
- **S05 — Élysée, Mitterrand address on Franco-German military cooperation, 13 May 1987.** [https://www.elysee.fr/francois-mitterrand/1987/05/13/allocution-de-m-francois-mitterrand-president-de-la-republique-a-loccasion-du-seminaire-franco-allemand-du-centre-des-hautes-etudes-militaires-sur-la-cooperation-militaire-franco-allemande-paris-palais-de-lelysee-mercredi-13-mai-1987](https://www.elysee.fr/francois-mitterrand/1987/05/13/allocution-de-m-francois-mitterrand-president-de-la-republique-a-loccasion-du-seminaire-franco-allemand-du-centre-des-hautes-etudes-militaires-sur-la-cooperation-militaire-franco-allemande-paris-palais-de-lelysee-mercredi-13-mai-1987) Records activation of military clauses of the 1963 Élysée Treaty from 1982, a joint security commission, exercises, and officer training. Supports late-1980s consultation and training precedents; the proposed spirit still preserves separate nuclear command.
- **S06 — Élysée, Giscard d'Estaing message to Soviet leaders, 27 October 1974.** [https://www.elysee.fr/valery-giscard-d-estaing/1974/10/27/message-de-m-valery-giscard-destaing-aux-dirigeants-sovietiques-a-loccasion-du-50eme-anniversaire-des-relations-diplomatiques-franco-sovietiques-paris-le-27-octobre-1974](https://www.elysee.fr/valery-giscard-d-estaing/1974/10/27/message-de-m-valery-giscard-destaing-aux-dirigeants-sovietiques-a-loccasion-du-50eme-anniversaire-des-relations-diplomatiques-franco-sovietiques-paris-le-27-octobre-1974) Describes the October 1970 political-consultation protocol and expanding France-USSR contacts and exchanges. French diplomatic archives also describe civilian science/technology contacts in medical, nuclear-energy, and space fields: [https://archivesdiplomatiques.diplomatie.gouv.fr/ark:/14366/vb72df03q1hs](https://archivesdiplomatiques.diplomatie.gouv.fr/ark:/14366/vb72df03q1hs). The proposed pact excludes military-technology transfer.
- **S07 — French Senate, Michel Debré's defense debate, 5 November 1970.** [https://www.senat.fr/comptes-rendus-seances/5eme/pdf/1970/11/s19701105_1805_1836.pdf](https://www.senat.fr/comptes-rendus-seances/5eme/pdf/1970/11/s19701105_1805_1836.pdf) Debré described deterrence as the priority while also arguing that France needed forces able to intervene and could not ignore European and Mediterranean security. Supports the orthodox Gaullist resource-allocation fork.
- **S08 — Élysée, Mitterrand press conference after Franco-German exercises, 24 September 1987.** [https://www.elysee.fr/francois-mitterrand/1987/09/24/conference-de-presse-de-m-francois-mitterrand-president-de-la-republique-a-lissue-des-manoeuvres-franco-allemandes-sur-la-cooperation-militaire-et-leventualite-dun-conseil-de-defense-entre-la-france-et-la-rfa-a-ingolstadt-jeudi-24-septembre-1987](https://www.elysee.fr/francois-mitterrand/1987/09/24/conference-de-presse-de-m-francois-mitterrand-president-de-la-republique-a-lissue-des-manoeuvres-franco-allemandes-sur-la-cooperation-militaire-et-leventualite-dun-conseil-de-defense-entre-la-france-et-la-rfa-a-ingolstadt-jeudi-24-septembre-1987) Mitterrand explicitly states France was in the Atlantic Alliance but outside its integrated command, with autonomous deterrence and alliance obligations. Supports the no-command-transfer constraint; the proposed US compact is counterfactual.
- **S09 — French Senate, Jean Lecanuet biography.** [https://www.senat.fr/senateur/lecanuet_jean000442.html](https://www.senat.fr/senateur/lecanuet_jean000442.html) Records the Centre démocrate (1966), CDS (1976), UDF role (from 1978), Lecanuet's European commitments, and his foreign-affairs/defense responsibilities. Supports the centre path's date and institutional identity.
- **S10 — Vie publique, Giscard interview on the UDF, 19 March 1998.** [https://www.vie-publique.fr/discours/253046-valery-giscard-destaing-19031998-elections-regionales-auvergne](https://www.vie-publique.fr/discours/253046-valery-giscard-destaing-19031998-elections-regionales-auvergne) Giscard says he founded the UDF in 1978 to unite Christian-democratic social justice with liberal republican economic and political freedom. Retrospective testimony; supports the coalition's component traditions, not a January 1970 party label.
- **S11 — Vie publique, Giscard statement after the European Council of Paris, 13 March 1979.** [https://www.vie-publique.fr/discours/132482-interview-de-m-valery-giscard-destaing-accordee-a-la-television-francaise-a-lissue-du-conseil-europeen-de-paris-mardi-13-mars-1979](https://www.vie-publique.fr/discours/132482-interview-de-m-valery-giscard-destaing-accordee-a-la-television-francaise-a-lissue-du-conseil-europeen-de-paris-mardi-13-mars-1979) Giscard describes the EMS entering into force in March 1979 and France's contribution. Supports the liberal stabilization/coordination path.
- **S12 — Élysée, Rambouillet summit joint declaration, 17 November 1975.** [https://www.elysee.fr/valery-giscard-d-estaing/1975/11/17/declaration-commune-des-chefs-detat-et-de-gouvernement-de-la-republique-federale-dallemagne-des-etats-unis-damerique-de-france-du-royaume-uni-de-grande-bretagne-et-dirlande-du-nord-d-italie-et-du-japon-reunis-pour-le-premier-sommet-des-pays-ind](https://www.elysee.fr/valery-giscard-d-estaing/1975/11/17/declaration-commune-des-chefs-detat-et-de-gouvernement-de-la-republique-federale-dallemagne-des-etats-unis-damerique-de-france-du-royaume-uni-de-grande-bretagne-et-dirlande-du-nord-d-italie-et-du-japon-reunis-pour-le-premier-sommet-des-pays-ind) Primary declaration naming France, FRG, USA, UK, Italy, and Japan and discussing shared economic challenges. Supports the historical partner context for consultation, not any later invented bilateral spirit.
- **S13 — French Senate, Jean Lecanuet's 1971 parliamentary activity.** [https://www.senat.fr/table-nominative-archives/lecanuet_jean000442/5R/1971.html](https://www.senat.fr/table-nominative-archives/lecanuet_jean000442/5R/1971.html) Records Lecanuet asking in 1970 whether East-West opening should be complemented by faster European unification as a condition of French progress and security.
- **S14 — French Senate, 5 November 1970 debate, Lecanuet's collective-defense intervention.** [https://www.senat.fr/comptes-rendus-seances/5eme/pdf/1970/11/s19701105_1805_1836.pdf](https://www.senat.fr/comptes-rendus-seances/5eme/pdf/1970/11/s19701105_1805_1836.pdf) Lecanuet argued for collective European defense and an organized European presence in the Mediterranean. His speech documents one political position, not a settled centre consensus.
- **S15 — French National Assembly, Lecanuet debate, 1973.** [https://archives.assemblee-nationale.fr/5/cri/1972-1973-ordinaire2/046.pdf](https://archives.assemblee-nationale.fr/5/cri/1972-1973-ordinaire2/046.pdf) Lecanuet criticized the slow movement toward European union and argued for common European security and a redefined Atlantic relationship. Supports a collective-security fork.
- **S16 — Council of the EU, historical timeline.** [https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/history/](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/history/) Records the 1985 Schengen signatories, including France and Belgium. Supports a later cross-border cooperation precedent; the proposed French-Belgian municipal resilience accord is explicitly counterfactual.
- **S17 — Parti Radical, institutional history.** [https://parti-radical.fr/history/1800/](https://parti-radical.fr/history/1800/) Records the 1973 split and creation of the MRG, the 1978 participation of the Radical Party in creating the UDF, and the differences between Servan-Schreiber's centrist grouping and Fabre's left-radical line. Official party history, read alongside parliamentary records.
- **S18 — French National Assembly, Robert Fabre biography.** [https://www2.assemblee-nationale.fr/sycomore/fiche?legislature=50&num_dept=2881](https://www2.assemblee-nationale.fr/sycomore/fiche?legislature=50&num_dept=2881) Records Fabre's 1972 split with the pro-centre line, leadership of the left-radical movement, and signature of the Common Programme. Supports keeping the radical identity fork visible.
- **S19 — French National Assembly, Servan-Schreiber's 1975 budget speech.** [https://archives.assemblee-nationale.fr/5/cri/1974-1975-ordinaire2/034.pdf](https://archives.assemblee-nationale.fr/5/cri/1974-1975-ordinaire2/034.pdf) Servan-Schreiber argued that public funds should move from unproductive subsidies and military expenditure toward collective services and local authorities. This supports a contested reformist position, not a claim that every radical endorsed unilateral disarmament.
- **S20 — European Commission, EU enlargement history.** [https://commission.europa.eu/topics/enlargement/together-eu_en](https://commission.europa.eu/topics/enlargement/together-eu_en) Records Spain's EEC accession on 1 January 1986. The proposed Iberian regional-governance and engineering compact is a date-gated counterfactual.

## Tag and implementation caveats

- Current checkout tags checked: `GER`, `ENG`, `USA`, `SOV`, `ITA`, `BEL`, and `SPR` are active. **Do not use `WGR` for West Germany:** it is absent from this checkout's `common/country_tags`; the active country tag is `GER`, with East Germany (`DDR`) separate in the 1970 registry. `SPR` is the active Spain tag; `SPA` is Andalusia in this checkout.
- Any offer to Spain must be unavailable before 1986. The French-Radical row is intentionally a later-date concept, not a 1970 diplomatic option.
- War and sanctions are continuing spirit-validity conditions in this concept. A later implementation must demonstrate that both countries' spirits are removed together on either condition; event acceptance alone does not establish continuous validity.
- The five target political identities follow the existing France design's groups. The Radical Party's split makes a single unchanged post-1973 identity questionable; implementation should use existing date/party-state data or keep later Radical/MRG treatment visibly provisional.
- Existing France research lane labels and target-year claims are drawn from `tools/france_1970/approved_design.json` and its research evidence, not inferred from the historical political sources above. Validate each exact technology and any special-project/DLC guard again before implementation.
