IMPERIA COLD WAR — France political programme revision 5

This is a design proposal awaiting the user's explicit approval. It changes no loaded focus, spirit, event, decision, localisation or interface files. The installed revision and its frozen approved_design.json are retained.

The consolidated proposal contains260 focuses:210 retained IDs plus50 fixed-country offers replacing24 generic foreign-offer focuses. Of the210 retained focuses,96 economic/personnel/armament packages are reworked and24 narrow research focuses are connected to their funded procurement chain. Political branches, party identities, start leaders, election systems and three starting national spirits are retained. CID and FN retain their existing dated slot20 identity guards; future research keeps actual DLC/project eligibility.

The twelve programmes retain separate political, economic, military and diplomatic content. Hard choices concern a funded institutional allocation, production methods, scarce procurement capital and professional versus reserve commitments. Country cooperation is usually complementary within the selected allocation, rather than an arbitrary ban on dealing with a second country. A shared Gaullist civilian détente offer and orthodox alliance-deconfliction offer remain independent of their procurement fork.

Examples of distinctions now represented are equipment-class production IC costs; efficiency retention after changing production; narrow hull/airframe/armour/electronics research; finite doctrine reductions; air-wing training XP; flying/naval accident chances; air fuel consumption; service-specific weekly payroll; land-equipment weekly upkeep; civilian productivity; resource extraction; electricity demand; investment slots; sanction resilience; tax revenue; and debt interest in percentage points. The source catalogue contains52 verified mechanics;53 distinct modifier keys appear across retained and revised programmes, in addition to equipment and finite-effect APIs. Base productivity growth, flat XP/day and flat counter-intelligence were not mislabeled as immediate percent output or percentage bonuses.

Each of the50 proposals names its actual country in the focus. Completion costs25 PP and opens recipient-owned Accept/Decline decisions for90 days; no initial French target-picker decision exists. Acceptance checks current French mandate, both countries' sovereignty, peace, eight bilateral Imperia sanction-array memberships, both balances and family locks again before any debit. Most proposed setups cost each country20 PP and$50M; the Algerian co-development costs are deliberately asymmetric. Effect maps and costs are explicit for every side. No proposed treaty contains finite knowledge/XP awards that can be farmed through reissue.

Accepted paired spirits are indefinite and survive ordinary government changes. Mutual war, bilateral diplomatic/financial/economic/military sanctions, withdrawal, disappearance or loss of sovereignty terminates both. Lifting sanctions does not silently restore an agreement. Six nonstacking families permit several distinct relationships while preventing another party from adding duplicate versions of the same family. The fixed-country Reissue decision is a renewal channel after180 days, not a new country-selection menu; fresh recipient consent and costs are required. Runtime maintenance must remain confined to the six active family/counterpart records, with no daily world-country scan.

The family limit and reciprocal effects are balance proposals. Native modifiers often act across a signatory's economy/armed forces, rather than exclusively on trade between the two states; the preview labels their actual scope. Infrastructure-repair bonuses are an abstraction of response engineering, not a new disaster or pollution simulation. The Algerian agreement gives efficiency and industrial training, not free gas or an imposed resource trade. No offer changes a foreign regime, alliance, military access, borders, units or technology.

Coordinates use the original30-unit party stride. Military relative columns8/12 now use rows1–4; country offers occupy columns18/20 and24/26 on rows4/6. The two independent consultation offers use column16,row1. Political/economic neighbours remain in their original slots. OR prerequisite buses retain the choice of either personnel institution before independent armaments allocation. Thirteen native navigation shortcuts and opening camera at progress_contracts are specified. The preview uses these exact source coordinates and existing Cold War focus art; text bounds and line routing are browser approximations, not an engine render. New treaty ideas must use appropriate smaller existing idea art at implementation, never focus-size textures as spirit icons.

Historical sources distinguish documented treaties/platforms, later developments and governing extrapolation. In particular: Old Guard is a playable hypothetical current; CID had no evidenced comprehensive foreign/defence platform; ecology-led1970 government is counterfactual; FN1973/1988 policy is not contemporary RN policy; the1972 Common Programme was proposed, not enacted; the rapid-action force was a later socialist direction. Project country tags are verified rather than assumed from vanilla: GER,DDR,SER,HOL,NRY,GRE andSWI among the17 recipients.

Validation is divided deliberately. VALIDATION.json checks exact graph closure and compatible-route arithmetic, provider/class references, source keys, bilateral benefits/costs and zero rows. Independent V5_MECHANICS_REVIEW.md and V5_LAYOUT_REVIEW.md record corrections and their limits. Neither source-model checks nor browser inspection proves live HOI4 effects, native tooltips, multiplayer ordering, cancellation timing or save migration.
