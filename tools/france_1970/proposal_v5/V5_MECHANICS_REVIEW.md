Findings resolved during review

1. The original 'aircraft' equipment alias was wrong for the liberal multirole research path. BBA gen_4_medium (common/technologies/BBA_aircraft.txt:269) enables medium_plane_airframe_2 and cv_medium_plane_airframe_2, whereas the generic fighter alias addressed small_plane_airframe. Legacy MR_Fighter2 (fixed_wing.txt:502) declares archetype=medium_plane_airframe in MD_x_plane_airframes.txt:1341–1344. The regenerated design correctly adds the separate multirole alias and uses it for armament_liberal_a and the liberal German/British production agreements. The existing fighter alias remains BBA small_plane_airframe versus legacy medium_plane_fighter_airframe, matching the separately verified interceptor/fighter paths.

2. The initial centre research parents were reversed. Naval Escort Engineering followed the land supplier/depot chain, and Territorial Fire Support followed escort shipyards/refit. The regenerated design correctly routes research_centre_a through armament_centre_b_followup (Fleet Training and Refit), and research_centre_b through armament_centre_a_followup (Common Depot Standards).

3. All 24 new strategy roots initially granted only a default 25%, one-use doctrine bonus, with most using land doctrine even for water, repair-credit and civilian producer networks. Those grants were unrelated filler rather than distinctive civil/foreign policy outcomes. The regenerated design removes the 24 ceremony roots and connects the 50 fixed-country offers directly to the funded matching armament followup. This removes the repetitive finite-award layer without reducing recipient consent.

4. The socialist service-life path was renamed to describe armoured maintenance/modernisation rather than a smaller fleet with an unrelated new-MBT promise. Its research and logistical emphasis can now be assessed as a longer-service programme; utility support production is separate from granting tanks.

Mechanical coverage and units

All new modifiers were compared with catalogue/provider evidence. No flat daily-XP or flat counter-intelligence modifier remains in the new bundles. Their problematic MD percentage overrides therefore do not contaminate the proposal's display units. One-time army/air/navy experience awards are separate native XP pools, not percentage modifiers. Reinforce-rate values such as 0.01 and 0.0125 represent +1 and +1.25 percentage points/hour. Doctrine cost factors are fractional cost changes (negative reduces cost) under common/doctrines/_documentation.md:29–37. Native finite doctrine awards use their own uses/bonus contract rather than technology categories.

The production-cost equipment keys are all aliases over actual equipment archetypes, not equipment_cost_multiplier_modifier. Tank medium_tank_chassis is supported for the NSB designer route and legacy MBT_2's declared archetype. The proposed provider is an idea's equipment_bonus, which retracts with idea removal, rather than a free-standing add_equipment_bonus. Utility targets both ordinary/heavy utility archetypes; signals, infantry and train aliases use their actual existing native classes. Multirole is now separated from the interceptor/light alias. Carrier derivatives are not implicitly promised by a ground-based airframe label; add them only to an explicitly scoped carrier programme with verified archetypes.

Fiscal and civil mechanics remain distinct: energy_use_modifier_civs and energy_use_modifier_mils scale the relevant demand base by 1+modifier; pop_energy_use_multiplier concerns population demand. Civilian-factory productivity is not a factory building grant. Country productivity growth is not instant output. Incoming investment capacity is integer slots. Interest-rate changes use the native additive percentage-point contract, while weekly expenditure modifiers apply to their actual existing budget bases. The regenerated design contains no zero-valued modifier rows in the inspected draft.

Bilateral consent, stacking and repetition

The agreement_rules specify six families and one active France-linked family agreement per signatory across French parties. That supports up to six concurrent enduring families instead of the former one-global-compact lock. All 50 foreign offers have empty awards arrays on both sides: there is no finite research/XP award to farm through reissue. Each side has explicit treasury_millions and Political Power charges; the asymmetrical Algerian terms are represented separately rather than inferred as identical.

The contract is sound only if implementation repeats all party/pending/date/peace/sanctions/funds/PP/family checks inside the acceptance transaction before its first debit. Check both balances against converted billion-unit treasury values (50 million = 0.05 billion; 75 million = 0.075 billion) before charging either country. Recipient accept/decline decisions own ROOT; France is explicit FRA, with no FROM-dependent recipient inference. Dispatch and reissue create a pending quote only. Multiple pending offers in one family must not permit two near-simultaneous acceptances: the shared family lock must be tested and set in the same guarded transaction. Proposal JSON alone cannot establish that future runtime code implements atomicity.

Accepted agreement modifiers survive ordinary government changes. Pending sender mandates remain government-sensitive. Withdrawal, mutual war, all eight local sanction memberships, disappearance and lost sovereignty terminate both active halves. No generic target-picker, forced alliance/access, foreign government change or automatic grant is required. Fixed-partner reissue decisions after cooldown provide new consent without unverified repeatable=yes focus syntax. Pending receipts need independent pair/family identities to prevent one quote from overwriting another.

Native idea cancel guards and bounded six-family/active-partner cleanup can enforce this without a daily world-country loop. Array removal while iterating is not source-proven safe; fixed-family dispatch or a deferred cleanup pass is preferable. Stale restored-country receipts must never identify a different current counterpart. Durable domestic finite awards require once-only receipts separate from accepted treaty state and party activation.

Compatible-route bounds — superseded baseline and current model

The earlier independent 996-route enumeration applied to the initial 284-node draft. It included foreign/strategy-node orientations and was incomplete as a proof of the final active domestic programme. Its old numeric envelopes are superseded; they must not be presented as final 260-node route coverage or current balance totals. Subsequent variety changes add service fuel, payroll, accident and retention effects, further invalidating those old figures as final sums.

Current VALIDATION.json is the relevant final source-model record. At this review it reports result=pass, 3,612 checks, 260 focuses, 50 offers, 52 catalogue entries and 53 used modifiers. It enumerates compatible complete routes for each active domestic programme and records independent per-modifier family overlay bounds, explicitly distinguishing those envelopes from one simultaneously attainable bundle. It retains starting and residual spirits separately and never adds mutually exclusive governments' domestic programmes together. Its engine_tested field is false. This review read the final model result; it did not rerun or certify an engine simulation.

The six-family rule prevents duplicate within-family stacking but permits the same equipment class or subsystem in different accepted families. Final envelope data therefore remains useful for assessing cross-family totals. A row can still be marginally irrelevant in a specific campaign if every target technology is researched, another source reaches an engine cap, the affected production line is absent or the country never uses that investment system. No blanket engine-noop or universal benefit claim follows from source sums alone.
