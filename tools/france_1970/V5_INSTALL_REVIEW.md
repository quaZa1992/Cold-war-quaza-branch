## Findings

The V5 source preserves native focus-tree conventions while making the programme guards visible. For example, `COLDWAR_FRA_progress_contracts` uses the `western_conservatism_are_in_power` trigger visible to the player and keeps the scenario, exact ruling-party slot, and earned receipt in `hidden_trigger`. Child focuses retain native prerequisite arrows and mutual exclusions; their effects show the PP charge and concise numeric dynamic-modifier changes. This follows the local 1970 German example `GEN_1970_expand_the_security_service` in `common/national_focus/mdcw_1970_generic_focus.txt`, which uses `add_tech_bonus` inside `completion_reward` with category, uses, and bonus fields.

The V5 military and research rewards are more conditional than that generic example, as intended by the approved design. `COLDWAR_FRA_armament_gaullist_b` previews a PP cost and air-doctrine/economic modifier changes, then selects the BBA or legacy equipment idea. The corresponding ideas cancel when the exact party or earned receipt no longer applies and select the correct aircraft archetype for DLC ownership. Its effect helper rechecks government, receipt, prerequisite, and exclusion conditions before recording completion. Across all 252 independently enumerated maximal legal routes, the source-model test reconstructed each programme modifier sum and equipment production-cost total, then removed and restored that party’s ideas without replaying finite rewards.

Research focuses expose meaningful native tooltips for unresearched target technologies and required special projects. For example, the Gaullist armor research focus checks the NSB project gate with `sp:sp_armoured_vehicle_project` and selects target technology IDs according to DLC ownership; completion helpers repeat those eligibility checks before issuing the one-time bonus. This is consistent with the native `add_tech_bonus` structure while avoiding unsupported equipment IDs in the wrong DLC profile. The source-model suite covers completed-tech and missing-project rejection for guarded research branches.

Startup is guarded by the project’s persistent `IMP_is_1970_scenario` predicate. `history/countries/FRA - France.txt` loads `COLDWAR_FRA_1970_focus` and invokes `COLDWAR_FRA_1970_initialize` inside that scenario block, preserving the existing 1970 Pompidou history, starting research, and economic setup. The initializer has a country-flag receipt and adds the three approved starting spirits exactly once: presidential republic, unsettled social compromise, and uneven modernisation. The daily/on-action refresh uses exact party and the CID/FN date boundary; party changes also refresh immediately. Refresh rebuilds active modifiers and removable equipment ideas from earned receipts without replaying PP, XP, doctrine, or research awards.

The 50 V5 country-offer focuses call their guarded send helper only from completion. The core’s `*_can_send` condition is checked again inside the helper, and the separate V5 offer implementation owns recipient consent, bilateral spirit lifecycle, no-war/no-sanctions validity, and migration of previously accepted legacy compacts. The reviewed migration deliberately preserves existing V4 receipts for the 210 retained focus IDs while treating the 50 V5 offers as new initiatives; the old 24 generic offer-focus receipts are not mapped onto them, which prevents bypassing the new parents and costs. Previously accepted legacy offers remain under their old IDs; pending legacy quotes may expire or cancel without a setup charge. Recipient-consent semantics were tested separately by the offer suite, not inferred from this core review.

## Comparison with installed main Millennium Dawn trees

I compared these outputs with the installed workshop copy at `E:/SteamLibrary/steamapps/workshop/content/394360/2777392649`. The source files reviewed were `common/national_focus/05_france.txt` (SHA-256 `b42f3b83177902c99d4eb860831674cc5d2c2318155eba2306ae4a5ed8f0d3ae`), `common/national_focus/05_russia.txt` (`db03d72e1fdbc239076eba9f7e9259e1791e909eab425186304cd6b14858b602`), and their English focus localisation files `localisation/english/MD_focus_FRA_l_english.yml` (`407ec84132cf97f522495beab4b474dd64380d3d7bfdcf6146d9c1b9f0021470`) and `MD_focus_SOV_l_english.yml` (`8a8a60b202fffa932fd5f8788b76281663e69747b69912dc5e37414673b82ac5`). These are local installed mod files, so the paths and hashes are the evidence; they are not claims about every upstream release.

For domestic economics, main-France `FRA_let_industry` (`05_france.txt:629-668`) costs 10 focus weeks, requires both `FRA_eternal_challenge` and `FRA_friend_eu`, and checks the social-democrat subparty plus a republic idea. Its English description (`MD_focus_FRA_l_english.yml:37`) explains an industrial-to-services policy choice; its reward changes party/outlook and four named economic variables, including industrial-complex speed `-0.03`, office speed `+0.075`, civilian-industry revenue `+0.075`, and tax efficiency `+0.025`. V5 `COLDWAR_FRA_industrial_participation` similarly uses a concise institutional policy description and a native numeric tooltip: 70 days, 50 PP, factory maximum efficiency `+0.05`, and efficiency gain `-0.025`. The framing matches the native policy-plus-modifier pattern while keeping the scenario receipt hidden.

For military content, main-Russia `SOV_reestablish_the_military_industrial_complex` (`05_russia.txt:13674-13706`) costs 3 focus weeks, requires `SOV_condemn_globalism`, excludes the civilian plan, and requires the relevant communist party plus an updated-military trigger. Its localisation (`MD_focus_SOV_l_english.yml:288-289`) is a short political statement. The reward combines a dynamic-modifier tooltip, arms-factory construction `+0.07`, a conditional popularity change, and a 720-day timed idea. V5 `COLDWAR_FRA_armament_gaullist_b` costs 70 days and 70 PP, requires either of its two military groundwork alternatives and the exact governing party, then displays air-doctrine `-0.08` and education-cost `+0.06` along with the DLC-appropriate removable airframe bonus. Both use named political intent and compact mechanical effects; V5 additionally removes its equipment benefit when the eligible party leaves office.

For foreign policy, main-France `FRA_friend_eu` (`05_france.txt:441-534`) costs 5 focus weeks, requires a republic idea, and conditionally shifts the matching party and outlook by `+0.05` in eligible Germany, Italy, Spain, England, and Belgium, with a further domestic `+0.10`; its localisation (`MD_focus_FRA_l_english.yml:30-31`) states the political rationale. `FRA_atlantist_drift` (`05_france.txt:708-749`) uses NATO-member opinion modifiers and a Soviet trust penalty. V5’s `COLDWAR_FRA_v5_gaullist_gre_01` costs 56 days and 25 PP to submit the Greek Mirage Maintenance Convention; its tooltip opens an accept/decline decision, and the recipient’s acceptance—not focus completion—creates the bilateral spirits. Those provide education-cost `+0.025` and air-fuel consumption `-0.04` on each signatory, with the offer’s localisation describing reciprocal maintenance and training. The agreements therefore preserve native concise focus effects and political prose while adding explicit counterpart consent and bilateral lifecycle rules that the selected main-France examples do not have.

## Verification and limits

`python tools/france_1970/test_core_v5.py` passes all 9 source-model tests. It checks inventory and unique IDs, helper guards, both DLC profiles, party mismatch, parent/exclusion conditions, one-time reward replay, research and project gates, start spirits and CID timing, and all 252 route totals including equipment ideas removed and restored after a party switch. These tests execute a small model of the emitted Clausewitz AST; they do not establish native parser acceptance, actual game tooltip rendering, multiplayer offer timing, or balance. No blocking source-level defect was found in the reviewed snapshot.
