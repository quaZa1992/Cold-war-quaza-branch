# IMPERIA COLD WAR — Project preferences

- New player-facing game text and localisation must be written in English only. Do not generate German or other translations unless the user explicitly requests them.
- Custom small-country starting spirits and focus programmes must not impose negative workforce-size modifiers. Represent specialist shortages with research penalties and align their focus remedies and previews.
- National programmes must fit the actual country's important parties, institutions and religious context. Do not invent Salafi/Shia paths for a non-Muslim country merely to fill MD outlook slots. Cuba's approved 120-focus limit and the rejection of available-workforce penalties for Cuban overseas/medical programmes remain Cuba-specific constraints.
- Player-facing localisation must suit the Cold War multiplayer RP setting and native MD presentation: use political and institutional language in names and descriptions, with concise numeric costs, requirements and consequences in effect tooltips. Keep implementation terms, debug explanations and code workflow out of normal player-facing prose.
- Effect fields must show only concrete native rewards, costs, signed modifier changes, named spirit changes and decision unlocks. Do not add explanatory custom-text paragraphs about programme ownership, consent, expiry, replay protection or implementation. Put necessary context in descriptions or visible requirements. Use native numeric formatters and standard MD headings where needed; a custom prose summary must never replace actual effect previews. Apply this to focuses, decisions, events and previews, including every branch of an approved tree.
- Recipient-approved focuses must always show both countries' complete prospective "If they accept" Effects directly in the focus tooltip and preview inspector: exact spirit names and every signed modifier, setup and recurring costs, buildings with slots, research and other rewards. Show the numbers before acceptance and while currently locked, unsigned or suspended. A spirit name, event title or later decision reference alone is insufficient. Keep real consent and activation guards in execution; previewing must never grant rewards or charge money.
- Whenever a focus, decision or event grants building levels, grant one extra state shared building slot per building level in the same state, before adding construction. This explicitly includes infrastructure, land forts and coastal forts, even when the building does not consume shared slots. Show both through native effects and keep the slot grant inside the same one-time transaction guard. Check actual province/state ownership, control and native capacity separately.
- Keep party focus branches visible and inspectable regardless of the governing party. Gate execution and party-programme activation through the actual ruling-party checks; do not use party-dependent `allow_branch` to hide their content.
- Reuse MD's existing party-in-power triggers and party-name localisation for visible party requirements. Do not invent labels such as "Governing political current" or combine present and future party names with arrows. Show the actual required party through the native MD yes/no requirement.
- Conversation with the user may remain in German.
- Do not use en dashes or em dashes in new player-facing text, previews or conversation. Use a colon, a full stop or ordinary compound-name hyphens.
- Cuban bilateral offers must originate from distinct Cuban national focuses. The recipient RP country receives the acceptance or refusal decision. Do not move Cuba's focus choices into a Cuban offer decision menu.
- Research and record historical precedents when adding or substantially changing national policies and selectable spirits. Use those examples to justify the benefits, costs and institutional trade-offs.
- Put the exact project marker `# IMPERIA COLD WAR — <feature>` (or the format's valid comment equivalent) at the very beginning of every new or modified custom code file, including generator templates and emitted output. Do not substitute the old `IMPERIA CW` abbreviation. Mandatory syntax stays first: put localisation's marker immediately after `l_english:`, and a skill's project metadata/body marker after its required frontmatter. In shared main-MD files, use a file-start comment identifying the custom integration, retain upstream notices/attribution, and also mark the actual custom additions rather than claiming the whole original file. Preserve required encoding and immutable approval snapshots.
- Begin project documentation and provenance records with `IMPERIA COLD WAR`. For strict JSON, put the project identifier in the relevant record rather than adding invalid comments or changing schemas used by runtime consumers. For binary images and other assets, use a companion provenance/notice file instead of inserting text into binary headers or adding visible watermarks. Record the source, original author/credit, applicable licence or permission, and modifications when known; explicitly mark missing information as unknown. A project marker does not establish ownership of third-party material or replace its licence. See `IMPERIA_COLD_WAR_NOTICE.md`.
- In agreement events, show costs and rewards as concrete numeric option effects in native MD style. Keep the description to a short explanation rather than repeating the effects in prose.
- Country-event pictures must use the current native MD photo frame and viewport, with appropriate imagery and unchanged outer frame/alpha pixels. A registered sprite or broad news picture is insufficient. Inspect the full donor window and adjacent option positions, widths and slots. The inspected MD frame is 217 x 163 with a 180 x 150 photo inset; recheck the live donor before reuse. Preserve this in the generator and distinguish a donor-composed preview from a live game screenshot.
- Approved standing Cuban bilateral agreements have no spirit expiry: both countries must exist, remain at peace with one another and impose no native embargo or any of the four mirrored Imperia sanction classes on one another. Suspend/restore both spirits without repeating setup fees or one-time assets; dependent deals require their actual active parent/membership spirit. Offers originate in Cuban focuses and reach the named RP recipient for real consent.
- Recurring monetary costs must use weekly MD units in runtime code and player-facing text. Convert source budgets before integration. Scripted budget literals must have at most five fractional digits; longer literals can be misread by the native fixed-point parser.
- Uranium starts with zero raw production and no built uranium mines. Surveyed deposits unlock paid development; they must not grant automatic state-history production. Audit complete donor GUI blocks, including adjacent positions, slot limits and entry widths; keyword-matched diff hunks alone do not establish a complete feature port.
- National-focus tooltips must hide internal scenario/date/country-routing checks and redundant predecessor receipts while retaining their real runtime guards. Show meaningful gameplay requirements, such as membership spirits, clearly.
- Organisation-specific focuses, including their entry focus, require the actual membership spirit in both availability and reward guards. Keep any necessary founding/admission agreement reachable before that member-only branch.
- Encoding: script files (`common/**/*.txt`, `events/*.txt`, `interface/*.gui|*.gfx`) must be saved as UTF-8 WITHOUT BOM. A BOM breaks the first statement of the file and, in `00_money_system.txt`, every inline math expression (the whole economy computes as 0). Only localisation `.yml` files use UTF-8 with BOM.
- Store local archives, native test profiles and repeated engine snapshots on `E:`, outside the mod directory. Use `E:/HOI4_Mod_Tools/mdcoldwar/runtime` for new private test runs and `E:/MDColdWar_Backups/Codex_Test_Archives` for retained test archives. Keep generators, compact reports, approved design inputs and actual mod assets in the repository. Never follow asset junctions when counting, copying or moving test data.
