## Engine conventions and limits



The compiler follows [native MD variant conventions](https://github.com/MillenniumDawn/Millennium-Dawn/blob/main/.claude/docs/oob-variants-reference.md). It preserves the researched bare-hull ordering described in [Paradox's January 2023 developer notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=394360&enddate=1675435046&feed=steam_community_announcements). It does not use a blanket modular `allow_without_tech` bypass.



Precise service-knowledge bundles are hidden, non-researchable native technologies with only documented fitted modules and, exceptionally, the exact native MD frame of an evidenced pre1970 operated model, without promoting its operator to the generic frame's later national research generation. Shared static models can use a common exact-type bundle when an ordinary unlock does not cover every DLC configuration in which that same design is created. Repeating an already available bare-type unlock in the alternative configuration creates no additional equipment or effect. They grant no industry modifiers, scientific bonuses, projects, buildings, money, ammunition stockpiles or deployed formations. Their role is to represent already known service equipment without copying an exporting country's entire research tree. Explicit nominal-year exceptions require evidence for the actual pre-1970 hardware and do not justify a future weapon or electronics package.



All new variant names are proper historical names or concise English descriptions. No images or model binaries are created by this task. No new military OOB, equipment quantities, fleets or air wings are supplied. Validation records distinguish source/model checks, a native startup observation, and unperformed save/reload or multiplayer checks.





The native medium aircraft primary slot accepts the existing MD cargo module, which was otherwise absent from its allowed categories despite a native medium transport role and GUI. No new weapon category or cargo module was introduced. An-2 uses a single propeller engine and native medium transport role. Its source: [Museum of Flight](https://www.museumofflight.org/Exhibits-and-Events/Aircraft/antonov-2-colt). P-3B uses the native maritime patrol role, four propeller engines, conventional first ASW payload and first MAD sensor tier. MAD existed on the original Orion well before the native MD 1970 sensor research date: [Lockheed Martin aircraft history](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/news/features/history/p-3-orion.html), [Wings of Freedom P-3B museum record](https://wingsoffreedommuseum.org/collection/lockheed-p-3b-orion/). These pages are evidence links; no imagery or source text was copied.
